# Blind Cells: The 88 Prompts as Perturbation

**A cross-source perturbation run against Kevin Kelly's *88 Prompts for Long Term Thinking*, read for what the framework has no field to author rather than for what it explains.**

**Status:** v0.1 draft — inbound direction. Companion: the outbound essay (what DR sees that the Long Now does not), unwritten and deliberately second; see §7. Builds on `seat-theorem-v1.md` (v2.6), `deferential_realism_paper_v8.md`, `commitment_systems/commitment_systems_sketch_v6.md`, `the_perturbation_principle.md` (§5.1, §6, §7.1).

**Source:** Kevin Kelly, "88 Prompts for Long Term Thinking," *The Technium*, published 2026-08-03; written January 2019 for internal use at The Long Now Foundation, of which the author was then co-chair. Prompts cited by their number in that list.

## 0. The declared seat

This document is seated and says so first.

**The seat:** that a source built independently of this framework, addressing the same object (commitments held across time), is a better perturbation of the framework's coverage than another instrument built inside it — and that the informative output is the **blind** column, not the agreement column.

Two things this seat is not. It is not a claim that the source is *right*; nothing here defers to Kelly's list as evidence about the world. And it is not a validation exercise: agreement between the list and the framework is expected, because the same operator holds the seat in both readings, and expected agreement carries no information (`the_perturbation_principle.md` §6 — an invariance is the null result one level up).

What the perturbation varies is **source**, holding seat fixed. That is a weaker move than varying the seat, and the footing rule is §5.1's: across distinct kernels the transfer is analogical, so a sibling *generates* a hypothesis and only an in-repo witness *rules* it. Kelly's list is the maximally distinct kernel — there is no mechanical alignment key between his prose and any authored field, so every mapping below is hand-authored in one pass. On the §7.1 grading that is **seat-constituted, not discovered**: ratifiable at best, silent-seat if adopted without ratification. Every finding below is therefore marked *inferred* and none may be cited as a detection result.

## 1. What the run is for

`the_perturbation_principle.md` names three columns in every diff operator: agreement (invariant), disparity (variant), and **blind** (uncovered). The essay-writing habit reads the first two. This run reads the third.

The question at each prompt is not *does the framework classify this?* but: **is there a field to author, a row to route to, or a verdict predicate to fire — and if not, is the absence declared?** `commitment_systems_sketch_v6.md` §11 exists precisely so that an empty region is never mistaken for a covered one. A blind cell that is already in §11 is not a finding; it is the discipline working. A blind cell that is not is either a build, a declaration, or a dissolution on inspection, and the essay's product is that routing, not the census itself.

## 2. Finding 1 — Corollary 3 presupposes an occupied confronting seat

**Grade: law-level. Routes to an operator ruling on `seat-theorem-v1.md` §5, not to a build.**

Corollary 3 is the only accountability the framework permits. Its content: seats are not interchangeable across time; the seat that stakes a prediction cannot occupy the later seat that confronts it; both are seated, but the earlier could not author the later's content. And the bound is stated with unusual care — what is secured is not that escape is impossible but that escape is *costly and visible*, legible as retreat. The closing line: the Theorem secures that the price exists and is visible; it does not secure that anyone pays it.

**Visible to whom.**

The corollary enumerates two dispositions at t3: the seat honors the confrontation, or it reabsorbs the disconfirming outcome by reinterpretation and pays the visible price. Both presuppose that a seat is *occupied* at t3 — that someone is standing at the later position to observe the retreat and register its cost.

Prompt 56 specifies a system for which that is false by design:

> *Let's design a system so that 90% of the benefits are delivered after we are all dead.*

Read against Corollary 3 this is not a moral proposal but a scheduling one. It arranges for the confrontation interval to fall where no seat capable of pricing the retreat is occupied. The disposition at t3 is then neither honoring nor reabsorption but a third thing the corollary does not name: **unattended** — the commitment is confronted by events with no occupant present to read the confrontation as confrontation.

Prompts 58 and 59 close the escape route the framework might otherwise take, which is to say the later seat is occupied by the beneficiaries themselves:

> *We are committed to representing the interests of the future to the present. The present needs no defenders. The citizens of the future are unborn, so we the living will be their proxy.*

The proxy relation runs one direction only. A proxy can speak for the position; the position cannot confront the proxy. So the seat that stakes the prediction and the seat nominally authorized to confront it are held by the same party, at the same time — which is exactly the non-interchangeability Corollary 3 relies on, defeated not by argument but by arrangement.

**Why this is a blind and not a disagreement.** The framework has no field for this. `cs_drift_state` authors a gap against a reference frame; `cs_terminal_attractor` extends it; neither carries a *scheduled confrontability* attribute. The `Acknowledged` bit records whether drift is acknowledged, not whether an acknowledger is structurally available in the relevant interval. And §6.5 already concedes the adjacent point about provenance — the acknowledged bit is authored, the confrontation path unbuilt — but concedes it as a limitation of the apparatus, not as a property an object can have.

**What resolution would change.** Either Corollary 3 gains a declared occupancy precondition (the temporal asymmetry secures visible cost *given* an occupied t3), or a defense is given for why the unattended case reduces to reabsorption. The first looks right and is cheap; the second would be a stronger result and I do not see how it goes.

## 3. Finding 2 — Q5's foreclosure-set is missing a member

**Grade: buildable, small, precise. The cleanest finding in the run.**

`seat-theorem-v1.md` §6.2 struck the interrogative type exemption and replaced it with a price: a question's seat-cost is what it structurally cannot return, **enumerable rather than felt**. Q5 is given with its price:

> *If this vanished, would the world rearrange or stay the same?* — the liveness test, and it is the human form of the σ/seat distinction itself: a thing whose removal rearranges nothing has no stakeholders and is fixed-by-σ; a thing whose removal forces rearrangement is a live parameter with interested parties.

Declared foreclosure: Q5 cannot return "wrong partition."

**The unenumerated second foreclosure.** Q5 is evaluated at a time slice. "Would the world rearrange" is asked of the world that presently exists, and its stakeholder detection is therefore contemporaneous. An arrangement whose interested parties are not yet present returns *stay the same* — no rearrangement, no stakeholders — and is routed to fixed-by-σ, a natural fact, not selected for the discipline's scarce attention.

That is a false negative by construction on exactly the class of object the Long Now is built to make. It is not an error in Q5; it is a cost Q5 pays that is not in its declared price list. §6.2's own standard is that the blind spot "can be listed in advance," and this one has not been.

**Proposed addition to Q5's foreclosure-set:** *Q5 cannot return a live parameter whose stakeholders are non-contemporaneous.* One sentence, no machinery, and it discharges the requirement §6.2 sets for itself.

**A note on scope.** This does not say Q5 should be fixed. A temporally-indexed liveness test ("would the world rearrange, at any horizon?") admits every arrangement whatsoever, since some future stakeholder can be constructed for anything — the question would return interested answers universally and stop selecting. The finding is that the current form's cost is real and unlisted, not that a better form is available.

## 4. Finding 3 — the battery's balance requirement, checked on the temporal dimension

**Grade: inferred; routes to a check, not a claim.**

§6.2 imposes a gate: the battery must contain a question that can positively return an answer terminating the battery, and can return it on cases where cost is present. The general form of the requirement — whatever the selection-seat, the battery owes at least one question pointed where the seat would prefer not to look.

Q4 is the missing-stakeholder detector: *If everyone agrees, who is not in the conversation?*

The open question is whether Q4 reaches non-contemporaneous absentees. If it does, Finding 2 is partly covered at the battery level and Q5's unlisted cost is compensated by a sibling question rather than being a hole in the battery. If it does not — if "not in the conversation" means *absent from a conversation that is happening* — then no member of the battery can surface a future stakeholder, and the balance requirement is unmet along the temporal dimension specifically, which is the dimension the configured-world selector has the least incentive to probe.

I cannot rule this from outside the repo. It is a one-run check against the audit directory that already exists (`audits/2026-07-25_oq255_seat_cost_measure/`): does the cost-present discriminator's corpus contain any case whose payer is non-contemporaneous, and if not, is that absence searchable or unsearchable in §11 item 1's sense?

## 5. Finding 4 — one token, two conditions

**Grade: buildable. Standard defect-pattern-1 instance.**

`cs_drift_ack_witness` carries `confrontation_path: "none_exists"`, and §6.5 states its meaning precisely: no external confrontation path exists because OQ-107 is unbuilt — *absence of a path, not a clean probe*. That is an apparatus limitation, correctly labeled.

Finding 1 exhibits a distinct condition wearing the same token: an object for which no confrontation path exists **by construction**, as a design property, independent of what the apparatus has built. An entry authored for a Long Now-shaped system emits the sentinel and means the second thing while the read site interprets the first.

This is `build_discipline.md`'s recurring shape — an absence presenting as a presence, one success-shaped token filling two holes — and it takes the standard fix: carry the provenance bit with the value. Two sentinels, `unbuilt_probe` and `designed_absent`, or one sentinel plus a discriminator field. Cheap; routes to ISSUES.

Note the dependency: this build is only correct if Finding 1's ruling goes the way I expect. If Corollary 3 turns out to absorb the unattended case, `designed_absent` is not a distinct condition and the token collision is imaginary.

## 6. Finding 5 — a candidate authored signal for the declared-open row

**Grade: candidate; routes to an OQ-227 amendment proposal, not a build.**

`commitment_systems_sketch_v6.md` §11 item 1 rules `sealed_closure` a declared-open row rather than an added terminal, on stated grounds: undetectable by construction (H¹ = 0, no pre-closure snapshot, no referent to check against), and adding an unreachable enum row with no possible positive control would counterfeit a finding. §6.6 adds that the near-miss search is *unsearchable, not concluded negative* — there is no authored referent-dissolution signal to search on.

The unsearchability is the specific thing this perturbation touches. A system that **declares in advance** that it intends to deliver its benefits beyond the confrontability of every party to it has supplied an authored signal — voluntarily, in its founding documents, before any closure has occurred. Prompt 56 is that declaration in its purest form; prompts 19, 55 and 57 are the same commitment stated more softly.

What this does **not** get you: detection of referent-dissolution. Sealed closure remains unrecognizable from every standing vantage and the ruling stands.

What it may get you: a **susceptibility** flag with a real positive control. The authored design-intent is a pre-closure snapshot of exactly the kind §6.6 says the closure case lacks — not because closure was caught in progress, but because the object announced its own architecture beforehand. A probe for *declared designed unconfrontability* is testable, can be shown to fire on a known case (Kelly's list), and can be shown not to fire on systems with live confrontation paths.

Whether susceptibility-without-detection is in scope is an operator ruling, and there is a real argument against: a flag that says "this system is the kind of thing sealed closure happens to" without any ability to say whether it has happened may be the counterfeit finding §11 was protecting against, wearing better clothes.

## 7. Findings withdrawn or weakened

Recorded because a census that only grows is not a census.

**The unborn as a missing observer position — withdrawn.** An earlier pass in conversation held that DR's four canonical positions (U₁–U₄) omit a position with stakes but no authoring capacity, and that Theorem 4's Oracle Gap makes site-incompleteness a live worry. `seat-theorem-v1.md` §8 dissolves this: the site is a framing Π, the partition of the field into background and dials is itself a seat, and DR §3.1 already declares the site choice as normative and geometry-relative rather than world-relative. The absence is declared, not blind.

The one-sentence residual: the *criterion* for site membership — implicitly, that a position can author a reading — is not itself stated as part of the declared Π. Recommend declaring it. Not a build.

**Multi-rate composition — weak candidate, may dissolve.** Prompts 37–39 claim a well-built system is deliberately stratified, the slow layer functioning as the referent the fast layer drifts against. Commitment Systems models one kernel, one authority structure, one drift vector per system; §11 item 3 declares no lifecycle state machine, which is adjacent but not this. There is no composition operator and no representation of one system's kernel serving as another's practice layer. I flag it and expect it to dissolve into "analyze each system separately, and the pacing claim is about a polity containing several systems, not about one system having layers." Left in only because dissolving it takes a witness I do not have.

## 8. The control on this document

Per §7.1, the recurrence of a framing across a foreign source is itself an invariance claim, and carries no information until the framing is shown able to fail to fit.

**It fails to fit on axis B, and that is the run's own control.** Kelly's list has no axiom-diff surface. `axiom_diff.pl` needs a kernel's foundational commitments and a rival reading's; the 88 prompts are a single reading with no contested premise and no sibling, and the `axiom_concept` vocabulary has nothing to align. The perturbation reaches axis A (time, drift, terminals) hard, reaches the seat-selection layer hard, and returns all-blind on B. That asymmetry is what Ω_E predicts — invariance under one axis should not strongly predict invariance under another — and a source that had fit all axes cleanly would have been the suspicious result, not the good one.

**What would falsify the run.** Finding 1 is falsified if an operator ruling shows the unattended case reduces to reabsorption under Corollary 3 as stated. Finding 2 is falsified if Q5's declared foreclosure-set already covers non-contemporaneous stakeholders by implication and I have read the enumeration too literally. Finding 3 is falsified in either direction by the Q4 check. Findings 4 and 5 are downstream of 1 and of an in-scope ruling respectively and fall with them.

**Citation discipline.** Nothing here is a detection result. Nothing here may be cited as corpus evidence — no entry was authored and no run was made. The `H¹` prohibition of DR §9.4 applies to the temptation this document was most exposed to: the argument in Finding 1 is a Corollary-3 consequence about temporal seat-occupancy, and it may not be restated as a claim about gluing status, naturalization, or authenticity, which live on machinery this document did not touch.
