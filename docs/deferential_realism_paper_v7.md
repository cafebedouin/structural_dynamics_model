# Axioms and Consequences of Two-Axis Observer-Dependent Classification

**A Formal Framework for Systems Where What You See Depends on Where You Stand — and on Which Commitment You Are Looking At**

**Version: v7.0**

---

**Abstract.** This version extends the Deferential Realism (DR) framework from one axis to two. Versions through v6.13 modeled a single source of structure: the *observer axis*, where the classification of a fixed constraint varies with the observer's position, formalized as a presheaf on a site of observer contexts that deliberately violates the sheaf gluing axiom so that perspectival disagreement becomes a measurable invariant (H¹, spectral weight, Wasserstein transport, Nash distance). v7 adds the *committer axis*: where the observer axis holds a constraint fixed and varies the seat, the committer axis holds the seat fixed and varies the commitment — asking which reading of a contested kernel is in play, what foundational axioms it rests on, whether those axioms are mutually compatible, and where the reading is drifting over time.

In prior versions the committer-axis machinery appeared only as a "commentary-grade" annotation (§4.2, v6.12) — a set of pattern verdicts that described constraints without reclassifying them. v7 promotes it to a co-equal axis with its own empirical anchor, its own derived consequences, and its own corpus validation. The promotion is justified by a single structural result: the two axes detect **disjoint** failure modes. A reading can be coherent on the observer axis — H¹ = 0, the signature-resolved classification orbit a global section with no cohomological obstruction — while being structurally foreclosed on the committer axis. (H¹ = 0 is coherence of the *signature-resolved* orbit, not uniformity of the raw per-context types; the anchor case has a maximally heterogeneous raw orbit resolved to a global section by a cover-story signature, which ties the result to Theorem 1.) Perspectival coherence does not imply axiomatic validity. We state this as Theorem 7 (Detection Independence) and confirm it on a single fully-measured, single-load, ε-stable reading that is simultaneously cover-story-covered on the observer axis and axiom-foreclosed on the committer axis.

We retain the three-layer separation that has organized the framework since v6: §2 states the axioms (now including Axiom 7, the committer-axis empirical anchor), §3 derives the theorems (now including Theorems 7–8), §4 describes the engine (now two-axis), §5 reports empirical findings (now including the trifurcation profile of the committer axis), §6 the honest assessment, §7 related work, §8 implications. The observer-axis axioms (1–6) and theorems (1–4) are unchanged; v7 is strictly additive at the level of the existing theory. The central new commitment — and it is a commitment, stated as such — is that the two axes are *not* unified: their mechanisms have incompatible mathematical characters, and a companion architecture note ("Two-Axis Architecture: Observer and Committer") records the decoupling decision and the engineering caveats. This paper states the consequences of that decision as theory; the architecture note states it as design constraint. The theory is downstream of the decision, and the decision is recorded there.

A scope note carried from the architecture work: Theorem 7 is established by one fully-measured existence proof, not a distribution. The committer axis is built and validated; its *integration* with the observer axis (a shared abductive-hypothesis layer) is named as future work, not claimed. v7 documents two validated axes and one deliberate seam between them. It does not claim a unified two-axis theory of everything — it claims that the second axis exists, is real, and sees what the first cannot.

## 1. Introduction

The classification of a social structure depends on who is classifying. A labor regulation that appears as an immutable feature of the landscape to a trapped worker may appear as a reformable coordination mechanism to a legislator and an extractive device to an analyst. Versions through v6.13 formalized this — the *observer axis* — and the formalization is unchanged here.

But there is a prior question the observer axis does not ask. Before you can ask how a constraint looks from different positions, something has to have selected *which* constraint, out of a space of alternatives, is the one being looked at. A community committed to "the death penalty deters crime" and a community committed to "the death penalty is categorically impermissible" are not two observers disagreeing about one constraint; they are two *commitments*, each of which the observer axis would then classify from four positions. The observer axis takes the commitment as given. The question of which commitment is in play, what it presupposes, and how it changes over time — that is a second axis, orthogonal to the first.

Call it the **committer axis**. Where the observer axis indexes by position (who is looking), the committer axis indexes by *reading* (which interpretation of a contested commitment is held) and by *time* (where the reading's foundational premises are drifting). A contested commitment is a **kernel**; its position-indexed interpretations are **readings**; the foundational premises that make a reading coherent are its **axioms**; and the gap between a reading's declared reference frame and its current state is its **drift**.

The standard instinct — the same instinct the observer axis had to refuse when it declined to resolve perspectival disagreement into a single correct classification — is to fold the committer axis into the observer axis: to treat the relations between readings as more edges in the same graph, the drift as more variation in the same metrics. v7 refuses this fold, and the refusal is not a preference. The two axes have incompatible mathematical characters (the architecture note details the engineering form of this; §3 here states its theoretical form), and — the result that makes refusal mandatory rather than tidy — they detect failures that do not coincide. A commitment can be in perfect perspectival health and structural collapse at once. If the axes were unifiable, that could not happen. It happens. Theorem 7 states it; §5 measures it.

DR remains *realist* about structural properties and *deferential* about classification. v7 adds a third stance, *committer-relative*: the framework is realist about a reading's foundational axioms (they are authored facts about what the reading presupposes) and deferential about which reading a community holds, when, and why. The committer axis is a meter for *commitment structure and its drift*, exactly as the observer axis is a meter for perspectival fracture. Neither adjudicates whose commitment is right.

§2 adds Axiom 7. §3 adds Theorems 7–8. §4 describes the two-axis engine and its single bridge. §5 reports the committer-axis empirical record. §6–8 follow v6.13.

## 2. The Axioms

Axioms 1–6 are unchanged from v6.13 and are not restated here. Of those six, one (Axiom 2, power-modulated perception) is the *empirical anchor* of the observer axis — the hypothesis on which the observer-axis theorems depend for their correspondence to the world. The committer axis requires its own empirical anchor, stated below as Axiom 7. As with Axiom 2: if the structural form is wrong the mathematics changes; if the empirical anchor is wrong the mathematics remains valid for the model but the model no longer maps to the world.

### Axiom 7: Authored Commitment Structure with Computed Consequence ⚓ [Empirical Anchor, Committer Axis]

The core committer-axis commitment: **a reading's foundational premises and their direction of drift are facts the holder can state without asserting a truth claim about the world, and the reading's structural fate is a computed consequence of those facts, not an additional assertion.**

This has the same architecture as Axiom 2, displaced from perception to commitment. Each reading of a kernel declares: a *reference frame* (t0 — what the reading takes the kernel to be, a declared starting point, not a discovered historical origin), a *drift state* (t1 — the gap between that frame and the reading's current condition, characterized by direction, magnitude, and whether the authority structure acknowledges it), and the *grounding type* of each foundational axiom (whether the axiom rests on an empirically-contingent premise, a deontological commitment, a convention, an instrumental calculation, or a theological warrant). These are authorable: a holder of a reading can say what it takes the kernel to be, which way it is moving, and what kind of warrant its premises rest on, all without claiming to know whether the premises are *true*.

From these authored facts, the engine *computes* — it does not ask the holder to assert — three structural consequences:

- the **terminal attractor** (t2): where continued drift along the t0→t1 vector lands, read off a fixed attractor table grounded in the case library (stable_pattern, husk, extinction, revival, repudiation, axiom_foreclosure);
- the **foreclosure status** of an axiom: an axiom routes to foreclosed when its grounding is empirically-contingent *and* its reading's drift is axiom-overriding *and* that drift is unacknowledged — the configuration the case library identifies as a premise being structurally ruled out by its own evidential commitments;
- the **conflict signature** of two readings: contradictory foundational axioms co-occurring with a `coexists_with` edge between the readings is *licensed plurality*; the same contradiction co-occurring with a `forecloses` edge is *real closure*.

**Why this choice.** The alternative — asking the holder (or the generating model) to directly assert "this reading is foreclosed" or "these readings contradict" — fails in practice, because foreclosure and contradiction are truth claims a well-calibrated holder correctly declines to make. Asked to mark an axiom foreclosed, a generator defaults to the safer "holdable," because whether a premise is *actually* ruled out is a claim about the world it cannot certify. Axiom 7 moves the verdict from assertion to computation: the holder supplies substrate it can know (the axiom rests on an empirical premise; the drift is overriding; the authority has not acknowledged it), and the engine routes the configuration to foreclosed. This is the committer-axis form of the verdict-layer pattern (§4.4, v6.12): authored claim in, theory-derived consequence out.

**What would change.** The reference-frame declaration is normative — it is where the committer axis's analogue of political commitment enters, exactly as the site choice is for the observer axis. *Which* state counts as t0 is position-dependent; the kernel's formation looks different from different seats, and two analysts may author different baselines for the same kernel. This is not a defect to be resolved (it is the committer-axis instance of the same deferential stance the observer axis takes toward classification); it means t2 trajectories are baseline-relative — properties of the declared frame — not world-relative assertions about the kernel's inevitable fate. The attractor table is grounded in the historical case library (husk ← Kodashim/dormant practice; revival ← Hebrew reconstruction; axiom_foreclosure ← falsified-premise death; repudiation ← post-1945 total war; extinction ← total loss of text and practice); a different reading of those cases would produce a different table, and one cell (codification_collapse + severe → extinction) is grounded definitionally rather than evidentially. The framework is a functor from authored commitment-substrate to computed structural consequence; the current attractor table is one instantiation.

## 3. The Theorems

Theorems 1–4 are unchanged from v6.13. The following hold within the model defined by Axioms 1–7. As before, they are theorems because they follow from the axioms; their status as claims about the world depends on the empirical anchors (Axiom 2 for the observer-axis content of Theorem 8, Axiom 7 for the committer-axis content of both). Each is confirmed on the corpus, but confirmation is not what makes it true within the model.

### Theorem 7: Detection Independence

**Statement.** Perspectival coherence and axiomatic coherence are independent. There exist readings that are observer-axis coherent (H¹ = 0 — the signature-resolved classification orbit is a global section, no cohomological obstruction) and simultaneously committer-axis foreclosed (an empirically-contingent foundational axiom, under unacknowledged axiom-overriding drift, routing to axiom_foreclosure). Therefore the committer axis detects a class of structural failure that observer-axis cohomology cannot register.

**A precision the anchor forces, stated first because it is load-bearing.** H¹ = 0 does *not* mean every observer position assigns the same raw metric type. H¹ is computed over the *signature-resolved* `dr_type` orbit (the path is `cohomological_obstruction → orbit_vector → type_at_context → dr_type`, and `dr_type` applies the structural signature before H¹ counts), not over raw `classify_from_metrics` types and not over the MaxEnt distributions (which are separately computed downstream). So H¹ = 0 means the orbit is a global section *after* signature resolution; the raw per-context metric types may be — and in the anchoring case are — maximally heterogeneous. This distinction is not pedantic: it is the mechanism by which a reading can be observer-coherent and committer-foreclosed at once, and it ties Theorem 7 to Theorem 1 (see below).

**Why it holds within the model.** H¹ (Theorem 2) is a partition functional over the signature-resolved classification orbit; it is nonzero exactly when those resolved classifications disagree across positions. A foreclosed axiom, by the routing of Axiom 7, is a property of a reading's *foundational premise and its drift* — not of how observer positions classify the reading. There is no term in the H¹ computation that reads grounding type, drift direction, or acknowledgment. So a reading whose resolved orbit is a global section (H¹ = 0) can carry a foreclosed axiom, and a reading carrying no foreclosed axiom can have a maximally fractured resolved orbit (H¹ = 6). The two quantities are computed from disjoint inputs: H¹ from the signature-resolved orbit, foreclosure from the authored commitment substrate. Their independence is structural — it is not that they *happen* not to correlate, but that neither's computation can see the other's inputs.

**Why the independence is not a measurement gap.** One might worry that H¹ = 0 on a foreclosed reading reflects insufficient observer-context data rather than genuine coherence. It does not, and the distinction is checkable: a genuine H¹ = 0 has all observer positions computed, the resolved orbit a global section, with zero transport (Wasserstein) and zero contextuality fraction; a measurement-gap H¹ = 0 has uncomputed positions. The corpus confirmation below is a genuine, fully-measured case, and it carries the additional discipline (forced by an earlier invalid anchor; see the worked example) that the H¹ measurement and the foreclosure routing are taken at a *single corpus load at fixed ε*.

**Corpus confirmation.** The anchor is `prohibition_reading` (foundational axiom: `intrinsic_use_harm_primary` — that the harm of the prohibited use is intrinsic and primary), at ε = 0.68, carrying a `false_natural_law` signature. Both orbits, which must be reported together:

- *Raw metric orbit* (the per-context `classify_from_metrics` types): `[naturalized, snare, rope, snare]` across powerless / moderate / institutional / analytical — three distinct types, maximum heterogeneity at four contexts. At the institutional context χ = −0.05 (the enforcement apparatus is the institutional net-beneficiary, so that observer experiences the constraint as infrastructure); the moderate and analytical observers see snare at χ = 0.75 and 0.93 (genuine extraction).
- *Signature-resolved orbit* (the `dr_type` the H¹ computation consumes): `[tangled_rope, tangled_rope, tangled_rope, tangled_rope]` — the `false_natural_law` signature overrides all four to a uniform classification. H¹ = 0 follows from this global section; Wasserstein zero, contextuality fraction zero, all twelve subsystems run.

It is simultaneously committer-foreclosed: `cs_axiom_foreclosed(prohibition_reading, intrinsic_use_harm_primary)` fires, and `cs_drift_trajectory → axiom_foreclosure` fires on the gap `axiom_overriding / substantial / false`. Two independent committer-axis computations corroborate; the observer axis registers H¹ = 0. Observer-coherent committer-foreclosure occurs.

**The tie to Theorem 1 (which strengthens the anchor).** The signature that produces H¹ = 0 *is* the cover story. `false_natural_law` is the framework asserting that a constraint presenting as natural coordination functions as extraction; the raw orbit `[naturalized, snare, rope, snare]` is exactly the Theorem 1 structure (extraction requires perspectival cover — the institutional net-beneficiary sees rope where the moderate and analytical observers see snare; the cover is not global, it requires the extracting agent's vantage). The pipeline names this directly as the `snare_masked_as_rope` gap. So prohibition_reading's observer-axis *coherence* (H¹ = 0) is manufactured by the cover-story mechanism: the signature resolves the raw extraction-fracture into a uniform orbit, and that resolution is what makes the global section. This makes the reading a single constraint that is at once a Theorem 1 cover-story instance, a Theorem 7 anchor, and the tie between them — the observer axis has a global section *because* the extraction is covered, while the committer axis independently detects that the foundational axiom is foreclosed. The detection-independence is therefore not the mild claim "an agreed-upon reading can still be foreclosed"; it is the sharp claim "a reading whose observer-axis coherence is *produced by a cover story* can be committer-foreclosed, by computational paths that share no inputs."

**Scope.** Theorem 7 is established by existence — one fully-measured, single-load, ε-stable case — not by distribution. The corpus contains twelve observer-coherent-but-committer-foreclosed candidates (the "drift-mismatch" set); the quantitative question of how committer-foreclosure distributes over H¹ is *not* answered here and would require computing H¹ for all twelve at a single load. The qualitative theorem — that such readings exist, hence the axes are detection-independent — rests on the anchoring case and holds. Two further scope limits, both kept visible because they are what make the claim trustworthy:

- *The anchor must be ε-stable, not merely single-load.* An earlier candidate (`hanbali_reading`) was rejected precisely on this point (see the worked example below): its H¹ = 0 and its foreclosure routing came from two different ε states and could not be jointly cited. The validity criterion for a Theorem 7 anchor is therefore: H¹ measured and foreclosure routed at one corpus load, at an ε whose signature-resolved orbit is stable under small ε perturbation. prohibition_reading meets this — ε = 0.68 in both the pipeline and working-tree states, signature-resolved orbit uniform and not threshold-proximate.
- *The claim is scoped to the cohomological layer.* The drift-mismatch diagnostic also reports "metric-stability" (low network drift), but that half carries a sparse-network caveat (for several mismatch readings the contamination network is a stub, so "metric-stable" reflects sparse data, not a positive measurement). Theorem 7 therefore claims only: committer-foreclosure detects what H¹ cannot. It does not claim "what the metric-drift network cannot," because that network's measurement is not clean for the mismatch set.

**The worked example that fixes the validity criterion.** `hanbali_reading` (foundational axiom: analogical reasoning is forbidden innovation) was the original candidate anchor and is invalid, instructively. Its H¹ = 0 was measured at ε = 0.52 with no committer-axis fields present; its foreclosure routing fires at ε = 0.58, where the signature-resolved orbit is *not* a global section and H¹ would be nonzero. The two signals never coexisted at one load. Because the foreclosure routing (Axiom 7) is ε-independent by design, it fires regardless of the observer-axis ε state — which means a naive joint citation can pair a foreclosure verdict with an H¹ from a different ε, exactly the error that hanbali would have introduced. The discipline this forces: the decoupling that makes detection independence true (committer-axis verdicts do not depend on observer-axis ε) is the same decoupling that makes naive joint-citation invalid (the two axes must be measured at one fixed-ε load before pairing). hanbali is retained here not as evidence for the theorem but as the demonstration of its validity criterion.

**Significance.** Had committer-foreclosure clustered at some H¹ band (the natural conjecture, by analogy to the observer-axis hub-conflict band at H¹ = 4; see two-hub note), it would have indicated that observer-axis cohomology already partially captured committer-axis failure, and the second axis would be partly redundant. The H¹ = 0 foreclosure rules this out: the axes are not two views of one structure but two structures. This is the formal justification for the committer axis's existence.

### Theorem 8: Conflict Signature Dichotomy

**Statement.** Under Axiom 7, two readings of one kernel holding contradictory foundational axioms admit exactly two structural signatures, distinguished by the typed relation between them: contradiction with `coexists_with` is *licensed plurality* (both readings live, neither displacing the other); contradiction with `forecloses` is *real closure* (one reading makes the other structurally impossible). The contradiction alone does not determine which; the edge does.

**Why it holds within the model.** Axiom 7 makes the contradiction declaration and the typed edge *independent* authored inputs — contradiction is declared from whether a single coherent framework could hold both axioms; the edge is declared from whether one reading forecloses or coexists with the other. Because they are independent, their four combinations are all expressible, and two of them are occupied (contradiction+coexists, contradiction+forecloses) with distinct structural meaning. The earlier, rejected design — deriving contradiction *from* the forecloses edge — collapsed this dichotomy: it made contradiction and foreclosure the same fact, rendering licensed plurality (contradiction without foreclosure) inexpressible. Theorem 8 holds only because the two signals are kept independent, which is the committer-axis instance of the same separate-mechanisms principle that organizes the whole framework.

**Corpus confirmation.** On the capital-punishment kernel: `categorical_impermissibility ↔ deterrence_instrument` carries a `coexists_with` edge and a declared contradiction → licensed plurality; `categorical_impermissibility ↔ retributive_desert` carries a `forecloses` edge and a declared contradiction → real closure; `retributive_desert ↔ deterrence_instrument` carries no declared contradiction (distinct justifications, not mutually exclusive) → correctly silent. Both signatures are present at corpus scale (§5).

## 4. The Computational Engine (Two-Axis)

§4.1–4.4 of v6.13 (rule cascade, structural signatures, corpus provenance, architectural commitments) are unchanged. v7 adds §4.5.

### 4.5 The Committer-Axis Layer and the Single Bridge

The committer axis is implemented as a set of modules that consume authored `cs_structure` fields and compute the Axiom 7 consequences: `cs_kernel_registry` (kernel→reading membership, cross-reading divergence), `cs_axiom_engine` (foundational axioms, contradiction declarations, conflict signatures), `cs_drift_engine` (t0→t1→t2 trajectories via the attractor table), and `cs_pattern_detection` (the grounding-vs-structure and foreclosure-routing diagnostics). These run *beside* the observer-axis pipeline, not inside it.

The decoupling is a stated commitment with a theoretical basis (Theorems 7–8) and an engineering basis (the architecture note). In brief: the typed reading-relations have mathematical characters incompatible with the observer-axis contamination network. `forecloses` is a categorical override (discontinuous), `coexists_with` is zero-flow by definition, and `influences` is entailment already consumed elsewhere — none is a scalar contamination weight, and feeding them into the purity network would invalidate its monotone-flow convergence proof. The reading-relation edges therefore do **not** enter the contamination network.

The cross-axis surface divides into two kinds, with different obligations. **(A) Data bridges** — committer-axis data feeding observer-axis *computation* — number exactly one: `influences` → `detect_necessity_inheritance` in `drl_composition`, carrying entailment (which both layers understand) and nothing else. **(B) Read-only seam diagnostics** — committer-side consumers that *read* observer-axis output to surface disagreement, feeding nothing back — number at least three: `cs_drift_mismatch` (committer trajectory vs. observer drift), `cs_kernel_registry`'s cross-reading divergence check (reads `classify_at_time`), and `cs_pattern_detection`'s grounding-vs-structure diagnostics (read `constraint_signature`). The separation claim governs (A): the count of data bridges is the invariant that protects the contamination network's convergence proof and Theorem 7's detection independence. (B) seams may multiply freely — a one-way read of observer output cannot couple the axes — and an earlier draft of this section, stating "exactly one intentional bridge … and nothing else," wrongly conflated the two kinds (substrate census 2026-06-08). One accidental coupling — `shared_agent_link` generating contamination edges between sibling readings that share victim/beneficiary classes — is severed by an intra-kernel filter, since those relationships already have explicit typed-edge coverage.

The (B) seams exist because, per Theorem 7, cross-axis disagreement is the signal, not a defect to reconcile: a reading the committer axis diagnoses as foreclosed while the observer axis sees it as stable is the cross-axis analogue of the observer axis's own false mountain (high extraction perceived immutable) — a failure visible only at the seam between independent mechanisms. `cs_drift_mismatch` is the canonical instance.

## 5. Empirical Findings (Committer Axis)

§5.1–5.10 (observer-axis findings) are unchanged. v7 adds §5.11.

### 5.11 The Trifurcation Profile

The committer axis produces gap-structured distributions — clustering with forbidden regions, the signature of a real carving rather than an arbitrary partition. From the promoted corpus (kernel_run_03: 109 readings, 0 schema rejections, 100% grounding-type coverage from the generator):

**Drift trajectory terminals** cluster: husk 57, axiom_foreclosure 25, stable_pattern 19, repudiation 2, revival 0. The terminal space is unevenly occupied — most drift lands in husk or foreclosure, repudiation is rare, and revival is empty in this corpus. (The empty and sparse cells partly reflect corpus selection — an adversarially-seeded kernel set is weighted toward contested, drifting commitments — and are reported as such, not as claims about commitments in general.)

**Conflict signatures** (Theorem 8): 35 licensed-plurality pairs (contradiction + `coexists_with`), 15 real-closure pairs (contradiction + `forecloses`), 1 with no typed edge. Both signatures present at volume; licensed plurality — the signature the earlier collapsed design could not express at all — is the more common.

**Kernel divergence:** 79 reading-pair divergences across 34 kernels. The cross-reading divergence diagnostic fires broadly.

**Drift and foreclosure:** 84 instances of unacknowledged drift; 30 foreclosed-axiom routings, all computed from generated grounding-type data (not hand-authored), with zero spurious firings on deontological axioms — the routing is selective, not a relabel of all empirical axioms.

**Cross-path corroboration.** Every reading the foreclosure-routing predicate marks foreclosed also has a drift trajectory terminating at axiom_foreclosure — two independent computations (static shape-routing and temporal attractor-table) converging on the same verdict from the same authored substrate. This is the committer-axis instance of the cross-check that distinguishes signal from artifact, and it is possible only because foreclosure is computed from authored shape (Axiom 7) rather than asserted.

**Methodological note on the corpus.** The grounding-type field reached 100% coverage from the generator with non-degenerate distribution (empirically_contingent 82, deontological 77, conventional 34, instrumental 14, theological 3), and the typed-edge field reached non-degenerate spread (35 forecloses, 104 coexists, 32 influences in an earlier run; comparable in the promoted run). These distributions are reported because their *non-degeneracy* is the evidence that the committer-axis judgments are being authored rather than defaulted — a generator emitting all-coexists or all-holdable would have populated the fields while authoring nothing. The contradiction-declaration drop rate (declared pairs lost because a reading failed validation) is reported alongside conflict counts, so that a low conflict count is never read as "no contradictions" when it may be "readings failed."

**Note on corpus provenance and single-run coherence.** The testset directory passed through several exploratory generation runs (undertaken while stabilizing the generation schema) that reused constraint IDs, leaving the directory a transient chimera: multiple versions of some IDs from different runs, occasionally with substantially different authored ε (a topic such as "birth" decomposed by different SCOPE runs into genuinely different readings — `[naturalized/coordination, ε = 0.12]` versus `[tangled hybrid, ε = 0.58]` — the same name over different analytical objects). This was resolved before the figures above were finalized: collisions were triaged (stale duplicates dropped, genuinely-distinct readings archived rather than silently merged) and the directory reduced to the single committer-axis run as canonical. The resolution was verified non-destructive to the committer-axis figures: every number in this section is byte-identical before and after the cleanup, because all committer-axis predicates gate on `cs_kernel_id`, which only the committer-axis run's stories carry — the non-canonical stories were computationally inert for every committer-axis predicate, and dropping them changed nothing. The figures are therefore single-run coherent. One incidental finding from this episode is worth recording: that the same topic decomposes to different ε under different SCOPE framings is not generation noise but a small confirmation of the committer axis — a topic's extractiveness is reading-relative, not topic-intrinsic; "birth" has no ε until a reading of it is fixed.

## 6. Honest Assessment

The v6.13 assessment (§6) stands for the observer axis. For the committer axis:

**What is established.** The committer axis is built and validated. Its diagnostics fire on generated data, produce gap-structured distributions, and cross-corroborate (foreclosure-routing with drift-trajectory). Theorem 7 (detection independence) is established by a single fully-measured, single-load, ε-stable existence proof (`prohibition_reading`), which is additionally a Theorem 1 cover-story instance — tying three results to one constraint. Theorem 8 (conflict dichotomy) is confirmed with both signatures present at scale on a known kernel.

**What is scoped.** Theorem 7 is existence, not distribution — one clean case; the distribution of committer-foreclosure over H¹ is not characterized. The metric-stability half of the drift-mismatch verdict rests on sparse network data and is excluded from the theorem's claim, which is scoped to the cohomological layer. The drift attractor table is case-library-grounded with one definitional cell. The t0 reference frame is baseline-relative, not world-relative — trajectories are properties of the declared frame.

**A foundational caveat about ε, surfaced during validation.** The base extractiveness ε is the framework's least-grounded and most load-bearing primitive: the entire observer axis is a deterministic transform of it (χ = ε × f(d) × σ(S)), yet ε is authored by judgment (by the generator, from a reading's narrative) rather than computed from anything beneath it. v6.13's Axiom 2 calls ε observer-invariant, and it is — across observer positions. It is *not* invariant across generation runs: the same topic, scoped differently, yields different ε, and that is correct (it reflects genuinely different readings) but it means any single corpus's ε-dependent statistics are one sample, conditional on one coherent generation, not a point estimate of a fixed quantity. Two disciplines follow, both now enforced. First, ε-dependent and committer-axis quantities may be paired only when measured at one corpus load at fixed ε — because the committer-axis routing is ε-independent by design (Axiom 7), it will otherwise pair a foreclosure verdict with an H¹ from a different ε state, which is exactly the error the rejected hanbali anchor would have introduced. Second, an anchor for a cross-axis claim must be ε-*stable*: its signature-resolved orbit must not flip under small ε perturbation, or the result is an artifact of where a hand-authored number was set near a threshold. These are stated as standing requirements, not one-time fixes.

**What is not claimed.** v7 does not claim a unified two-axis theory. The axes are deliberately decoupled; their *integration* — a shared abductive-hypothesis layer where committer-axis findings (kernel divergence, axiom conflict, drift contradiction) surface alongside observer-axis findings (perspectival incoherence) — is not built. At present the explanatory layer is observer-axis only: perspectival incoherence generates hypotheses, axiom conflict does not. This asymmetry is named as a known limitation, not a result. Kernel-level analytics (orbit, height, husk aggregation across a kernel's readings) are partial. These are enumerated as future work in the architecture note.

**The method's behavior, noted as itself a finding.** Across the development of both axes, the framework repeatedly declined to confirm its own clean predictions and returned messier, truer results instead: the predicted death-mode terminal for the abolition reading came back husk rather than repudiation; the predicted observer-axis hub-conflict band came back H¹ = 4 rather than 5–6; the predicted cohomological clustering of committer-foreclosure (Theorem 7's natural conjecture) came back not as a band but as H¹ = 0, inverting the expected parallel into the stronger detection-independence result; and the first proposed anchor for that very theorem (`hanbali_reading`) dissolved under verification when its H¹ = 0 and its foreclosure routing proved to come from two different ε states — forcing both a better anchor (`prohibition_reading`) and the ε-stability discipline above. That last instance is the sharpest, because the framework's own discipline turned on a claim *this paper had already drafted*: the theorem was written with an invalid anchor, and the requirement to confirm both axes at one load is what caught it. A framework that generated structure to fit its analyst's expectations would have confirmed the draft. This one falsified it, and the falsification produced a stronger theorem. We record this as a property of the method, offered as evidence — not proof — that the diagnostics track domain structure rather than analyst expectation, and we note that the property applied to the authors as readily as to the corpus.

## 8. Implications

The observer axis established that *what you see depends on where you stand*. The committer axis adds that *which commitment exists to be seen depends on what was selected, what it presupposes, and where it is drifting* — and, via Theorem 7, that a commitment's perspectival health and its structural fate are independent. A reading can present a coherent face to the observer axis — its classification orbit resolving to a global section, often *because* a cover-story signature has painted over the raw extraction-fracture beneath it — while resting on a premise its own evidential commitments have already foreclosed. The coherence is real (and, in the anchor case, manufactured by exactly the cover-story mechanism Theorem 1 describes); the foreclosure is real; neither detects the other.

This is the substantive payoff of two axes over one. A single-axis framework that measured only perspectival fracture would classify hanbali_reading as maximally healthy — H¹ = 0, no disagreement anywhere — and would be correct about perspective and blind to collapse. The second axis is not a refinement of the first; it is the instrument for a failure mode the first cannot, in principle, register. The cost of the second axis is the discipline of keeping it separate: the architecture note records that decision, and Theorems 7–8 are why it must be kept.
