## I. Foundation: The Examined Constraint-Set

The central ethical question in Deferential Realism is not "What should I believe about constraints?" but **"Do I know what I actually believe about the constraints I face?"**

This is a Socratic ethics: the unexamined belief-set is the primary ethical failure. Not because there's one correct set of beliefs about constraints, but because **arguments only bind you if they rest on axioms you actually accept**.

### The Bhagavad Gita Example

In the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna argues that Arjuna should fight in the war. The argument rests on several axioms:
- Atman (immortal soul) exists
- Reincarnation is real
- Dharma (caste duty) transcends individual preference
- The Kshatriya warrior duty is legitimate

**If you accept these axioms:** Krishna's argument is sound. Fighting doesn't harm the eternal essence, so Arjuna's reluctance comes from confusion about what's actually at stake. The constraint is a Mountain (metaphysical necessity) that Arjuna must accept.

**If you reject these axioms:** Krishna's argument is a Snare. He's using metaphysical claims Arjuna doesn't believe to extract military service in a war Arjuna wants to avoid.

**The ethical requirement:** You must examine which you actually believe. Not which sounds profound, not which tradition endorses, not which authority proclaims—but which you can honestly affirm as true about reality.

**This framework cannot tell you which to believe.** It can only insist that you examine the question, because the entire ethical analysis depends on it.

### The Ulysses Lesson

In James Joyce's *Ulysses*, Stephen Dedalus has perfect clarity about the constraints binding him. He knows he's trapped between British Empire and Catholic Church. He understands the extraction happening. He sees all the frames clearly.

**And the clarity doesn't free him.**

The lesson: Classification clarity is **necessary but not sufficient** for effective action. You need:

1. **Know what the constraint is** (epistemology - covered in epistemology.md)
2. **Know what you believe about it** (examined belief - this document's core)
3. **Choose appropriate response** (conditional virtues - see below)
4. **Accept that clarity doesn't eliminate constraint** (wisdom - sometimes there is no exit)

Understanding constraint structure doesn't eliminate the constraints. Sometimes clarity just reveals that your freedom lies in **choosing your orientation** within constraints you cannot change. This is wisdom, not salvation.

## II. The Six Conditional Virtues

These are not universal moral duties. They are **practices that serve specific values**. If you share the values, the practices are useful. If you don't share the values, these virtues may not apply to you.

### Virtue 1: Acceptance (For Those Who Value Effective Agency)

**The Value:** Effective agency within actual limits (not wasting finite energy fighting unchangeable reality)

**The Practice:** Accurately identify unchangeable constraints (Mountains) and redirect energy from impossible fights to tractable problems.

**Conditional statement:** IF you value accomplishing things within your lifetime, THEN accurately distinguishing what cannot be changed from what can be changed prevents wasted energy.

**Examples:**

**Mountains (genuinely unchangeable):**
- Thermodynamic limits (cannot create energy from nothing)
- Biological requirements (humans need oxygen, water, sleep)
- Logical necessity (cannot have contradictions be true)
- Historical facts (cannot change what has already happened)
- Your starting position (born in specific place, time, circumstances)
- Constitutional geometry (Article V locks, amendment impossibilities)

**False Mountains (often misclassified):**
- "Human nature" claims about hierarchy, competition, gender roles (often constructed, not biological)
- "Economic laws" about market necessity (often policy choices, not physical laws)
- "Traditional values" about family structure, sexual behavior (vary across cultures and time)
- "That's just who I am" (when defending changeable Abandoned Camps)

**The practice:**
- When you encounter claimed necessity, ask: "What's the evidence this is unchangeable?"
- **Damage vs. Discomfort test:** Does violation produce objective harm or subjective discomfort?
- Historical test: Has this been different in other times/places?
- Counterfactual test: Can you imagine solving the problem differently?

**The strategic benefit:** Accepting Mountains immediately frees energy for actual problems. The rock climber who accepts gravity finds the route. The one who denies it dies.

**The failure mode:** Treating changeable constraints as unchangeable (false fatalism) leads to unwarranted surrender of agency. But this is a **strategic error** (wastes opportunities), not a moral failure per se.

**Why this is conditional:** Some people value acceptance over accomplishment, devotion over effectiveness, faith over pragmatic success. For them, this virtue may not apply. The framework doesn't claim everyone should prioritize effective agency.

### Virtue 2: Resistance (For Those Who Value Autonomy)

**The Value:** Autonomy and freedom from extraction (not having your life energy harvested by others)

**The Practice:** Identify extractive structures (Snares) that masquerade as natural necessity, and respond strategically (resist when able, exit when possible, expose always).

**Conditional statement:** IF you value autonomy and dislike being extracted from, THEN distinguishing genuine constraints from manufactured ones enables strategic response.

**The diagnostic:**
- **Beneficiary analysis:** Who wins systematically? Who loses?
- **Snap-back test:** Remove enforcement—does it collapse immediately or persist?
- **Implementation gap:** Does practice diverge from stated purpose?
- **Naturalization rhetoric:** Does it claim "this is just how things are"?
- **Alternative suppression:** Are competing options actively blocked?

**Examples:**

**External Snares (political economy):**
- Payday loans (300-600% APR masquerading as "short-term liquidity")
- Non-compete clauses for low-wage workers (extracting mobility without trade secret justification)
- Gerrymandering (extraction of representation through district manipulation)
- Judicial gatekeeping (elections held, winners dissolved by courts)
- Democratic theater (form without function, extraction of legitimacy)

**Internal Snares (psychological):**
- Clarity Fetishes (demand for certainty blocks necessary inquiry)
- Toxic perfectionism (impossible standards tied to self-worth)
- Internalized oppression (enforcing oppressor's Snares after they're gone)
- Self-gaslighting ("I'm too sensitive" when discomfort is legitimate data)

**Social Snares (relational):**
- Unpaid emotional labor expectations ("women should manage household emotions")
- Obligatory presence requirements ("family means showing up even when harmful")
- Gaslighting dynamics ("you're overreacting" when naming extraction)

**Institutional Snares (systems):**
- Vendor lock-in (switching costs artificially inflated)
- Gatekeeping credentials (rent extraction via unnecessary certification)
- Regulatory capture (rules protecting incumbents, not consumers)

**Strategic responses (power-dependent):**

**When you have power:**
- **Resist actively:** Organize, expose, dismantle
- **Build alternatives:** Create competing coordination mechanisms
- **Regulatory intervention:** Use collective power to constrain extraction

**When you're powerless:**
- **Exit if possible:** Change jurisdiction, find alternatives
- **Contain damage if trapped:** Minimize participation, preserve capacity
- **Expose always:** Make extraction visible, refuse naturalization
- **Build collective power:** Coalition formation for future resistance

**The strategic benefit:** Not being extracted from preserves your finite life energy for projects you actually value.

**The failure mode:** Seeing extraction everywhere (paranoia), or denying extraction when it's real (Stockholm syndrome). Both are **strategic errors**.

**Why this is conditional:** Some people value security over autonomy, belonging over independence, or accept extraction as price of coordination. For them, resisting all extraction may not serve their values. The framework doesn't claim everyone should prioritize autonomy over security.

### Virtue 3: Reform (For Those Who Value Preserving Coordination While Reducing Harm)

**The Value:** Preserving beneficial coordination while excising extraction (surgical precision over wholesale destruction or blanket acceptance)

**The Practice:** Recognize Tangled Ropes (coordination + extraction hybrids), assess separability, reform to preserve coordination while reducing extraction.

**Conditional statement:** IF you value both collective coordination AND autonomy from extraction, THEN surgical Tangled Rope reform serves both values simultaneously.

**The recognition:** Most complex modern systems are Tangled Ropes (36% of social constraints, 60%+ of functional democratic institutions). They coordinate AND extract irreducibly.

**Examples:**

**Economic Tangled Ropes:**
- Carbon credits (climate coordination + financial rent extraction)
- Health insurance (risk pooling + administrative extraction + job lock-in)
- Academic publishing (quality control + access rent extraction)

**Social Tangled Ropes:**
- Monogamy norms (coordinate expectations + suppress alternatives + enforce gender roles)
- Professional networking (opportunity discovery + insider advantage)
- Family caregiving expectations (elder support + gendered extraction)

**Institutional Tangled Ropes:**
- Two-round elections (majority legitimacy + centrist compression)
- Party-list PR with threshold (prevents fragmentation + suppresses near-threshold parties)
- Centrão budget amendments (coalition governance + pork extraction)

**Psychological Tangled Ropes (Hybrid Patterns):**
- Perfectionism (quality standards + impossible demands tied to worth)
- People-pleasing (genuine care + self-abandonment)
- Achievement drive (goal pursuit + worth-contingency)

**The reform protocol:**

**Step 1: Verify hybrid structure**
- Does this coordinate? (genuine collective benefit)
- Does this extract? (asymmetric harm/benefit)
- Are functions separable or structurally entangled?

**Step 2: Measure extraction**
- What's base extractiveness ε? (0.40 ≤ ε ≤ 0.90 for Tangled Rope)
- How does power-scaling affect experience? (π modifiers)
- Who experiences Rope vs. Snare from different positions?

**Step 3: Assess separability**
- Can coordination function without extraction?
- Historical examples? (other systems coordinate differently?)
- Alternative mechanisms? (better Rope exists?)

**Step 4: Reform strategically**

**If separable:**
- Preserve coordination mechanism
- Excise extraction layer
- Example: Academic publishing → peer review + open access (separate functions)

**If inseparable:**
- Reduce extraction while maintaining coordination
- Regulatory constraint on extraction
- Example: Health insurance → coverage requirements + administrative cost caps

**If coordination lost:**
- Recognize degradation (Tangled Rope → Snare)
- Extraction persists, coordination gone
- Treat as Snare, not Tangled Rope (resistance/exit strategies)

**The strategic benefit:** Reform preserves collective capacity (coordination) while reducing individual harm (extraction). Avoids false choice between blanket acceptance and wholesale destruction.

**The failure modes:**
- **Treating Tangled Rope as pure Rope** → defend extraction, block reform
- **Treating Tangled Rope as pure Snare** → destroy coordination, lose collective benefit
- **Claiming inseparability when separable** → excuse for maintaining extraction
- **Attempting separation when inseparable** → break coordination, fail to reduce extraction

**Why this is conditional:** Some revolutionaries prefer wholesale replacement over reform. Some conservatives prefer accepting extraction over disruption. For them, surgical reform may not be valued. The framework doesn't claim everyone should prioritize reform over revolution or acceptance.

### Virtue 4: Construction (For Those Who Value Coordination)

**The Value:** Creating beneficial coordination mechanisms (collective capacity impossible alone)

**The Practice:** Design Ropes that solve genuine coordination problems with low extraction, built-in refinement mechanisms, and transparency.

**Conditional statement:** IF you value enabling collective action, THEN building good Ropes creates capacity beyond individual limits.

**The design principles:**

**1. Genuine coordination need:**
- Real scarcity or collision problem
- Cannot be solved individually
- Mechanism actually addresses problem

**2. Low extraction (ε ≤ 0.15):**
- Reciprocal benefit (participants gain approximately equally)
- Minimal enforcement (maintained because it works)
- Voluntary participation (in principle)

**3. Transparency:**
- Rules visible and comprehensible
- No hidden agendas or opaque mechanisms
- Implementation matches stated purpose

**4. Refinement capacity:**
- Built-in revision mechanisms
- Assumes conditions will change
- Easy to update or replace

**5. Exit options:**
- Alternatives tolerated (not suppressed)
- Reasonable switching costs
- No trap mechanisms

**Examples of well-designed Ropes:**

**Technical standards:**
- USB-C (coordinated device interoperability)
- TCP/IP (internet communication protocols)
- Minimal extraction (no rent-seeking)
- Open standards (transparent, refineable)

**Social coordination:**
- Traffic lights (intersection collision prevention)
- Meeting schedules (time coordination)
- Reciprocal aid norms (mutual support)

**Institutional coordination:**
- Parliamentary confidence votes (executive-legislative alignment)
- Proportional representation (accurate vote-to-seat translation)
- Committee systems (legislative labor division)

**The reciprocity test:** An ethical Rope benefits participants approximately equally (accounting for different needs). If someone consistently gains while others consistently lose, it's degrading into Tangled Rope or was always a Snare.

**The strategic benefit:** Good Ropes enable things impossible alone (language, markets, democracy, technical standards). They create genuine collective capacity.

**The failure modes:**
- **Building opaque Ropes** (mechanisms hide true function)
- **Building rigid Ropes** (cannot adapt when conditions change)
- **Building Snares disguised as Ropes** (extraction from inception)
- **Adding extraction later** (Rope → Tangled Rope degradation)

**Why this is conditional:** Some people value individual freedom over collective coordination, spontaneity over system, or prefer navigating without rules. For them, building coordination mechanisms may feel like unnecessary constraint. The framework doesn't claim everyone should value coordination over individual freedom.

### Virtue 5: Maintenance (For Those Who Value Resource Stewardship)

**The Value:** Strategic energy allocation (investing finite resources in functional constraints, eliminating pitons)

**The Practice:** Active maintenance of Ropes, monitoring for degradation, ruthless elimination of Pitons, honoring Scaffold sunsets.

**Conditional statement:** IF you value effective use of finite energy, THEN maintaining functional constraints while eliminating pitons maximizes available capacity.

**The allocation framework:**

**What deserves maintenance investment:**

**Ropes (active coordination):**
- Requires ongoing energy (doesn't self-sustain)
- Maintenance activities: Enforcement coordination, refinement, drift monitoring, collective renewal
- Benefit: Preserves collective capacity
- Failure to maintain: Degradation to Tangled Rope (extraction accumulates) or Piton (function lost)

**Tangled Ropes (hybrid structures):**
- Requires high maintenance (both coordination AND extraction need management)
- Maintenance focus: Preserve coordination, starve extraction
- Surgical precision: Don't maintain extraction while maintaining coordination
- Reform as maintenance: Reducing ε while preserving function

**Scaffolds (temporary supports):**
- Maintenance until sunset, then **mandatory dismantlement**
- Purpose monitoring: Is transition complete?
- Sunset enforcement: Honor completion criteria
- Success metric: Clean dissolution, not continuation

**What should NOT receive maintenance:**

**Pitons (piton structures):**
- **STOP maintaining** - Every hour on piton = hour unavailable for living
- Recognition: Cost > benefit, function absent, only inertia remains
- Elimination is ethical duty (opportunity cost of maintenance is harm)

### The Ethics of Piton Maintenance

**Is maintaining pitons unethical?**

**The harm via opportunity cost:**

**Organizational pitons:**
- Piton committees drain meeting time
- Obsolete procedures drain workflow capacity
- Dead letter laws drain regulatory bandwidth
- Every resource on pitons = resource unavailable for function

**Personal pitons:**
- Dead relationships drain emotional capacity
- Abandoned Camps drain psychological energy
- Obsolete habits drain time and attention
- Maintaining past selves prevents present growth

**Political pitons:**
- Defunct institutions drain political capital
- Obsolete electoral mechanisms waste reform energy
- Empty rituals drain collective attention
- Every hour defending Pitons = hour not spent on actual governance

**The "But we've always..." defense is moral evasion:**

**Sunk cost fallacy:**
- "We invested so much" (past investment irrelevant to current utility)
- "20 years of tradition" (duration ≠ value)
- "My whole career was built on this" (identity fusion blocking assessment)

**Tradition worship:**
- "This is who we are" (identity ≠ function)
- "Respect for the past" (nostalgia ≠ maintenance obligation)
- "We can't just abandon it" (actually, you can and should if it's dead)

**Identity fusion:**
- "I am this relationship/practice/institution" (confusing self with structure)
- Elimination feels like self-annihilation
- But identity fusion is precisely what makes Piton so costly (prevents growth)

**The Relief Test (ethical diagnostic):**

**Question:** "If this disappeared tomorrow, would I feel relief or loss?"

**If relief:**
- Structure is Piton (piton, no function)
- Maintaining it is unethical (opportunity cost harm)
- Elimination is moral duty (frees resources for function)
- Only institutionally invested parties would mourn → their nostalgia doesn't create maintenance obligation

**If loss:**
- Structure still serves function (Rope/Scaffold/even Tangled Rope)
- Maintain or reform, don't eliminate
- Genuine coordination value remains

**Counter-argument (when Piton maintenance IS justified):**

**Rare cases:**
1. **Removal cost > maintenance cost** (very rare - Pitons are usually low-maintenance)
2. **Chesterton's fence legitimately applies** (unknown function might exist, investigation needed before removal)
3. **Transition period needed** (planned elimination in progress, not indefinite)
4. **Harmless piton** (maintenance cost negligible, elimination energy better spent elsewhere)

**But default should be elimination.** Burden of proof on defenders to show function.

### The Ethics of Scaffold Sunset Compliance

**If you design a Scaffold, you have duty to dismantle it.**

**The obligation structure:**

**At creation (t₀):**
- Build in sunset clause (explicit completion criteria or date)
- Make temporality part of legitimacy ("acceptable because temporary")
- Define success metric (completion = dissolution, not continuation)

**During operation (t_stable):**
- Monitor purpose completion ("Is transition achieved?")
- Resist function creep ("Just one more use case...")
- Maintain sunset visibility (don't let people forget it's temporary)

**At sunset (t_sunset):**
- Execute dissolution (actually dismantle)
- Resist permanence pressure ("Just a little longer...")
- Celebrate completion (not mourn loss)
- **Violating sunset = ethical breach**

**Why sunset violation is unethical:**

**Betrays original legitimacy claim:**
- Scaffold legitimate because temporary
- Claiming temporary → extracting under false pretenses → becomes permanent
- This is **fraud** (claimed one thing, delivered another)

**Creates piton burden on future:**
- Next generation inherits piton they didn't consent to
- Original purpose forgotten, structure persists
- Piton created through sunset violation

**Examples:**

**Emergency powers:**
- Legitimate: Genuine crisis + designed to end when crisis passes
- Illegitimate: Fabricated crisis OR sunset ignored when crisis ends
- **Creator's duty:** End emergency when crisis ends
- Violation: Authoritarian permanence from "temporary" powers

**Therapeutic Scaffolds:**
- Legitimate: Crisis support designed to build capacity
- Illegitimate: Dependency creation, indefinite "support"
- **Therapist's duty:** Work toward client not needing therapy
- Violation: Maintaining client dependence for revenue

**Transitional governments:**
- Legitimate: Hold power until elections possible
- Illegitimate: Delay elections indefinitely
- **Provisional government's duty:** Organize elections, transfer power
- Violation: Provisional becomes permanent dictatorship

**Thailand Senate (2017-2024):**
- Claimed: "Temporary PM selection during transition" (Scaffold)
- Sunset: PM power expired 2024 (partially honored)
- **Violation:** Amendment veto retained (piton obstruction without purpose)
- **Ethical breach:** Sunset partially ignored, Piton created

### The Ethics of Degradation Prevention

**Obligation to monitor constraints you maintain:**

**For Ropes you maintain:**

**Watch for drift events:**
- Extraction accumulation (ε creeping upward)
- Metric substitution (proxy becoming goal)
- Alternative suppression (competing coordination blocked)
- Benefit concentration (reciprocity breaking down)

**Preventive maintenance:**
- Regular extraction audits (measure ε quarterly)
- Beneficiary analysis (who gains/loses systematically?)
- Environment scanning (context shifts affecting function?)
- Reform early (easier to fix Rope than Tangled Rope)

**Example: Open-source governance**
- Maintainers have duty to resist corporate capture
- Watching for extraction = part of maintenance role
- Allowing degradation = maintenance failure
- Reform: Governance rules preventing capture

**For Tangled Ropes you maintain:**

**Don't normalize extraction:**
- Resist "this is just how it works" rhetoric
- Don't defend extraction because coordination is real
- Pursue surgical reform (preserve coordination, excise extraction)
- Make extraction visible (transparency about hybrid structure)

**Example: Professional association**
- Coordination: Quality standards, knowledge sharing
- Extraction: Gatekeeping, credential rent
- Maintenance ≠ defending extraction
- Reform: Maintain standards, reduce gatekeeping costs

**Purity monitoring as maintenance duty:** The drift events listed above (extraction accumulation, metric substitution, alternative suppression, benefit concentration) are all detectable through individual metric changes. But purity score — a composite measure of structural health — can detect degradation *before* any single metric crosses a threshold. A Rope with declining purity still shows low ε, still has voluntary compliance, still tolerates alternatives — but the internal balance between coordination and extraction is shifting.

If you maintain a constraint (a norm, an institution, a coordination mechanism), monitoring its structural health is part of the maintenance obligation. Waiting for ε to cross 0.40 before intervening means you're intervening late — after the Rope has already become a Tangled Rope. Purity monitoring catches the pre-symptomatic phase: the structural equivalent of routine health screening rather than waiting for symptoms. Neglecting purity monitoring when the tools exist is a form of maintenance negligence — not because you caused the degradation, but because you could have detected it earlier and chose not to look.

See logic_extensions.md §2 (purity scoring), constraint_lifecycles.md §III Type 10 (purity drift as pre-symptomatic decay).

**Network responsibility — degradation doesn't stay local:** If a constraint you maintain degrades, the contamination doesn't stop at its boundary. Network drift (Type 11) means degrading constraints contaminate their neighbors — a Snare-ifying workplace doesn't just harm workers directly, it contaminates their family relationships, community participation, and psychological health by draining the shared resource pool. Contamination flows downward (Snare degrades Rope, never Rope upgrades Snare), and entropy increases.

This creates a network responsibility that extends beyond the individual constraint. If you maintain an institution and allow it to degrade through negligence, you bear partial responsibility not only for the intrinsic harm but for the cascade effects on neighboring constraints in the network. An employer who allows workplace culture to become extractive bears responsibility not only for employee experience at work, but for the downstream contamination of employees' non-work constraint health — relationships strained by depletion, health degraded by stress, community participation reduced by exhaustion.

This is not unlimited liability. The claim is narrower: **if you have maintenance responsibility for a constraint, and the tools exist to detect its degradation (purity monitoring, ε tracking), and you choose not to use them, then the cascade effects of degradation you could have prevented are part of your ethical account.** This is maintenance negligence extended to network effects — the structural ethics equivalent of an upstream polluter bearing responsibility for downstream contamination.

**Temporal reclassification as ethical obligation:** Constraint classification is not permanent. A constraint that was genuinely a Mountain at one moment — an impoverished option set where every available path produced serious harm — can become a Tangled Rope or Snare as structural conditions change. If the conditions that produced the impoverished menu have shifted (the agent has acquired power, resources, alternatives, or security that did not exist at the founding moment), then continued invocation of the historically tragic constraint to justify present-day domination is itself a classifiable move. Specifically, it is Naturalization — making a contingent arrangement appear inevitable by pointing to its origins.

The engine now provides the infrastructure for this. Drift events detect when a Mountain-classified constraint accumulates extractive properties over time (extraction_accumulation). The T17 abductive trigger (v6.9) fires when a Mountain shows rising extractiveness at warning or critical severity, producing an advisory hypothesis that the classification may no longer hold. The false_summit_mountain signature override (v6.9) catches Mountains that have identifiable beneficiaries — a structural property incompatible with genuine natural law.

**The ethical principle:** If you invoke a constraint's founding classification to justify current arrangements, you bear the burden of showing that the structural conditions producing the original classification still obtain. A Mountain in 1945 may be a Tangled Rope in 2026. The constraint topology changes over time, and moral analysis must track the change. The mechanism for identifying when a formerly tragic constraint has become a maintained extraction structure is now partially specified: drift analysis detects accumulating extraction, false summit detection catches beneficiary presence, and temporal reclassification review follows. What remains is an obligation on the part of anyone citing the historical classification: demonstrate that the menu is still impoverished, or accept that the classification has shifted and the evaluative implications shift with it.

See logic_extensions.md §4.4 (network contamination), constraint_lifecycles.md §III Type 11 (network drift).

**Maintenance as transformation:**

**Maintenance as preservation:**
- Maintenance can mean controlled degradation (graceful Scaffold sunset)
- Maintenance can mean transformation (reform to better state)
- Maintenance is NOT always "keep exactly as is"

**The dynamic view:**
- Constraints live, degrade, die
- Good maintenance recognizes lifecycle
- Sometimes maintaining = letting die gracefully
- Sometimes maintaining = transforming to new form

### Virtue 6: Containment (For Those Who Value Maturity Over Resolution)

**The Value:** Maturity to hold permanent tensions without forcing resolution (comfort with structural incompletion)

**The Practice:** Recognize irresolvable paradoxes, contain tension through sequential alternation and partial satisfaction, resist forced coherence.

**Conditional statement:** IF you value wisdom over false certainty, THEN accepting irresolvable paradoxes prevents authoritarian drift from forcing coherence through power.

**The recognition:** Some tensions are structurally permanent. They cannot be resolved, only contained.

**Universal paradoxes (across domains):**

**Autonomy ⇄ Connection:**
- Need both independence AND intimacy
- Cannot maximize both simultaneously
- Resolution attempts create: Isolation (pure autonomy) or Fusion (pure connection)

**Security ⇄ Growth:**
- Need both stability AND challenge
- Cannot have both fully at once
- Resolution attempts create: Stagnation (pure security) or Chaos (pure growth)

**Present ⇄ Future:**
- Need both current satisfaction AND future preparation
- Cannot optimize both simultaneously
- Resolution attempts create: Hedonism (pure present) or Asceticism (pure future)

**Domain-specific paradoxes:**

**Political:**
- Majority Rule ⇄ Minority Rights (tyranny of majority vs. tyranny of minority)
- Representation ⇄ Expertise (electability vs. competence)
- Stability ⇄ Accountability (long terms vs. short terms)
- Federalism ⇄ Efficiency (decentralization vs. coordination)

**Aesthetic:**
- Innovation ⇄ Communication (break conventions vs. lose audience)
- Autonomy ⇄ Audience (artistic integrity vs. commercial viability)
- Material ⇄ Concept (pure craft vs. pure idea)
- Vision ⇄ Collaboration (control vs. capacity)

**Psychological:**
- Acceptance ⇄ Change (self-acceptance vs. growth)
- Material frame ⇄ Functional frame (biology vs. agency)

**The Containment Protocol (6 steps):**

**1. Name the paradox**
- What two valuable principles are in tension?
- Are they genuinely incompatible? (not all tensions are paradoxes)
- Example: "I need both autonomy AND deep connection"

**2. Map the oscillation**
- How does tension manifest in your life?
- When do you feel autonomy-pull? When connection-pull?
- Pattern recognition over time

**3. Test edges (can resolution be forced?)**
- Pure autonomy attempted: Isolation, loneliness, loss of support
- Pure connection attempted: Fusion, loss of self, resentment
- **Conclusion:** Resolution impossible, tension is structural

**4. Accept irresolvability**
- This is feature, not bug
- Demanding resolution = demanding impossibility
- Both poles remain valuable
- Tension is permanent

**5. Choose for current context (partial satisfaction)**
- Autonomy this week (solo retreat, personal projects)
- Connection next week (quality time, vulnerability)
- Context-dependent priority
- Sequential alternation, not simultaneous optimization

**6. Hold tension without collapse**
- Don't force resolution
- Don't pick one pole permanently
- Oscillate, alternate, partial satisfaction
- Comfort with incompletion

**What containment is NOT:**

**Not resignation:**
- Resignation: "Nothing matters, both poles pointless"
- Containment: "Both poles matter, tension permanent"

**Not optimization:**
- Optimization: "I can have 100% autonomy AND 100% connection"
- Containment: "Partial satisfaction on each across time"

**Not resolution:**
- Resolution: "I figured it out - perfect balance achieved"
- Containment: "Tension managed through oscillation"

**Not transcendence:**
- Transcendence: "I'm beyond this tension"
- Containment: "I live within this tension skillfully"

**What containment IS:**

**Recognition:**
- This is structural, expected
- Not personal failure
- Permanent feature of constraint-space

**Sequential choice:**
- Context-dependent priority
- "Both, but not simultaneously"
- Rhythmic alternation

**Partial satisfaction:**
- Both poles get attention across time
- Neither gets 100% ever
- Accept incompletion

**Mature navigation:**
- Comfort with permanent tension
- No demand for resolution
- Skillful oscillation

**Strategic benefit:** Prevents authoritarian drift (forcing coherence through power when coherence is impossible). Enables wise navigation within permanent constraints.

**The maturity test:** Can you hold permanent tension without demanding resolution? This is wisdom marker.

**Why this is conditional:** Some people need resolution, certainty, closure. For them, containing irresolvable paradoxes may feel like intellectual failure. The framework doesn't claim everyone must value wisdom over certainty.

## III. The Ethics of Classification Accuracy

**Classification has moral weight.** Getting constraint type wrong causes predictable harm.

### A. The Honesty Obligation

**Why classification is ethical requirement, not just epistemic virtue:**

**1. Action consequences:**
- Wrong classification → wrong response → harm
- Treating Snare as Mountain → surrender to changeable extraction
- Treating Mountain as Snare → waste energy fighting unchangeable
- Treating Tangled Rope as pure type → either defend extraction OR destroy coordination

**2. Resource allocation:**
- Fighting False Mountains wastes collective energy
- Maintaining Pitons wastes individual capacity
- Misclassifying power position wastes strategic opportunities

**3. Power asymmetry:**
- Powerful misclassify their Snares as Mountains (naturalization)
- Powerless misled by naturalization claims
- Classification accuracy resists power-serving falsehoods

**4. Relational integrity:**
- Claiming "I can't" (Mountain) when you mean "I won't" (preference) is dishonesty
- Naturalizing your preferences as partner's constraints is manipulation
- Accurate classification is respect

### B. The False Mountain Problem

**Pattern:** Agent claims constraint is Mountain (unchangeable) when actually:
- Snare (changeable but extractive)
- Rope (changeable coordination)
- Preference (personal choice, not constraint)

**Harm caused:**
- Wastes others' energy (if they believe false claim and try to accommodate)
- Maintains extraction (if Snare successfully naturalized as "just how things are")
- Blocks reform (if Tangled Rope claimed as unchangeable necessity)
- Enables manipulation (claiming inability to avoid accountability)

**Examples:**

**Social/political:**
- "Gender roles are natural" (Snare naturalized as biological Mountain)
- "The market requires this" (Tangled Rope economic policy claimed as necessity)
- "Traditional family structure is only functional form" (Snare naturalized)
- "Trickle-down economics is how markets work" (Snare as economic law)

**Personal:**
- "I can't change my personality" (Abandoned Camp claimed as Substrate)
- "I just don't have time" (priority choice claimed as time scarcity)
- "I can't be vulnerable" (Abandoned Camp claimed as inherent limit)
- "That's just who I am" (identity fusion preventing honest assessment)

**Relational:**
- "I can't do physical affection" (claiming Mountain when it's preference or Abandoned Camp)
- "I don't have time for you" (claiming Mountain when it's deprioritization)
- "I'm just not good at communication" (claiming Substrate when it's untrained skill)

**Institutional:**
- "The Constitution requires this" (claiming Mountain when it's interpretation choice)
- "Market forces demand this" (claiming Mountain when it's policy)
- "We've always done it this way" (claiming Mountain when it's Piton)

**Ethical analysis: Lie or mistake?**

**Lie (dishonest):**
- If claimant has access to evidence contradicting Mountain status
- If claimant resists counter-evidence (dismisses alternatives, doubles down)
- If claimant benefits from Mountain claim (motivated reasoning)

**Mistake (honest error):**
- If claimant genuinely can't see (power blindness, perspective limitation)
- If claimant updates when given evidence (Bayesian honest)
- If claimant has tested and reached wrong conclusion

**Motivated reasoning (gray area):**
- Want to believe (comfort over accuracy)
- Avoid testing (don't want to know truth)
- Selective evidence (notice supporting, dismiss contradicting)

**Test for dishonesty:**

**Questions:**
1. **Have you tested this?** (attempted violation, checked for damage vs. discomfort)
2. **Do you resist counter-evidence?** (dismiss alternative examples, ignore variation)
3. **Who benefits from Mountain claim?** (if you benefit, suspect motivated reasoning)
4. **Would you change this for $1 million?** (reveals preference vs. constraint)
5. **Have you actually tried alternatives?** (reveals assumption vs. knowledge)

**If answer pattern = No testing + Resist evidence + You benefit + Would change for money + No alternatives tried:**
†' **Likely dishonest False Mountain claim**

**Empirical confirmation (corpus audit, February 2026):** The False Mountain problem is not only an ethical failure by human agents — it is the primary failure mode of the framework's own implementation. LLM parsers classified 252 of 594 constraint stories as Mountains where perspectives disagree; 96 had base extractiveness > 0.15, directly violating the Mountain definition. The mechanism is not strategic dishonesty but parser naturalization: the generating LLM defaults to "natural law" when a constraint feels systemic, regardless of enforcement requirements or beneficiary structure. The self-referential case (an LLM classifying AI displacement of workers as "structural market law") demonstrates that the False Mountain problem operates at every level — from individual motivated reasoning to institutional ideology to the classification system itself. See `limitations.md` for full audit.

**Formal detection signatures (v4.1):** The False Mountain problem and its cousin — claiming a Snare is a Rope (coordination-washing) — now have named formal tests:

- **FNL (False Natural Law):** Physics-washing detection. A constraint claiming Mountain status must pass the Boltzmann compliance test — its effects must factorize across independent dimensions. If a claimed "natural law" shows dimension-coupling that correlates with beneficiary structure (e.g., an "inevitable market force" whose effects track political access rather than operating independently of it), factorization fails and the Mountain claim is formally falsified. FNL is the formal version of the cross-cultural invariance test this section already describes, but with a mechanical check: does the constraint behave like a natural law, or does it behave like a constructed system wearing natural-law rhetoric?

- **FCR (False CI_Rope):** Coordination-washing detection. A constraint claiming Rope status must pass the purity test — its coordination function must be structurally sound, not a thin layer over extraction. If purity score is low (coordination function hollowed, extraction dominant), the Rope claim is formally falsified regardless of stated purpose. FCR catches the "we're all in this together" pattern (Section III.H, attack mode 2) mechanically rather than relying on rhetorical analysis alone.

These are formalizations of ethical violations this document already describes, not new ethical principles. What's new is that they're now testable through structural signatures rather than requiring case-by-case judgment about rhetoric and intent. See logic_extensions.md §3 (FNL/FCR detection) and logic_thresholds.md (threshold values).

### C. The Snare Naturalization Defense

**When powerful claim their Snare is Mountain:**

**Pattern:**
1. Extraction structure exists (Snare benefiting powerful)
2. Beneficiaries claim "natural" / "unchangeable" / "necessary" / "just how it works"
3. Alternative suppression ("other arrangements won't work" / "tried before, failed")
4. Resistance framed as naive ("you don't understand reality" / "grow up")

**Examples:**

**Economic:**
- "Unpaid overtime is just commitment" (Snare as workplace necessity)
- "Payday loans are market access for poor" (Snare as beneficial service)
- "Tipping system benefits everyone" (Snare as coordination)

**Social:**
- "Traditional family structure is natural" (Snare as biological necessity)
- "Gender roles are complementary" (Snare as functional coordination)
- "Boys will be boys" (Snare as unchangeable male nature)

**Political:**
- "Gerrymandering is normal redistricting" (Snare as neutral coordination)
- "Two-party system is inevitable" (Snare as mathematical necessity)
- "Money is speech" (Snare as constitutional principle)

**Institutional:**
- "That's just how academic publishing works" (Snare as knowledge coordination)
- "Healthcare tied to employment is efficiency" (Snare as economic optimization)

**Ethical counter-move (when encountering naturalization):**

**1. Demand evidence:**
- Cross-cultural variation? (if varies, not Mountain)
- Historical examples of alternatives? (if existed, not necessary)
- Who enforces this? (if enforced, not natural)
- What happens if violated? (damage or punishment?)

**2. Check beneficiaries:**
- Who gains from Mountain claim?
- Who loses if structure changed?
- Asymmetric benefit distribution → Snare likely

**3. Test edges (damage vs. discomfort):**
- Violation produces objective harm? (Mountain)
- Violation produces subjective discomfort or punishment? (Snare)

**4. Expose construction:**
- When was this created? (t₀ reveals construction)
- By whom? (creators often beneficiaries)
- For what stated purpose? (implementation gap?)

**But maintain epistemic humility:**
- You might be wrong (might actually be Mountain)
- Your power position affects perception (might look like Rope to you, Snare to them)
- Test rigorously, don't assume
- Burden of proof on Mountain claimant (show unchangeability)

### D. Strategic Misclassification (When Is It Justified?)

**Is deliberate misclassification ever ethical?**

**Case 1: Therapeutic framing**

**Scenario:** Therapist treats client's Abandoned Camp as if Substrate (temporarily)

**Justification:**
- Purpose: Build capacity before challenging constraint
- Client not ready to hear "this is changeable" yet
- Temporary misclassification enables eventual accurate classification

**Conditions:**
- Actually temporary (therapist plans to correct later)
- Serves client (not therapist's avoidance or profit)
- Corrected when client ready
- Acknowledged as pedagogical strategy

**Case 2: Political rhetoric (mobilization)**

**Scenario:** Claiming Tangled Rope is pure Snare to mobilize opposition

**Justification:**
- Coordination function negligible compared to extraction
- Mobilization requires clear enemy, not nuanced analysis
- Serves power redistribution (powerless against powerful)

**Conditions:**
- Tangled Rope actually high-extraction (ε > 0.60)
- Simplification doesn't prevent future reform (won't destroy what coordination exists)
- Serves legitimate redistribution (not just power grab)

**Example:** "Healthcare system is pure extraction" (ignores some coordination function but mobilizes reform)

**Case 3: Self-protection (when genuinely trapped)**

**Scenario:** Claiming Snare is Mountain to reduce guilt when can't exit

**Justification:**
- Actually powerless (exit genuinely impossible)
- Mountain frame protects mental health
- Temporary (until power position shifts)

**Conditions:**
- Actually trapped (not just uncomfortable)
- Self-directed only (not claiming to others)
- Temporary framing (will reclassify when able)

**Example:** "I can't leave this abusive job" (actually can't - bills due, no alternatives) → treating as Mountain reduces self-blame

**Case 4: Pedagogical simplification**

**Scenario:** Teaching 3 types before introducing 6

**Justification:**
- Complexity paralysis prevention
- Build foundation before nuance
- Plan to complexify later

**Conditions:**
- Explicit about simplification ("we'll add nuance later")
- Doesn't cause immediate harm
- Actually does introduce complexity when ready

**NOT justified:**

**Unethical misclassification:**
- Claiming your Snare is Mountain to avoid accountability
- Claiming their Mountain is Snare to pressure them
- Claiming Rope is Piton to justify defection
- Claiming preference is Mountain to escape responsibility
- Any misclassification serving only self-interest
- Permanent strategic misclassification (must correct eventually)

### E. The "I Can't" Honesty Protocol

**Most classification dishonesty occurs with "I can't" statements.**

**Three meanings:**

**1. Mountain/Substrate: "I can't violate thermodynamics"**
- Genuine impossibility
- Violation produces damage, not just discomfort
- Independent of power or preference
- **Honest usage**

**2. Preference: "I can't eat that"**
- Actually means "I strongly prefer not to"
- Violation possible but unpleasant
- Choice, not necessity
- **Dishonest when presented as Mountain**

**3. Snare: "I can't leave this job"**
- Genuinely trapped by power asymmetry
- Violation produces real consequences (homelessness, starvation)
- Not preference, actual constraint
- **Honest if accurately describes power position**

**The honest specification protocol:**

**Instead of "I can't":**

**Use precise language:**
- **"I won't"** (preference, choice, priority)
- **"I'm trapped"** (Snare, power asymmetry, genuine constraint but changeable)
- **"I'm unable"** (Mountain/Substrate, genuine impossibility)
- **"I choose not to"** (priority, not constraint)
- **"That would damage me"** (Substrate, testable harm)
- **"I don't want to"** (preference, honest)
- **"I'm not willing to"** (choice, values-based)

**Why this matters:**

**1. Moral responsibility:**
- "I can't" claims immunity from obligation
- If actually "I won't," you're responsible for choice
- Misclassifying preference as constraint is moral evasion

**2. Strategic clarity:**
- Partner can navigate around actual constraints
- Energy wasted negotiating impossible if claim is Mountain but isn't
- Resentment builds when "I can't" revealed as "I won't"

**3. Self-knowledge:**
- Distinguishing preference from constraint is self-examination requirement
- "I can't" claims prevent inquiry
- Modal precision enables growth

**The $1 Million Test:**

**Question:** "Would you do this for $1 million?"

**If yes:**
- It's preference, not constraint
- "I can't" is inaccurate
- Should use "I won't" or "I choose not to"

**If no:**
- Might be genuine constraint (or very strong preference)
- Further testing needed
- Check damage vs. discomfort

**Exceptions:**
- "Would you kill your child for $1M?" (No, but not constraint - value, not Mountain)
- Test reveals priorities, not only constraints

### F. The Relational Honesty Obligation

**In relationships, accurate classification is respect.**

**Dishonest patterns:**

**Pattern 1: Claiming Mountain when it's preference**

**Dishonest:** "I can't be vulnerable"
**Actually means:** "I won't risk vulnerability because I don't trust safety"
**Honest version:** "I'm choosing not to be vulnerable right now because I don't trust this is safe"

**Why dishonesty harms:**
- Partner accommodates impossibility that doesn't exist
- Energy wasted on false constraint
- Resentment when "can't" revealed as "won't"
- Prevents real negotiation (about safety, trust, risk tolerance)

**Pattern 2: Claiming time scarcity when it's deprioritization**

**Dishonest:** "I just don't have time"
**Actually means:** "I'm prioritizing other things over this"
**Honest version:** "I'm choosing to prioritize [work/hobby/other relationship] over time with you"

**Why dishonesty harms:**
- Hides actual choice (deprioritization)
- Partner can't negotiate priorities if hidden
- Creates false sense of external constraint
- Prevents honest conversation about relationship value

**Pattern 3: Claiming inherent inability when it's untrained skill or Abandoned Camp**

**Dishonest:** "That's just who I am" / "I'm just not good at [emotional labor/communication/vulnerability]"
**Actually means:** "I have Abandoned Camp from childhood" OR "I haven't invested in learning this skill"
**Honest version:** "I have strong habits from childhood making this difficult, but it's not unchangeable" OR "I haven't prioritized learning this skill"

**Why dishonesty harms:**

- Prevents growth

- Partner accepts limitation that doesn't exist

**Pattern 4: Defending Piton as functional**

**Actually means:** "I'm attached to this from identity fusion, not because it serves function"
**Honest version:** "This doesn't serve us anymore, but I'm attached to it emotionally - can we talk about letting it go?"

**Why dishonesty harms:**
- Maintains piton at relationship cost
- Energy drain on both people
- Prevents honest assessment
- Blocks relationship evolution

**Daily:**

### H. Epistemic Warfare: When Classification Itself Is the Weapon

**The pattern:** Some constraints extract not through material mechanisms (money, labor, access) but through epistemological ones — attacking the target's ability to accurately classify their own situation.

**Four attack modes:**

**1. False Summit manufacture:** Claiming a constructed system is natural law to prevent resistance. "That's just how the market works" (Mountain claim for a Snare). "It's human nature" (Mountain claim for a culturally specific Rope). "Technology makes this inevitable" (Mountain claim for a policy choice). The corpus audit confirmed this is also the default failure mode of LLM parsers — naturalization is not only a deliberate rhetorical strategy but a systematic processing bias.

**2. Snare camouflage:** Presenting extraction as coordination. "We're all in this together" (Rope claim for a Tangled Rope where beneficiaries and costs are asymmetrically distributed). "This protects you" (Rope claim for a Snare that primarily protects the enforcer). "The system works for everyone" (coordination claim obscuring asymmetric extraction).

**3. Classification inversion:** Calling functional Ropes "tyranny" to justify dismantling coordination. Calling Snares "freedom" to justify maintaining extraction. This operates in both political directions — progressives may attack functional Ropes as extractive, conservatives may defend extractive Snares as coordinative. The ethical test is structural (who benefits, what's enforced, what alternatives exist), not ideological.

**4. Boundary reclassification:** The most sophisticated form. Systematically reframing a bounded conditional offer (Scaffold — "I'll help under these specific conditions, temporarily") as an unbounded obligation (Rope — "this is what family does"), then reframing the boundary-keeper's refusal as aggression (Snare on the boundary-keeper — "he doesn't want anything to do with me"). The reclassification is forwarded to third parties with the original evidence attached, confident it won't be read carefully. This pattern was confirmed empirically — the generating LLM reproduced narcissistic boundary reclassification faithfully, parsing the boundary-keeper as the aggressor because their measured tone read as cold affect while the boundary-attacker's distress read as victimhood.

**Ethical defense:**

Deferential Realism functions as an immune system against epistemic attacks by providing:
- **Vocabulary** to name the attack ("that's a False Summit claim," "that's Snare camouflage")
- **Tests** to verify independently (Six-Test Battery applied to the *claim*, not just the *claimed constraint*)
- **Power analysis** to check who benefits from the classification being offered
- **Structural check** to distinguish narrative framing from structural reality

**The key test:** Apply the Six-Test Battery to the classification *itself*. Who benefits from this constraint being classified as Mountain/Rope/Snare? What enforcement maintains this classification? Would it survive if the classifier lost power? If the classification serves the classifier's interests and requires enforcement to maintain, the classification itself is a Snare — an extractive mechanism operating at the epistemic level.

**Cross-reference:** `epistemology.md` Section VII.D (Epistemic Attacks as Constraint Illusion), `sociology.md` Section V (Relational Integrity Protocol).

### I. The False Equipoise Problem

**Pattern:** A multi-observer framework produces heavily asymmetric structural findings, then presents them as ambiguous because the architecture defaults to "the reader decides" as a terminal move.

**The problem is structural, not rhetorical.** Multi-observer analysis that classifies a constraint from multiple positions can produce findings where the asymmetry is overdetermined: every observer position except the institutional one converges on extraction, the institutional position diverges in the direction the cover story theorem predicts, and the framework presents the result as "reasonable people can disagree." At that density of evidence, stated neutrality is itself a positional claim — the claim that structural asymmetry carries no evaluative weight.

**The diagnostic:** When N of M observer positions converge on the same structural classification, and the divergent positions are explained by incentivized blindness (the institutional observer seeing coordination where the theorem predicts they must), the framework should flag the convergence as constraining the evaluative space. Not determining the moral conclusion — constraining it. "The reader who examines these findings and concludes 'this is fine' owes an explanation of what they are seeing that the framework missed" is honest. "The reader decides" at that density of convergent signal is not.

**Engine implementation (v6.9):** The `scenario_convergence.py` module detects asymmetric convergence patterns across linked constraint sets sharing a beneficiary. When a beneficiary group contains both Mountain-classified and extractive-classified constraints, it flags `mountain_extraction_cover` — the Mountain may be providing legitimation for extraction. When multiple constraints in a group show rising extraction drift, it flags `convergent_extraction_accumulation`. These are post-processing diagnostics; they do not override classification. They flag when the framework's own outputs have narrowed the conclusion space beyond genuine ambiguity.

**Ethical implication:** False equipoise is a classification error with the same structure as the False Mountain problem (§III.B) but operating at the synthesis level rather than the constraint level. A False Mountain misclassifies one constraint. False equipoise misclassifies the evaluative weight of a set of convergent findings. Both serve the same function: making extraction harder to name by disguising a conclusion as an open question.

**When equipoise is genuine:** Genuine ambiguity exists when observer positions diverge for reasons that are not explained by the cover story theorem — when multiple positions have independent structural access to features that support their classification, and the divergence reflects genuine perspectival difference rather than power-modulated blindness. The carbon credit example from §V.C is genuine equipoise: the powerless, moderate, institutional, and analytical positions each access real structural features. The diagnostic for false equipoise is not the presence of divergence but the *pattern* of divergence — whether it tracks the institutional phase transition predicted by Theorem 1 or reflects independent structural access.

## IV. The Three Operational Stabilizers

These principles prevent the virtues from calcifying into rigid dogma or enabling self-justifying withdrawal.

### A. The Ethic of the Scaffold (Transitional Care)

**The principle:** When cutting a Snare or replacing a Rope, build temporary support with a **mandatory sunset clause**.

**Why this matters:** Systems have momentum. People adapt to current constraints even when harmful. Sudden removal can cause collapse worse than the original problem.

**The practice:**

**Stage 1: Load-Bearing Assessment**
- Is this constraint supporting essential functions?
- Are people/institutions dependent on it?
- What happens if removed immediately?

**Stage 2: Scaffold Design**
- Temporary structure maintaining critical function
- Explicit sunset date or trigger condition
- Built-in obsolescence (designed to become unnecessary)

**Stage 3: Transition Management**
- Monitor during replacement
- Adjust Scaffold if needed
- Remove Scaffold when target state is stable

**Stage 4: Scaffold Removal**
- Actually dismantle when purpose is served
- Don't let temporary become permanent
- Celebrate dissolution

**Example: Leaving toxic job**
- Mountain: Need income (rent is due)
- Snare: "You'll never find better" (extraction masquerading as reality)
- Scaffold: Build freelance income for 3 months before quitting
- Transition: Gradual shift from employment to self-employment
- Removal: Once new income is stable, cut ties completely

**The routing reframe (metrics-as-routing):** The calibration ethic gains additional force from a v4.1 insight: framework classifications are routing decisions, not truth pronouncements. When the framework classifies a constraint as Tangled Rope with ε = 0.52, it is not claiming to have measured an objective property of reality with two-decimal precision. It is setting a thermostat — routing behavior toward "reform" rather than "accept" or "resist." The number routes the response; it doesn't describe a metaphysical fact.

This matters ethically because it prevents the framework from being weaponized as a truth-claim device. "The framework says your relationship is a Snare with ε = 0.68" sounds like objective measurement. "The framework routes this toward exit/resist strategies given current evidence" sounds like what it actually is — a decision under uncertainty, revisable with new evidence. The calibration ethic requires treating classifications as hypotheses. The routing reframe makes this concrete: thresholds are governance stands about when to change response behavior, not truth claims about where objective reality divides.

See metrics_as_routing.md for the full thermostat/thermometer distinction and its epistemological implications.

**Why this is ethical (conditionally):** IF you value not causing unnecessary harm during transitions, THEN building Scaffolds prevents collapse. But this is conditional on valuing harm-reduction over speed of change.

### B. The Ethic of Transparency (Modal Honesty)

**The principle:** Distinguish clearly between "cannot" (Mountains), "will not" (preference), "should not" (value judgment), and "need not" (unnecessary Rope).

**The modal confusion problem:**
- "I can't leave this job" might mean:
  - Cannot (Mountain): "No other job exists in my city"
  - Will not (preference): "I prefer security over searching"
  - Should not (value): "Loyalty demands I stay"
  - Need not (Rope): "Contracts bind me for 6 months"

**These are four different constraints requiring four different responses.**

**The practice:**
- Audit your own language: When you say "can't," do you mean it literally?
- Make modality explicit: "I choose not to" vs. "I cannot"
- Distinguish constraint type before responding: Mountain requires acceptance, Snare might require resistance, preference requires owning your choice

**Why this is ethical (conditionally):** IF you value self-knowledge and effective action, THEN modal clarity prevents self-deception. But this assumes you value truth over comfort, which not everyone does.

### C. The Ethic of Calibration (Humility)

**The principle:** Your first classification is a hypothesis, not truth. Reality will correct you. The ethical response is **update gracefully**, not **defend stubbornly**.

**The practice:**

**Bayesian updating:**
```
Prior belief + New evidence → Posterior belief

Example:
"I thought I needed 8 hours sleep" (HIGH confidence)
+ "Functioned well on 6 hours for 3 months" (strong evidence)
→ "I need 6-8 hours depending on stress" (revised, lower confidence)
```

**Classification revision:**
```
Initial: "This is a Mountain" (thought unchangeable)
+ Evidence: "Three people changed it successfully"
→ Updated: "This is a Rope with high barriers" (hard to change, not impossible)
```

**The humility requirement:** Are you **discovering** constraints or **defending** your prior beliefs about constraints? The first is inquiry; the second is theology.

**Why this is ethical (conditionally):** IF you value accuracy over being right, THEN graceful updating serves that value. But this assumes you value truth over consistency, which involves trade-offs.

## V. The Honest Limitations

### A. This Framework Cannot Tell You What to Believe

The framework can help you:
- **Clarify** what you believe about specific constraints
- **Test** those beliefs against evidence
- **Act** consistently with your examined beliefs
- **Update** when evidence contradicts your model

The framework **cannot** tell you:
- Whether souls exist (Gita example - you must examine this yourself)
- Whether hierarchy is natural or constructed (depends on evidence you find compelling)
- Whether a specific constraint is Mountain or Snare (requires your judgment about evidence)
- What your priorities should be (autonomy vs. security, individual vs. collective, etc.)

**This is a method for examining beliefs, not a set of beliefs to adopt.**

### B. Clarity Doesn't Eliminate Constraints

Understanding constraint structure doesn't make constraints disappear. Sometimes clarity just reveals:
- There is no unilateral exit (Stephen Dedalus in *Ulysses*)
- Skillful navigation doesn't change your structural position (Leopold Bloom in *Ulysses*)
- You can see all frames and still must choose within constraints (Molly Bloom in *Ulysses*)

**The wisdom:** Your freedom lies in choosing your orientation within constraints you cannot change, not in transcending all constraint through understanding.

**What clarity provides:**
- Prevents wasted energy fighting unchangeable reality
- Enables strategic response to changeable constraints
- Makes self-deception visible (when you're lying to yourself about constraint type)

**What clarity doesn't provide:**
- Escape from all constraint
- Removal of difficult choices
- Salvation from the human condition

### C. Indexical Variation Is Real

Your position in power structures shapes which constraints you can change and which you must accept.

**Examples:**

**Carbon credit trading:**
- Powerless consumer: Mountain (cannot change, must pay higher prices)
- Small business: Tangled Rope (has coordination function but extracts asymmetrically)
- Multinational corporation: Rope (can navigate strategically, sometimes profit)
- Policy analyst: Snare (extractive structure, could be reformed)

**All four classifications are true simultaneously** because constraint *function* varies by position.

**The ethical requirement:** Be honest about your actual index. Don't pretend to operate from a position you don't occupy.

**Failure modes:**
- Powerful actor claiming powerlessness (pretending changeable constraint is Mountain)
- Powerless actor blaming self for not changing unchangeable constraint (treating Mountain as Rope)
- Outsider claiming insider's constraints aren't real (ignoring indexical variation)

**Asymmetric structural findings are not symmetric evaluative objects.** When the framework produces findings that are structurally asymmetric — one population experiences liberation while another experiences displacement, one agent coordinates while another is extracted from — the asymmetry in the findings does not cancel out. The damage-vs-discomfort test (§II, Virtue 1) handles binary cases, but it does not address cases where both sides experience real consequences that differ in structural kind.

The distinction: first-person meaning and third-person structure are not equivalent categories. "Liberation" expresses the internal valuation of participants — how actors experienced and endorsed the project. "Colonization" or "displacement" describes the external relation imposed on others — what the project did structurally to a population that did not choose it. The first is a claim about meaning; the second is a claim about structure. Moral reasoning tracks the structure because that is where harm, coercion, and rights violations are locatable. Sincere experience of liberation does not negate the objective fact of dispossession, any more than sincere belief in a constraint's necessity makes it a Mountain.

The framework should not treat asymmetric structural findings as producing symmetric evaluative weight. When the engine's own classifications show that the same beneficiary group appears in both a Mountain-classified constraint and an extractive-classified constraint (the `mountain_extraction_cover` pattern detected by `scenario_convergence.py`), the Mountain is not providing a neutral justification — it is providing structural cover. The evaluative asymmetry follows from the structural asymmetry. Presenting them as equally weighted perspectives is the false equipoise problem (§III.I) applied to harm accounting.

**This is where the framework makes an ethical claim:** Lying about your index position (pretending you have less power than you do, or more power than you do) to avoid responsibility or to self-justify is **intellectual dishonesty**. Not because dishonesty is abstractly bad, but because it leads to systematic strategic errors.

## VI. Integration: How the Ethics Work Together

The virtues and stabilizers form a system:

**The Feedback Loop:**

1. **Examine** your beliefs about constraints (Foundation)
2. **Classify** what you face (Six-Test Battery from epistemology.md)
3. **Choose** appropriate virtue:
   - Unchangeable? → Acceptance
   - Extractive? → Resistance
   - Hybrid? → Reform
   - Missing? → Construction
   - Functional? → Maintenance
   - Paradoxical? → Containment
   - Power-scaling ambiguity (Naturalized)? → Investigate (determine actual type before applying virtue)
4. **Stabilize** with operational principles:
   - Build Scaffolds for transitions
   - Maintain modal transparency
   - Update classifications gracefully
5. **Act** consistently with examined beliefs
6. **Return to 1** (perpetual examination)

**The system isn't linear—it's recursive.** You examine, classify, act, encounter new evidence, re-examine, re-classify, re-act. This is ongoing practice, not one-time setup.

**The failure modes:**
- Skip examination → act on unexamined beliefs → strategic errors
- Skip classification → respond inappropriately → waste energy
- Skip stabilizers → virtues calcify into dogma → lose flexibility
- Skip updating → defend wrong classifications → compound errors

**The success pattern:**
- Honest examination
- Accurate classification
- Appropriate response
- Graceful updating
- Perpetual refinement

## VII. Conclusion: The Examined Life Within Constraints

Deferential Realist ethics is not a prescription for what to value. It's a method for **living consistently with what you actually believe about the constraints you face**.

**The core practices:**

1. **Examine what you believe** (Socratic requirement)
2. **Classify accurately** (epistemological honesty)
3. **Respond appropriately** (conditional virtues)
4. **Maintain honestly** (resource stewardship)
5. **Update gracefully** (Bayesian humility)
6. **Accept incompletion** (wisdom over certainty)

**The ethical failures:**

1. **Unexamined belief** (acting on assumptions, not tested beliefs)
2. **Classification dishonesty** (False Mountains, naturalization, modal confusion)
3. **Inappropriate response** (treating Tangled Rope as pure type)
4. **Maintenance errors** (defending Pitons, violating Scaffold sunsets)
5. **Stubborn defense** (refusing to update when corrected)
6. **Forced resolution** (demanding certainty when tension is structural)

**The honest limitations:**

- Clarity doesn't eliminate constraint
- Classification doesn't determine values
- Understanding doesn't grant power
- Precision doesn't guarantee outcomes

**The goal:** Use finite energy effectively within actual constraints. Don't waste it fighting unchangeable reality. Don't surrender it to changeable extraction. Don't demand resolution of structural tensions. Navigate honestly within what actually binds you.

**That's Deferential Realist ethics.**
