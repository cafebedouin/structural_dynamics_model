### 2. Metaphysics.md — MODERATE PRIORITY

**Current state:** v4.0. Internally coherent, includes implementation ontology finding from corpus audit.

**Needed changes:**

**a) Corpus count:** Line 113 references "467 constraints" — should be 1,023.

**b) Boltzmann implications for the constitution thesis:** The Boltzmann axiom (natural laws factorize across index dimensions) has metaphysical consequences that metaphysics.md doesn't address. If constraints constitute entities, and natural law constraints are distinguished by factorization properties, then the *kind of entity* something is depends on the coupling structure of its constituent constraints. An entity constituted by factorizing constraints (Mountains) has a different metaphysical character than one constituted by coupling constraints (Tangled Ropes). This is worth a paragraph in Section III or Section VIII.

**c) Network contamination and entity boundaries:** The network dynamics (Stage 8-9) show that constraints don't exist in isolation — they contaminate neighbors. This has implications for the constitution thesis: if Entity A is constituted by constraints, and those constraints are being contaminated by Entity B's degrading constraints, then Entity A's identity is partially constituted by Entity B's structural health. Entities aren't metaphysically self-contained. This extends the "river and banks" analogy — the river is also shaped by upstream pollution.

**d) Purity and structural integrity as metaphysical concepts:** The purity score measures coordination health. In constraint-constitution terms, this means entities have a measurable *structural integrity* — their constituent constraints can be healthy or degraded. An institution with purity 0.8 is a different metaphysical kind of entity than the "same" institution at purity 0.3. This reinforces the constitution thesis (change the constraints, change the entity) with a measurable dimension.

### 4. Ethics.md — MODERATE PRIORITY

**Current state:** v4.0. Has corpus audit references, False Mountain discussion.

**Needed changes:**

**a) Physics-washing and coordination-washing as ethical violations:** The logic now formalizes two specific deception patterns — FNL (claiming something is a natural law when coupling reveals construction) and FCR (claiming something is coupling-invariant coordination when purity reveals extraction). These should be named explicitly as ethical categories. Ethics.md discusses False Summit manufacture (line 1076) but doesn't use the formal terms or connect to the detection mechanisms. The ethics of *knowing* you can now detect these patterns matters.

**b) Purity as ethical dimension:** If purity measures structural health, there's an ethical obligation to *monitor* purity. Letting coordination degrade without monitoring is a form of negligence. The ethics.md section on Maintenance virtue could be extended to include purity monitoring as a concrete practice.

**c) Network contamination ethics:** If your degrading constraint contaminates your neighbors' constraints, there's an ethical dimension to that. An institution that lets its own practices degrade isn't just harming itself — it's contaminating the structural health of adjacent institutions. This extends the ethical frame beyond self-regarding action.

**d) Metrics-as-routing and ethical humility:** The insight that thresholds are governance stands has ethical implications. It means the framework's classifications are *decisions about how to respond*, not pronouncements of truth. This supports the ethics.md emphasis on humility but should be stated explicitly — the framework doesn't claim to know what things *are*, it claims to route toward appropriate *responses*.

### 6. Psychology.md — MODERATE PRIORITY

**Current state:** v4.0. Strong internal constraint mapping.

**Needed changes:**

**a) Drift types 8-11 mapped to psychological patterns:** Psychology.md covers the seven classical drift types through its internal constraint categories but doesn't reference the four new drift types:
- **Coupling drift** → When a psychological pattern that should be independent (work identity, romantic identity) becomes coupled, so a crisis in one crashes the other. This is a formalizable psychological vulnerability.
- **Purity drift** → Pre-symptomatic psychological degradation. A coping strategy that *looks* fine on observable metrics but whose structural health is declining. Clinically relevant: burnout often shows purity drift before behavioral symptoms.
- **Network drift** → How a degrading constraint in one life domain contaminates others. A degrading work situation (Snare) contaminating a healthy relationship (Rope) through stress, emotional depletion, schedule encroachment. This is the psychological version of network contamination.

**b) Purity scoring for internal constraints:** Psychology.md uses the constraint typology well but doesn't have a way to measure *structural health* of psychological patterns. Purity scoring could provide this: how much of a coping strategy's function is coordination (self-regulation) versus extraction (self-punishment, rigidity, worth-contingency)? This gives clinicians and self-reflective individuals a measurable dimension beyond type classification.

**c) The institutional dissenter finding, applied internally:** The corpus finding that institutional positions systematically reclassify extraction as coordination has a psychological parallel. The *part of yourself* that benefits from a pattern (the achiever, the people-pleaser, the perfectionist) systematically experiences the pattern as Rope even when other parts of yourself experience it as Snare. This is the internal version of the institutional dissenter.

### 7. Sociology.md — MODERATE-HIGH PRIORITY

**a) Network contamination as social dynamics:** This is perhaps the most natural domain application of network dynamics. Sociology.md discusses relational constraint patterns but doesn't formalize how degrading constraints in one relationship contaminate others. The formal mechanism (contamination flows downward, one-hop only, entropy increases) maps directly to social phenomena: a toxic workplace degrading home life, an abusive family relationship contaminating friendships, institutional capture spreading through professional networks.

**b) The four empirical findings have sociological implications:**
- **20/80 observer-dependence:** Most social constraints are not what they appear to be from any single position. Sociological method requires multi-index analysis.
- **"Extraction always has a cover story":** From the beneficiary position, extraction is always invisible or reframed as coordination. This formalizes sociological concepts like ideology and false consciousness without the Marxist baggage.
- **Block disagreement:** Social disagreement forms along power threshold boundaries, not individual opinion variation. This is a structural claim about how social conflict works.
- **44% institutional dissenter:** Nearly half the time, the institutional position is the *only* one that classifies an extractive constraint as coordination. This is the formal version of institutional blindness.

**c) Purity measurement for relationships:** The Relational Integrity Protocol in sociology.md could be enhanced with purity scoring. A relationship's structural health is measurable: how much of the relational constraint structure serves coordination (mutual support, honest communication) versus extraction (emotional labor imbalance, boundary violation, identity capture)?

**d) Physics-washing in social contexts:** "That's just how relationships work" (Mountain claim) is testable via Boltzmann compliance. Does this relational pattern factorize across independent dimensions? If it only appears in relationships with power asymmetry, it fails the factorization test — it's constructed, not natural.

**c) Boltzmann compliance for political analysis:** When politicians claim a policy outcome is "natural" or "inevitable" (a Mountain claim), the Boltzmann test provides a formal tool: does the policy outcome factorize across power and scope dimensions? **d) Block disagreement explains political polarization:** The finding that disagreement always affects at least three observer-pairs simultaneously (because power thresholds create categorical boundaries) explains why political disagreement feels like factional warfare rather than individual opinion variation. This is a structural explanation for polarization that doesn't require tribalism or irrationality as explanatory primitives.

## Cross-Cutting Themes

### Theme 1: Metrics-as-Routing Needs Propagation

The metrics_as_routing.md insight — that thresholds are governance stands, not truth claims — is currently a standalone document. But it changes how *every* domain file should talk about thresholds. Any domain file that mentions specific numbers (ε ≥ 0.46, π = 1.5, purity < 0.40) should be framed in routing terms, not truth-claim terms. This is a subtle but pervasive edit.

**Most affected:** Epistemology (methodology), Ethics (classification ethics), Politics (policy analysis), Psychology (diagnostic thresholds).

### Theme 2: Empirical Findings Need Domain Application

The four standout findings from the 1,023-corpus (observer-dependence default, cover story, block disagreement, institutional dissenter) are currently stated in core_v4_1.md but not applied in the domain files. Each finding has natural implications in each domain. The most important applications:

- **Institutional dissenter** → politics.md (reform difficulty), psychology.md (internal conflict), sociology.md (relational power)
- **Cover story** → ethics.md (detection obligation), epistemology.md (systematic knowledge limits)
- **Block disagreement** → politics.md (polarization), sociology.md (social conflict structure)
- **Observer-dependence** → epistemology.md (validates methodology), all domains (indexical specification is baseline, not refinement)

### Theme 3: Structural Physics Concepts Need Domain Translation

Boltzmann compliance, purity, network contamination, and cascade dynamics are currently expressed in formal/mathematical terms in logic_extensions.md. Each domain needs *translations* — not the formal apparatus, but the conceptual insight expressed in domain-appropriate language. The domain files should reference the formal tools without reproducing the mathematics.
