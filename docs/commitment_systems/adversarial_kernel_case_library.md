# Adversarial Kernel Case Library

*The adjudicated output of an eight-model red-team against the diachronic commitment-dynamics theory (the predecessor "Commitment Systems and Possibility Space," now re-carved as "The Decay and Transmission of Commitments"). Eight independent models were instructed to break the theory's joints, not illustrate them — a confirming case counts for nothing; only a case that survives a defender's absorption counts. This document deduplicates the returns, adjudicates each distinct case as a real break / absorbable illustration / artifact, and records what each did to the theory.*

*Provenance and confidence: each case is a real (or realistic) historical example as proposed by a red-team model. The cases' evidential weight comes from being actual trajectories in the world, but the historical details (dates, counts, attributions) are as the models asserted them and have not been independently verified here. Where a detail is load-bearing and uncertain, it is flagged. Treat this as a curated test set, not a fact-checked encyclopedia — the value is in which joints the cases break, not in the precision of any single date.*

*How to read the verdict column: a case is a REAL BREAK if a defender of the theory cannot absorb it without circularity or stipulation; an ILLUSTRATION if the defender absorbs it cleanly (these are discarded as non-breakers); an ARTIFACT if the "break" is an effect of the measurement method rather than the theory (e.g. beneficiary-hunting finding beneficiaries by construction).*

## Summary of what broke

The mechanisms (decay, reservoir, symbol/commitment split, naturalization, catastrophe) split cleanly from the kinematics (the five motions). The mechanisms mostly survived, with three strong-form corrections forced by real cases. The five-motion taxonomy did not survive as a carving: it was shown non-exhaustive (missing top-down and contraction motions), with two cells collapsing (nascent/site-expansion) and one acting as a junk drawer (swap). Convergent breaks — the same case found independently by multiple models — are the trustworthy ones and are marked.

## D5 — The catastrophe theorem

**Original claim:** A population structurally CANNOT retain hard-won tacit catastrophe-knowledge across generations of non-catastrophe, because retaining tacit content requires exercising it and exercising it requires the catastrophe. The forgetting is adaptive.

This was the most-attacked joint (seven of eight models) and the most common choice for single strongest breaker. It does not survive as a theorem.

**Aneyoshi tsunami stones** — REAL BREAK, the decisive case. A Japanese village inscribed stone markers after earlier tsunamis (the models cited 1896 and 1933) with a warning not to build below the line. For decades of non-catastrophe (the models cited ~78 years to 2011), villagers exercised a *behavioral* land-use commitment — building above the marker, refusing development below it, teaching the line to children — and survived the 2011 tsunami while neighboring towns relying on engineered seawalls did not. *Defender's absorption:* call it a symbol-husk (the stone) or claim the memorial ritual was the exercise. *Why it fails:* what persisted was actionable land-use competence, not a commemorative husk; retention happened across non-catastrophe generations, and forgetting would have been maladaptive — the inverse of the theorem's "adaptive forgetting." [Load-bearing dates asserted by models; the structure of the case holds regardless of exact years.]

**Dutch flood-control culture** — REAL BREAK (convergent). After the 1953 North Sea flood, institutionalized drills, dike-inspection routines, evacuation knowledge, and education maintained tacit preparedness across non-catastrophe generations. Same structure as Aneyoshi at national scale.

**Aviation / nuclear safety** — REAL BREAK. Stall recovery, crew resource management, meltdown-avoidance — tacit, embodied, situational competence retained for decades without recurring catastrophe, via simulators, drills, and near-miss analysis. *Defender's absorption:* "simulators are proxy catastrophes, so exercise still requires the catastrophe." *Why it fails:* if drills count as the required exercise, D5 collapses into D1 (all knowledge needs exercise) and loses its distinctive claim; the special "requires the catastrophe" content is gone.

**Jewish ritual catastrophe-memory (Tisha B'Av, Kodashim, Passover)** — WEAKER than rated. Several models offered these as decisive D5 breaks. They are weaker, because D5 already *predicts* the commemorative husk survives — what persists in these cases is mourning-practice and boundary-norms, not survival-competence. They break D1's reachability (see below) more cleanly than they break D5.

**The lone non-breaker:** one model (Grok-flavored) could not break D5 and conceded simulations "approximate but don't fully refute." That concession is the defender's best case, not a defense — it's the gradient the corrected theory adopts.

**What it did to the theory:** D5 demoted from theorem to gradient. *Tacit catastrophe-knowledge decays by default; is rescuable by costly deliberate simulation; the rescue is lossy and more complete for proceduralizable knowledge (land-use rules, checklists) than for deep embodied competence; simulation competes for finite attention and is first to lapse as peace lengthens.* The "adaptive forgetting" insight survives as a budget decision under long peace, not a structural necessity.

## D2 — Culture-level finite capacity

**Original claim:** A population holds only so many live commitments; committing to one occupies capacity its alternatives need to stay reachable. Commitment is zero-sum against the live set.

Broken decisively and convergently (six of eight) at the *culture* level by one repeated structure: cultures accumulate live commitments without shedding, via compartmentalization and layering.

**Legal pluralism** — REAL BREAK (convergent, the strongest single D2 breaker). Multiple personal-law regimes coexisting under one state, for decades, with no observable zero-sum shedding (models cited India's overlapping Hindu/Muslim/Christian/Parsi/secular marriage law; post-1945 European stacks layering human rights, welfare, EU and national and customary law; US constitutional interpretive schools held simultaneously). *Defender's absorption:* redraw the boundary of "a commitment" wider, or partition into sub-domains so capacity is finite "within" each. *Why it fails:* independently identified as circular by four models — if "finite" expands by re-bounding whenever violated, it predicts nothing.

**The four Sunni madhhabs** — REAL BREAK. Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali coexisting for centuries, each with live, contradictory kernels on the same domain, held across the same nested population without shedding.

**Shinbutsu-shūgō (Shinto/Buddhist syncretism)** — REAL BREAK / partial. Both ontologies held simultaneously for centuries. *Defender's absorption:* one syncretic meta-kernel, or different domains (life vs. death). *Why it half-fails:* the meta-kernel move makes D2 unfalsifiable; the domain move concedes capacity is not zero-sum against the live set but only within arbitrarily narrow domains.

**What it did to the theory:** the culture-level zero-sum claim is retired. The individual-cognitive bound and the ecological (language-death) bound were unattacked and survive. Culture-level commitment is governed by *reservoir survival*, not a zero-sum ceiling — which the theory's own reservoir mechanism (a commitment survives in one specialist reservoir) already implies, and which is what defeats the zero-sum framing.

## D1 — Strict reachability

**Original claim:** Reachability reduces to extant practice — a commitment with zero living practitioners is gone, unrecoverable.

Broken by revival cases. This break is the most *productive* in the set, because it forced a merger that strengthened the theory.

**Modern Hebrew** — REAL BREAK. Spoken/native Hebrew sat at zero lived practice for ~two millennia (surviving only liturgically — a symbol) and was reconstructed into a living daily language. *Defender's absorption:* liturgical reading was the exercise, or it was a nascent kernel. *Why it fails:* reciting a husk is not native language generation (commerce, slang, secular life); and the reconstruction drew its grammar and vocabulary from the supposedly out-of-reach symbol. A dead tacit commitment was resurrected from a surviving symbol — which strict D1 says is impossible.

**Classical Latin revival (Renaissance humanists)** — REAL BREAK. Spoken/stylistic Classical Latin was gone (medieval Latin had diverged); humanists reconstructed the earlier form from texts. No one was practicing the Classical form, yet it became reachable again.

**Rabbinic preservation of Temple sacrifice (Kodashim)** — REAL BREAK / COLLAPSE with D4. The detailed form of sacrifice studied continuously for ~1,900 years with zero performance. *Why it matters:* if "study" counts as exercise (saving D1), then D1's "exercise" expands to include exactly what D4 calls "symbol surviving in archives" — and the two mechanisms become indistinguishable. The case forces the D1/D4 relationship to be resolved.

**The cross-document insight (decisive):** one model attacked D4 by arguing it never makes a prediction distinct from D1 (wherever a symbol survives without practice, disuse already explains the loss). That attack is *refuted by the Hebrew/Latin cases other models supplied*: strict D1 predicts "gone, unrecoverable"; the symbol/commitment split predicts "symbol survived, reconstruction possible." They diverge — and reconstruction happened. So D1 and D4 do diverge in prediction, the attack on D4 fails, and the correct move is to *merge* them: reachability = extant practice ∪ symbolic reconstructability, where a reconstructed commitment inherits only its symbolic fraction and re-improvises the tacit fraction at a loss.

**What it did to the theory:** strict reachability is false; the merged reachability-with-reconstruction mechanism is the spine's load-bearing center.

## D3 — Path-naturalization ("no beneficiary")

**Original claim:** A commitment can look natural purely because its alternatives lapsed through disuse, with NO beneficiary — distinct from the ordinary case where a hidden beneficiary sits behind a cover story.

This is the subtlest verdict in the set: the *wording* breaks, but most of the breaks are *artifacts* of the attack method.

**QWERTY** — proposed by four models as a D3 break, and an ARTIFACT in all four. The argument: QWERTY looks natural but has clear beneficiaries (manufacturers, training institutions, network incumbents), so "no beneficiary" is false. *Why this is an artifact, not a clean break:* the models hunted for beneficiaries and found them — which is exactly what a beneficiary-indexed search does by construction, the same blindness D3 itself names, inverted. QWERTY was never a clean D3 case (it has obvious network-effect beneficiaries), so "QWERTY breaks D3" really means "the models picked an example that was never D3." The convergence here is a measurement artifact, not signal.

**Free market naturalization, printing-press Reformation** — same artifact shape. Beneficiaries found because beneficiaries were sought.

**The real correction (from the non-adversarial reviewer):** the clean distinction is not *beneficiary vs. none* — it's *engineered closure at the time of naturalization vs. lapsed closure that later accretes post-hoc defenders*. A commitment can be naturalized by lapse (no one engineered the alternatives' death) and then acquire beneficiaries who defend the status quo. The naturalizing mechanism (lapse) and the maintaining mechanism (later beneficiaries) are distinct; conflating them made "no beneficiary" both attractive and false.

**What it did to the theory:** "no beneficiary" wording is dead. The mechanism survives — lapse-naturalization is structurally distinct from extraction-naturalization, and a beneficiary-hunting instrument is blind to it — but on probation, with ZERO validated clean instances and a sharper definition requiring only the absence of *engineered* closure.

## The motions (M-set) — the largest casualty

**Original claim:** Cultural change decomposes into exactly five motions of a window over possibility space: climb, drop, swap, nascent, site-expansion.

The five-motion taxonomy is not a carving.

**Missing motion — top-down imposition** — REAL BREAK (convergent, 3/8). The five motions assume *endogenous* change (climbs from a fringe, internal swap). They have no cell for a commitment installed at the top of the stack and forced downward into dominant practice. Cases: the Meiji calendar/dress imposition; the Turkish alphabet reform (Law 1353, 1928) — Latin-script Turkish had effectively zero practitioners before the law and was imposed top-down within months, so there was no fringe to climb from; the Mormon polygamy→monogamy reversal under US federal coercion (Manifesto, 1890) — a swap driven exogenously, not internally revised. One model proposed "M6 exogenous override"; two proposed "cascade/drill-down" — the same gap, named three ways.

**Missing motion — contraction** — REAL BREAK. The post-1945 disappearance of "winnable great-power total war" as a *thinkable* doctrine — a commitment becoming not merely fringe but unthinkable, removed from the possibility space. The taxonomy has site-*expansion* (the space gains a point) but no inverse, even though the theory's own mechanisms (decay, the window shedding states) describe contraction. The kinematics claim contraction; the motion list has no cell for it.

**Collapse — nascent vs. site-expansion** — REAL BREAK. No criterion distinguishes "this became *thinkable*" (M5) from "this was first *held*" (M4). Cases: intellectual property as a legal category (~Statute of Anne, 1710); electronic/digital money; zero-as-a-number entering Western mathematics. Each is describable as both, with no principled cut. This is the synchronic/diachronic seam surfacing in the kinematics — the boundary between "the space changed" and "the occupied set changed."

**Junk drawer — swap** — SURVIVED by being too permissive. "The held commitment changes content without changing level" is so unconstrained it absorbs nearly any horizontal change not obviously a climb or drop. Robust the way a residual category is robust — by being whatever-else. Two models flagged this independently.

**Survived — drop.** A dominant commitment falling to contested fringe is descriptively robust. The repeated attack "drop just IS decay" (M2 collapses into D1) is a *category error*: it conflates the kinematic description (what moved) with the dynamic mechanism (what drove it). The theory should state that layer distinction explicitly, but the attack is not a successful break.

**Non-break — overdetermination of composite events.** Several models offered the Protestant Reformation, Vatican II, gold→fiat, and dueling's decline as "overdetermined" — fitting M1+M2+M3+M5 simultaneously, so the classification is arbitrary. This is a NON-BREAK: a taxonomy of *elementary* motions is not refuted by *composite* events being composite, any more than "translation + rotation" is refuted by a tumbling object. The defender absorbs it cleanly ("complex events are composites of motions"). The exception is the M4/M5 collapse above, which is a real seam, not mere composition.

**What it did to the theory:** the five-motion taxonomy is demoted from a carving to an informal descriptive vocabulary. Climb/drop/swap plus the added imposed and contraction motions are kept as description, explicitly disclaimed as a joint-carving. Change is a flow driven by the mechanisms, not a set of discrete labeled moves.

## Joints that survived

**D4 — symbol/commitment split.** Rated most robust by three models. The decay-rate asymmetry (symbol slow/archivable, commitment fast/exercise-dependent) is observable across liturgical, legal, and linguistic domains, and the attack that it is redundant with D1 fails on the reconstruction cases (see D1 above). It is the spine.

**Individual-cognitive and ecological capacity bounds.** Unattacked; independently attested. Finite capacity survives at the extremes and fails only in the middle (culture level).

**M2 drop** (as description, not taxonomy joint) — see above.

## How to use this library

This is the dynamics theory's native test bed — the cases it must survive, the equivalent of the paradoxes that forced the original trifurcation's joints into existence. The theory was previously tested only against its own confirming illustrations (Spartan exposure, family famine-knowledge, obsolete-craft vocabulary), which is why the predecessor over-claimed: confirming cases cannot carve a theory. These adversarial cases carve it.

Two cautions for the next user. First, the convergent breaks (same case from multiple independent models — Aneyoshi for D5, legal pluralism for D2, top-down imposition for the M-set) are the trustworthy ones; a lone clever break is weaker. Second, the QWERTY/D3 cluster is the cautionary case: four models converged on it and they were all committing the same measurement artifact (beneficiary-hunting finds beneficiaries), so *convergence is not proof* — convergence on an artifact is still an artifact. When extending this library, the test for a real break remains the defender's-absorption test: can a defender of the theory absorb the case without circularity or stipulation? If yes, it is an illustration; discard it. Only the un-absorbable cases carve.
