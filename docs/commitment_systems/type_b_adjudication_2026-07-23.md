# Adjudication: Commitment Systems is a refinement on Type B (2026-07-23)

**Question.** Is *Commitment Systems and the Architecture of Drift* (this directory;
current sketch v5.2) a refinement on Type B of the `docs/debugging_philosophy.md`
trifurcation, or a fourth type? Raised in a Claude-web planning conversation
2026-07-23; the counter-position ("blocked Type A" / a new "extraction-generated
Type D") was argued and withdrawn. Recorded here so the classification ruling is
findable from the doc family, not only from chat.

## The ruling (operator): types are individuated by their FIX, not their generating mechanism

The trifurcation's Stage 2 is a remedy test — *does freezing the frame eliminate the
contradiction?* — not a mechanism test. "Drift-generated" is a label on the Type A
bucket, not the criterion for entering it. Applied to a commitment system: declare the
kernel at t₀, observe practice at t₂, and frame-fixing returns a clean report of a
plank replaced — no contradiction. The pass is trivial and uninformative, because a
drifting institution is not a confused reasoner; the taxonomy diagnoses defects in a
reasoning instrument, and the frame test here isn't measuring anything. So the
presence of drift and time does not route Commitment Systems to Type A. Same logic
for Type C: fixing the observer index is necessary but not sufficient.

## What the refinement is, stated precisely

Debugging Philosophy's Type B (kernel failures: revise the axioms) **silently assumed
the reviser exists**. Russell got ZF; the Liar got Tarski — mathematics had standing
to patch its own kernel and a community that wanted the patch. That assumption is
invisible inside mathematics and false nearly everywhere else. Drop it and B splits:

- **Revisable-B** — the Debugging Philosophy cases; kernel revision is available and
  wanted.
- **Blocked-B** — kernel revision is structurally unavailable to the only parties
  with standing. Commitment Systems is the theory of this half; the five patterns
  are the map of what blocked-B does with drift it cannot ratify. Interpretive
  accretion is blocked-B's most successful workaround; anchored fixity without an
  accretion layer is blocked-B with no workaround (the one that shatters); Sparta at
  Leuctra is unacknowledged drift that has finished converting into a structural
  problem — the conversion is one-directional (the *Everything Becomes Taste* arrow).

## The withdrawn Type D

"Extraction-generated" (an agent with stakes in the confusion persisting; fix =
change who has standing to acknowledge) collapses into B: the authority structure is
part of the system's constitution, so its fix is kernel-level. What survives of the
objection is not a fourth type but exactly the revisable/blocked split above — the
re-description "revise the standing axiom" loses usefulness when the only licensed
revisers are structurally unable to act, and *that* is the property blocked-B names.

## Marked open notes (operator's to take or leave)

1. **The Euclid exception has a second face.** The no-drift case (axioms determine
   the space deterministically) still contains a seat — at the *choice of axiom set*
   rather than in the operation. The parallel postulate read as necessity for two
   millennia while a coherent otherwise stood beside it unbuilt. Deterministic where
   facts decide, declared where seats decide.
2. **Cost of remedy-identity.** If types are fixed by their fix, the framework is a
   theory of *repair*, not of drift — drift is the given, repairability the subject.
   "The Architecture of Drift" names the half that isn't the contribution. A naming/
   emphasis question, not a defect; unresolved.
