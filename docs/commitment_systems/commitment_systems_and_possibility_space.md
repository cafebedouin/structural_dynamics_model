# Commitment Systems and Possibility Space

*A theoretical sketch, v0.1*

---

**Status, stated up front and meant.** Everything in this document is conjecture. It is a theory of how commitment systems relate to the space of commitments they did not make — developed through reasoning, not measurement, and offered as a structure to think with, not a finding. It has been tested once, against the Deferential Realism (DR) constraint corpus, and that test came back null: the apparatus this document describes left essentially no fingerprint in the corpus where, if it were a feature of the DR engine, it should have appeared (see §10). That null is the reason this material lives in its own document rather than in the commitment-systems framework proper. The theory is not refuted — a null in one corpus is not a refutation of a theory of culture — but it is unsupported, and the reader should hold every claim below as "this is what would follow *if* the structure is real," never as "this is how things are." Where the document reaches for physics or mathematics, it is reaching for *candidate formalisms* whose fit is unverified, not borrowing their authority. The interesting thing here is a seed. It is planted in this document precisely so it can be dug up and checked later, with this status note attached.

---

## 1. The dimension the engine does not index

The DR engine measures one thing well: how the classification of a fixed constraint depends on the position of the observer. Its index is the observer — power, exit options, time horizon, scope. Given a constraint, it tells you that the powerless see a snare where the institutional see coordination, and it measures the structure of that disagreement. This is the *observer axis*, and it is built, validated, and load-bearing.

But a constraint does not arrive from nowhere. It exists because some population committed to a particular kernel out of a space of kernels it could have committed to instead. The Spartan exposed the deformed infant on the hillside; the naming-ceremony culture grants personhood at a rite weeks after birth; the modern WEIRD society locates the boundary somewhere between conception and birth and argues bitterly about where. These are not three observer positions on one constraint. They are three different *commitments* — three different kernels, drawn from a space of possible kernels, most of which no one is currently holding.

The observer axis takes the constraint as given and varies the seat you read it from. There is a second question it cannot ask: *why this kernel and not another, out of what range, held by whom, and what happened to the alternatives.* Call this the **committer** dimension. It is not a coordinate on the observer. The observer axis tells you what a constraint looks like from where you stand. The committer dimension tells you which constraint exists to be looked at, and what the unchosen alternatives were. The two are orthogonal: a full reading of the pregnant fifteen-year-old's situation needs both — the committer question (which moral-standing kernel her society committed to, against the sea of kernels it did not) *and* the observer question (given that kernel, what the resulting constraint costs her from the position she occupies). She lives at the intersection.

The conjecture of this document is that the committer dimension has structure — that the space of possible commitments is not a flat inventory but a shaped, moving, finite thing — and that several phenomena the existing framework treats as separate (cross-cultural difference, the naturalization of the contingent, cultural transmission and its failures) are faces of that single structure.

## 2. The reachable window over possibility space

Begin with the space of possible kernels — every commitment a population *could* hold about some domain. For moral standing: conception, implantation, viability, birth, naming-day, the Spartan hillside, and forms we have no current word for. This space has a property worth naming: most of it is unoccupied, and the unoccupied part is largely invisible. You see the kernel a population holds. You do not see the kernels it could have held and didn't — those register only as absence, and only when something prompts you to look for them.

A first candidate formalism, offered and immediately qualified: this resembles the **Dirac sea** — a filled background of states, most unobservable, where what you detect is not the sea but the *hole* in it, the occupied or vacated state that shows against the background. The committed kernel is the occupied state. The unchosen alternatives are the sea: present, structuring, unseen except as absence. This maps cleanly onto one of the framework's existing moves — the question "if everyone agrees this is fine, who is not in the conversation?" is the question of which states in the sea have no occupant to speak for them. The absent are the unchosen made briefly visible.

But "a population commits to a kernel out of a space" is underspecified in a way that turns out to matter, because **a population is not one thing.** It is a nested stack: a civilizational band (the loose "WEIRD" envelope), within it nation-states, within those cultural communities, local communities, and finally individuals. A kernel is held *at a level*. And the live phenomena are vertical: a commitment held by a handful can climb the stack to become the frame everyone takes for granted, or a commitment that was the frame can drop back down to a contested fringe. The committer dimension is therefore indexed not by "the population" but by *(kernel × level)*, and its most interesting dynamics are movements along the level axis.

What an analysis can actually access at any moment is not the whole space but a **window** over it: the kernels currently reachable — thinkable, committable, live somewhere in the stack. The Spartan hillside is no longer in the reachable window for a modern WEIRD population; it has fallen out, not refuted but unreachable, you cannot easily get there from the current committed state. Gay marriage entered the reachable window over a few decades, climbing from a fringe commitment to, in many places, the frame. The window is what is currently committable. It is a strict subset of the full possibility space, and — this is the core claim — **it drifts.**

## 3. The window drifts by use, and decays by disuse

What governs the drift? An early temptation was to model the window as *growing* — new possibilities continually becoming reachable, in the manner of Kauffman's "adjacent possible" in biology, where each realized configuration opens new adjacent ones. This is wrong, and wrong in an instructive way: it is monotonic. The biological adjacent-possible only expands. Possibility spaces for commitments *contract* as readily as they expand. Kernels that were once reachable become unthinkable and drop out. Are we not men? We are also capable of de-evolution — the loss of a reachable state is as real as the gain of one.

So the window does not grow; it *translates* — picking up adjacent states on one edge while shedding them on the other, with no fixed net direction. This is, said without ornament, an **Overton window over kernels**: what is committable shifts, in both directions, over time.

The mechanism, conjectured: **reachability is maintained by use, and unexercised alternatives decay out of the window.** This is the evolutionary principle, not by analogy but possibly by the same logic — a capability held in the repertoire by being exercised, atrophying when it is not, with the environment shaping what is worth exercising. The invention of the fork is associated with the spread of the overbite: a bite configuration stopped being exercised, and the capacity for it lapsed. The committer version: a population commits to a kernel, the alternatives go unexercised, and they decay out of the reachable window — not by anyone deciding against them, simply by no one keeping them live.

This collapses what first looked like three competing drivers — environmental pressure (conditions change what is committable), endogenous closure (committing to one kernel forecloses others), and recombination (new kernels assembled from held ones) — into a single channel. Disuse is how all three operate. Environment changes what gets exercised; commitment changes what gets exercised; recombination is exercise opening new adjacency. In every case the unused alternative starves. The bidirectionality the de-evolution point demands falls out for free: the window gains states where use opens new adjacency and loses states where disuse lets old adjacency lapse.

This makes the window **operationalizable in principle**, which abstract "drift" never was. You do not need to survey the whole possibility space. You need only ask: *is this alternative still being exercised by anyone in the stack?* An alternative still practiced by a surviving subculture is still in the reachable window and could climb. An alternative practiced by no one is gone. Reachability reduces to extant practice somewhere in the population stack. And this is exactly why fringe survival matters and why the climb is possible at all: **the subculture that still exercises an alternative is the reservoir that keeps that kernel reachable for the whole stack.** When the last practitioner stops, the door closes.

## 4. Finite carrying capacity, and a second naturalization

If the window were unbounded, disuse-decay would be a curiosity — nothing would be forced out, things would merely accumulate. The claim acquires teeth only if carrying capacity is **finite**, and the conjecture is that it is, at several nested levels at once.

- **Per individual:** a cognitive bound. A person holds only so many live distinctions — a Dunbar-like ceiling on the active repertoire. At this level the bound is roughly fixed, and it is where personal path-naturalization happens.
- **Per language or culture:** *not* fixed, but bounded by users times domains. Total vocabulary grows over centuries — which looks like a refutation until you see the mechanism: it grows by *adding speakers and domains*, not by raising the per-person ceiling. "Butter churn" and "coppersmith" survive not in everyone's active lexicon but in *specialists'* — the reservoir structure of §3. The culture-level capacity is a coverage: how many domains still have at least one live reservoir. (Open sub-question, unresolved: whether capacity within a *layer* — common usage, say — is finite and crowds, while the union across specialist reservoirs escapes the crowd. The butter-churn case cuts both ways: the term survived in a reservoir, accretion-without-loss, but left common usage, suggesting the common layer sheds to make room.)
- **Per ecology:** consolidating. The total number of human languages is shrinking. The ecology cannot sustain unbounded distinct possibility-spaces; they consolidate, and high-reach languages crowd the rest.

The finite-capacity claim has a consequence that reorganizes the framework's relationship to its own central concept. If capacity is finite, **committing to a kernel does not merely fail to exercise the alternatives — it occupies capacity the alternatives would need to stay reachable.** Commitment is zero-sum against the reachable window. And this exposes a second conservation law, distinct from the one the DR engine already measures.

The engine's False Natural Law (FNL) machinery detects *beneficiary-naturalization*: a constraint presents as a mountain — natural, unauthored, just how things are — but has a beneficiary structure inconsistent with that presentation, so it is reclassified as naturalized. FNL extraction is zero-sum *in extraction*, at an instant: the beneficiary gains what the victim loses, and there is always someone holding the other end.

The committer dimension implies a second, structurally different naturalization. Call it **path-naturalization**: a kernel looks like a mountain not because someone is extracting behind a cover story, but because *its alternatives have lapsed out of the reachable window through disuse.* "Moral standing begins at birth" presents to a modern WEIRD observer as natural, obvious, the way things are — but the Spartan and naming-ceremony alternatives were once reachable and have simply fallen out of the window. History did the naturalizing. And here is the sharp version, which should not be softened: **path-naturalization has no beneficiary.** No one closed the Spartan option; it stopped being exercised and lapsed. The conservation law it violates is zero-sum *in possibility, over time*, not zero-sum in extraction at an instant — and a non-existent alternative has no holder across the table, because the table got smaller. This is precisely why a beneficiary-hunting engine is blind to it: the engine asks "who benefits," and the honest answer is "no one in particular — the alternative simply starved." The most thoroughly naturalized constraints may be the ones with no beneficiary at all, naturalized by sheer historical disuse, and these are the ones a beneficiary detector cannot see by construction.

The serf working the Church's land, in the framework's existing terms, is FNL-naturalized: there is a beneficiary, the Church, that the mountain-presentation conceals. But "personhood begins at birth" for a modern is *path*-naturalized: there may be no beneficiary of the boundary itself; the alternatives have just left reach. Two roads to a false mountain — one paved by a beneficiary, one by history — and the engine was built to detect only the first.

## 5. The motions of the window, and the case that breaks the formalism

If the committer dimension is a window drifting over a possibility space indexed by level, the dynamics decompose into distinct motions, and they are not the same phenomenon under one name. Contemporary examples make them concrete:

- **Climb** — a commitment moving up the stack, fringe to frame. Gay rights over decades. A hypothetical: a fringe philosophy becoming a civilization's operating frame the way Stoicism saturated late-republican Rome.
- **Drop** — a commitment moving down, frame to contested fringe. Abortion access in the United States, committed at the national level in 1972 and de-committed back to the state and cultural-community levels in 2022. Vertical motion, downward.
- **Same-level swap** — the committed kernel changing content without changing level. Conscription to volunteer military: a national-level commitment swapped for a different national-level commitment, no climb or drop.
- **Nascent** — a new commitment forming at the bottom of the stack, not yet in motion, observable before we know whether it climbs. Subcultures incorporating AI into religious practice are a current instance: a kernel being born, its trajectory undetermined.
- **Site expansion** — and this is the one that strains the whole apparatus. "Rights," far enough back, did not exist as a kernel to be committed to or rejected. It was not in the possibility space at all; the space *acquired a new state.* This is not a window translating over a fixed space, nor a section shifting on a fixed site. It is the *space itself* changing — gaining points that were not there before.

Site expansion is worth dwelling on because it marks the boundary of the formalism. A *presheaf* — the mathematical object the DR engine uses for observer-disagreement — is defined over a fixed site. If the site itself gains points over time, you do not have a presheaf with shifting sections; you have a site that is a function of time, a harder object — a moving base, a topos that is itself evolving. The Dirac-sea picture also breaks here: Dirac's sea is a fixed filled background, and "rights emerging from nothing" is a *growing* vacuum, which Dirac does not model. Other physics does — vacuum phase transitions, where the potential reshapes and new minima (new available ground states) appear; false-vacuum decay, where a new lower-energy vacuum nucleates locally and *expands as a bubble* through the old one, which is an almost too-apt picture of a fringe commitment nucleating and converting the surrounding population as it climbs. These are different models with different empirical commitments (is kernel-emergence driven by changing conditions, by competitive displacement of an incumbent, or by recombination of existing kernels?), and the honest position is that we cannot tell which from the armchair, and that the use-decay principle of §3 may subsume all of them if emergence is just the opening edge of the window's bidirectional drift. The point worth keeping: *if* a meaningful fraction of committer-dimension content is site-expansion type, the sheaf formalism is the wrong tool for that fraction, and we would learn that the moment we tried to count it.

## 6. Culture is commitment, and depends on transmission

The frame that holds all of this: **culture is commitments, and culture depends on transmission.** This is not a gloss. It is the definition that makes the committer dimension a theory of culture rather than a theory of constraints. A commitment that is not transmitted is not held; a commitment held by no living practitioner is a dead kernel; culture is the *currently-transmitted, currently-exercised set of commitments* — which is exactly the live reachable window of §3. Transmission *is* the exercise that holds capacity against decay. Stop transmitting, and the kernel decays out of the window regardless of whether its symbol survives somewhere in an archive.

This last clause is the hinge, and it requires a distinction the rest of the document has been building toward. There are two things that can decay, at very different rates:

- The **symbol** — the word, the written rule, the codified form. Slow to decay; archive-protectable.
- The **commitment** — the lived practice, the tacit knowledge, the felt content of the kernel as something done rather than referenced. Fast to decay; maintained only by live exercise.

A teacher of Bengali once asked: when did famine last happen in your family? For many of us the answer is *not in living memory.* We know the word "famine." We understand the concept. We have never lived it and know no one who has. The word is fully reachable — systematized, defined, deployable. The *commitment* — the bodily knowledge of hunger, the social arrangements famine forces, what people do and become under it — is gone, decayed through disuse the moment famine stopped happening to us. **Systematization preserved the word and lost the commitment.** The dictionary entry is a husk of a kernel whose living content has lapsed. And the quiet danger is that holding the husk *feels like* holding the kernel: you know the word, so you feel you have access, but the access is to the form, not the practice.

This corrects an overclaim worth flagging. It is tempting to say systematization is the technology that lets a kernel sleep and reactivate — the dormancy-cheating backstop. It is, but only *partially*, and only for the symbolic fraction of the kernel. **Dormancy-survivability tracks the symbolic fraction of the commitment.** High-symbolic kernels — legal codes, mathematical results, written doctrine — sleep and reactivate, because most of their content lives in the symbol. Justinian's code sat dormant and reachable for centuries, available to be reactivated in the medieval revival; this is the framework's existing "dormant-container activation," now given a mechanism. High-tacit kernels — famine-living, wartime practice, craft knowledge, the felt interior of a religious observance as opposed to its written creed — *die* when they go dormant, leaving only a husk-symbol. This predicts which revivals restore the kernel and which restore only its name: a tradition reactivating a lapsed tacit practice from its texts gets the husk and must re-improvise the living content, which is why revivals so often feel like reconstructions rather than continuations.

## 7. The carrying-capacity theorem of catastrophe

Two of the document's threads — disuse-decay and finite capacity — meet at a conclusion that should be stated plainly because it is the bleak heart of the theory and the place where softening it would be dishonest.

Consider a hard-won, intensely tacit commitment: the knowledge of how to live under catastrophe. Wartime practices — threat-reading, rationing instinct, solidarity under siege, the specific competence of living when the power grid is a target. Famine practices. The lived content of mass atrocity, which the survivors carry and the word "Holocaust" or "Holodomor" only points at. This knowledge is overwhelmingly tacit, and tacit content decays through disuse, and *peace removes the conditions that exercise it.* So it goes dormant, and because it is tacit it does not survive dormancy, and it dies — fast, within a generation or two — leaving a systematized husk (a memorial, a word, a museum) that feels like retention and is not.

The conclusion: **a population structurally cannot retain hard-won tacit catastrophe-knowledge across generations of non-catastrophe, because retention requires exercise and exercise requires the catastrophe.** The "never again" that becomes "again" is not a moral failing of the descendants. It is a carrying-capacity theorem. The kernel that would prevent the next catastrophe cannot be transmitted, because transmitting a tacit commitment means keeping it live, and keeping it live means maintaining the conditions that make it necessary — which is the thing you are trying to avoid. Ukraine's wartime vocabulary and practices are intensely live now; the theory predicts they will go dormant and die in peace, exactly as a family's famine-knowledge died once famine stopped arriving. And the final turn of the screw: **the decay is adaptive.** You should not keep wartime commitments live in peacetime; capacity is finite and peace needs that capacity for other things. The forgetting is the system reallocating finite possibility-space to live concerns. The structure that dooms each generation to relearn catastrophe is the same structure that lets it live well between catastrophes. There is no free version.

## 8. Why this would change the framework's epistemic type

The DR framework, as built, is descriptive and atemporal. It is a meter for perspectival fracture: it tells you how a constraint is classified from each position at an instant, and it explicitly refuses to adjudicate or to predict — meter, not compass. This refusal is principled and correct for what the engine measures.

The committer dimension, *if* real, changes the type of claim available, and this may be its most important consequence. Path-naturalization is not a statement about a snapshot. To say "this looks like a mountain because its alternatives lapsed" is to make a claim about a *counterfactual trajectory* — it was once reachable; a different path would have kept it reachable; a specific move could reopen it. The moment you have a trajectory through possibility space with a drifting window, you can say which kernels are about to leave the window and which closed alternatives could be reactivated by what intervention. That is *prediction* — and prediction earned honestly, grounded in path-dependence rather than prophecy. The committer dimension would be the first part of the framework to license forward-looking claims, and it would do so without abandoning the meter's discipline, because the predictions are about reachability dynamics, not about what anyone *should* do.

This also dignifies a piece of machinery the DR paper currently treats as optional polish. The "temporal site extension" — tracking how constraints change type over time — is dormant in the engine, framed as a nice-to-have pending longitudinal data. The committer dimension reframes it as *the only instrument on which path-naturalization is visible at all.* Without trajectory data, the engine cannot distinguish a genuine mountain from a kernel whose alternatives history closed. The dormant temporal site is not a feature request; it is the missing instrument for the committer dimension's signature. (Whether anything occupies that signature is, of course, the open empirical question — §10.)

And the dimension is cross-scale in a way that grounds a claim the base framework asserts but cannot mechanize. The same window-drift operator, indexed at different levels of the population stack, reads as *psychology* at the individual level and *sociology* at the group level. Learned helplessness is path-naturalization at n=1: the alternatives — agency, exit — have lapsed out of the person's reachable window, and the situation presents to them as a mountain. Trauma held as an unrevisable kernel, which the base framework already names under brittle anchored-fixity, is path-naturalization at n=1. The Spartan option leaving the WEIRD window is the identical operator at civilizational scale. The framework's strongest cross-domain claim — that brittle fixity fails identically across personal, organizational, and relational systems — would get its mechanism here: it is identical across scales because it is literally the same window-drift, indexed at different levels.

## 9. The five questions as the committer interrogation protocol

There is an existing teaching artifact — five questions for looking at things twice: who benefits and who pays; what does this look like from the position you would least want to be in; is this doing coordination *and* transfer; if everyone agrees this is fine, who is not in the conversation; and if this rule disappeared tomorrow, would the world rearrange itself or stay the same. They were written as a practical discipline and they happen to be the committer dimension's interrogation protocol, which is probably not a coincidence — they were written by someone already thinking in this structure.

Mapped to the apparatus above: Questions 1 and 2 read the *occupied state* — who holds the committed kernel and who bears its cost, read from the worst seat. Question 4 reads the *sea* — who is not in the conversation is the question of which states in the possibility space have no occupant to speak for them, the unchosen and the absent made briefly visible. Question 5 — would it rearrange or stay the same — is the *contingency test*, and it is the one with a subtlety the committer dimension exposes. A kernel can be *locally* immovable (genuinely unreachable from the current state — you cannot get to the Spartan option from here) while being *historically* contingent (it was reachable once; a different trajectory would have kept it so). The snapshot version of Question 5 cannot tell these apart. It sees "this won't rearrange if pushed" and reads mountain — when the real reason it won't rearrange may be that the alternatives have left the reachable window, not that it is natural. The five questions, run across time rather than at an instant — which the artifact itself recommends, comparing your reading now to your reading before — are how you catch path-naturalization by hand, without an engine.

## 10. What this rests on, what tested it, and what would test it next

The honesty this document owes its reader is a clear account of its evidential standing.

**What tested it.** The committer dimension makes a checkable prediction about the DR engine: if the engine encounters committer-dimension content it structurally cannot resolve (questions about which kernel was committed to, about population level, about constraint-space contingency, about beneficiary-less naturalization), that content should accumulate in the omega variables — the engine's catch-all for unresolved analytical residue. An omega taxonomy was run against the corpus, classifying omegas into observer-sheaf residue, ordinary empirical questions, and four committer-candidate categories (kernel-alternatives, population-nesting, constraint-space contingency, path-naturalization). The result, against a classifiable base of roughly 319 omegas: the corpus is overwhelmingly observer-sheaf and empirical residue. The four committer categories together drew four omegas, all from files explicitly about AI alignment governance — a domain where "kernel" is native object-level vocabulary, the textbook tautology trap — and all flagged as such. Population-nesting, constraint-space contingency, and path-naturalization drew **zero** omegas each. The path-naturalization category, which this document treats as one of its sharpest predictions, was empty: the one candidate had an identifiable beneficiary and was ordinary FNL.

So: the committer dimension has **no fingerprint in the constraint corpus.** This is the central honest fact about the theory and the reason it lives here rather than in the framework. The structure is internally coherent and possibly true as a theory of culture; it is, on the one test it has faced, corpus-absent.

**What this null does and does not mean.** It does not refute the theory. The DR constraint corpus may simply be the wrong place to look — it is built from constraints chosen for observer-axis analysis, by a generation process that may never have been prompted toward committer-axis content. A theory of cultural possibility-space being absent from a corpus of power-indexed constraints is roughly what you would expect even if the theory were true. But "you would expect a null even if it were true" is also exactly what a believer says about an unfalsifiable theory, and that danger should be named: a theory that predicts its own absence everywhere we can currently look is a theory that has not yet earned belief.

**What would test it next.** The single most promising unrun check is the State of the Union corpus — roughly 918 omegas from U.S. policy history (1945–present), a domain where "kernel" is *not* native vocabulary and where committer content, if it exists anywhere in the engine's reach, could appear without the tautology that contaminated the alignment files. Policy history is also the most plausible home for population-nesting content (commitments climbing and dropping levels) and for chosen-versus-unchosen-alternative content. It was sampled (three files) and not fully classified. The disciplined prior, after the first null, is that it returns another null — and it is worth running for the clean null as much as for the long-shot signal.

Beyond the corpus, two questions would have to be answered before the central formalism could be trusted even if the empirics turned favorable:

- **The morphism question.** This document has used "reachable from" and "adjacent" as if the possibility space had structure — as if it were a site with morphisms, the precondition for the sheaf and window language to be more than metaphor. That is unverified. It may be that the space of kernels is an unstructured set with no meaningful adjacency, in which case the Dirac-sea language survives (a filled background of states) but the sheaf and window language does not. The test is by hand: take a real kernel-family — the moral-standing kernels are a good candidate — and ask whether there is a defensible notion of one kernel being reachable-from or adjacent-to another, or whether the relation is empty. Until that is answered, this document should be read as using "window" and "sheaf" *aspirationally*, naming a structure it hopes the space has, not one it has shown.
- **The level of the capacity bound.** The finite-carrying-capacity claim (§4) is stated at three levels and is firmest at the individual (cognitive) level and the ecological (language-death) level; the middle, culture-level bound is the one most likely to fail, since vocabularies appear to accrete domains without obvious loss. Whether capacity is finite *per layer* (common usage crowds; the union across reservoirs escapes) or only at the individual and ecological extremes is unresolved and would discriminate between materially different versions of the theory.

## Coda

This is a seed, deliberately preserved with its failure attached. The ideas are, to the people who built them, the most interesting thing the larger investigation produced — and they are, on present evidence, unsupported in the one corpus that could have backed them. Both of those are true at once, and the discipline that holds them together is the whole point: an attractive structure is not evidence for itself, no matter how many of its parts agree with each other, and the way you find out whether a beautiful idea is also a true one is to keep looking for the place it leaves a mark. This document records where to dig, what was hoped, and what the first dig found. It is offered in that spirit — not as a theory of how commitment and possibility actually work, but as a precise account of a structure worth testing, so that the next person to find it beautiful finds the null beside it.
