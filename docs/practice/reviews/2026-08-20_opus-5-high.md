Read it cold, without v0.6, the concealment paper, or the audit directory. Here's what I found, roughly in order of severity.

## 1. The paper is not readable on its own, and its own doctrine says that's fatal

The status block declares this paper canonical for the practice while routing five load-bearing things elsewhere: the five-record table (v0.6 §3.4), the promotion test's full statement (§8.2), the eleven instances (§7.4), the operator's jurisdictions (§9.2), and the entire derivation of "framing" and "compression" (concealment v0.4). "Cited, never restated" is a defensible repo policy and an indefensible paper policy.

The sharpest version of this: **§III.3 requires that a claim carry its witness in the same turn the claim is made**, and argues that a claim in one substrate does not witness an artifact in another. This paper's central empirical claims are all made in one substrate and witnessed in another. §V.4 — the negative control, the move the paper is proudest of — rests entirely on the eleven instances at v0.6 §7.4, which are never described here. I cannot audit the control. By the paper's own rule I should downgrade it to OPEN.

Related and larger: **nowhere in 517 lines is there a description of the repository.** What research is this? What domain, what size, how many sessions, how many people, over what period, producing what? A cold reader's first three questions go unanswered, which makes every "here," "this record," and "the population" unanchored. "Pre-spend arc" (§IV.1) is the population boundary for the paper's main empirical claim and is undefined.

## 2. The negative control looks like it fails

§V.3 is the load-bearing falsification move: three classes the account correctly *excludes*, therefore the lens discriminates. But each excluded class has a corresponding practice inside §III:

- **Structural contradictions** are excluded — yet **Structure is axis two of §III.2**, with "two framings contradict inside one system" written in the framing column. The distinction you presumably want (axiom-level contradiction vs. invariant violation) is never drawn, so as written §V.3 contradicts §III.2.
- **Stochastic churn** is excluded — yet churn floors are a named practice in §III.3, and *k* redraws at byte-identical input is exactly "hold everything fixed, vary one dimension." It fits the frame perfectly. Excluding it demonstrates the opposite of what you want.
- **Loud destructive replacement** is excluded — yet "Prove before you replace" is a named practice in §III.3.

If the account both excludes a class and prescribes an instrument for it, the exclusion isn't discrimination. Right now the control reads as three cases where the frame was told not to fit rather than three where it didn't. This needs either a real repair or an admission that the control didn't run.

## 3. The evidence cannot carry the title

Title and framing are at genre altitude: *a development practice for research whose workers do not persist*. The evidence is one repository, one operator, self-observed, over roughly one arc in August 2026. The local hedges are scrupulous — "floor, not measurement," "undenominated," "never as a rate" — but they buy credibility that then gets spent on unhedged structural claims ("one conclusion is forced," "every instrument in this practice is the same move," "the taxonomy's fourth axis").

Worse, by §V.5's own admission the catch bit has never recorded a decline, and §III.3 says a population with no decline anywhere makes the question unanswerable. So: **the paper contains no affirmative evidence that the practice works.** Not weak evidence — none, by its own standard. The positive record is one paired draw with n=1 per arm (§II.2) and a count of the practice's own failures (§IV.1). That's a design rationale, not a validated practice, and the framing should say so.

## 4. §IV's arithmetic doesn't close

§IV.1: twelve total; ten "discovered by the red light"; five "committed inside instruments built to catch the earlier ones." §IV.3: ten "caught by a party comparing a claimed value against the artifact." Are those the same ten? "Red light" and "a human re-reading the substrate" are different mechanisms, and §IV.3's whole point is that gates didn't do it. Either way, **two of the twelve are never accounted for** — in a section about false absences. Overlaps between the ten and the five are unstated.

Also confusing: "the drift list reported twelve changed sources when two had changed" (§V.4) collides with the twelve of §IV.1. And "not one was caught by a gate reading its own output green" is muddled — a gate reading its own output green is the failure, not a catching mechanism.

## 5. Specific overclaims

- **"Worse than having no instrument"** (§II.3) rests on an untested psychological claim that a reader facing a green tick checks less than one facing nothing. If you believe it, the instrument should have been retired; you kept it. Either soften the claim or explain the inconsistency.
- **"One conclusion is forced"** (§I.2). The five properties admit other responses: redundancy and ensembling (cheap + recursively applicable licenses it), doing the work yourself, or attacking non-persistence directly with memory and retrieval. Non-persistence is treated as a fixed constraint; by mid-2026 that's contestable, and the paper never says why it's not pursuing it.
- **"Almost no literature at all"** (§I.1). This is the sentence a hostile reviewer opens with. There is a real body of work on context engineering, agent harnesses, evals, and spec-driven development. And the paper has **zero external citations** — in a paper whose central discipline is citation aptness. Positive controls, pre-registration, negative controls, and churn floors are borrowed from science and mutation testing; "borrowed structures" appears once, uncredited. §I.3's standing hazard box says *run the comparison, then decide*, and then no comparison is run.
- **"The three axes"** (§III.2) followed by §III.4 declaring a fourth. Beyond the title bug: the enumeration's completeness is never argued, and §V.2's unification claim depends on it being exhaustive over the record.
- **§IV.2 typed "ANALYTIC, evidenced empirically here."** §0 says analytic means it follows from the derivation. "Declaring a framing produces a new artifact with a new framing" is either a theorem, in which case the twelve are decoration, or a contingent regularity at n=12 undenominated. The paper wants both.

## 6. What's missing

- **Cost. Any cost.** §I.1 says most working hours go to building instruments; §V.1 names unbounded apparatus growth as the central hazard; the cap is invoked repeatedly and its number is never given. No token counts, no rule count, no hours, no ratio of instrument-building to object-level output. This is the paper's most conspicuous absence, because **the only visible product of the apparatus in this document is more apparatus.**
- **Retired rules.** The cap forces retirement. What got retired, and did anything break? That's a real empirical record you're sitting on, and it's the best available evidence that the promotion test discriminates.
- **Transferability.** Single operator, no succession, no redundancy, no second-reader check on the operator's judgment — and §IV.4 documents the operator applying an unstated criterion twice. Would a second person reproduce these calls? Unaddressed.
- **Worked examples.** The best moments are one-line compressions: `identical: True` on two empty sides, `partition_check: 186 == 185`, the detector that named itself, the manifest pinning sixteen texts and nothing executable, the canary aimed at a channel with 46× headroom. Each should be a page with the actual artifact shown. Your own doctrine demands the witness.
- **§II.4** is a stub — three types named, no instance, no instrument, no evidence.
- No abstract, no definitions section, no venue or audience signal.

## 7. Terms used before they're defined

**False absence** is the paper's central defect class and is never defined here. Also undefined or unresolvable cold: *read site*, *seat*, *success-shaped token*, *the instrument stratum*, *pre-spend arc*, *the standing gate*, *aptness*, *graduation step*, *catch bit* (defined late, in §V.1, after use), and the inline pin `CWC:A2@31548228`.

## 8. The style is arguing against the thesis

§I.2 says fluency is uncorrelated with reliability and that this breaks the review reflex. §0 acknowledges the risk. But the prose is relentlessly aphoristic — bolded closers every third paragraph, *the lens discriminates*, *a suspicion is not a run*, *the diff is proof* — and the effect is that the paper *reads* far more established than its evidence base supports. Compression this high also hides the gaps above: the three-exclusions problem in §V.3 survives because each exclusion is stated as a verdict rather than argued. A reader who slows down finds fewer arguments than the surface promised.

The honest fix is unglamorous: fewer aphorisms, more instances, one expanded case study, a cost table, and a paragraph saying what the repository is for.
