# One Seat, Audited

*The verification corollary to the Seat Theorem. Supersedes the "Two-Seat Theorem" draft — which was wrong in its name, its count, and its taxonomy. The supersession is the document: the corrections are what it has to teach.*

## What it replaces, and why

The draft claimed a contentful verification factors into two seats — **Sₚ** (provenance, declarable, governed by Corollary 2a) and **Sₒ** (orientation, undeclarable, governed by Corollary 3) — and called the result a *sibling theorem* to the Seat Theorem, on the strength of "self-opacity" as new seat-structure. Three corrections, each forced by a confrontation rather than chosen:

1. **Orientation is not a seat.** It is a holder's *relation* to a seat. Sₒ was miscast.
2. **Self-opacity is not new seat-structure** — it is the ordinary fact that the holder of a relation can lose access to it. The sibling claim was reification; this is a **corollary**, not a sibling.
3. **Provenance is not a seat either.** It is the *showing-face* — what gets audited, not what is audited against. Sₚ was also miscast.

Neither of the "two seats" was a seat. The one seat was the reference reality both were audited against — **correspondence** — which the draft never named, having folded it into Sₒ.

## The one seat

For a contentful verification there is exactly one seat: **the reference reality the verdict answers to.** Which reality that is depends on the question. For a *correspondence* question — "is this true / did this happen" — the seat is correspondence: what is actually the case. (Coupling: contentful ⇒ seated. This is the Seat Theorem's seat, in the verification domain; nothing new.)

## The discriminator — the one durable addition, and it is general

Seat and face are **not** told apart by "does varying it change a verdict" — that test passes for the face too (a face varies on its own inputs). They are told apart by **audit direction**:

> The **seat** is what is *audited against*. A **face** is what is *audited*. The audit is **one-directional**.

A signature (provenance) is audited against correspondence: a signed thing can still be false about the world. Correspondence is never legitimately audited against the signature — to rule a thing true *because* it is signed is not a reverse audit, it is the fraud (below). So in a correspondence question, correspondence is the seat and provenance is a face.

This discriminator is **not verification-specific** — it is the general seat/face test, confirmed across substrates (the CS engine audits grounding against the metric reality, one-directional; v7's committer axis reads the observer axis and feeds nothing back). Its cross-substrate statement is deferred to v8; here it is stated generally and worked in the verification domain.

## The two faces — already 2a and 3, not new

Orientation is the holder's relation to the one seat, in two faces, each already governed by the Seat Theorem:

- **Showing (external).** How the selection is presented — provenance offered *as if* it settled correspondence. Governed by **Corollary 2a** (declared-vs-concealed). The deepfake forges this face.
- **Keeping (internal / temporal).** Whether the selection is held unconfrontably over time. Governed by **Corollary 3** (honor-vs-reabsorb), and gameable by a patient holder who performs honoring while reabsorbing.

Neither face is a seat. Both are audited against the one seat.

## Fraud

The single pathology: **present a face as the seat** — provenance ("signed") read as correspondence ("true"). This is Corollary 2a's unique inconsistency — the no-seat pose — localized to verification. "Verified ✓" means *signed*; it is read as *true*; the gap between them is where the fraud lives, and the gap is the whole business model of selling a checkmark.

## The measure — verification-specific

The one genuinely domain-local quantity: **the cost of making the showing-face diverge from the seat** — the cost of forgery. "Facts decide" is the limit where that cost is high (the face cannot cheaply diverge from the seat); it is an **economic** condition, not an epistemic one. Adversarial generation lowers the cost and widens the region where face-as-seat is cheap — which is why the same correspondence question can be near-settled in one era and wide open in the next with the truth of the matter unchanged.

## The cap — standing open work, deferred

The seat may itself be **authored** rather than world-anchored: correspondence is often adjudicated against a reference that is someone's selection (a chain of custody, an institution's attestation, the engine's ε). The audit then runs face-against-authored-reality, not face-against-world. The architecture — one seat, one-directional audit — is sound regardless; the seat's **substrate-independence** is the open problem, handed to v8, not closed here.

## Falsifier

One-seat **falls** if a reverse audit appears — correspondence audited against provenance, truth ruled *by* the signature, as a legitimate computation rather than the fraud. It does not, in any substrate examined. That is the standing kill-condition for any successor.

## Taxonomy

**Corollary** of the Seat Theorem, not a sibling. It adds one *general* discriminator (audit-direction) and one *domain* measure (forgery-cost); it introduces no new seat-structure. The sibling claim rested on self-opacity, which dissolved into "a relation whose holder can lose access to it."

## Note on the revision history

This document's history is the discipline it describes. Two-seat was staked and lost (to a challenge); sibling was staked and lost (to the reification check); second-seat was staked and lost (to a probe); each fell to a confrontation rather than being reasoned away in advance. The corrected statement is held by the falsifier above, not asserted — which is the only status the Seat Theorem permits any contentful claim to have.
