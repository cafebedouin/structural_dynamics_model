# Design Omega Log

**A running ledger of the engine's *design-level* Omega variables — the blocking dependencies the
framework rests on — sorted by kind (Ω_E / Ω_C / Ω_P) with resolution status. This is the ledger;
`design_discipline.md` §6 is the prose that argues each entry. New design Omegas are appended here
as they surface; status is updated as they resolve (Ω_E/Ω_C) or as the declared cut is restated
(Ω_P).**

**Scope.** This logs *design / engine-architecture* Omegas, not per-constraint perspectival gaps.
Per-constraint Omegas are detected and reported by the pipeline in `outputs/omega_report.md`
(auto-generated by `python/omega_reporter.py`) — a different, machine-produced artifact. Do not
file design entries there (it is overwritten each run). The Ω_E/Ω_C/Ω_P taxonomy is defined in
`docs/omega_variables.md`.

**The kind matters.** An Ω_E or Ω_C entry is **closeable** — measurement (Ω_E) or a definitional /
framework choice (Ω_C) resolves it. An Ω_P entry is **constitutively open** — undecidable from
inside the engine; it resolves only to a *declared, chosen cut*, taken by whoever bears the
frame's consequences, not by more analysis. Logging an Ω_P as if it were closeable work (e.g. as a
plain ISSUES.md OQ) is the conflation error `design_discipline.md` §6 names: it falsely promises
the question will close with effort. The "Resolution" field for an Ω_P names the seat, not a task.

## Ω_P — Constitutively open (a chosen cut, not a pending answer)

### ΩD-P1 — Is the coupling-score the right operationalization of natural-vs-constructed?
- **Statement:** The Boltzmann coupling-score gives a robust, wide-margin partition (the engine is
  internally consistent); whether the score *measures the right thing* is undecidable from inside.
- **Kind:** Ω_P. **Status:** open by construction — no sweep closes it.
- **Resolution (the seat, not a task):** a human taking a declared seat against the
  operationalization — the stream essay's "is the witness real or is it the fold?" question.
- **Cross-refs:** `design_discipline.md` §6; ISSUES.md OQ-30 (FNL coupling lock), OQ-49 (FNL
  override prevalence — internal-consistency evidence, not validity evidence).

### ΩD-P2 — Is the engine's selector (Q1, who-benefits-who-pays) the right master axis?
- **Statement:** Axiom S3 put to itself. No analytic ranking of rival selectors is available from
  above; decision authority sits with whoever bears the frame's consequences — which is itself the
  beneficiary problem the selector names.
- **Kind:** Ω_P. **Status:** open by construction; the selector stays declared-and-defended, able
  to lose a round.
- **Resolution (the seat):** consequence-bearers, not the analyst.
- **Cross-refs:** `design_discipline.md` §4 (what the engine is for), §6; Axiom S3.

### ΩD-P3 — Is the truly symmetric same-power conflict a real configuration or a thought experiment?
- **Statement:** Two parties of equal enforcement capacity, opposing stakes, neither dropping seat.
  Appears to be a *non-configuration* — it tends to resolve into asymmetry (→ relational
  re-seating) or framing difference (→ committer axis).
- **Kind:** Ω_P. **Status:** flagged, not resolved — whether it is irreducible is open.
- **Resolution (the seat):** would require exhibiting an irreducible instance that does not
  collapse to asymmetry or to the committer axis.
- **Cross-refs:** `design_discipline.md` §4, §6.
