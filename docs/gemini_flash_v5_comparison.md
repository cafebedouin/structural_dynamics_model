# Gemini Flash 2.0 Corpus Analysis — Comparison with Haiku 4.5

## 1. Corpus Overview

| Property | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| Constraints generated | 960 | 887 |
| Pipeline steps | 36/36 | 36/36 |
| Pipeline time | 103s | 61s |
| Parse failures | 30 beta seeds | 0 (1 null chi) |
| Perspectives per constraint | 4 (uniform) | **2–6 (variable)** |
| Agent power values used | 4 (standard grid) | **6** (adds organized, powerful) |

**First structural difference:** Gemini Flash generates variable perspective counts (mode=5, range 2–6) and uses additional agent_power values beyond the standard 4-context grid. Haiku locked to exactly 4 perspectives per constraint. The pipeline still computes chi for the standard 4 contexts regardless, so sheaf cohomology operates on the same 4-node graph — but the richer perspective structure feeds into the narrative classifications.

## 4. Unstable Findings (Corpus-Dependent)

### 4a. Descent Rate

| Metric | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| H¹=0 (descent) | 656 (68.3%) | **180 (20.3%)** |
| H¹=3 | — | 353 (39.8%) |
| H¹=5 | — | 320 (36.1%) |
| H¹=6 | 70 (7.3%) | 19 (2.1%) |

**Radical difference.** Haiku's corpus had 68% gauge-invariant constraints; Gemini Flash has only 20%. 

**Mechanism:** Gemini Flash generates higher epsilon values for tangled_rope (mean 0.56 vs Haiku's distribution). Higher epsilon pushes constraints into the snare zone at the analytical context while the false_ci_rope override holds them as tangled_rope deterministically. This creates per-context type disagreement → H¹>0. The spectral audit confirms: 448 constraints are snare at the analytical context but only 109 are claimed snare — the 339-constraint gap is exactly the override population with H¹>0.

**Paper implication:** The descent rate is NOT a universal property of constraint classification. It is sensitive to the epsilon distribution generated by the LLM. The *existence* of a nonzero descent rate is robust; the *magnitude* is corpus-dependent.

### 4b. Snares in H⁰

| Metric | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Snares in H⁰ | **0** | **1** |

The sole exception is `ad_synaptic_deficit`: a false_natural_law snare with ε=0.75. Its raw MaxEnt gives 99.9999% snare; the override gives 95% tangled_rope. It has H¹=0 because the 4-context cohomology sees agreement, but its claimed type is snare. It's a borderline case (confidence=0.01, rival=tangled_rope at 0.95) — the classification confidence report flags it as the only borderline snare.

**Paper implication:** Qualify from "zero snares in H⁰" to "snares in H⁰ are vanishingly rare (0–1 across corpora), consistent with the theoretical prediction." The one exception is a borderline false_natural_law edge case.

### 4c. MaxEnt/Orbit Overlap

| Metric | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Hard disagreements | 183 | **38** |
| In multi-type orbit | 166 (90.7%) | 21 (55.3%) |
| Single-type orbit disagreements | 17 | 17 |

The number of hard disagreements dropped 5× while the single-orbit count stayed exactly the same (17). The overlap percentage dropped because the denominator shrank: Gemini Flash's higher epsilon concentration means more constraints cluster deeply within type boundaries, reducing MaxEnt disagreements. The 17 single-orbit disagreements are near-boundary constraints — their count is stable.

### 4e. Override Impact

| Metric | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Chi overrides | 473 (12.3% of evaluations) | **0** |
| Band changed by override | 54.8% | **3.5%** |
| Top type changed by override | 45.6% | **2.9%** |

**Dramatic difference.** Haiku's corpus had 473 chi violations; Gemini Flash has zero. This confirms the chi violations were a Haiku-specific artifact — Haiku was copying the exemplar's hardcoded chi arithmetic into the Prolog, creating formula violations. Gemini Flash, generating from the same exemplar, did NOT copy the chi arithmetic.

The minimal override impact (3.5%) means Gemini Flash's constraint parameters naturally fall where the overrides expect. The false_ci_rope conditional override (3× boost to tangled_rope) is active for 689 constraints but only changes 26 type assignments — the constraints are already where the override would put them.

| Metric | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |

| Corrected T13 fires (analytical) | 8 (2.6% of H¹>0) | **12 (1.7% of H¹>0)** |

| Corrected all-context fires | — | 572 (81.0%) |

The oracle gap is larger with Gemini Flash: 1.7% vs 2.6% at analytical context. The 12 firing constraints are all false_ci_rope (same mechanism). The d_FR separation between Group A (genuine fires, mean 0.450) and Group B (audit artifacts, mean 0.005) is 94×, confirming the corrected T13 is far more selective.

### 4g. Boundary Population Structure

| Boundary | Haiku 4.5 | Gemini Flash 2.0 |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| tangled_rope→snare N | 438 | **500** |
| Best fit | gaussian_mixture_2 | **beta** |
| Shape | 85/15 bimodal | U-shaped (Beta(0.16, 0.16)) |
| Coalition × snare χ² | 37.23 | **373.25** |
| snare→tangled_rope N | 234 | 86 |

The boundary fit changed from Gaussian mixture to beta distribution, but both are non-normal and both show the coalition structure signature (χ² highly significant in both). The beta fit is actually more informative: Beta(0.16, 0.16) is a U-shaped distribution with mass at both extremes — constraints are either firmly tangled_rope or firmly snare, with few in between. This is the same bimodal structure the Gaussian mixture captured, just fitted better.

The coalition × snare cross-tab χ² is an order of magnitude larger (373 vs 37), driven by the cleaner split_field vs institutional_dissent separation in Gemini Flash's data.

## 9. Paper Implications

1. **Descent rate claim needs revision:** Cannot cite 68.3% as the descent rate. Frame as "varies by corpus (20–68%), confirming that some but not all constraints are observer-independent."

2. **Oracle gap reframing validated:** The per-context table approach (already adopted in the transition document) is correct — the single multiplier (38× or 59×) is unstable.

3. **Sheaf Laplacian is the strongest finding:** Identical to 4 decimal places across two different LLM generators. This is the paper's most defensible quantitative claim.

4. **Snares in H⁰:** Qualify to "vanishingly rare (0–1 per corpus)" rather than absolute zero.

5. **v1.2 exemplar is necessary but may not be sufficient:** Gemini Flash anchoring at 74.6% is worse than Haiku's 43%, despite both seeing the same exemplar. Gemini Flash may need explicit instructions to vary d-values, or multiple exemplars with different d-patterns.

6. **Signature distribution is generation-dependent:** The emerges_naturally boolean is the key driver — different models default differently, flipping the signature distribution entirely. The pipeline needs to normalize this upstream or the paper should not report signature percentages.

## 10. Open Question: Why Is the Laplacian Identical?

The Laplacian eigenvalues are identical because they depend ONLY on the restriction map ratios r₁₂, r₂₃, r₃₄, which are computed from the *mean chi values per context* across the corpus. With 74.6% of constraints sharing one d-pattern, the mean chi per context is dominated by that pattern, producing the same ratios as Haiku's 43% concentration. As long as the exemplar's d-pattern dominates, the Laplacian will be identical. This is a feature (framework geometry is stable) but also means the Laplacian can't distinguish between corpora — it measures the *context graph structure*, not the constraint population.
