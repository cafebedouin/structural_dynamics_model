# Bangladesh 2026: Interim Scoring of the Lock-In Predictions

*Interim scoring (2026-08-18) of "How Bangladesh's Interim Government Is Locking In
Constitutional Change Before Democracy Can Deliberate It" (published 2026-02-09,
three days before the vote). A commitment-systems prediction registered before the
CS sketch paper existed: the post's subject is pre-emptive constitutionalization —
binding future democratic choice before it can be exercised. Two of four claims are
resolvable now; two carry 2027–28 resolution dates and stay open.*

## Resolved

**P1 — "BNP wins a clear plurality of votes and the largest bloc of seats"; kill
condition: BNP <40% of seats.** CONFIRMED, exceeded. Unofficial Election Commission
figures: BNP ~209 of 297 seats (~70%) — not a plurality but a two-thirds
supermajority; Tarique Rahman sworn in as prime minister February 17. Note the
overshoot direction: the prediction under-priced the very consolidation dynamic it
named (the H1 signature from the Colombia scoring, third occurrence). The overshoot
also *sharpens* the post's core concern rather than softening it: a two-thirds bloc
can amend the constitution at will, so the "anti-unilateral change" provisions face
exactly the unilateral power they were written to constrain.

**P2 — "The July Charter passes on a national yes/no vote" with a significant
margin; kill conditions: <55% yes nationwide, or narrow passage (<60%).**
CONFIRMED — by 0.26 points. Early reports ranged 62.74–68% yes, but the Election
Commission's revised gazette (February 26) put the corrected figure at **60.26%** —
clearing the <55% kill line by 5.3 points and the pre-registered narrowness line by
a quarter of a point. Scored as confirmed at the edge of its own band, and worth
recording as a calibration datum: the pre-registered "narrow" threshold turned out
to sit almost exactly on the real number. The sub-claim about the geographic
concentration of No votes (Awami-aligned areas, urban professionals) is
UNRESOLVABLE from sources checked here.

## Open (resolution dates attached)

**P3 — Within 18–24 months of the new parliament seating, at least one
"anti-unilateral change" provision is visibly hollowed out** — via judicial
exception-carving, legislative workarounds, or parallel institutions — with the
erosion looking "technical, procedural, and boring." Resolution window:
**~2027-08 to 2028-02.** This is the post's central commitment-systems claim: that
entrenchment imposed *before* deliberation converts into drift *after* it.

**P4 — Extortion/informal extraction continues regardless of winner and is
incorporated into the new ruling coalition's territorial control**; kill condition:
measurable decline in extortion cases within 12 months. Resolution: **~2027-02.**

## Scoring notes

The post predates the Forecast Register format but scores cleanly because it did
the two things v2.2 later required: kill conditions with numbers, and claims split
by horizon. Its two unresolved claims are the theory-bearing ones — the resolved
pair established the setup (dominant bloc + passed charter) that makes P3's
lock-in-then-hollowing mechanism testable at all. Diarize P4 for February 2027 and
P3 for the 2027-08–2028-02 window.

**Fired: latent** — both resolved claims confirmed (one at the edge of its own
band); the discriminating claims are open by design, on the world's schedule.
