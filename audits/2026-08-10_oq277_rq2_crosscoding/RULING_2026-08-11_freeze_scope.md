## 1. The ruling

`HANDOFF_TWINS_AND_DRIVER.md` scheduled four remaining tasks (twins, packets, driver, freeze) and
named neither **direction (iii′)** nor the **n=8 escape check**, both of which are in the frozen
design (`ISSUES.md` OQ-277 → *Design* → *Three comparisons* and *Escape check*). Neither had units,
controls, or a line in any state table. The scope question was put to the operator rather than
resolved by the cheaper path.

**Ruled: OPTION 1 — include both before freeze.** One freeze, one spend, a total interpretation
table. The operator's reasoning, recorded because the deciding argument was *not* the one this
instance offered:

> "Freezing a prereg whose interpretation table is pre-registered as *total* while two designed
> rows are silently absent is the experiment enacting its own subject. But that's the argument
> against option 2, and it isn't the deciding one — option 3 or 4 would answer it too, and
> cheaper."

The three deciding arguments, as ruled:

1. **The escape check is the row with the highest external value in the design.** It is the only
   instrument in this arc bearing directly on §4.5's headline, and §4.5 is the number the paper's
   discipline is justified by. Everything else — both matrices, the floor, the residues — refines a
   comparison *between two taxonomies*. The escape check refines the figure the paper *publishes*.
   An arc that found two denominator defects and a positional-parse defect in that statistic must
   not ship without measuring its recall bound.
2. **It is the cheapest row remaining.** Eight directories, in a unit format already produced 26
   times; the marginal cost is extraction volume, now a known quantity, and a handful of coding calls.
3. **(iii′) cannot be split from the escape check**, because both are calibration rows for the same
   primary reads. Deferring (iii′) would measure the inter-rater row against coder behaviour *in a
   different run* — and the extension rule's own logic (a different protocol is not changes-n-only,
   §E) makes that a separate experiment rather than a deferred row of this one. Option 4 has the
   mirror problem. **Splitting calibration from what it calibrates is the expensive kind of cheap.**

### 1.1 Conditions attached to the ruling (binding)

- **C1 — escape-extractor independence.** The escape units are extracted under the same conventions
  as the primary units, **by an extractor who has not seen the primary sample's results.**
- **C2 — the operator confirmation gate stands unchanged.** The coder *proposes* an escape hit; the
  operator *rules* before the row fires. Unchanged from the frozen design.
- **C3 — say in the prereg that the quarantine mechanism has never been exercised.** §H.2's
  quarantine has k=0 to date (§M). If an escape hit arrives it will be **the first live test of a
  control we do not know works**, and the prereg must state that rather than presenting the
  quarantine as a working safeguard.
- **C4 — the coder prompts are prereg content under the same freeze.** Drafted now, reviewed at
  freeze, md5'd with everything else. Operator's framing: the prompt is the single highest-leverage
  authored artifact in the run and *currently the only part of the design with no witness at all* —
  everything else has a frozen file, an md5, or a control. To be noted in §J as the shape the arc
  has now found five times: **an authored text sitting outside the artifact discipline.**
- **C5 — two prompt properties, specified by the operator, not inferred:**
  - **`other` is presented as a first-class outcome, never a residual.** A coder reading `other` as
    a failure to classify will under-report it, and *both* the both-residue row and the escape check
    depend on `other` being reachable. The decoys test whether the coder **can** return it; the
    prompt wording determines whether it **will**. (These are two different controls on one
    property — the decoys cannot catch a prompt-induced reluctance, because a reluctant coder still
    returns `other` on a decoy that is obviously neither taxonomy.)
  - **No confidence, no rationale, no hedging alongside the label.** k=3 unanimity *is* the churn
    instrument; a coder invited to qualify its answer produces a second, uncontrolled signal an
    adjudicator will be tempted to read. **One label, from a fixed vocabulary, nothing else.**

**Therefore the escape extraction is handed to a fresh extractor instance**, exactly as the primary

### 2.2 Consequences, and the one point that is the operator's

1. **n≈15 → n=10.** The (iii′) inter-rater row's resolution changes: at n=10 one unit is 10%, and
   with 10 units unanimity/agreement is reportable but per-pattern agreement is not — the row stays
   what the design already called it, *calibration data, not a verdict*. The prereg carries n=10
   with its MDE, never "~15".
2. **P3 has ZERO exemplar coverage in either source.** The inter-rater row therefore **cannot speak
   to P3 at all**, and no amount of n buys it — the population is empty, not thin. This is the same
   shape as §O's declared calibration residue (no P6 anchor in direction (i), no multi-membership
   anchor in direction (ii)) and is declared the same way: **any P3 result from (iii′) is
   UNCALIBRATED, and the row's coverage is P1/P2/P4/P5/P6 only.** Recorded before the run, so it is
   a known limit rather than a discovered one.
3. **Three of the ten are already direction-(i) anchors** (#3, #4, #7). This is the declared-overlap
   situation §1.5 of the handoff already ruled on for the three Wu units serving in both directions:
   intended and clean — different runs, a stateless coder — **provided it is declared and the
   double-membership is never used twice in one accounting.** The (iii′) row reports with and
   without the three anchor members, since including them measures partly the same calls the H3
   anchor-recovery check already consumed.
4. **OPEN FOR THE OPERATOR — the boundary itself.** Whether to accept **n=10** (CLAUDE.md's
   six-pattern block, the boundary `anchors.json` already uses) or to widen the population to
   recover n≈15 by admitting the exemplars attached to the *other* Build Discipline rules
   (false-absence, the relocating confound, consistency-vs-discrimination, and the rest). Widening
   is not free: those incidents are named as instances of *different* rules, so their P1–P6 label
   would have to be **assigned rather than read off** — which is coding, and the extractor may not
   do it. **Recommendation: accept n=10 and state the boundary in the prereg.** Not decided here.
