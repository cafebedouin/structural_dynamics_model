## ITEM-S

The shared commitment all parties argue about is: 'what makes a debate round legitimate/fair, i.e., what grounds the authority of the ballot to decide a winner.' Every faction in this file — T/Framework advocates, game-theory critics, organizing-pedagogy advocates, and anti-topicality dissenters — agrees that *some* answer to 'what legitimates the outcome of this round' must exist; they read that commitment radically differently.
