## What was measured

The graduation step OQ-242 pre-specified: re-run `run_trajectory_report` under option (b)
and diff the family partition. Option (b): purity contributes to `stability_distance/3`

purity component is dropped and the remaining three weights renormalize

## Limitation the ruling accepts (recorded, not fixed)

After renormalization, 3-component and 4-component distances are compared against the same
fixed 0.15 twin threshold. Renormalizing makes them nominally commensurable, but a distance
over three terms is not distributed like one over four (fewer terms, different variance), so
rows in the unscored fifth are thresholded on a slightly different basis. Today this costs
one twin pair in 2828. The future-proofing argument for (b): as the unscored stratum grows,
option (a)'s fabrication gets monotonically worse, while (b) degrades into this
well-understood arity limitation.
