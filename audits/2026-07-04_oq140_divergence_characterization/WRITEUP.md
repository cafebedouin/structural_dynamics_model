## Headline

The routing sink's majority address (`author_engine_divergence`, **277/512 records on 96/128
constraints**) is **not one population and is mostly not kind-shaped.** Cutting the mechanical
confound *before* decomposing re-ranks the whole picture and yields exactly one validated,
twin-replicated kind over the confound-free ~20%.

1. **The confound is near-total (committed quantity).** ε is uniform across seats in **96/96**
   divergence constraints, so **100% of per-seat engine-orbit variation is driven by per-seat
   `d`** (institutional has the lowest `d` in 92/96, diverges on 93/96). Partition: **confound-
   free (G-A, uniform orbit) = 56/277 (20.2%); confound-exposed (G-B+G-C) = 221/277 (79.8%)**.
   The 221 are *granularity-expected* — a seat-blind claim necessarily diverges from a seat-
   varying engine wherever `d` crosses a boundary — not a content disagreement.

2. **The confound re-ranks the kinds (the substantive answer).** Pre-confound, the dominant
   stratum was `tangled_rope→snare` (111) — the OQ's implicit lead. Post-confound it is fully
   confound-exposed and does not appear in G-A at all. The surviving confound-free lead is
   `rope→scaffold` (36 records / 9 constraints), which the OQ treated as secondary.

3. **Three axes the OQ proposed as cuts are degenerate** (each one value over all 277):
   `provenance.mismatch` 273 `none` / 4 severe; `author_mode` 100% `seat_blind_claim`;
   `detector` 100% `nl_absent`. Only granularity class and type-pair carry live structure.
