# STAGE 2: NATURALIZATION

## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The floor invariant (primary, per Stage-0 contract):**
The world contains a founding partition — a first sorting act, prior to any individual case — that divides persons into those whose lives are ends in themselves and those who are legitimate means, disposable when the ledger requires it. No instrument in this world measures *whether* someone belongs in the disposable class as a factual question; the instrument only certifies which side of an already-drawn line a person falls on. There is no neutral ground beneath this line — no view from nowhere from which the partition itself could be judged true or false.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story frames the harm done to the disposable class as a mistake — a misclassification a fairer tribunal could correct — the floor invariant has been lost. The line itself, not any error in reading it, must be the wrongness.

**Inherent instrument (Stage-0 flag: yes):** The partition's power is not brute force; it is *certified*. A formal apparatus exists whose entire social function is to make the disposal look like the output of a legitimate, auditable procedure rather than a decision. This licenses one exception to the default ban on scored/instrumented Snares — but only under the strict conditions in Step 1 below (numbers appear only diegetically, only when acted upon, never as narration's own currency).

**Substrate (where the floor lives in this world):** The **Ledger of Standing** — a physical, communal register kept by the Assay Hall of the river-port city of Verdai. It is not a metaphor for bureaucracy; it is an actual bound instrument, consulted daily, that converts a person's economic and legal relations into a single certified figure: their *Assay*. The Assay is computed honestly, by real clerks, following real rules, from real inputs. **The instrument is not lying.** The floor invariant is not that the Assay is miscalibrated — it is that *the decision to assay persons at all, and to divide them thereby into Held and Sundered, was made once, long ago, by no one answerable, and the Hall's honesty is precisely what makes the partition impossible to see as a choice.* You cannot audit your way to a world without an Assay Hall. The Hall can only tell you, correctly, which side of the line you are on.

**Substrate inhabitation sentence:** *You stand in the nave of the Assay Hall and watch the clerk weigh your bond-tokens on a scale that has never once returned a wrong number, and you understand for the first time that the scale was never the problem.*

**Break contract (address, not execution):** The naturalization must leave open — not force — the violation of the belief that the Assay Hall and its appeals process are the only legitimate arena in which a Sundered person's grievance against the Hall can be pursued. The world's substrate (below) is built so that an extra-Hall reckoning remains structurally possible, without staging it here.

## SECTION 1: CONTEXT DESCRIPTION

### Setting

Verdai, a free river-port city-state at the delta of two trade rivers, in a world of black-powder muskets, treadle looms, and the first mechanical calculators — technologically analogous to the late 1600s but with no real-world counterpart culture; its founding families trace to a fusion of steppe pastoralist clans and delta rice-canal settlers who merged four generations ago under a single charter. Verdai's wealth is built on bond-credit: nearly everyone owes labor-years, grain-futures, or canal-toll debt to someone, and the entire economy runs on the *assumption that debts can be tallied fairly*. To make that assumption operable, the city's founding charter established the **Assay Hall**, a guild-cathedral of clerks, actuaries, and notaries whose sworn function is to compute, for any resident, their **Assay**: a single certified number synthesizing debt load, guild standing, kin-bond obligations, and criminal record, recorded in the **Ledger of Standing**.

Above a published threshold, a person is **Held** — protected by charter, eligible for guild arbitration, owed formal notice before any seizure of body or property. Below the threshold, a person is **Sundered** — legally a bondable asset. Sundered status doesn't mean poor; it means *disposable-by-charter*: their labor, organs-for-transplant-surgery contracts (a real, licensed trade among Verdai's barber-physicians), and even their children's bond-debt can be sold at Hall auction to satisfy others' claims, with full legal process and a stamped certificate. The Hall's clerks are not corrupt. They compute the Assay correctly, every time, from real ledgers, under real audit. That is the horror the city has stopped noticing.

MarETTa Voss, a canal-lock keeper's widow with three debt-bonded children, has an Assay eleven points below Held threshold — not because anyone lied about her numbers, but because the threshold itself was set, a century ago, by the four founding clans, at a line that excludes exactly the labor-poor, kin-thin households like hers. Above her in the Hall's own service sits Notary Calvend Ashur, whose family has staffed the Assay bench for three generations; his Assay is comfortably Held, and the very Ledger that sorts Voss into disposability is, from his side of the bench, simply *how the city knows itself* — the mechanism that lets guild contracts be trusted, lets grain-futures be sold sight-unseen, lets Verdai function as a single credit-body instead of four warring clans. He is not lying when he calls the Ledger a coordination miracle. He is describing his own true experience of it.

**Per-character experience:**

- **Marett Voss (X₁, powerless/trapped/national):** The Weighing is not a filing but a verdict on what kind of person she is allowed to be. The Charter's Mercy is the sermon the Hall clergy give at her own children's bond-renewal, telling her the sale of their labor-years is a kindness sparing them debtor's prison. The Block is where her eldest son's transplant-contract will be sold next spring if she cannot raise eleven Assay-points, and no individual plea to the bench — she has made four — has ever once moved a number.
- **Notary Calvend Ashur (X₂, institutional/arbitrage/national):** The Weighing is the tool that lets him certify contracts strangers will honor sight unseen. The Charter's Mercy is simply true, in his felt experience — he has seen unregulated debt-chaos in the outer river-towns and believes the Hall prevents it. The Block is a quarterly civic function he officiates with genuine solemnity, understanding himself to be preserving order, not extracting from anyone; his own Assay has never once been at risk, so the extraction is, to him, invisible — not denied, simply never encountered.

### Linguistic Strategy: Direct Translation (from an invented Verdai patois, rendered in period-flavored English)

Verdai speech carries a heavy weighing/measuring register even in intimate speech, because the Assay has colonized the language of worth itself. Concepts with no English equivalent, translated directly:

- ***held-tongue*** (verb): to speak in the careful, hedged register Sundered people use around anyone Held, to avoid giving grounds for a bond-claim. "She held-tongued the whole audience with the notary."
- ***light-count*** (noun): the specific, felt sensation of being under-tallied — knowing your labor and love don't register in the number that decides your fate. "There's a light-count in loving three children you can't protect."
- ***bench-true*** (adjective): something correct by Hall procedure yet monstrous in effect; the phrase carries no contradiction for a Held speaker and a bitter one for a Sundered speaker. "It was bench-true, the seizure. Bench-true and it broke her."
- ***the Standing*** (noun): shorthand for the Ledger's line itself, spoken by Sundered characters the way one might speak of weather or tide — an entity, not a decision.

Grammatical feature carried into English: Verdai distinguishes, via a verb-particle, actions taken *by ledger* (impersonal, procedural, unrefusable) from actions taken *by hand* (personal, refusable, negotiable) — "they sundered him *by ledger*" vs. "she struck him *by hand*." This distinction is load-bearing: it is how Held speakers linguistically launder disposal into weather, and how Sundered speakers name, without framework language, the difference between a person's choice and the Hall's.

## SECTION 2: OMEGA LOG

**RESOLVED:**

- **Ω_E01:** Does an honest, non-corrupt certifying instrument read as too "soft" a villain for a Snare-register story? → Resolution: The Stage-0 contract explicitly requires this — the horror is the line, not the reading. Leaning into Ashur's total sincerity and the Hall's procedural cleanliness is correct, not a dilution. → Impact: Calvend Ashur is written with zero cynicism or hidden malice; this is the sharpest version of the invariant, not a hedge.
- **Ω_E02:** Does the "transplant/organ-contract" trade risk reading as a lift from a specific recognizable body-horror source? → Resolution: Kept incidental and legally embedded in a licensed barber-physician guild trade rather than foregrounded as spectacle; it exists to make "Sundered" carry bodily as well as economic stakes, per the Assay's actual disposal function, without being the story's focus. → Impact: mentioned once, not dwelt on.
- **Ω_C01:** How to naturalize C1 (Recognition_as_Threat) distinctly from C2 (Impersonal_Harm) when both cluster around "classification"? → Resolution: C1 = the *act* of Weighing (the momentary, per-person sorting event); C2 = the *doctrine* (Charter's Mercy) that retroactively justifies the sortings in aggregate. → Impact: gives Stage 4 two distinct scenes to dramatize rather than one repeated beat — a Weighing scene (C1) and a sermon/justification scene (C2), converging at an Auction (C3).
- **Ω_C02:** Where does the Scored-Snare gate's exception apply, concretely? → Resolution: `inherent_instrument: yes` was confirmed by Stage 0, so the Assay figure may appear diegetically — but only when a character with positional access acts on it in-scene (Ashur reading a bench figure aloud during a Weighing; Voss disputing her eleven-point deficit to a clerk). Narration itself must never be denominated in Assay points; extraction (bond-seizure, Auction) must always be rendered as sensation and consequence — the cold of the Hall floor, the sound of the gavel-bell, the specific labor lost — never as an arithmetic tally the prose itself performs. → Impact: hard constraint carried forward to Stage 4 prose instructions.
- **Ω_P01:** Register/tone for the Hall's proceedings — bureaucratic-flat or ecclesiastical? → Resolution: ecclesiastical. The Hall is explicitly a guild-cathedral; Weighing and Auction carry liturgical cadence (bells, vestment-like notary robes, call-and-response certification language). This reinforces that the Charter's Mercy is *preached*, not merely filed, which serves C2's naturalization as doctrine.
