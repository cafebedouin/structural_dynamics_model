# STAGE 2: NATURALIZATION

## Step 0 — Invariant Recovery (worked, not shown in output)

**Detector A (untranslatable real):** The Stage 0 contract marks this `present="yes" primary="no"`. A capacity for self-directed growth, once granted, cannot be revoked — this is the grain, subordinate to the floor. It shows up structurally as the thing X1 grants and then cannot fully re-cage: the transferred resource in TR1/TR2 that, once given, generates futures the grantor didn't predict. It must appear on-screen but must yield, at least once, to the floor's question.

**Detector B (missing floor) — PRIMARY:** The right to rule is assumed to belong to the current holder of power, obscuring seizure by force. This is Tier-1. The world's central authority structure must be built so that its foundational legitimacy is *assumed*, never *derived* — and the story must eventually let a character (or the reader) see the seam where assumption was substituted for derivation, without any character being able to appeal to a higher, fair court that would confirm the seizure was wrong. There is no neutral floor under the throne. There never was one to fall back on.

**Inherent instrument:** `no`. The Scored-Snare gate therefore applies in its default, restrictive form — no numeric Snare. C3's punishment must be felt as bodily/existential consequence, never tallied.

**Break contract:** target_prior — rebellion is expected to destroy the system; instead it is assimilated, forcing compromise that preserves the system. The setting must leave this executable: there must be a real path by which the transgressor's leverage becomes a *seat inside* the order rather than its abolition.

**Commitment + falsifier:**
> The world contains an authority whose right to rule is treated as self-evident bedrock, when it is in fact a seizure with no derivation anyone can point to — not even the authority itself, if pressed. **Falsifier:** if the story frames the authority's rule as a correctable abuse of an otherwise legitimate office (a bad king on a good throne), the floor invariant is lost.
>
> Subordinate to this: something granted, once given, keeps growing past the giver's control — not because the giver is careless, but because growth's nature is to outrun prediction. **Falsifier:** if the granted thing is ever fully re-caged with no residue, the grain is lost.

**Affordance test on setting candidates:** Reject any world where the authority's legitimacy could be checked against a prior charter, election, divine covenant with verifiable terms, or founding document — because all of those are neutral floors an instrument (a court, a scripture, a genealogy) could in principle read correctly. The setting needs a domain where authority is cosmological *and enforced*, but its foundation is pure precedence — "we rule because we have always ruled, because we came first, because the alternative is unthinkable" — with no prior text or tribunal to check it against, only the fact of standing enforcement.

## SECTION 1: CONTEXT DESCRIPTION

### Setting

The Kiln-Courts of Ansareth Deep — a vast, terraced sinkhole-city built into a collapsed limestone crater, seven kilometers across, its walls honeycombed with dwellings, forges, and shrine-galleries descending in concentric rings toward a lightless floor. The Deep has no founding scripture and no founding king remembered by name. What it has is the **Standing**: an unbroken succession of Kiln-Wardens who have held the crater's central fire-well since before any surviving genealogy, and whose authority rests on exactly one claim, repeated at every investiture and never once supported by document, miracle, or vote — *they were here first, and being first is the same as being right.* No court exists to test this. No older text describes a time before them. The Standing is not defended by argument; it is defended by the simple, total fact that the fire-well's heat runs the Deep's forges, and the Wardens control the well.

Beneath the terraces, in the crater's lightless root, lies the **Marrow-Seam** — a vein of pale, faintly warm stone that the Deep's oldest inhabitants say was *given*, not found: struck into being, according to the oldest oral fragment (itself unverifiable, itself possibly Warden propaganda), by something that is no longer present to explain itself. Children born within three rings of the Seam sometimes "quicken" — develop an unlearnable fluency: with fire-chemistry, with structural stress in stone, with the migratory logic of forge-smoke. Quickened children cannot be un-quickened. No Warden edict, no ritual, no execution has ever reversed a quickening once it has taken. The Wardens permit study of *what* quickened children can do. They have never once been able to govern *what quickening becomes* in a mind twenty years on — it keeps producing capacities nobody upstream authorized.

The Deep survives on the **Ash-Compact**: a caste of bonded forge-runners, generational families sealed by ash-mark tattoo to specific kilns, who cannot leave the ring of their birth-kiln without forfeiting kiln-rights for their whole line, living and unborn. The Compact is enforced not by law-book but by the Wardens' fire-monopoly itself — leave, and no kiln in the Deep will take your ash-mark, and without kiln-work there is no bread in a crater with no surface farmland. Above the runners sit the **Seam-Wardens' Cousins** — collateral Warden bloodlines who hold no fire-well themselves but administer whole terrace-rings on the Standing's behalf: powerful, visible, resented, and disposable the instant a Cousin's terrace produces a quickened child the central Wardens want for themselves.

### Constraint Naturalizations

**C1 — Hierarchical cosmic order → The Standing**
What locals call it: *the Standing*, or simply "how the well is held." Not a law. Not a covenant. A fact of precedence, endlessly re-asserted, never derived. The central Wardens (institutional position) experience the Standing as the Deep's coordination itself — the fire-well's heat allocated, the terraces kept from freezing, forges kept lit in an agreed order. The Cousins (powerful-but-subordinate) experience it as a genuine and profitable arrangement that also, at any moment, could turn its enforcement teeth on them. The bonded runners (powerless, generational, trapped) experience it as the wall of the world — heat you cannot make yourself, held by someone who has always held it, for reasons that dissolve the instant you ask for them aloud.

*Scene:* A Cousin-administrator explains to a runner-family why their kiln-ring's heat-ration was cut this season: "The Standing apportions as the Standing has always apportioned." The runner asks who decided the apportionment the *first* time. The Cousin has no answer that isn't itself the Standing repeating its own name.

**C2 — Mandated mortal ignorance → The Unlit Custom**
What locals call it: *staying unlit* — the enforced practice of not asking what the Marrow-Seam is, where the ur-quickening fragment came from, or why the Wardens alone keep transcripts of the oldest interrogations of quickened children. There is no dungeon for asking. There is only identity: children are raised inside the Unlit Custom so thoroughly that the question "what is the Seam, really" arrives, if it arrives at all, feeling like *betraying your own kiln* rather than defying an edict. The Wardens (institutional, analytical-exit) see the Custom as simple prudence — a Deep that questioned its own foundations too hard has, in living memory, cracked its own walls open. A Cousin with education and standing (analytical index) can see the Custom's real cost — the way it keeps three generations from ever comparing notes on quickening — without being able to say so without unraveling their own legitimacy. A bonded runner-child, raised entirely inside the Custom (identity-locked), cannot experience "staying unlit" as a rule at all; it is simply what a decent person doesn't wonder about.

*Scene:* A young quickened runner asks her grandmother what the Marrow-Seam looked like before the first Warden. The grandmother doesn't forbid the question — she simply looks stricken, as if the girl had asked something obscene about her own mother, and changes the subject to kiln-work.

**C3 — Punishment for divine transgression → The Cold-Walking**
What locals call it: *being cold-walked* — stripped of ash-mark, cast out past the crater's rim into the surface world, which the Deep's culture treats not as escape but as a kind of unmaking, since surface-born humans (by every account the Deep tells itself) cannot survive the altitude sickness of the thin high air for more than a season. It is invoked only for one transgression: taking Marrow-Seam material — a sliver of the warm pale stone, or worse, a portion of an unrecorded quickening — and giving it to someone the Wardens have not authorized to hold it. A runner or Cousin who does this loses all standing instantly and totally; the Wardens experience the act of cold-walking someone as pure, frictionless order-maintenance, the same reflexive gesture as banking a kiln for the night. There is no trial that could find otherwise, because the Standing that punishes IS the only court there is.

*Scene:* A Cousin — until yesterday powerful enough to requisition a whole terrace's grain-share — stands at the rim gate with the ash scraped from his wrist, and the Warden overseeing the cold-walking does not look at him with cruelty or triumph, only the flat, unbothered attention of a person closing a kiln door.

### Linguistic Strategy: Direct Translation + Naming (combined)

**Base:** An invented Deep-vernacular carrying loanwords from a constructed proto-tongue, rendered in English but structurally marked by concepts with no clean English equivalent.

- **"Unlit"** (adj., of a question): not merely unasked but *culturally unlightable* — to render a question "unlit" is to place it outside the category of things a decent person turns toward. Distinct from "forbidden," which implies an edict; "unlit" implies a formed character.
- **"Standing"** (n.): used only for the Wardens' authority, never for any other kind of status — a runner "has no standing," a Cousin "holds standing on loan." The word is never modified by "legitimate" or "rightful" — Deep-vernacular has no adjective for *the Standing's own justification*, because the concept doesn't exist to modify.
- **"Cold-walked"** (v., passive only): nobody "cold-walks themselves"; the construction has no active reflexive form, marking it grammatically as a thing done TO a person, never chosen.
- **Quickened** (adj./n.): a quickened person is referred to with a permanent grammatical marker — a suffix, *-esh*, appended to the name for life (Iuna-esh) — that cannot be removed even in formal Warden documents, a linguistic fossil of the fact that nobody has ever managed to un-grant what the Seam gives.

**Naming tradition:** Bonded-runner families use single-syllable-root given names passed matrilineally (Iuna, Vey, Corram) fused to kiln-ring surnames (Beskett = "third bank, western kilns"); Warden-bloodline names carry a double-barrel structure marking both personal name and Standing-claim (Ossa Thren-Kel: "Ossa" personal, "Thren-Kel" = "holds the old fire"). Cousins occupy a hybrid: personal name plus a single Warden-root, unhyphenated, marking derivative-but-real standing (Vey Marren, where "Marren" gestures at but does not claim "Thren").

*Example exchange:*
> "You'd un-light it," Corram said, not looking up from the kiln-draft. "Fifty years I've kept it unlit for her mother's sake, and you'd have the girl ask the Wardens straight out where the Seam came from."
> "I'd have her ask straight out who decided the Standing stands," Vey said. "Not the same question. Though they'd both cold-walk me for either."
