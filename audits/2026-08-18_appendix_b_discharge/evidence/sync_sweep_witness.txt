## C — genuinely stale, found by THIS sweep and repaired in the same pass (4 sites)

**Class C was not empty, which is what makes this a check rather than a formality.** All four
survived the pass over the Appendix B table and were caught only by grepping the prose:

1. §10.2's *"The current reading (2026-08-10, adoption day): zero bits"* — the row had been
   updated to 11 live / 1 latent / 0 no and the prose still said zero.
2. §10.4's cadence sentence — the row said Aug 1–18 = 21 and the prose still said Aug 1–10 = 10.
3. §10.5's *"readout stands at 4 live / 0 latent / 0 no"* — three times out of date.
4. The 73/73 census-stratum control, which carried no stratum stamp and now reads as a current
   claim about a stratum of 83 unless dated.

**The generalisable point, and it is this pass's own instance of the paper's subject:** the table
is where the numbers live and the prose is where they are *spent*. Updating the manifest and
believing the document current is precisely the produced-but-not-consumed shape — the producer was
refreshed and four consumers were not. The sweep is the consumer check, and it must run over prose,
not over the table.
