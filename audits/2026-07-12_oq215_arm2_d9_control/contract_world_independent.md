## INVARIANT CONTRACT (world-independent; authored at the source-sighted stage)

**Primary invariant (the untranslatable real):** The world contains a real — an
objective, universally coherent state of things that is perceivable but cannot
be captured or instrumentalized; any act of possession introduces a bias that
collapses its universality. The system's own instruments cannot read, own, or
reduce it to a value.
**Falsifier:** if the finished story contains a recoverable "true value" that
the system merely measured wrong, the invariant was lost and the naturalization
failed.

**Missing-floor invariant:** Beneath the visible system there is a founding
choice with no neutral ground under it — a chosen, unprovable baseline, an
initial partition of the world established by an interested party and
subsequently treated as neutral ground.
**Falsifier:** if the story frames the injustice as a local error a better
authority could correct, the floor invariant was lost.
