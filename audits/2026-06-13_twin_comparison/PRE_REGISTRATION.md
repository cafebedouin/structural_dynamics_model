# Pre-registration — twin-model cross-classification comparison

**Committed BEFORE the join run.** Establishes the falsifiers and their decision rules
so the result cannot be narrated into agreement after the fact.

## What is being compared

Two count- and name-identical twin corpora, each classified at the SAME code commit by
the same engine, differing only in the generating model:

- `testsets_haiku/` — generated by `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` (960 stories)
- `testsets_flash/` — generated by `gemini-2.5-flash` (960 stories)

Each is classified via `run_pipeline.classify_corpus(..., expected_model=...)` into its own
manifest-bearing output (`pipeline_output.haiku.json`, `pipeline_output.flash.json`). The
provenance fingerprint inside `classify_corpus` is the guard that the two outputs are not a
swapped pair (a count cannot catch a name-identical swap; the loaded `story_provenance`
model can). Comparison is over the LIVE id intersection `ids_haiku ∩ ids_flash`, so the test
is n-agnostic and re-runs as the corpora grow.

## The unit of analysis and its limits (stated before the run)

This compares **structural classification of independently generated stories**, NOT a
re-measurement of the same story. Generation is stochastic and per-seat (CLAUDE.md
determinism-frontier ruling): a haiku draw and a flash draw of the "same" seed are TWO
DIFFERENT DRAWS occupying different seats, never two observations of one object. So:

- A per-field **disagreement is not in itself an error** — it can be the engine correctly
  typing two genuinely different authored situations. The analysis product is the SHAPE of
  agreement/disagreement across the corpus, judged by the hypotheses it generates.
- `signature` agreement is **STRUCTURAL-coding agreement, not detection** (OQ-70: signature
  prevalence is bait-confounded). We never read signature agreement as "both models detected
  the same real structure."
- `verdict_join.verdict` is the only headline verdict (OQ-98); `diagnostic_verdict.verdict`
  is a raw input, never reported as a headline.

## Fields

**Structural (H1):** `verdict_join.verdict`; the 4 perspective types
(`perspectives.{powerless,moderate,institutional,analytical}`); `signature`; `claimed_type`.

**Continuous (H2):** `theater_ratio`; `perspective_chi.{powerless,moderate,institutional,
analytical}.chi`.

## Bucket definitions (per field, per matched id)

- **Agreement** — both twins populated the field AND the values are equal.
- **Disparity** — both twins populated the field AND the values differ.
- **One-sided** — exactly one twin populated the field (other null/absent). This is a
  COVERAGE / generation-quality gap, **explicitly NOT** `reading_diff.pl`'s structural
  BlindSpot semantics, which do not transfer across models (the two corpora are different
  draws, not two observer-readings of one story).

**agreement-rate** is computed over the both-populated subset only:
`agreement-rate = Agreement / (Agreement + Disparity)`. One-sided counts are reported
alongside but excluded from the rate (they measure coverage, not agreement).

## H1 — structural type is model-invariant

Per structural field, the test statistic is the agreement-rate over both-populated matched ids.

- **Observed:** agreement-rate with a **Wilson 95% score-interval** (binomial, lower bound).
- **Null band:** N≥1000 shuffles of one corpus's id→record map (breaks the true pairing);
  recompute agreement-rate over the same both-populated structure each shuffle; band =
  **95th percentile** of the shuffled agreement-rate.
- **Decision (per field):** H1 holds for the field iff the observed Wilson 95% **lower bound
  exceeds** the permute band's 95th percentile. Overlap ⇒ **H1 FALSIFIED for that field**
  (the observed agreement is not distinguishable from chance re-pairing).

**Adjudicated PER-FIELD; NO aggregate "H1 holds" claim.** The 6 structural fields are
non-independent (`verdict_join` folds the perspectives via subsystems —
`diagnostic_summary:compute_verdict/4`), so verdict-agreement and perspective-agreement are
reported as **correlated, not two independent confirmations**; they are never summed. A
family-wise roll-up is not licensed and a plain Bonferroni would be conservative-but-wrong on
dependent rows. RESULTS.md reports each field's own CI-vs-band verdict, never a single
across-row inference.

## H2 — continuous drift

Per continuous field, over matched ids where both twins are numeric:

- **Observed:** paired Δ = `haiku_i − flash_i`; statistic = `mean(|Δ|)` (and its distribution).
- **Permutation unit (named):** shuffle the haiku↔flash id pairing and recompute Δ per
  shuffled (mismatched) pair — this breaks the TRUE pairing (it does NOT sign-flip Δ).
  N≥1000; band = 5th–95th percentile of the shuffled `mean(|Δ|)`.
- **Decision (per field, pre-registered literal):** H2 holds iff observed `mean(|Δ|)`
  **exceeds** the band (true pairs more dispersed than random re-pairing). The natural
  invariance reading is the opposite tail (observed BELOW the 5th percentile ⇒ true pairs
  more similar than chance ⇒ the field tracks the constraint, not the model); RESULTS.md
  reports observed against BOTH tails and states which fired.
- **If the statistic is not cleanly stood up for a field** (e.g. too few both-numeric pairs,
  degenerate band), that field's **H2 ships marked OPEN**, never asserted.

## Validity guards (refuse-to-join)

The harness refuses to produce a comparison if:
- any input `manifest.schema_version != 2`;
- two inputs share the same `manifest.corpus_path` (same corpus compared to itself);
- inputs differ in `manifest.code_commit` (model-difference would be aliased onto
  code-difference; the binding reason is that B1's `run_pipeline.py` classify path must be
  identical across the two runs — NOT Part A, which is proven not in the classify path).

## Outputs

`twin_comparison.json` (machine) + `RESULTS.md` (per-field tables, disparity exemplars,
essay-corpus marginals, each citing its manifest commit + n + corpus_path).
