**Verdict.** The blind read **independently rejected RULE A (option-locus / define-dominant)** with
define-dominant stated as a live option: *"enumerating the options does not enumerate the resolution
operation; type by the operation the text names, not the option-locus."* None of the 5 typed Ω_C.
So **define-dominant is rejected by two independent reads** (the hand key + this one), and the
family **splits** (Ω_P / Ω_E / restatement) — it is not uniform Ω_C and not uniform Ω_P.

**Sharpening the rule (the blind read's contribution):** the general principle is **"type by the
named resolution operation"** — decider-locus→Ω_P is its special case where the operation is a
value/authority decision (id19), and Ω_E where the operation is a world-observation (id28, id38).

**Residual (per-omega, not #1):** id28 is Ω_E-vs-Ω_P unresolved between the two reads — both agree
the decider is the external committer frame; they differ on whether the *primary* operation is the
survey (Ω_E) or the decision (Ω_P). A fine split, not a threat to the #1 ruling.

**Honest limit:** N=2 reads, both anchored to the omega_variables vocabulary; the rejection of
define-dominant is text-grounded (the approach clause names an external operation), not merely
prior-driven — about as independent as available without an adversarial define-dominant proponent.
