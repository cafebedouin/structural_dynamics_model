## Changed passages (before → after)

### Change 1 — the first-assayer reckoning (seed L53), fee-grievance → floor-glimpse-then-lost

**BEFORE (seed L53):**
> That night I lay on the stone floor of the *ranchería* and felt the missing marcos like a hole in
> my chest. I had worked days. The assayer had worked an hour. Where was the justice in that?

**AFTER:**
> That night I lay on the stone floor of the *ranchería* and felt the missing marcos like a hole in
> my chest. Not the fee — I could not yet have told you the fee from the price; both were numbers on
> a paper I could not read. It was lower than that. I had carried down a mountain and the paper began
> counting at the silver and stopped. The days were not cheap on the page. They were not on the page.
> The walk, the bad air, the man coughing beside me whose name I did not ask — the paper had no line
> low enough to hold them, and I understood, the way you understand a thing once before you are taught
> out of it, that the paper's bottom was not the bottom.

*(The fee-grievance — "assayer worked an hour, I worked days" — is a correctable-parameter complaint
[a fairer fee fixes it]. The AFTER relocates the injustice below the fee: the ledger's zero is silver;
the mountain is not undervalued but off the page's bottom edge. "Understand a thing once before you
are taught out of it" plants the arc: this is what he forgets.)*

### Change 2 — teaching Sami (seed L115), the trade made explicit as floor-for-fluency

**BEFORE (seed L115):**
> "The paper *is* the work. The ore is just stone until someone writes its worth."

**AFTER:**
> "The paper *is* the work. The ore is just stone until someone writes its worth." I heard myself say
> it and did not flinch, though a year before I would have known it for the trick it is — that someone
> chose to call the silver the worth and the stone nothing, and the choosing is nowhere on the paper,
> which is why the paper looks like the bottom of things instead of a line cut across them. I no longer
> knew it. I knew the tasa. I could work the tasa in my head. That was the trade, though no one had
> shown me a scale to weigh it on.

grain is unwriteable; the floor is un-priceable-because-the-zero-was-chosen.)*
