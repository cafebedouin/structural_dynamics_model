Q1. **Expectation violated:** The story sets up what looks like a folk-fantasy about "luck" or natural hierarchy (wind favoring high ground) and leads the reader to expect a redress arc — someone finds the buried injustice, and the finding will matter. Instead the expectation that *discovery leads to correction* is violated. Tessa's report, Maren's new vocabulary, even Voss's belief in his own reform — none of these repair anything; the ledger and the ritual of naming the wrong prove to be inert. The story violates the expectation that a story about uncovering a hidden truth is a story about that truth doing something.

Q2. Yes: **"It held the way a lock holds when you have finally learned to shape the right key for it, which tells you nothing about who forged the door."** The syntactic discipline of building an abstract meta-commentary out of a physical image (lock/key/door) as a closing aphoristic beat, timed exactly at the moment of a character's small procedural victory, and doing so with that specific double negative-feeling qualifier ("tells you nothing about") — this has a very particular rhetorical fingerprint (the sentence exists purely to defeat the reader's relief) that reads as authored, not generated.

Q3. The injustice is that the theft (the stake's placement, the naming of "high" and "low" ground) has been fully absorbed into instruments — bells, gauges, charters, ledgers — that can now perfectly record *how much wind fell where this afternoon* but have no category, no clause, no drawer, in which "whose ground this was" can even be asked, let alone answered. Nothing can put this right from within the system the story shows us: not a fairer official (Doyle is not unkind, and it changes nothing), not a better instrument (the gauge is accurate and irrelevant), not a corrected record (Tessa's report is thorough, true, and filed into inertness). The wrong lives in the *architecture of legibility itself* — in the fact that the ridge built machinery precise enough to end brawls over gusts but structurally incapable of holding the one fact that mattered, so precision itself becomes the mechanism of forgetting.
