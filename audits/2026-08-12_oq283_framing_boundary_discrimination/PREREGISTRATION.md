## 0. The confound, declared first because it is not repairable

**This control is NOT blind, and the reason is on the record.** In the session immediately prior to
writing it, this instance read `docs/concealment/concealment_without_a_concealer_v0_4.md` §5.4 and
the OQ-277 audit log, and therefore already held the strongest candidate POSITIVE (the pin
manifest's unstated genre rule) before designing the criterion. A criterion designed with its
confirming instance in hand is a criterion that will confirm.

**Consequence for what this control may license.** Its value is **not** in finding a positive —
that was in hand. Its value is in (a) the criterion being stated in advance of the *classification*,
so it can be applied to cases nobody chose for it, and (b) the **DECLINE**: whether the criterion,
applied to a population it did not select, refuses anything. A criterion that classifies every
member of the population as separating is not discriminating, and the correct verdict in that case
is UNRESOLVED, not SEPARATION.

Per `build_discipline.md` → *A positive control demonstrates DISCRIMINATION, not detection*, the
grade claimed here is therefore set by the **decline**, and the decline must be
**naturally-arising** — drawn from the repository's own record, not authored for this run.

## 1. The question, stated so it can come out either way

The concealment paper describes framing loss at a boundary. `seat-theorem-v1.md` **already**
contains two things that may exhaust it:

- **§8** — the framing Π is a seat (the partition of Φ into background and dials is a live
  parameter, hence a standpoint). The paper's Π *is* this Π; its §3.2 says so and deliberately
  retreats to a weak form so as not to depend on it.
- **Corollary 2a** — concealment is the unique inconsistency, and declaration is the sole residue:
  *"A contentful verdict that does not show its seat is not neutral but concealed."*

**If boundary framing-loss is just an undeclared seat, then the concealment material is Corollary 2a
applied at boundaries** — an application, not a new structure, and no axis-level change to
`deferential_realism_paper_v8.md` / `commitment_systems_sketch_v6.md` is warranted.

## 2. The discriminating criterion, fixed now

Corollary 2a is an **imperative addressed to a party who holds the seat and can show it.** It is
dischargeable exactly when some party, at production time, held the framing as a datum.

> **The criterion.** For each instance: *at production time, did any party hold the framing as a
> datum they could have written down?*
>
> - **HELD** → 2a covers it. The failure is a refusal, an omission, or a slip of declaration.
> - **NOT-HELD** → 2a's imperative is undischargeable — not refused by anyone, not available to
>   anyone. The framing was the operation being performed, not a datum (paper §3.3: *"the producer
>   *was* the framing"*), or was inexpressible from the position (Mode 3).

This is the paper's own Mode 1 / Mode 2–3 line (§5.3, §9.1: *"A discipline addressed to withholding
reaches Mode 1 and does not reach Modes 2 and 3"*), restated as a test against seat-theorem rather
than against honesty norms.

## 4. Population, fixed now

The repository's own record — commits, audit directories, and the V04 consolidation manifest —
**because it is naturally-arising**: these artifacts were produced as work, not authored as
fixtures for this control. Authored decoys are the weakest ladder grade and are not used.

Two decline shapes are sought explicitly, and their absence is itself a reportable result:

1. **HELD-and-not-carried** — a producer who ran the check, held the result, and shipped an
   artifact that did not carry it. Predicted to exist; if it does not, the criterion is one-sided.
2. **Framing carried** — a boundary-crossing artifact that *does* carry its framing, so no failure
   occurs even though Π differs across the boundary. Predicted to exist.

## 5. What this control cannot settle, stated so the writeup cannot quietly widen

- It does **not** establish that the material is a third **axis**. Separation from 2a is necessary,
  not sufficient; v8 §5.2's declared exterior (the relational layer) is the other candidate home,
  and choosing between them is a design ruling (Ω_C), not this measurement.
- It does **not** rank Modes 2 and 3 against each other.
- It is a classification over a **convenience population** of one repository, so a count here is
  not a rate anywhere.
