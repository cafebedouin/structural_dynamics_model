# schema_shape.txt — the SHAPE REGISTER for the narrative_ontology namespace (OQ-308).
#
# SCOPE: this file covers the 63-predicate DECLARED NAMESPACE — every (name, arity) declared
# `:- multifile` or `:- dynamic` into narrative_ontology by any non-tests module in the repo.
# It does NOT cover "the 40 corpus-schema predicates". Those 40 are the subset story files
# write, and their ROLE=corpus-schema rows in module_boundary_allowlist.txt are a DERIVED VIEW
# of this register, not a peer of it: a row exists there IFF the (name, arity) is written as a
# qualified head by some story file. Arm E checks that derivation in both directions. 23 of the
# 63 are correctly rowless in the allowlist and are NOT missing anything.
#
# THE ANCHOR IS COMPUTED, NEVER READ FROM A LIST. python/module_boundary_check.py scans every
# non-tests module for declarations into the namespace, so a declaration landing in a SEVENTH
# module tomorrow becomes a register member with nobody adding it anywhere, and arm E goes red
# until it has a row here. That totality is the whole escape-removal guarantee: it is the one
# place the opt-in escape ("a new predicate is unguarded until someone remembers it") could
# re-enter, and it would re-enter invisibly, as a member not visible as a missing row.
#
# WHY REPO-WIDE AND NOT A NAMED-MODULE SCAN. A named-module scan yields 61. The two it misses
# are cs_axiom_engine.pl's four CS axiom predicates and intent_fact/4 — the latter declared
# only in scenario_manager.pl, in no narrative_ontology block and no allowlist row, with zero
# writers and zero readers. A member nothing watched.
#
# WHAT THESE ROWS ARE AND ARE NOT. LEGS= and the ARG shapes were TRANSCRIBED from the corpus as
# it stands. They are a DRIFT RATCHET, not a specification: they detect that the corpus moved,
# and they cannot tell you the declarations were right to begin with. See the arm F and arm G
# docstrings in module_boundary_check.py, which say the same thing where it is executed.
#
# GRAMMAR, and which shapes are actually ENFORCED, are in module_boundary_check.py's
# schema_shape section. In short: DECL= is a LIST (15 members are multi-module); LEGS= is TOTAL
# over the five legs and `any` costs a reason naming the leg; ARG<i>= is TOTAL over 1..arity,
# with {a,b,c}, number and text enforced by arm F while open/cid/atom/compound are
# documentation only; DISPOSITION=deletion-candidate is REQUIRED where writers and engine
# readers are both zero.
#
# Fail-closed: a malformed row is a gate failure, never a skipped line.
