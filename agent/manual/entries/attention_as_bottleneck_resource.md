# The Finite Cognitive Aperture: Attention as Bottleneck Resource

*For: attention deficit populations, epistemic commons, marginal signal producers*

**The Tell.** Individuals and communities with marginal social capital, institutional access, or signal-production capacity cannot acquire attention for legitimate claims, needs, or contributions (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The constraint resolves mandatrophy by revealing the distinction between biological limits and institutional amplification. The biological limit (human attention is finite) is real and immutable — this is a genuine 'mountain' fact about cognition.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if institutional dominates — constraint is Tangled Rope or Snare (suppression and extractiveness are governance choices, potentially reformable).
