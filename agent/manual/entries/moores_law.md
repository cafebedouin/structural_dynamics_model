*For: rival chip design methodologies, manufacturing cost sustainability, materials science constraints*

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. Moore's Law resolves the mandatrophy by revealing the constraint's hybrid nature. It appears as coordination (Rope) to dominant beneficiaries who use it to synchronize ecosystems. It appears as mixed (Tangled Rope) to moderate agents constrained but not broken by it.
