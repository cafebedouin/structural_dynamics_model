# Student Loan Interest Accrual During Deferment

*For: deferred borrowers, forbearance borrowers, low income graduates*

**The Tell.** Borrower placed in deferment due to unemployment, hardship, or enrollment in graduate school.

**Action under indeterminacy.** Assume extraction until shown otherwise. MANDATROPHY RESOLUTION (CONCEPTUAL): The constraint avoids the mandatrophy by distinguishing the coordination function (accrual-as-moral-hazard-prevention) from the extraction mechanism (accrual-as-debt-accumulation). Pure coordination (Rope) would require that accrual equally benefit borrower and servicer—it does not; borrowers in deferment uniformly experience extraction.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if high dependency — accrual is essential to the servicer's extraction mechanism (Snare or Tangled Rope classification).
