# Permissive Regulatory Framework for PFAS Chemicals in Consumer Products

*For: consumers exposed to pfas, public health infrastructure, environmental contamination*

**The Tell.** Powerless, trapped, global exposure (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Assume extraction until shown otherwise. The constraint avoids the false-coordination trap by recognizing that PFAS regulatory permissiveness cannot be framed as pure coordination. A genuine rope would require beneficiaries and victims to have symmetric benefit-cost ratios and comparable exit options — e.g., all firms adopting a shared standard that restricts everyone equally.

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: what level of epidemiological evidence triggers regulatory action: observed health outcomes in exposed populations, or prospective causation from animal models?
