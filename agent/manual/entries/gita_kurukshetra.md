# The Duty of the Kshatriya (Warrior Caste)

*For: individual moral agency, kinship bonds, non warrior castes*

**The Tell.** Arjuna's position before Krishna's teaching (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. This constraint resolves mandatrophy by revealing how linguistic naturalization ('dharma is cosmic law') masks institutional extraction. The philosophically sophisticated Gita argument — that action without attachment (nishkama karma) reconciles duty with morality — is itself part of the extraction mechanism. It allows warriors to perform duty without psychological rebellion by relocating moral agency to metaphysical framework.

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: can individual warriors exit the caste role through renunciation, skilled labor change, or gender/age transitions?
