# The Lindy Effect

*For: novel ideas, emerging paradigms, institutional innovators*

**The Tell.** Fresh intellectual work lacks the accumulated legitimacy of established ideas and must overcome higher verification barriers (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The Lindy Effect resolves mandatrophy through explicit perspectival pluralism. The constraint is NOT 'is this coordination or extraction?' but 'for whom is it coordination vs extraction?' Novel idea creators experience extraction; established frameworks experience coordination; institutions experience hybrid effects; organized alternatives experience scaffolding.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if <25% violated — effect is strong and may reflect genuine quality signal (Mountain).
