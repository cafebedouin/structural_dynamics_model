# Reputation as Distributed Enforcement Mechanism

*For: trapped actors without negotiation leverage, new entrants without history, marginalized groups with structural disadvantage*

**The Tell.** Cannot exit local labor market or geographic region.

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The constraint is legitimately Tangled Rope from the analytical perspective because it exhibits both genuine coordination function (solving adverse selection and moral hazard in anonymous markets) AND asymmetric extraction (lock-in effects, algorithmic rent-seeking, disproportionate punishment of powerless actors). The coordination function is real — without reputation systems, anonymous market exchange would face severe trust problems.
