# The Ontological Lake (Fish and Turtle Parable)

*For: cross boundary understanding, external knowledge claims*

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. Is this constraint epistemological (Mountain, based on logical limits) or institutional (Tangled Rope / Snare, based on suppression)? The parable structure deliberately creates ambiguity. One reading: the fish's limitation is logical — a water-only being cannot conceive of land. No amount of institutional change will give the fish the sensory apparatus to perceive air.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if unbridgeable — the constraint has genuine epistemological core that institutional structures merely reinforce.
