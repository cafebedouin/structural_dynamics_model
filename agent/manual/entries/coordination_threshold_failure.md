# The Critical Mass Chasm

*For: bootstrapping participants, network switching victims*

**The Tell.** Early participants who join before critical mass is reached experience zero utility while bearing all adoption costs (setup time, learning curve, social friction) (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The critical mass chasm resolves mandatrophy by demonstrating that the classification depends entirely on temporal position within the constraint's lifecycle. During bootstrap (T=0-2), the mechanism is functionally necessary: the chasm solves the cold-start problem, and some form of coordination threshold is unavoidable. From this phase, the constraint is Tangled Rope (genuine coordination function + unavoidable asymmetric costs).
