# The Exit Door Mirage

*For: late stage retail participants, illiquidity trap victims*

**The Tell.** Enters during period of apparent high liquidity; discovers exit option is illusory when attempting withdrawal during market stress (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Assume extraction until shown otherwise. The liquidity illusion resolves mandatrophy by distinguishing between legitimate coordination (market-making that enables price discovery) and extraction (temporal asymmetry in exit capacity). The snare classification prevents misidentifying this as pure coordination by flagging the high suppression (0.68) and the distribution of costs — trapped agents bear them, informed agents escape them.

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: do market makers actively suppress liquidity signals through inventory management to maintain the appearance of liquidity, or is liquidity genuinely available but constrained by inventory?
