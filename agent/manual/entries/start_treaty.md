# START Treaty Expiration and Nuclear Arsenal Limits

*For: non nuclear states, global nuclear stability*

**The Tell.** Countries without nuclear weapons cannot exit the strategic competition between US and Russia.

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. Initial intuition treats START expiration as a pure snare (extraction of global stability by two nuclear powers). But the constraint exhibits genuine coordination function (deterrence stabilization through mutual vulnerability). Without treaty constraints, both sides face incentive to maintain strategic balance—neither wants the other to achieve decisive advantage. This is not false coordination (like a protection racket).
