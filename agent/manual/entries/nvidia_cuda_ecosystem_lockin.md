# Nvidia CUDA Ecosystem Lock-in

*For: competing gpu manufacturers, ai researchers without cuda access, alternative accelerator platforms*

**The Tell.** Individual researchers and smaller organizations cannot easily migrate from CUDA due to training investment, library dependencies, and performance requirements.

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The tangled_rope classification is structurally justified and avoids the mandatrophy trap. The constraint exhibits both genuine coordination function (CUDA does solve the heterogeneous accelerator abstraction problem at scale) AND asymmetric extraction (Nvidia captures disproportionate value; competitors and users face persistent disadvantage).

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if achievable — architectural lock-in is weaker than enforcement lock-in, tangled rope rather than snare.
