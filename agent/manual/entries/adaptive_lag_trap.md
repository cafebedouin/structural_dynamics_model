# The Velocity Mismatch Anchor

*For: entrants operating at edge, consumers denied innovation, regulator institutional authority*

**The Tell.** A startup or entrant operating at the technological edge faces regulatory rules written for yesterday's technology (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The core tension is whether the regulatory lag is (a) coordination necessary to manage genuine risk (Rope), or (b) extraction mechanism protecting incumbent rents (Snare). This is the mandatrophy — is the slow rule-making process justified by careful risk assessment, or does it exist to suppress competition?

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if truly effective — scaffold sunset timeline is real (10-20 years).
