# Quota Ratchet Asymmetry in Performance Management Systems

*For: front line workers, middle management, worker health outcomes*

**The Tell.** Experiences pure extraction: every efficiency gain becomes a permanent baseline, every capacity decline is ignored (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The quota ratchet asymmetry resolves the mandatrophy by demonstrating that the classification depends on the observer's structural position. The constraint is NOT purely extractive (it has genuine coordination function—organizations need mechanisms to set and adjust performance targets), and it is NOT purely coordinative (the asymmetry creates systematic transfer of health and economic costs from institution to worker).

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if organizations genuinely cannot measure decline.
