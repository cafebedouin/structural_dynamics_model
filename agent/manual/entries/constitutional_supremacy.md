# The Supremacy of Written Constitutions and Judicial Review

*For: legislative majorities, executive prerogative, popular sovereignty in moment*

**The Tell.** A legislature passing a statute faces the hard constraint that courts may invalidate it (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. Constitutional supremacy resolves the mandatrophy by showing that the constraint is legitimately tangled rope, not pure snare misclassified as rope. The coordination function is genuine: constitutions do need supremacy to be binding, and majoritarian impulses do need checking.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if substitutes exist — judicial review is a contingent institutional choice (Tangled Rope/Piton).
