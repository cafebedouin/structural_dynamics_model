# State-Funded AI Companionship for Seniors

*For: senior social autonomy, intergenerational care labor, elder community infrastructure*

**The Tell.** The AI companion fills a genuine void but at the cost of normalizing human connection as outsourced commodity (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The tangled rope classification resolves the potential mandatrophy by identifying that the program has BOTH genuine coordination function AND asymmetric extraction. Coordination: the robot genuinely helps isolated seniors and solves the state's visibility problem. Extraction: the program substitutes for systemic investment in community care, extracts from family labor, displaces community organizations, and creates vendor lock-in.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if high substitution (visits drop >30%).
