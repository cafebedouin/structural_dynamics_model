# The Perpetual Alarm Fatigue

*For: mass population attention reserves, genuine emergency responsiveness, collective decision fatigue*

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The tangled rope classification resolves the potential confusion between 'crisis signal saturation is coordination infrastructure' (which would suggest rope) and 'it is pure extraction' (snare).

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: what reduction in alert frequency would restore civilian responsiveness to genuine high-stakes warnings without losing coverage of important-but-non-critical events?
