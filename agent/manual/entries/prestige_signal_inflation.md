# The Credential Red Queen

*For: late entrants and majority seekers, economic mobility aspirants*

**The Tell.** Entry-level workers cannot exit the credential treadmill (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The constraint exhibits both genuine coordination (credentials provide reliable signals; employers need filtering mechanisms; aspirants need credible progress markers) and asymmetric extraction (artificial requirement escalation; systematic advantage for high-income actors; perpetual cost escalation for low-income seekers). The mandatrophy is resolved by recognizing that both functions are simultaneously real and structurally coupled.

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: do high-income actors have systematically better ability to acquire and maintain credential advantage than low-income actors?
