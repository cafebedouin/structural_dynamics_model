# Negative Emissions Arbitrage Market

*For: vulnerable populations, future generations, ecosystem carbon sinks*

**The Tell.** Small island nations and low-lying agricultural regions bear the full cost of temperature overshoot while wealthy emitters purchase future negative emission credits at present-day prices (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. This constraint satisfies the Tangled Rope gate through explicit structural data. It has both a coordination function (capital allocation to mitigation projects) and asymmetric extraction (temporal arbitrage allowing present emissions to be deferred). It requires active enforcement (carbon accounting standards, credit issuance protocols, trade agreements).

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if legitimate hedge — market is Rope from trader perspective, Tangled Rope from vulnerable populations.
