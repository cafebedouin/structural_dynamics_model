# The Compliance Time-Trigger (Latent Regulatory Bomb)

*For: innovation wave entrants, technology deployment velocity, compliance cost bearing entities*

**The Tell.** A startup or new market entrant discovers mid-deployment that dormant regulatory provisions activate once their technology crosses a threshold (market share, user base, deployment density) (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The latent regulatory bomb exemplifies how mandatrophy resolution hinges on whether a constraint is protective coordination or protective extraction. The incumbent industry benefits, so the question is: do innovation wave entrants also benefit from the safety function the provision was designed for?

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if clearly communicated — classification shifts toward Rope (coordination with asymmetric information) and Scaffold (temporary problem with known sunset).
