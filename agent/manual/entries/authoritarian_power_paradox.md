# The Paradox of Authoritarian Centralization

*For: peripheral agents, diagnostic capacity, system resilience*

**The Tell.** Information channels are monitored; dissent is suppressed; exit requires abandonment of social/economic ties (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The tangled-rope classification resolves the mandatrophy by showing that centralization does provide genuine coordination benefits (unified command, reduced negotiation overhead, rapid resource mobilization) AND asymmetric extraction (suppression of peripheral voices, information control, forced compliance). The paradox is not whether centralization works for coordination — it does.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if technologies create genuine escape.
