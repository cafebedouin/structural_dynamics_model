# The Lifecycle of a Fiat Currency

*For: currency holders, savers on fixed income, future generations*

**The Tell.** Individual with savings denominated in the currency has no exit: cannot easily convert to alternative stores of value without regulatory obstacles, taxation, or capital controls (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. This constraint resolves mandatrophy by showing that tangled rope is the stable classification: genuine coordination function (medium of exchange, credit creation) coexists with asymmetric extraction (inflation tax, financial repression, regulatory barriers to substitution).

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if necessary — extraction is hidden fiscal restructuring, not coordination failure.
