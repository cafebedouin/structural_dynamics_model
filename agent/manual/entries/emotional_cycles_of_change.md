# The Kelley-Connor Cycle of Change

*For: adopter psychological burden*

**The Tell.** At stage 3 (Valley of Despair), the adopter is trapped by sunk investment (time, emotional effort, organizational commitment) and cannot retreat to prior practices without visible failure (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. This constraint avoids mandatrophy through explicit perspectival pluralism. The Tangled Rope classification (primary) correctly identifies that the cycle has BOTH a genuine coordination function (helping organizations manage change at scale) AND asymmetric extraction (psychological cost concentrated on adopters).

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if >70% contingent — Valley is a Snare that could be redesigned to Rope or Scaffold through better support.
