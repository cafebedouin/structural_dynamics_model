# Role Capture Through Cost Asymmetry in Organizational Ethics

*For: individual moral agency, whistleblowers, junior employees, organizational integrity*

**The Tell.** Intervention costs are immediate and personal (job loss, blacklisting, retaliation), while complicity costs are diffuse and delayed (guilt, moral injury) (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. This constraint resolves mandatrophy by demonstrating that tangled_rope is the correct analytical classification when a structure exhibits both genuine coordination and asymmetric extraction. The cost asymmetry is not pure extraction (snare) because role differentiation does enable collective action and organizational function — flat structures face real coordination costs.

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: what is the actual base rate of retaliation against whistleblowers vs the perceived rate, and how does this gap affect intervention decisions?
