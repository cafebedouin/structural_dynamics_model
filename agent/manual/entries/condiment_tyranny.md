# The Tyranny of the Default Condiment Offering

*For: consumer choice diversity, niche condiment producers, culinary pluralism*

**The Tell.** Small-scale artisanal and regional condiment makers face insurmountable barriers to shelf space and table presence in standardized food service (and increasingly theatrical).

The coordination function (supply chain efficiency) is genuine and substantial. The extraction function (gatekeeping niche producers, suppressing consumer choice diversity) is also genuine and substantial.
