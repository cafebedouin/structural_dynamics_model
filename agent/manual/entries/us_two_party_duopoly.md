# The U.S. Two-Party Political Duopoly

*For: third party movements, independent candidates, ideological minorities, electoral competition*

**The Tell.** A viable candidate from outside the two major parties faces ballot access barriers (signature collection, filing fees) (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Lean toward extraction. The mandatrophy is resolved by recognizing that the duopoly is neither pure coordination (rope) nor pure extraction (snare). It is tangled rope: a genuine coordination mechanism (two-party aggregation reduces voter decision complexity, structures legislative cooperation) that generates asymmetric extraction (ideological minorities trapped by vote-splitting).

**Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: at what ideological distance from the major parties does the spoiler dynamic become mathematically inevitable (vote splitting guarantees defeat)?
