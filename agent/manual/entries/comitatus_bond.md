# The Germanic Comitatus Code

*For: bound thanes, non warrior commons*

**The Tell.** A thane who swears the comitatus bond sacrifices individual choice; death before the lord is not optional but covenantal (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The comitatus resolves mandatrophy by demonstrating that genuine coordination (assembling a war band, creating stable hierarchies) can coexist with asymmetric extraction (thane autonomy loss, commons subsidy). The constraint is not 'really just coordination' or 'really just extraction' — it is both integrated. The mandate is coordination: solve the collective action problem of military organization.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if rings are substantive compensation.
