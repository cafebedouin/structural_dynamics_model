# The Necessary Day Job for Creatives

*For: creative practitioners, artistic output quality*

**The Tell.** Creative practitioner cannot exit wage labor without sacrificing basic subsistence (and increasingly theatrical).

**Action under indeterminacy.** Hold both possibilities. The constraint resolves mandatrophy by distinguishing genuine coordination function from extractive asymmetry. The day job IS a coordination mechanism — it solves the labor supply/demand problem through wage employment. But the coordination benefit is skewed toward employers through the subsistence dependency mechanism. Creative workers cannot reject the employment offer because they lack alternative subsistence sources.

**Revision condition.** This reclassifies if recoverable through scheduling changes.
