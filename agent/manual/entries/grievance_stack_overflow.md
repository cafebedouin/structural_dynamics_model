The administrative authority genuinely experiences rope: their coordination problem (processing complaints efficiently) is real and has real solutions (capacity expansion). **Revision condition.** Reclassify when you can answer: can complaint processing capacity be expanded through resource investment, or is there an architectural ceiling below which it cannot operate?
