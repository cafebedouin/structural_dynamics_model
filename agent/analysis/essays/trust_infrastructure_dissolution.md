## VI. The Omegas: What Remains Genuinely Open

The engine generated omegas (genuine uncertainties) that survived the extraction process. These are not findings parked as questions—these are empirical or conceptual gaps where the framework's own premises don't resolve the uncertainty.

**The gap:** For agents without temporal resources to accumulate full behavioral distributions, compressed signals (powerless treatment, small binding acts) are the bootstrap heuristic. Whether they approximate full distribution epistemic function at useful reliability is empirically open.

**Why it's genuine:** The framework acknowledges this (source text Ω₁) but provides no empirical validation. The engine's omega resolution asks for N=30+ instances measuring whether powerless-treatment signal correlates with full-distribution character assessment. This is testable but untested.

**What it means for the framework:** If compressed signals are unreliable, the framework creates two-tier epistemology—those with observation resources get warranted trust, those without get heuristics that may fail. If compressed signals are reliable, the framework's emphasis on longitudinal observation may be overstated—the Tuesday mechanism may not be necessary for character assessment.

### Ω₃: Threshold Asymmetry

**The gap:** Where two trusted persons hold genuinely different thresholds for the same decision, character warrants the relationship but does not resolve the disagreement. Trust must be domain-aligned, not merely character-validated. Where alignment cannot be achieved, acceptance of asymmetry is the functional answer.

**Why it's genuine:** The framework (source text Ω₃) acknowledges this but provides no decision procedure. Two people with validated character can disagree about thresholds (when to leave a job, when to end a relationship, when to take a risk). Character observation tells you they're trustworthy, not that their threshold is correct.

**What it means for the framework:** Character-based trust is necessary but not sufficient for decision-making. The framework emphasizes observation accumulation for character assessment but doesn't address how to handle threshold disagreements between trusted persons. This is particularly acute under thinned conditions where you can't afford to discard trusted relationships over threshold disagreements.
