## I. The Timing Mechanisms: When Constraint Becomes Crisis

This matters because Mountains and Ropes have different reform pathways. ## IV. Perspectival Fractures and the Difficulty of Reform

### The Cover Story Theorem

The engine instantiates Theorem 1 (Cover Story) for five constraints: iran_conflict_spending, tariff_revenue_volatility, congressional_action_window, limit_arrival_timing, and x_date_timing. This is not metaphorical. The engine computes type classifications from declared metrics across four canonical observer positions (powerless, moderate, institutional, analytical). When different observers produce different types, that is structural evidence that the constraint's *actual operation* varies by position—not that observers have different opinions about the same operation.

- Powerless observer: Tangled_rope (contested coordination)
- Moderate observer: Snare (extractive trap)
- Institutional observer: Rope (benign coordination)
- Analytical observer: Snare (extractive trap)

The institutional observer sees a functional revenue mechanism. The moderate and analytical observers see an extraction mechanism masked as revenue policy. The powerless observer sees contested coordination—recognizes both the coordination function and the extraction but lacks power to change it.

The H¹ cohomology band of 5 means this classification fracture is driven by *both* Hub 1 (power-scaled extraction) and Hub 2 (effective immutability). Different observers not only experience different extraction levels (Hub 1) but also face different change barriers (Hub 2). For institutional observers, the constraint is genuinely changeable—they have the authority to modify tariff policy. For powerless observers, the constraint is effectively immutable—they have no mechanism to influence tariff policy.

This creates a specific reform pathology: institutional observers, for whom the constraint is changeable, perceive it as benign coordination and see no need for reform. Powerless observers, for whom the constraint is effectively immutable, perceive it as extraction but lack power to change it. The observers who could change the constraint don't see a problem; the observers who see a problem can't change the constraint.

### The Oracle Gap

This is the formal statement of a phenomenon the source material exhibits: confident assertions about debt limit mechanics that miss critical structure. From the powerless observer position (individuals receiving Social Security, military pay, Medicare services), the debt limit is a survival constraint—when the X Date arrives, their payments stop. All three classifications are *simultaneously true* because they describe different aspects of the mechanism's operation. A classical oracle—a model that looks carefully from one position—will be confident in its classification but will miss what the other positions reveal. The source material exhibits this pattern: high confidence in the "payment authorization" framing, no acknowledgment of the extraction structure.

### The Thermodynamic Coupling

The engine instantiates Theorem 5 (Functor Axiom—violated) for six constraints: iran_conflict_spending, tariff_revenue_volatility, economic_condition_uncertainty, congressional_action_window, limit_arrival_timing, and x_date_timing. The theorem states: "Classification does NOT factor through a single Boltzmann distribution. Observer positions are thermodynamically coupled—the constraint's type depends on which observers you condition on, not just their individual measurements."

This is the most technically demanding finding but also the most structurally important. A constraint satisfies the Functor Axiom if its type classification is independent of observer position—you can compute the type from metrics alone without knowing who is observing. Mountains and Ropes typically satisfy this axiom. Snares and Tangled_ropes typically violate it.

The Boltzmann compliance score measures whether the constraint's metric profile factors into independent dimensions. A score of 0 (compliant) means the dimensions are independent—extraction, suppression, and theater can be varied independently. A score > 0.3 (non-compliant) means the dimensions are coupled—changing one forces changes in others.

Congressional_action_window: Boltzmann score 1.0 (maximum coupling), threshold 0.33. The extraction, suppression, and theater dimensions are maximally coupled. You cannot reduce extraction without reducing suppression and theater in lockstep. This is the formal signature of a constructed mechanism—the dimensions are coupled *by design* to produce a specific outcome.

Compare treasury_cash_discretion: Boltzmann score 0 (compliant), genuine coordination. The dimensions are independent. Treasury can adjust cash levels without changing enforcement or theatrical elements. This is the formal signature of a natural coordination mechanism—the dimensions vary independently because they serve different operational functions.

The thermodynamic coupling creates a specific reform barrier: you cannot incrementally reduce extraction from coupled mechanisms. Reducing extraction requires simultaneously reducing suppression and theater, which means the mechanism stops functioning in its current form. This is why "reform the debt limit while preserving its coordination function" is structurally difficult—the coordination function and the extraction function are thermodynamically coupled in the current mechanism.
