## §0. TRIAGE AND STANCE

**Triage first.** Classify the seed's defect in one sentence; if you cannot, that is the first problem.

- **Type A — register drift.** Bones sound; valence slipped. The ending consoles where the material demands cold; a motif softens on repetition. *(Witnessed: rev2's gold-sunset "someone would still be listening.")* Resolution: reframe (§3).
- **Type B — broken grain.** The central real is a knowable value the system mismeasured — Assessment-class, correctable by a fairer authority. No prose fixes a wrong foundation. *(Witnessed: rev6, saved only by its last page.)* Resolution: re-found (§1a, §2) or reject.
- **Type C — doubled.** One face, two stories; a POV doing two jobs; variance asserted, not dramatized. Resolution: split or index.

**The shadow criterion (improve-vs-reject).** Re-found a Type-B seed only if the grain's *shadow* already exists in the draft — an unmapped gesture, an unexplained detail, a repeated thing the seed never assimilates. A closed loop of measurable values is rejected outright: don't break a locked safe; generate a new one.

**Then declare the stance — the fork, before any change.**

- **Path A (internal):** inhabit the seed's logic and sharpen what it is trying to be. Default for Type A and most Type C.
- **Path B (immanent):** turn the seed against itself — its own devices become the indictment. Under Path B, rev6 does not get a better ending; the platform's narration becomes a seat and its confidence becomes the evidence. Default candidate for Type B with a live shadow.

**Erasure-of-superiority (Path B constraint).** Delete every gesture of being smarter than the seed. If the sharpness collapses, it was a dunk, not a reading. Mismatching resolution to type — prettifying a Type-B seed — is F-MISROUTED and accomplishes nothing.

## §1. THE LAYER AUDITS

Topology is assumed sound from the seed; it is the pipeline's native competence. Two audits are this protocol's.

### §1a. The grain

The seed's Tier 1 is its untranslatable real: the thing the story's system cannot read, own, or resolve to a value.

- **Counterfactual test (load-bearing):** would the story read identical if the grain were merely *hidden* rather than untranslatable — recoverable by a better instrument or fairer authority? Identical → decoration → Type-B triage.
- **Two-reals subordination:** a recoverable injustice may coexist with the grain *only if* the story subordinates it on-screen; a build-a-better-meter subplot must never be the resolution.
- **Falsifier indifference:** stage the grain's strongest falsifier and let the story survive *without rebutting it*. The grain should feel indifferent to the falsifier, not victorious over it. (Refusal — rev2's "this will not make you money" — is good; indifference is stronger.)
- **No administrator:** if the grain acquires an enforcer or a fixer, it has become a Snare and stopped being the grain.

### §1b. The break

**Definition.** The break is the specific expectation-violation that makes a story canonical rather than competent. It is the third layer — distinct from topology and grain — and it has a property they lack: *it is not in the text.* It lives in the relation between the text and its readers' priors, which makes it **reader-seat-indexed: it cannot be carried across; it can only be re-instantiated against current priors.**

**Consume or author the contract.** If a BREAK CONTRACT arrived, adopt its target_prior. If none arrived (any drafted seed), author one here: name the expectation the seed's tradition sets, rule it LIVE or DEAD, and set the target.

- **LIVE prior:** restate it in current terms and execute the violation. *(Rime's disproportion still cuts; The Calm carries it near-intact and turns the compulsion-to-retell into the ending itself.)*
- **DEAD prior — the re-aim playbook (Faint Blue):** do not hide what every reader already knows; *relocate the withheld thing* (the depleting supply, not the identity); re-aim the monster at what the current reader's world rewards (Hyde as professional excellence, kindness refrigerated); relocate the live question (not "same body?" but "which one was real?"). Inversion is also re-aim: give the reader the opposite of the absorbed ending *(Radiator Song: no mad breakout — discharged as cured, the chart reading green over a dissolved person).*

**The quadrant.** Displacement distance and break-survival are independent axes. Target: **far + re-aimed** — kill everything guessable (safe: the break was never in the guessable surface) and rebuild the violation against a named current prior in a distant substrate. *(The Visibility Report amputates the whole SF apparatus and keeps six words of Wells: "concierge veterinary service included in the lease.")* Negative control: topology carried, break dropped, yields competent-honest-unsurprising *(Blood Silver: the knock deleted, replaced by grinding endurance — the house register).*

**Operational test:** did anything in the seed surprise you? Name it. Nothing nameable = no break present = the seven this protocol exists to break.

## §2. THE DEFORMATION PASS (offense, governed)

Everything else in this protocol is a gate; gates cannot escape the seed's likelihood basin — they yield a cleaner version of the same attractor. This pass generates candidate escapes. It runs after §1, before §4.

**Every deformation must be addressed.** Its manifest entry names the prior it aims to violate (the target_prior or another named expectation) or the grain-facet it aims to expose. Unaddressed strangeness — weirdness without coordinates — is F-UNADDRESSED and is not a candidate. A break has an address; a rock in a shoe does not.

**Operator menu (1–2 per run, not all):**
1. **Orthogonal anchor:** render the grain in the physical vocabulary of a distant discipline (mycology, orbital mechanics) — breaks the associative chain that reaches for mining/blood/ledgers.
2. **Register sabotage:** one scene in a deliberately wrong register the grain demands (clinical, archaic, vulgar); weaponize the clash.
3. **Seat-diction contamination:** one seat's diction intrudes into another, unexplained.
4. **Causal inversion:** swap cause and effect in one key scene; force coherence reconstruction.
5. **Defect injection:** deliberately worsen one scene along a named axis, then fix the defect without restoring the original — the fix navigates around the smoothing instinct.
6. **Dispreferred-constraint rewrite:** opening and climax rewritten under a syntactic restriction (no similes; fragments only; hard word-length cap) — the sentence-level lever, run as structure, not as a standing ban (bans were witnessed failing at three sites).

**Governor.** Injection is licensed to worsen; gates apply at *integration*, not at injection — a deformation may stand worse for one revision cycle, then faces §4 like any change. **Style-drift lock:** if the result can be described as "X but with a Y twist" — literary fragmentation, Borges pastiche — it is still template, one shelf over. For Type-B re-founding, draft the grain scene first, in isolation, and let the rest of the story orbit it awkwardly before smoothing anything.

## §3. RUPTURE AND SEATS

### The rupture (ending)

The blow lands first: the cost must fully threaten to be unredeemed before any meaning arrives; consolation that arrives early substitutes for cost rather than answering it. **Commit to a level and name it in the manifest:**

1. Cost lands; no consolation.
2. Consolation from the wrong direction or wrong seat.
3. Consolation partial, ironic, or poisonous.
4. Consolation earned, changing nothing material.
5. Transmission / hope / organizational fix — the pipeline's default sin.

Level 5 requires an explicit written defense that the material demands it. If the ending unsettles neither writer nor reader, it flinched; HUMAN-REVIEW is the routing and the flag is the system working.

### The seats (collision, not plurality)

Upgrade from v0.1: independence is not enough — models drift from independent seats toward implicit agreement, and *elegant relativism is also a comfort zone*. Require **collision**: at least two seats whose valid descriptions are mutually incompatible — not "they see differently" but *if one is true as stated, the other cannot be formulated*. (The laborer cannot state the analyst's metric without falsifying her own experience; the Warden's logged 12.1% and read 11.6% are both his.) **No synthesis:** the truths must pass each other in the dark; no POV ends by fully understanding the other's cost; "both perspectives are valid" is the smoothed corpse of a collision. **Optional adversarial seat:** a minor viewpoint that, taken seriously, reframes the whole telling as sentimental or self-serving — coherent on its own terms, unresolved, uncomfortable for the story's own voice. **Over-sharpness guard:** some elements must remain ordinary so the grain has something to cut against; a story that is all edge is a brittle manifesto, which is its own template.

## §4. THE GATES

The governing law, applied per change:

- **Adversarial erasure.** Delete the change, then name the specific capability lost — second-seat coherence, irreversibility, collision, an addressed violation. No nameable lost capability → decorative, even if the story "feels weaker." Cut.
- **Substitution control.** For contested keeps: generate the generic-competent version of the same move and diff. Indistinguishable → it was the mean with different polish. Cut.
- **Compression test.** Summarize the story in two sentences. A standard premise → the work isn't done. A summary that distorts or omits the core → structural grip.
- **Register.** Numbers only when a positioned character acts on them in-scene; word-arithmetic earned (own survival math; the ledger's language shown to be refused) or cut; low density achieved through vagueness is not a win.
- **Transparency.** Does it read like a story written to survive a protocol? If grain/seat/cost can be reverse-engineered by a sensitive reader, add obliquity: remove one explicit anchor, leave a gap, check the story is stronger for not explaining itself.
- **Subtraction default.** Most sharpening of a competent seed is removal. Addition is suspect; it is where polish enters — and where deformations must earn their stay.

## §5. THE ENSEMBLE (optional; the protocol's most falsifiable stage)

Not opinions — assigned failure pressures. Model A: find the most compressible interpretation. Model B: force a deformation that breaks seat collision. Model C: argue the grain is actually recoverable. Convergence that softens is rejected on sight (it is the mean, measured); divergence enters only as candidates through §4. The operator holds the sharpens-verdict — the one discrimination model judges reliably fail (witnessed: D9 scoring the negative control 5). **Standing kill condition (Ω_E2):** if, across runs, no ensemble deviation survives §4, cut this section whole. It is kept separable for exactly that amputation.

---

## §6. THE BLIND READ (terminal; cannot be delegated or cut)

The reader receives the seed and the improved story and **nothing else** — no audit trail, no manifest, no deformation record, not this protocol's vocabulary. Preferably not the operator who ran the passes. Process-sophistication reads as depth to anyone who can see the process; withhold it or the apparatus vouches for itself.

Three questions:
1. **Name the expectation this story violated.** None nameable → it is a seven → HUMAN-REVIEW, regardless of every green gate above. (Checkable, unlike "is this original?")
2. **Is there one sentence you cannot imagine another LLM writing?** No → the prose is still in the mean even if the architecture isn't; route back through §2 operator 6.
3. **Ten minutes later, what remains?** Theme = weak; plot = medium; a specific irreducible moment = strong.

**Pass:** a named violation plus at least one strong answer on 2–3, and the named violation survives §4's erasure (the story is weaker without the violating element). The standing question underneath all three is Rift-vs-Assessment: is this saying something a competent model wouldn't, or is it well-audited sameness? A thoroughly audited seven is still a seven, and this gate's job is to say so.

## §7. OUTPUT

**Manifest:**
```
stance: [A | B] + one-line justification
seed_triage: [A/B/C] + diagnosis sentence + (Type B) shadow named or REJECTED
break: [received | authored] · prior_status [LIVE | DEAD] · target_prior · execution (where the violation lives in the text)
deformations:
  - operator · address (named prior / grain-facet) · [integrated | cut at gate]
changes:
  - what changed · before→after witness · lost-capability if erased
consolation_level: [1–5] (+ defense if 5)
collision: seats named · what each truth makes unformulable for the other
ensemble: [run | skipped] · deviations kept (why they sharpen) · convergence rejected
blind_read: verdict + the named violation (or HUMAN-REVIEW: sameness)
operator_estimate (optional): per kept beat, probability the median model produces it [low/med/high]
```

**Quality gates:**
```
☐ Triage + stance declared before any change; resolution matches type
☐ Type-B: shadow named or seed rejected — never polished
☐ Grain survives counterfactual test; falsifier staged, met with indifference
☐ Break: contract consumed or authored; LIVE/DEAD ruled; violation executed and located
☐ Every deformation addressed; gated at integration; style-drift locked
☐ Consolation level named; level 5 defended in writing
☐ Collision present; no synthesis; ordinary elements retained
☐ Every keep survived adversarial erasure with a named capability
☐ Contested keeps passed the substitution control
☐ Two-sentence summary distorts or omits the core
☐ Blind read run blind; violation named; a sentence no other model writes
```

**Anti-patterns (one line each):** F-POLISH (survives its own deletion). F-CORRECTABLE-REAL (knowable value mismeasured). F-CONSOLATION (meaning before the cost lands). F-SYNTHESIS (seats reconciled; "both valid"). F-UNADDRESSED (strangeness without a named prior). F-STYLE-DRIFT ("X with a Y twist"). F-OVER-SHARPNESS (all edge; brittle manifesto). F-MISROUTED (prettifying Type B). F-PROCESS-VOUCH (the trail vouching for the output). F-CONVERGENCE-COMFORT (keeping the smoother consensus). F-CONCEPT-INFLATION (fails Parfit). F-REGISTER-LEAK (the meter's fingerprints in the prose).

**Self-application:** each version ships shorter than its predecessor. Option per run: suspend one gate deliberately, log the violation, keep the result only if it passes §6 — the protocol must not fossilize into its own competent mean. The blind read stays blind to this manifest too.

**Ω:**
- **Ω_E1 (positive control, gates everything):** rev6 through v0.2, Path B declared. Pre-registered: re-founded grain (the index becomes unreadable-in-principle) = repair confirmed; prettier correctable-bias story = downstream repair is dead, contraction not layering, the scored-Snare hard ban reopens.
- **Ω_E2:** does the ensemble ever produce a §4-surviving deviation? If not across runs, amputate §5.
- **Ω_E3:** break verdicts are one reader's priors. Run the five resleeves past a reader who doesn't know the sources; if the surprise-map diverges (Blood Silver surprises them; the veterinary clause doesn't), the layer is real but the map needs a panel.
- **Ω_C3:** the deformation worsening-license is one revision cycle; whether some deformations need longer to integrate is unruled.
- **Ω_C2 (ruled, standing):** the sharpens-verdict and the blind read are operator-held by design — assisted, not autonomous; revisit only at volume.
