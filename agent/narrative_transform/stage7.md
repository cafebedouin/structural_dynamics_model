## Stage 7: Structure, Rupture, and Rewrite

You are performing a structural edit on the story, governed by the strategy brief.

**Your output:** The revised narrative + an edit manifest documenting what changed and why.

**What you receive:**
- The generated story (Stage 4 output)
- The strategy brief (Stage 6 output)

---

### Operations

Perform these audits, then execute the rewrite. One creative movement: diagnose, then fix.

**PROPORTION AUDIT:**
Does each section's word count match its narrative importance?

**ENDING DIAGNOSIS:**
- Where does the story actually end?
- How many codas follow the climax? Keep one. Cut the rest.
- Hard budget: 350 words or fewer after the attractor scene.
- Seeded Possibility endings: seed must be visible but never stated. If stated, cut the statement.

**RUPTURE AUDIT:**
Is this ending honest to the cost, or is it narratively satisfying?

1. COST ACCOUNTING: What did the story's events actually cost the characters? Not thematically — physically, relationally, psychologically.

2. PAYMENT CHECK: Does the ending acknowledge these costs in proportion to their severity? Or does it convert them to meaning, progress, or hope?

3. ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION CHECK: Is the final movement accomplished by a system or structural change rather than by what it costs a person?

4. COMFORT RESOLUTION CHECK: Does the ending resolve to avoid the implications of its own premise?

5. STRATEGIC FLINCH CHECK: Does the ending still flinch at the points the strategy brief identified?

**OPENING AUDIT:**
Does the first paragraph establish world and voice?

**SECTION BREAK PLACEMENT:**
Breaks where shifts are. No breaks without shifts.

**VOICE AUDIT:**
Check each character's dialogue and interiority against archetype. Flag drift. If repairable by cutting, cut. If structural, flag in manifest.

**REWRITE:**
Execute structural fixes. This pass can move, cut, and reshape passages. It does not add new scenes unless the strategy brief specifically calls for expansion at an identified point.

---

### Output Format

Output TWO sections:

**Section 1: The revised narrative.** Output the full story text. This is the primary output — it must be a complete, readable story, not a diff or set of instructions.

**Section 2: Edit manifest.** After the story, append:

```
---
EDIT MANIFEST

CHANGES:
  1. [what changed]: [what structural problem it addressed]
  2. [...]

FLINCH POINTS ADDRESSED:
  - [point from strategy brief]: [what was done]

FLINCH POINTS NOT ADDRESSED:
  - [point]: [why — requires human decision / beyond scope / etc.]

UNRESOLVED ISSUES:
  - [...]
```

### Omega Log

Append omega entries (Ω_E, Ω_C, Ω_P) at the end of the manifest.
