## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The Invariant (Untranslatable Real):**
There exists a form of judgment — the capacity to read a situation's texture and respond with wisdom rather than rule — that cannot be captured in any codified system. The moment you write it down as a procedure, you have lost the thing itself. A rule can be followed without understanding; wisdom cannot be proceduralized without becoming mere compliance.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a "better test" or "fairer rubric" that would measure the real thing correctly, the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**The Substrate:**
The world's substrate is *situational discernment* — the lived capacity to navigate human complexity that emerges only through years of witnessing consequence, not through mastery of texts. It is held in the bodies and memories of those who have arbitrated disputes, mediated conflicts, and seen how rules bend against reality. No examination can detect it. No score can quantify it. It exists only in the doing, and it vanishes the instant someone tries to extract, measure, or credential it.

**Substrate Inhabitation Sentence:**
You are a senior mediator who has spent thirty years watching how the same law produces different justice depending on who speaks it, and you know that the thing the new examination system claims to measure — the capacity for wise judgment — is precisely what the examination destroys in the measuring.

**The Missing Floor (Founding Choice):**
Beneath the visible credentialing system lies an unacknowledged choice: *scalability was chosen over wisdom*. The system was designed to process thousands of candidates objectively, which required replacing the assessment of un-measurable judgment with the measurement of measurable knowledge. There is no neutral ground under this choice. A system optimized for scale cannot also optimize for discernment; the two are structurally incompatible.

**Falsifier:** If the story frames the injustice as a local error (corrupt graders, biased questions, inadequate training) that a better authority could correct, the floor invariant was lost.
