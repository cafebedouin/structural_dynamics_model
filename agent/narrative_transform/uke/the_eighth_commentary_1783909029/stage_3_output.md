# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Stage 1 Selection:** Negotiated Equilibrium

**Verification:**
- **Constraint profile:** Tangled Rope dominant (C₁ for X₁, C₂ for X₂, C₃ for X₂), Snares present but not dominant (C₂ for X₁, C₃ for X₅)
- **Rationality model:** BIR (satisficing, risk aversion, principal-agent conflicts)
- **Transformation rules:** All blocked by network structure
- **Attractor Compatibility Matrix:** Tangled Rope + BIR → Negotiated Equilibrium ✓

**ATTRACTOR CONFIRMED:** Negotiated Equilibrium

The system persists in hybrid state. Mae Siri continues implementing flawed sorting mechanism. Nok either integrates into system (if passes) or exits without rupture (if fails). Phra Wimon maintains belief in textual objectivity. The Division continues extracting from Lung Daeng while credentialing Nai Seng. No revolutionary rupture, no deterministic collapse — bounded extraction stabilizes through beneficiary class resistance to reform.

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected:** The Beneficiary (primary) + The Condemned (counterpoint)

**Justification:**

This pairing serves the Break Contract ("understand the system's inhuman function, not expose corruption") through structural blindness rather than moral revelation.

**Primary Voice: Phra Wimon (X₃) as The Beneficiary**
- **Structural fit:** Institutional position (π = -0.2), experiences all three constraints as Rope/coordination, genuinely cannot see extraction from this index
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Equilibrium (Beneficiary maintains system through sincere belief in its fairness)
- **Power range:** π ≤ 0.4 ✓ (institutional position)
- **False CI_rope signature:** All constraints read as coordination from X₃ index ✓
- **Tone:** Warm, measured, pedagogical — Phra Wimon believes he is protecting candidates from favoritism
- **Dark comedy mechanism:** Reader sees extraction (Nok's three-year preparation, Lung Daeng's debt spiral); Phra Wimon sees fair sorting. The gap generates discomfort without authorial judgment.

**Why this works for the invariant:** Phra Wimon has never mediated a dispute. He scores textual fidelity because that's what the examination measures. From his position, the system IS working — it produces ranked candidates objectively. He cannot see that the thing being measured (text-memory) is not the thing the position requires (situational wisdom). His blindness is structural, not moral. He is kind, competent, and completely wrong about what the system does.

**Counterpoint Voice: Nok (X₁) as The Condemned**
- **Structural fit:** Powerless position (π = 1.5), experiences C₂ as Snare (χ = 0.98), trapped exit
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Tragedy (from reader position), ✓ Equilibrium (Nok does not rupture system)
- **Tone:** Immediate, experiential, somber
- **Function:** Provides the physical experience Phra Wimon cannot see. Not a rebuttal voice (Nok never argues with the system), but an experiential record. The reader performs the extraction math by holding both perspectives simultaneously.

**Voice combination strategy:** Counterpoint Voice. Phra Wimon is primary narrator (60% of scenes), Nok is secondary (40%). They never meet. Phra Wimon scores Nok's examination without knowing his name. The reader sees both: Phra Wimon's sincere belief in textual objectivity, Nok's three years of memorization in a language his father doesn't speak. Neither voice explains the gap. The gap IS the story.

**Alternative considered and rejected:**
- **The Witness (Mae Siri):** Would see both extraction and coordination, making the system's dysfunction visible within the text. This serves exposition, not the Break Contract. The reader must discover the gap, not have it explained.
- **The System:** Would produce cold procedural horror but lose the human warmth that makes Phra Wimon's blindness devastating. The Beneficiary's kindness is structural necessity — if he were cruel, the reader could dismiss him as corrupt rather than recognize him as structurally blind.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected:** Multi-POV Parallel (Beneficiary/Condemned counterpoint)

**Structure:**
- Alternate between Phra Wimon (scoring examinations in Nakhon Pathom) and Nok (preparing for/taking examination)
- Same temporal arc: the three days of the *kan song* plus preparation/aftermath
- Reader sees indexical variance before characters do
- No analytical observer — no character explicitly names the gap

**Revelation mechanics:**
- **Act 1:** Establish both positions. Phra Wimon explains examination fairness (blind scoring, textual objectivity). Nok experiences preparation burden (borrowing texts, memorizing in candlelight, poling sampan to capital).
- **Act 2:** Examination itself. Phra Wimon scores responses, noting citation accuracy and logical structure. Nok writes responses, citing texts he barely understands, hoping examiners accept citation without comprehension.
- **Act 3:** Results and aftermath. Phra Wimon posts scores, satisfied with objective process. Nok sees red ink (pass or fail — Stage 4 decides), experiences outcome as natural/inevitable.

**Critical constraint:** Neither voice performs realization. Phra Wimon never doubts the system. Nok never articulates that the examination measures the wrong thing. The reader must hold both truths: the system is fair (from Phra Wimon's index) AND extractive (from Nok's index). Both are correct. The collision is the point.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Short story (4,000–5,000 words)
- Rationale: Dual POV requires space for both voices to establish credibility. Flash would force exposition. Novelette would require subplot development that dilutes the core collision.

**POV:** 
- **Phra Wimon:** Third person limited
- **Nok:** First person

**Rationale for POV split:**
- Third limited for Phra Wimon creates slight distance, allowing reader to see his blindness without the text pointing at it. "He believed the system was fair" reads differently than "I believe the system is fair" — the former permits reader doubt without narrator doubt.
- First person for Nok creates immediacy and prevents analytical distance. Nok cannot explain his own experience in systemic terms; he can only report what happens. "I memorized words I barely understood" is experiential record, not analysis.

**TENSE:** Past tense (both voices)
- Rationale: Present tense would create false urgency. Past tense allows both voices to report completed events without suggesting either has gained perspective. Neither voice is retrospective/analytical — both report the past as if it were natural.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 2 primary (Phra Wimon, Nok), 3 secondary (Mae Siri, Lung Daeng, one other examiner)
- Rationale: Mae Siri appears briefly in Phra Wimon's sections (she delivers examination materials, discusses scoring rubrics). Lung Daeng appears briefly in Nok's sections (Nok's father, provides material grounding for boat-dwelling poverty). Third examiner (Phra Somchai) provides Phra Wimon someone to voice his beliefs to without seeming like internal monologue.

**NAMING:** Cultural (Thai naming conventions from Stage 2)
- Phra Wimon (title + given name)
- Nok (nickname only — given name never used, signals informality/poverty)
- Mae Siri (title + given name)
- Lung Daeng (kinship term + nickname)

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

Code-switching occurs in Nok's sections only:
- **When Nok speaks to his father (Lung Daeng):** Shift to *phasa khlong* (Khmer-Thai creole). Signal through syntax simplification and kinship terms. Example: "Lung, the nets?" vs. formal "Father, shall I check the fishing nets?"
- **When Nok reads the Texts:** Shift to *phasa nak* (formal Pali-Thai). Signal through complex sentence structure and archaic vocabulary. Example: "Natural disaster does not absolve contractual obligation" (Text citation) vs. "The flood took the catch" (Nok's voice).
- **What the switch signals:** The gap between Nok's lived language and the examination's required language. The Texts speak a language Nok's father doesn't speak. This is not cultural color — this is the constraint's mechanism.

Phra Wimon's sections: No code-switching. He speaks exclusively in formal register (rendered in standard English prose with slight formality). He has no colloquial register because he has no informal relationships in the text.

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** 
Not imposed — emerges from constraint dynamics:
- Phra Wimon: Satisfaction in objective fairness, mild pride in protecting candidates from bias
- Nok: Exhaustion, hope, resignation (depending on outcome — Stage 4 decides)
- Reader: Discomfort from holding both truths simultaneously

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Attractor reached (Negotiated Equilibrium)

Two possible endings (Stage 4 chooses):

**Option A (Nok passes):**
- Phra Wimon posts scores, satisfied with fair process
- Nok sees his name in red ink, feels relief/disbelief
- Final scene: Nok reports to Mae Siri for training, begins learning that text-memory ≠ judgment
- Equilibrium: Nok integrates into system, will become another Mae Siri (implementing flawed mechanism)

**Option B (Nok fails):**
- Phra Wimon posts scores, satisfied with fair process
- Nok sees red ink without his name, poles sampan home
- Final scene: Lung Daeng asks if Nok will try again next year; Nok does not answer
- Equilibrium: System persists, Nok exits without rupture, another candidate will take his place

Both endings serve Negotiated Equilibrium. Neither ruptures the system. Neither exposes corruption (there is no corruption — the system works exactly as designed). The reader understands the system's inhuman function: it measures text-memory and calls it merit, and this is structurally blind to the thing the position actually requires.

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

**Phra Wimon (The Beneficiary):**

*Phenomenological register:* Phra Wimon notices order, pattern, procedure. He pays attention to textual fidelity (citation accuracy, logical structure) and to the fairness of process (blind scoring, standardized rubrics). He does not notice bodies, exhaustion, or material conditions. When he thinks about candidates, he thinks about their responses, not their circumstances.

*What kind of noticing does this character do?*
- Notices: Handwriting quality (indicates education level), citation precision, logical coherence
- Does not notice: Ink quality (cheap vs. expensive), paper condition (borrowed vs. owned texts), hand tremors (exhaustion, hunger)

*What does the constraint feel like in his body?*
Nothing. The constraint does not touch him. He experiences the examination as intellectual work (reading, scoring, discussing rubrics with other examiners). His body is comfortable: he sits in a temple hall with good light, eats temple meals, sleeps on a proper mat. The examination is his job, not his ordeal.

**Nok (The Condemned):**

*Phenomenological register:* Nok notices material conditions, physical sensations, and the gap between his language and the Texts' language. He pays attention to what his body can and cannot do (how long he can study before his eyes blur, how much rice he can eat before guilt about family resources stops him). He does not notice systemic patterns — he experiences the constraint as natural/inevitable.

*What kind of noticing does this character do?*
- Notices: Candle burn rate (study time remaining), hand cramps (hours of copying), hunger (temple charity portions), the weight of borrowed texts (physical and metaphorical)
- Does not notice: That the examination measures the wrong thing, that other candidates have tutors, that the system is constructed rather than natural

*What does the constraint feel like in his body?*
- **Preparation:** Eye strain from candlelight reading, hand cramps from copying texts, hunger from skipped meals (saving money for examination fees), exhaustion from poling sampan while memorizing
- **Examination:** Hand tremor from three days of writing, mental fog from sleep deprivation, nausea from performance anxiety, the physical weight of sitting still in formal posture for hours
- **Aftermath (if fails):** Numbness in arms from poling home, the specific ache of wasted time (three years of preparation, gone), the hollow feeling of having nothing to show

*What has the body learned to do that the mind hasn't named?*
Nok's hand has learned to form the characters of *phasa nak* even when his mind doesn't fully understand the words. His body has learned the rhythm of memorization (read, recite, copy, repeat) without his mind grasping that this rhythm is not the same as understanding. His eyes have learned to scan for citation markers in the Texts without his mind recognizing that citation is not wisdom.
