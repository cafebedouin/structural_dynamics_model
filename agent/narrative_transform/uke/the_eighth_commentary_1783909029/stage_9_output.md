REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The story earns its central recognition through dramatic accumulation rather than assertion. Mae Siri's specific cases (fishing village borrowing at impossible rates, rice seller losing the boat) provide concrete evidence that enables Phra Wimon's final understanding. The parallel structure—Nok's examination failure intercut with Phra Wimon's scoring process—creates productive tension between what the system measures and what it misses. The prose maintains restraint throughout; the climactic insight in Section VII arrives as earned conclusion rather than thematic declaration.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
Section VII's expansion, while necessary to dramatize the recognition, occasionally drifts toward over-explanation. The passage "To make the examination adequate, they would have to make it unfair. To keep it fair, they had to accept its inadequacy" states what the preceding evidence has already shown. The section would be stronger if it trusted the reader to arrive at this conclusion from Phra Wimon's thought process alone, without the narrator crystallizing it into explicit formulation.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story contains no "better test" or "fairer rubric." Phra Wimon's final recognition is that fairness and adequacy are structurally incompatible—the examination's objectivity (what makes it fair) is precisely what prevents it from measuring wisdom (what would make it adequate). The real (situational discernment) remains unreadable to any codified system. The story ends with Phra Wimon preparing next year's examination not because he's found a solution but because he must endure the constraint.

BREAK:
The story violates the expectation that understanding a system's flaws leads to reform or resistance. Phra Wimon achieves full recognition of the examination's incommensurability—it produces administrators who "cite the Texts while villages starve"—yet continues preparing the next examination because "the alternative was worse." This meets the contract's target_prior: the examination's stated purpose (selecting wise administrators) differs from its real function (producing scalable objectivity), and this function is revealed through the behaviors it selects for (citation accuracy, textual precision) rather than the values it claims (justice, wisdom).

READINESS:
The story is structurally sound and thematically coherent, with one localized weakness in Section VII where the narrator over-articulates what the drama has already established. This does not compromise the story's integrity but represents a minor loss of restraint at the climactic moment.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
