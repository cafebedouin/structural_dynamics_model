# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected: ☑ Deterministic Tragedy**

**Justification:**
The constraint topology locks into tragedy through three structural features:

1. **C₆ (Organization Barrier) blocks the only high-energy transformation (TR₁)** that could prevent collapse. The collective solution exists theoretically but cannot actualize because the structure preventing organization is self-reinforcing.

2. **TR₂ (Resource Exhaustion) is the default trajectory.** Without collective action, individual extraction continues → resource depletion accelerates → extraction intensifies → hands fail faster. This is deterministic given the blocked transformation.

3. **The rationality model is PIR (Powerless Individual Rational).** Wei acts rationally given his index (meet quota or starve, borrow or default, stay or abandon family investment). His rational choices compound the trap. Chen acts rationally given his index (enforce standards that worked for him, trust institutional wisdom). His rational choices maintain the system. Both are correct from their positions. The tragedy emerges from rational action under constraint, not from error.

**Attractor compatibility check:**
- PIR model: ✓ (Wei and Chen both act rationally within their indices)
- Snare-dominant topology: ✓ (C₁ and C₆ are Snares for Wei; C₅ False Mountain)
- Blocked transformation: ✓ (TR₁ requires overcoming C₆, which is structurally impossible)
- No deus ex machina available: ✓ (Board won't reform, paper crisis won't resolve, hands won't heal)

**Why not other attractors:**
- **Negotiated Equilibrium:** Requires BIR model and negotiation capacity. Wei has neither.
- **Revolutionary Rupture:** Requires collective action, which C₆ prevents.
- **Seeded Possibility:** Would require showing how organization becomes possible. The topology shows it doesn't.

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Primary Voice: ☑ The Condemned (Wei)**

**Justification:**
Wei is inside the constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape, acts rationally given limited information and structural position. He experiences the quota system as natural/inevitable, not constructed. His voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective. He knows his hands are failing. He knows the debt is compounding. He doesn't know (cannot see from his index) that the system is designed to produce this outcome, that Chen's "fair meritocracy" is false CI_rope signature, that the collective could change everything.

**Voice properties:**
- **Tone:** Tragic, somber, with moments of dark humor (Wei's jokes about "Wei-style calligraphy")
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Deterministic Tragedy (Condemned voice natural for tragedy)
- **Rationality model:** ✓ PIR (Wei's limited information and powerless position define the voice)
- **POV:** First person (Stage 4 decision, but strongly recommended — immediacy essential)
- **Temporal register:** Present tense for copying scenes (trapped in the moment), past tense for reflection (rare, brief)
- **Analytical ceiling:** Wei can describe what happens. He cannot explain why the system produces it. He can count (debt, quota, days). He cannot theorize (extraction, indexical variance, coordination failure).

**Secondary Voice: ☑ The Beneficiary (Chen) — Counterpoint structure**

**Justification:**
Chen genuinely believes the system is fair. From his index (moderate power, survived the Long Hall, promoted through merit), the constraint reads as coordination: the quota system filters for discipline, the wall examination teaches precision, the paper crisis is being "monitored." He is likeable, reasonable, kind even — he offers loans at "fair" interest, he remembers his own struggles, he believes he's helping. The reader sees what Chen cannot: that his comfort depends on Wei's extraction, that his "merit" was partly luck (stronger hands, family support, promotion timing), that his enforcement maintains the Snare.

**Voice properties:**
- **Tone:** Warm, assured, pedagogical
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Tragedy (from reader's position — Chen's blindness is part of the tragedy)
- **Rationality model:** ✓ PIR (Chen acts rationally given his index; his blindness is structural, not stupidity)
- **POV:** Third limited (Stage 4 decision, but recommended — distance allows reader to see around Chen's blindness without Chen becoming self-aware)
- **Structural blindness:** Chen cannot see that the quota system extracts rather than selects. He cannot see that his position depends on others' failure. If he becomes aware, the voice collapses.

**Voice Combination Strategy: Counterpoint Voice**

Wei (first person, present tense, trapped in sensation) alternates with Chen (third limited, past tense, comfortable in explanation). The reader performs the extraction math. Neither narrator can.

**Critical directive for Stage 4:**
- Wei never explains the system. He experiences it.
- Chen never doubts the system. He trusts it.
- The gap between their experiences is the story. Don't close it.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: ☑ Multi-POV Parallel (with late structural recontextualization)**

**Structure:**
- **Part 1 (Chapters 1-4):** Alternate Wei/Chen POVs. Reader sees indexical variance through parallel experience of same events:
  - Ch 1: Wei's copying day (quota pressure, hand pain, wall examination fear)
  - Ch 2: Chen's supervision day (quality control, loan management, Board meeting)
  - Ch 3: Wei's evening (debt calculation, Feng conversation, boarding house)
  - Ch 4: Chen's evening (family dinner, reflection on his own Long Hall years)

- **Part 2 (Chapters 5-7):** Intensification. Wei's hands worsen. Paper crisis becomes visible. Feng covers Wei's quota more frequently. Chen notices quality decline, reports to Board, receives non-response.

- **Part 3 (Chapters 8-9):** Convergence toward tragedy. Wei attempts to organize (tests C₆), fails, is marked. Chen enforces consequences (assigns impossible quota) believing he's maintaining standards. Feng's hands fail completely covering Wei's shortfall.

- **Coda (Chapter 10):** Wei alone. Feng dismissed (or dead — Stage 4 decision). Wei still copying. Hands worse. Debt higher. Wall examination tomorrow. The system persists. Wei persists within it. No resolution, only continuation.

**Revelation mechanics:**
- Reader sees indexical variance from Chapter 1 (Wei experiences quota as Snare, Chen experiences it as fair filter)
- Reader sees False Mountain (C₅ paper crisis) by Chapter 6 (Chen trusts Board, Wei sees degradation, reader knows collective action could solve it)
- Reader sees coordination failure (C₆) by Chapter 8 (Wei's organization attempt fails structurally, not because he's incompetent)
- Characters never see what reader sees. Wei dies (or continues) without understanding the system. Chen dies (or continues) without doubting it.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** ☑ Novelette (12,000-15,000 words)
- Rationale: Need space for both POVs, constraint activation across multiple scenes, slow degradation arc. Too short and the tragedy feels rushed. Too long and the repetition (copying, quota, debt) becomes tedious rather than oppressive.

**POV:** 
- Wei: ☑ First person, present tense
- Chen: ☑ Third limited, past tense
- Rationale: Tense shift marks indexical difference. Wei trapped in present moment. Chen comfortable in reflection.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 primary (Wei, Chen, Feng) + collective as background presence
- Rationale: Wei/Chen provide indexical contrast. Feng provides Rope (C₃) and its dissolution (TR₃). Collective remains potential, never actualized (C₆ demonstration).

**NAMING:** ☑ Cultural (Chinese names, historically appropriate)
- Wei (魏) — common surname, means "tall/lofty" (ironic given his powerless position)
- Chen (陳) — common surname, means "to display/arrange" (fitting for supervisor)
- Feng (馮) — common surname, means "to gallop" (ironic given his immobility)
- Given names to be determined in Stage 4 based on character depth needs

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**
- **Code-switching occurs:** 
  - In dialogue (Wei/Feng speak Mandarin, Chen switches between Mandarin and Literary Chinese, Board notices are pure Literary Chinese)
  - In Wei's internal monologue (Mandarin base, occasional Literary Chinese phrases he's memorized but doesn't fully understand)
  - In Chen's internal monologue (fluid switching, Literary Chinese for institutional thoughts, Mandarin for personal thoughts)

- **The switch signals:**
  - Power (who can deploy Literary Chinese fluently)
  - Intimacy (Mandarin = private speech, Literary Chinese = institutional speech)
  - Alienation (Wei's inability to speak Literary Chinese marks his exclusion from institutional authority)

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** 
Emerges from constraint dynamics:
- Wei's love for Feng (C₃ Rope) makes leaving impossible
- Chen's genuine belief in fairness (false CI_rope signature) makes him enforce extraction without guilt
- The collective's latent power (C₆ barrier) makes the tragedy avoidable in theory, inevitable in practice

**ENDING STRATEGY:** ☑ System persists, agent removed (or broken)
- Feng dismissed/dead (TR₃ — mentorship bond dissolved by external pressure)
- Wei still copying, hands worse, debt higher, alone
- Chen still supervising, still believing the system works
- The archive continues. The quota continues. The tragedy continues.

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

**Wei (The Condemned):**

*Phenomenological register:* Hypervigilance to bodily failure + material counting obsession

Wei's attention is split between his hands and his numbers. He notices:
- The tremor that starts in his right thumb after the third hour of copying
- The way cold makes the brush feel like holding a stone
- The specific quality of pain (sharp in the knuckles, dull in the wrist, burning in the forearm)
- The exact count (folios completed, folios remaining, days behind on quota, copper coins owed, days until next loan payment)

He does not notice:
- The beauty of the characters he's copying
- The content of the documents (he copies without reading)
- The other clerks' faces (except Feng's)
- The passage of seasons (except as they affect his hands)

**Physical markers:**
- Ink stains on his right hand that never fully wash out
- The way he flexes his fingers unconsciously (testing for stiffness)
- The specific angle he holds the brush (compensating for tremor)
- The taste of cheap tea (all he can afford) that he associates with morning quota calculation

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ This is not a metric (not "pain level 7/10"). This is attention mode. Wei lives in his hands and his numbers because those are the only things he can control, and he's losing control of both.

---

**Chen (The Beneficiary):**

*Phenomenological register:* Comfort-based noticing + pedagogical framing

Chen's attention is drawn to signs of order and disorder. He notices:
- Which clerks arrive early (discipline)
- Which clerks' posture degrades over the day (lack of training)
- The quality of brush strokes (technical precision)
- The temperature of his office (comfortable, unlike the Long Hall)

He does not notice:
- The specific pain in clerks' hands (he remembers pain abstractly, not sensorially)
- The mathematical impossibility of some quotas (he trusts the Board's calculations)
- The fear in clerks' faces during wall examination (he sees pedagogical opportunity)
- The way his comfort depends on others' extraction (structural blindness)

**Physical markers:**
- The smooth motion of his own brush (hands still strong, well-maintained)
- The warmth of his office brazier in winter
- The weight of his supervisor's seal (institutional authority made tangible)
- The taste of good tea (he can afford quality)

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ This is not a metric. This is a mode of attention that notices comfort and interprets discomfort as pedagogical opportunity. Chen's body is comfortable, so he experiences the system as comfortable.

---

**Feng:**

*Phenomenological register:* Pain-as-background + protective attention toward Wei

Feng's attention is split between managing his own pain and monitoring Wei's state. He notices:
- The specific angle that makes his arthritis bearable
- The moment when Wei's tremor starts (earlier each week)
- The quality of silence (Wei's silence when calculating debt vs. Wei's silence when defeated)
- The weight of the brush (heavier each year)

He does not notice:
- The content of documents (copying is pure muscle memory now)
- His own future (he lives in the present, with Wei)
- The possibility of reform (too old, too tired, too invested in Wei's survival)

**Physical markers:**
- The way he holds the brush (three-finger grip, compensating for joint damage)
- The sound of his joints (audible clicks when he flexes his hands)
- The specific kind of cold that makes copying impossible (damp cold, not dry cold)
- The taste of the pain medicine (bitter, expensive, barely effective)

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ This is not a metric. This is attention mode shaped by chronic pain and love. Feng's body is failing, but his attention is on Wei's body, which is failing faster.
