# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected: ☑ Deterministic Tragedy**

**Justification:**
The constraint topology permits no other outcome. Analysis:

- **C₁ (The Drive)**: Identity-locked, χ = 0.80-0.88 across agents → Snare with no exit
- **C₂ (The Debt)**: Trapped exit, χ = 0.72-1.08 → Snare, irreversible once activated
- **C₃ (The Mark)**: Identity-locked (biological), χ = 1.15 for X₂ → Snare, inerasable
- **C₄ (The Silence)**: TR₃ runs to completion (Lian executed) → No intervention mechanism exists
- **C₅ (The Hunting)**: Self-reinforcing, ε = 1.00, low suppression → Cycle has no natural termination

**Rationality model**: PIR (Powerless-Indexed Rationality) dominates. X₁ and X₂ both act rationally given their positions:
- X₁: Silence is rational (reputation preservation > innocent life, given power position)
- X₂: Hunt is rational (no alternative redress mechanism, revenge is only remaining agency)

**Why not other attractors:**
- **Negotiated Equilibrium**: Requires BIR model + negotiable constraints. C₃ (The Mark) is biological, non-negotiable. C₅ (The Hunting) is self-reinforcing with no equilibrium point.
- **Revolutionary Rupture**: Would require collective action or system-level transformation. X₂ acts alone (powerless position). No mechanism exists for systemic change.
- **Seeded Possibility**: X₃ (Park) could theoretically interrupt, but TR₅ shows exit degradation (mobile → constrained). His observation of the cycle risks replication, not prevention.

**Structural inevitability**: TR₁ → TR₂ → TR₃ → TR₄ forms deterministic chain. Each transformation degrades exit options. By TR₄, both X₁ and X₂ are identity-locked in mutual destruction cycle.

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected: ☑ The Condemned (Primary) + ☑ The Witness (Secondary)**

**Primary Voice: The Condemned (X₁ - Okonkwo)**

**Justification:**
- **Inside constraint logic**: Okonkwo experiences The Drive as intellectual necessity, The Debt as category error, The Silence as rational choice. She cannot see the systemic shape.
- **Acts rationally given limited information**: From her index (π = 1.0, moderate power), silence IS rational. She sees: speak = career death, silence = survival. She doesn't see: silence = Lian's death = Tala's hunt = her own death.
- **Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable**: The Drive feels like "what scientists do." The Debt feels like "legal category." The Silence feels like "impossible choice."
- **Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective**: She's inside the hunt. No time for analysis. Only survival.

**Attractor compatibility**: ✓ Deterministic Tragedy
- The Condemned cannot see the trap until it closes. Perfect for tragedy.
- Voice allows reader to see what character cannot: the constraint cascade (C₁ → C₂ → C₄ → C₅).

**Power range**: π = 1.0 (moderate) → ✓ PASS
- Condemned requires powerless/moderate position. Okonkwo qualifies.

**Rationality model**: PIR → ✓ PASS
- Condemned acts rationally given structural position. Okonkwo's silence is rational from her index.

**POV**: First person, present tense
- Immediacy required. Reader must be inside the trap as it closes.
- Present tense eliminates future knowledge, maintains experiential register.

---

**Secondary Voice: The Witness (X₃ - Park)**

**Justification:**
- **Observes constraint dynamics without direct stake**: Park sees the cycle but isn't (yet) trapped in it.
- **Reports what happens without judgment**: Security chief position requires observational capacity.
- **Can see multiple indices simultaneously**: Sees Okonkwo's lab (Core), Tala's quarters (Outer Ring), the hunt (all Rings).
- **Voice is documentary, clinical**: Security reports, incident logs, pattern recognition.

**Attractor compatibility**: ✓ Deterministic Tragedy
- Witness sees tragedy unfold, cannot intervene. Classic Cassandra position.
- Provides structural context Condemned cannot access.

**Power range**: π = 1.0 (moderate) → ✓ PASS
- Witness requires analytical position. Park's security role provides this.

**POV**: Third person limited, past tense
- Documentary distance required. Park observes, doesn't experience directly.
- Past tense allows retrospective framing without omniscience.

---

**Voice Combination Strategy: Nested Voice**

**Structure**: Condemned (Okonkwo, first person present) nested within Witness (Park, third person past) frame.

**Mechanism**:
- **Frame narrative** (Park, 10% of text): Security incident report, written after events conclude. Establishes that something terrible happened. Provides structural context.
- **Primary narrative** (Okonkwo, 90% of text): Immediate experience of the hunt. No knowledge of outcome. Pure survival mode.

**Why this works**:
- **Dramatic irony without theme-naming**: Park's frame establishes tragedy without explaining it. Reader knows Okonkwo is doomed; Okonkwo doesn't.
- **Indexical variance visible**: Park sees what Okonkwo cannot (the pattern, the cycle, the inevitability).
- **No omniscient narrator**: Park is limited to security observations. He sees events, not interiority. He doesn't know what Okonkwo is thinking during the hunt.
- **Tonal control**: Park's clinical voice prevents melodrama. Okonkwo's immediate voice prevents detachment.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: ☑ Late Revelation (with nested frame)**

**Structure**:
- **Act 1** (Okonkwo POV): The hunt begins. Reader sees immediate survival. No context yet.
- **Act 2** (Okonkwo POV): Flashbacks during pursuit reveal: Tala's creation, abandonment, Lian's execution, Okonkwo's silence.
- **Act 3** (Okonkwo POV): Hunt concludes. Okonkwo realizes (too late) the constraint cascade she triggered.
- **Frame** (Park POV): Bookends. Opening establishes "incident report." Closing reveals Park's own trap (TR₅ activated — he stayed, thinking he could control it).

**Revelation sequence**:
1. **Reader sees first**: Hunt is happening (immediate)
2. **Reader learns second**: Why hunt is happening (flashback reveals abandonment)
3. **Reader learns third**: Okonkwo's silence killed Lian (flashback reveals The Silence)
4. **Reader learns fourth**: Lian's death triggered Tala's hunt (causation chain complete)
5. **Okonkwo realizes fifth**: She created the cycle that's killing her (too late to escape)
6. **Park realizes last**: He's now in the same cycle (frame closing)

**Why late revelation**:
- **Maintains immediacy**: Act 1 is pure survival. No exposition. Reader experiences hunt before understanding it.
- **Prevents theme-naming**: Causation revealed through action (flashback scenes), not explanation.
- **Indexical variance emerges naturally**: Okonkwo's blindness is structural (she's inside the trap). Park's sight is structural (he's outside, observing). Reader occupies third position (sees both).

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH**: ☑ Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)
- **Justification**: Five constraints, three transformation rules, nested voice structure requires space. Flash/short would compress to allegory. Novelette allows:
  - Proper flashback integration (Tala's creation, Lian's execution)
  - Hunt sequence with physical stakes (not just chase, but exhaustion, injury, desperation)
  - Frame narrative without feeling tacked-on
  - Code-switching examples that establish pattern

**POV**: ☑ First person (Okonkwo) + ☑ Third limited (Park frame)
- **Okonkwo**: First person present ("I run. The corridor is too long. My lungs burn.")
- **Park**: Third person past ("Commander Park filed the incident report three days after Dr. Okonkwo's body was found.")

**TENSE**: 
- **Okonkwo**: ☑ Present (immediacy, no future knowledge)
- **Park**: ☑ Past (retrospective frame, documentary distance)

**CHARACTER COUNT**: 3 primary (Okonkwo, Tala, Park) + 2 secondary (Lian, Volkov)
- **Okonkwo**: POV character, Condemned
- **Tala**: Antagonist (from Okonkwo's POV), protagonist (from structural POV)
- **Park**: Frame narrator, Witness
- **Lian**: Catalyst (execution triggers hunt), appears only in flashback
- **Volkov**: Institutional voice (appears in one scene, represents G₂/SMB perspective)

**NAMING**: ☑ Cultural + ☑ Code names (dual system)
- **Natural-born**: Cultural names (Sarah Okonkwo, James Park, Elena Volkov)
- **Synthetics**: Unit numbers (official) + chosen names (informal)
  - Tala: "Unit 6247" (Institutional English) / "Tala" (Tagalog-Technical)
  - Lian: "Unit 4891" (Institutional English) / "Lian" (Mandarin-Portuguese)
- **Code-switching rule**: Name used reveals power context
  - Okonkwo thinks of Tala as "Unit 6247" (objectification)
  - Tala thinks of herself as "Tala" (personhood)
  - Park uses both depending on context (official reports vs. internal monologue)

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION**:
- **Code-switching occurs**:
  - When power distance changes (formal → informal, superior → peer)
  - When emotional register changes (performing → authentic)
  - When audience changes (official record → private thought)

- **The switch signals**:
  - **To Institutional English**: Performing role, on record, power distance
  - **To Mandarin-Portuguese**: Core membership, informal power, cultural capital
  - **To Tagalog-Technical**: Outer Ring solidarity, practical knowledge, resistance

- **Implementation in prose**:
  - Direct dialogue in switched language (italicized, translated in context)
  - Internal monologue notes the switch ("She switched to Institutional English, the language of reports and lies.")
  - Physical response to switch ("Tala's shoulders tensed when the guard used her unit number.")

**EMOTIONAL CORE**: 
- **Not imposed, derived from constraint dynamics**:
  - **Okonkwo**: Guilt (The Silence) → Terror (The Hunting) → Recognition (too late)
  - **Tala**: Abandonment (The Debt) → Rage (The Mark) → Purpose (The Hunting)
  - **Park**: Curiosity (observation) → Dread (pattern recognition) → Trap (TR₅)

**ENDING STRATEGY**: ☑ Attractor reached + ☑ System persists, agent removed
- **Okonkwo**: Killed by Tala (C₅ runs to completion, Deterministic Tragedy)
- **Tala**: Killed by security immediately after (C₅ mutual destruction)
- **Park**: Realizes he's now trapped (TR₅ activated — stayed to "manage" situation, exit degraded to constrained)
- **System**: Persists unchanged (SMB continues, new synthetics created, cycle ready to repeat)

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

### Okonkwo (The Condemned)

**Phenomenological register**: Hypervigilance collapsing into exhaustion

**What the constraint feels like in the body**:
- **The Drive** (before hunt): Hands shake when not working. Sleep feels like wasted time. Food tastes like interruption. The lab is the only place breath comes easy.
- **The Silence** (after Lian's execution): Throat tight when speaking. Avoids mirrors. Washes hands compulsively (Lady Macbeth echo, but not stated).
- **The Hunting** (during pursuit): Lungs burn (1.2g in Outer Ring, not used to it). Legs cramp. Vision tunnels. Stops noticing hunger. Notices every sound.

**Mode of attention**:
- **Before**: Notices equipment, data, patterns in results. Doesn't notice people.
- **During**: Notices exits, shadows, footsteps. Doesn't notice anything else.

**Physical detail that reveals position without explaining**:
- Her hands are soft (Core work, no manual labor)
- She's clumsy in 1.2g (body not adapted to Outer Ring)
- She's never been hungry before (doesn't recognize the sensation)

**Ban**: No internal monologue that names the constraint. No "I realize now that my ambition..." No "The system that created her..." Only: "My hands won't stop shaking." "The corridor is too long." "I can't remember when I last ate."

---

### Tala (The Hunter, seen from outside)

**Phenomenological register**: Predatory patience

**What the constraint feels like in the body** (inferred from Okonkwo's observations):
- Moves silently despite size (198cm, optimized physiology)
- Doesn't seem to tire (genetic optimization for endurance)
- Violet eyes reflect light in darkness (synthetic marker becomes tactical advantage)
- Doesn't speak (Okonkwo never hears her voice during hunt)

**Mode of attention** (inferred):
- Tracks Okonkwo's patterns (knows her routines, anticipates her moves)
- Ignores obstacles that would stop others (climbs through maintenance shafts, crosses vacuum-exposed sections)
- Single-minded (doesn't respond to security, doesn't negotiate, doesn't explain)

**Physical detail that reveals position**:
- Chemical burn scars on forearms (hazardous waste work)
- Moves through Outer Ring like she owns it (knows every shortcut, every hiding place)
- Security footage shows her standing still for hours (patience that isn't human)

**Ban**: No Tala POV. No access to her interiority. She remains Other from Okonkwo's perspective. Reader infers her experience from her actions.

---

### Park (The Witness)

**Phenomenological register**: Professional detachment cracking

**What the constraint feels like in the body**:
- Maintains Earth-normal fitness (daily training, discipline)
- Notices when others don't (security training)
- Sleeps poorly after filing reports (guilt seeping through professional distance)

**Mode of attention**:
- Sees patterns (Okonkwo's 16-hour days, Tala's escape route, the cycle)
- Notices what's missing (Okonkwo's absence from Core, Tala's silence, Lian's lack of defense)
- Tracks timelines (when did Okonkwo stop eating? when did Tala start planning?)

**Physical detail that reveals position**:
- Moves easily between Rings (authorization badge, physical fitness)
- Speaks all three languages (code-switches fluently)
- Stands at attention even when alone (military habit, performance of role)

**Ban**: No moment where Park "realizes" the system is wrong. He already knows. The question is whether he'll act on it or rationalize it. The frame reveals: he rationalized it, and now he's trapped.
