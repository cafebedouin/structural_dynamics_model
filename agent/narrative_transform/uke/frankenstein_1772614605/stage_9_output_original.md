# REVIEW ASSESSMENT

## STRENGTHS:

The story's architecture is ruthlessly effective—three perspectives that spiral inward toward the same moral trap, each voice distinct and necessary. The pacing shifts work: Okonkwo's breathless panic, Park's methodical investigation slowing under the weight of what he's discovering, Tala's cold precision during the killing that breaks into devastating clarity afterward. The ending refuses comfort without becoming nihilistic—Park sees everything, writes the complaint, deletes it, and his hands never stop shaking. That's the cost made visible: understanding changes nothing about what you do, but it destroys who you are.

## BIGGEST WEAKNESS:

The story needs more body. At 7,800 words it reads like a detailed outline of a novella rather than a complete work. The hunt section establishes Okonkwo's terror but never shows us what the work meant to her beyond compulsion—we need to see the Drive as something more complex than addiction. Tala and Lian's relationship exists in exposition; we need scenes of them together so the loss has weight beyond concept. Park's investigation is structurally sound but emotionally thin—we see him discovering the pattern but don't feel the accumulating dread. The world-building (Outer Ring vs Core, the station's class structure, the specific textures of life forty-two light-years from home) remains skeletal. This isn't a prose problem or a structural problem—it's a scope problem. The story is working at novella scale but written at novelette length.

## READINESS:

The story is structurally complete and thematically coherent, but it's operating significantly below its natural length. The skeleton is strong; the organs are missing.

## ROUTE: **STRATEGY**

The story needs expansion, not revision. The next strategy brief should focus on: (1) deepening Okonkwo's relationship to her work—show us what the Drive feels like from inside, what she's reaching for beyond just "I wanted to know if I could"; (2) dramatizing Tala and Lian's relationship through scenes rather than summary; (3) extending Park's investigation to let dread accumulate through daily operations, not just evidence review; (4) building the station's world through specific sensory details and social textures. Target length: 28,000-32,000 words. The current version is a strong 8,000-word proof of concept for a novella that wants to exist.
