# REVIEW ASSESSMENT

**STRENGTHS:**
The prose is alive at the physical level—the lattice resonance sequence delivers immediate sensory fact without cushioning or explanation. The story trusts its central discovery: constraint and capacity are the same thing, shown through body knowledge rather than stated. The pacing now matches function: investigation opens fast, choice settles slow, and the ending sits with cost rather than resolving it.

**BIGGEST WEAKNESS:**
The elder reference remains a single gesture without structural integration—it reads as deliberate restraint at this length, but the story hasn't earned that restraint because the elder never becomes more than a permission-granting device. Either develop the elder as a second point of contact with the substrate (making the protagonist's choice part of a lineage) or remove the reference entirely and let the discovery be fully solitary.

**READINESS:**
The story is close but not yet publishable—the elder problem is solvable in a single targeted pass.

**ROUTE:** STRATEGY

---

The biggest weakness is specific and addressable: the elder functions as narrative permission ("someone else did this first, so the protagonist can too") but never becomes structurally necessary. The rest of the prose is ready—no other significant weaknesses remain.
