**Confidence:** High

**Reasoning:**

The recommendation asks people to **abandon adjudication** while the structures being analyzed continue to produce material harm. This violates a Mountain: **moral agents cannot suspend judgment about ongoing injustice**.

The essay's own logic reveals the problem:

> "The historiographical struggle cannot be resolved by better evidence or clearer concepts... [but] structural clarity is a prerequisite for moral judgment that isn't self-deception."

1. If frameworks are incommensurable, structural clarity cannot exist (clarity requires commensurability)

**The deeper issue:** The essay treats "incommensurability" as a Mountain (natural constraint on knowledge) when it may be a Noose (power arrangement that benefits from irresolution).

- No framework to better predict outcomes
- No framework to better explain evidence
- No framework to better guide action

- Colonial framework better predicts Israeli policy (settlement expansion, transfer logic persistence)
- Liberation framework better explains Jewish historical experience (genocide, statelessness)
- **Both frameworks guide action** (international law uses colonial framework for occupation, uses liberation framework for Jewish self-determination)

This suggests the frameworks are **complementary** (each captures different aspects) rather than **incommensurable** (mutually exclusive). The essay's recommendation to abandon adjudication may itself be extraction - it serves analytical elites who benefit from perpetual debate while harming those who need actionable clarity.
