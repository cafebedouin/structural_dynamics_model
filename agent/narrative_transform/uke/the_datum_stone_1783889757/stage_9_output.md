REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The five-part structure lets the same injustice refract through five different registers of complicity — Maren's inherited numbness, Kell's helpless recitation, Tessa's clerical unease, Voss's untroubled self-regard — without any of them becoming a mouthpiece. The physical details (the rope worn smooth, the cut rope's "flat" sound versus a wheel's proper creak) do the emotional work instead of naming it, and Maren's final scene — glancing at the gauge-post instead of trusting her own hands — dramatizes the invariant's loss with real precision.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The Doyle exchange in Section V resolves too cleanly as a demonstration piece: Maren invokes a clause, Doyle confirms it, the wheel turns — the mechanism is almost too legible in its irony, laying out "this is what the Charter can and can't see" with slightly more explanatory neatness than the rest of the story allows itself. It doesn't break the invariant, but it's the one place the story's hand is most visible.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS — there is no recoverable correct schedule anywhere in the text. Maren's clause-victory gets her "this surge, this wheel-turn, and not one ounce more"; the stake's original placement is named as a purchased choice, not a mismeasurement, and the story explicitly refuses to supply the corrected version of either the Breath or the zero-point. The ending confirms this: no one restores the rope, no clause exists for what was actually lost, and the dog's restlessness — the last trace of the old, unregistered knowledge — passes "unremarked into the wind."

BREAK:
The expectation violated is that Maren's discovery of the injustice will lead to it being righted — the ledger scene in Section I sets up a classic wrong-uncovered arc, and the story instead shows the wrong being formally legible and administratively inert simultaneously (Tessa's report "filed... to become historical texture," Maren's clause winning her a single afternoon while the stake's history stays permanently out of the Charter's reach). This matches the contract's target_prior almost exactly: the story doesn't argue the system needs a fairer zero-point, it shows that the categories of fairness available can't reach the zero-point at all. Named independently, then checked — they meet.

READINESS:
Very close; the piece is doing sophisticated, disciplined work and only the Doyle-scene's slight over-explicitness keeps it from being fully assured.
