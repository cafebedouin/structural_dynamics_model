How exactly does public education prevent authority collapse? What if transparency reveals that institutions are more uncertain than the public can tolerate? The Scaffold may be necessary but insufficient.

**Overall:** This document demonstrates what reality-grounded policy analysis looks like. It acknowledges constraints, specifies timelines, identifies veto points, and proposes empirical validation paths. The central thesis may be wrong, but the methodology is sound.
