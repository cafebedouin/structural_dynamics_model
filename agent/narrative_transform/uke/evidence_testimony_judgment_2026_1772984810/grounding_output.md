**Constraint 3 (Judgment Irreducibility):** Evidence requires interpretation → motivated reasoning exploits interpretive latitude. This is about *evaluation mechanics*.

The constraints do appear structurally distinct: you could solve Constraint 1 (extend observation capacity) without solving Constraint 3 (judgment still required). The distinction appears valid, though the essay could make this test more explicit.

The perspectival gap is interesting but doesn't uniquely identify tangled rope structure.
