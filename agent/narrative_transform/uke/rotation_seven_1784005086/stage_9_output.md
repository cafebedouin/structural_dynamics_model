REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The wrist-press motif and the weather-as-institution imagery carry real weight without being over-explained; the story trusts small physical gestures (a hand checking wood is still wood, fingers unsteady on a wrist) to do emotional work that dialogue never has to do. Sannat's near-miss at the rail — pausing, closing the slate, walking on without writing anything — is genuinely well-handled ambiguity, giving an institutional figure a flicker of interiority without resolving it into either villainy or redemption.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
Dov's forgery subplot is undercooked relative to the weight the story wants it to carry — it's introduced, executed, and abandoned in less than a page, and the "he could not make himself go down the stair" beat that's supposed to complicate it lands as a shrug rather than a reckoning. The story gestures at his guilt/shame/self-delusion (the unexamined warmth, the hand not wanting to let go of the drawer) but never gives it room to actually cost him anything on the page, so it reads as a plot device for getting him to the rail scene rather than a fully inhabited moral event.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story is careful never to let the forged "present" marking, or Sannat's private hesitation, or the board's final result function as a hidden true score. Ines "held" — the mark stays exactly where it was — and the forgery in the drawer changes nothing about the board, is never discovered, never acted on, never surfaces as a correction mechanism. The love itself (the wrist-press, the Second Tongue) is explicitly kept off every slate, with no suggestion that a fairer Sannat or a more perceptive proctor would have detected and rewarded it. The real stays unreadable to the system, as required.

BREAK:
The text sets up an expectation of external-facing action once Dov gains scriptorium access and the forbidden-knowledge frisson of "Resonant Integrity" is introduced — a reader braces for either exposure of the system's fraudulence or a rescue/reform attempt via the forged document. Instead the story closes that door deliberately (the forgery is filed, inert, discovered by no one) and redirects the climax entirely inward, to the wrist-press in the dark that changes nothing about the board. This matches the contract's target_prior well: victory is redefined as the preserved gesture itself, under total, acknowledged systemic defeat ("changing nothing about what the board would say... in another season, or the one after that").

READINESS:
Close, but the forgery subplot needs either more development or more explicit narrative acknowledgment of its own weightlessness — right now it occupies prime real estate in Part II without earning its space.
