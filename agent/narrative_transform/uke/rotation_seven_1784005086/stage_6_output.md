STRATEGY BRIEF

IMPLIED AUTHOR: A patient, exacting witness to institutional cruelty who refuses both sentimentality and despair — one who trusts physical detail (the wrist, the shelf, the slate) over explanation, and who believes meaning survives only in what cannot be transcribed. This author is willing to let readers sit in ambiguity rather than hand them a diagnosis. Where the current draft occasionally lapses into explaining its own metaphysics (the "word that did the work of a number" passage), the implied author should be pulled back toward the register that already dominates the piece: Sannat's slate with "nowhere to put" what he sees, the board read "with her whole body," Ines's stillness as unwitnessed engineering. Notice, skip: this author notices texture, temperature, timing, and gesture; skips mechanism. It does not explain why the system fails to see — it only shows that it doesn't, repeatedly, from different angles.

AUDIENCE: Adult literary readers drawn to quiet, structurally patient fiction about institutional violence and intimacy under surveillance (comparable readership to Kazuo Ishiguro's *Never Let Me Go* or Jenny Erpenbeck) — readers who don't need resolution but do need the withholding to feel earned rather than coy. They will tolerate slow accumulation and repetition-with-variance (the wrist-touch recurring) but will notice immediately if the story over-explains its central conceit.

SCOPE: Novelette-length short story. Current length 3,407 words is thin for the material's ambitions — the forgery subplot, the board, and the wrist-touch each deserve more room to breathe without added exposition. Target range: 5,500–7,500 words. This stays well inside the feasible ceiling (11,468 words) and allows expansion through scene and sensation rather than through explanation — more texture, not more thesis.

CREATIVE DEEPENING: The richest unexploited material is the *asymmetry* between Ines's and Dov's relationship to the same system — she experiences it as weather, permanent and unnarratable; he experiences it as a room whose workings he can suddenly see, which tempts him toward the false hope of legibility. This asymmetry is already present but under-dramatized. Deepening should come through: (1) more scenes of Ines's sensory budgeting-of-warmth logic extended into new textures (sound, food, fatigue) rather than restating the wrist-touch; (2) Sannat's near-miss at the slate — "somewhere on that slate a place for what he'd just felt" — deserves its own fuller beat, since he is the story's other unwitnessed witness and currently only flickers at the margins; (3) the drawer image (the fair copy going nowhere) is strong and should be trusted more, with less narration of its meaning.

FLINCH POINTS:
1. "It was a word that did the work of a number without ever having to submit to being wrong" and the surrounding paragraph analyzing Resonant Integrity's emptiness: The draft is explaining the invariant to the reader — converting the unmeasurability of love/worth into a legible epistemic critique (bad category, inconsistently applied) that a smarter exam could fix. Honesty requires that Dov's discovery stay partial and experiential — vertigo, not thesis. He should feel that something is wrong without being able to fully articulate the shape of the wrongness, and the narration should not complete the articulation on his behalf.
2. The drawer meditation ("A kindness with nowhere to travel... he understood, all the way down, the shape of what he had made"): this over-resolves Dov's arc into full self-aware clarity. The draft dignifies his failure with insight in the same motion it depicts the failure. Honesty requires leaving some of his understanding incomplete or wrong — he might believe he's understood something (control, exposure of fraud) when the story's structure suggests he hasn't, and the narration shouldn't correct him into full accuracy.
3. Sannat's hand hovering at the slate, "checking... whether it had appeared there since the last time" — this is close to right (Ines can't decode it either) but slightly over-narrated in its final clause ("closed the slate against his chest and walked on"), which resolves his interiority a beat too cleanly. Could hold longer in uncertainty.

PATTERN CHECK: Concern, narrow and specific — not the systemic-hope pattern (the story correctly refuses reform, escape, or organizational change; the wrist-touch changing nothing is the correct anti-resolution). The flinch here is subtler: intellectual over-resolution. The draft doesn't soften the system's cruelty, but it over-explains the *mechanism* of that cruelty through Dov, converting an ontological unmeasurability into an epistemic bug. This is a distinct failure mode from the pipeline's usual default and should be corrected precisely — by trimming explanatory sentences and trusting demonstrated scenes — without pushing the story toward artificial bleakness or manufactured villainy it doesn't need.
