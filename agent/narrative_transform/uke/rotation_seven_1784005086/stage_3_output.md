# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

**Selected: ☒ Seeded Possibility**

Verification against Stage 1/2 material:

- BIR confirmed (Stage 1 §4) — C2's survival despite Supp(C3)=0.80, and X3/Sannat's Tangled Rope hybrid status, both rule out perfect-optimization (PIR). BIR ↔ Seeded Possibility is a licensed pairing.
- The invariant contract (Stage 2 §0) explicitly defines victory as *preservation of an internal, instrument-illegible state* (the Second Tongue / Shelf-Bond) rather than reconfiguration of the Rank. This is the defining signature of Seeded Possibility, not Deterministic Tragedy — the falsifier is already built in ("if a fairer exam would have found the same love and certified it, the naturalization failed").
- Rejects Negotiated Equilibrium (no bargaining channel exists between Ines/Mireille and C3 — trapped exit, Supp=0.80) and Revolutionary Rupture (no TR reaches C3's constraint logic; TR4 is gated by unresolved C5 and produces Naturalized, not Rupture).
- **Verification: PASS.** Attractor, rationality model, and invariant contract are mutually consistent.

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Ines)**
**Secondary: The Builder, capped and reverting toward Condemned (Dov / X2_prime)**

Justification:

- Ines requires PIR-model or Mountain-dominated, powerless index (π≥1.0) — she sits at χ=0.96 under C3, powerless/trapped/biographical. The Condemned archetype's compatibility with Seeded Possibility is explicit (✓). Her voice cannot be Witness or Guide — she has no analytical distance and no pedagogical stance; she experiences the Rank as weather, not construction. This is the correct register for someone for whom "release means unpersoning."
- Dov (X2_prime) is the risk point. His post-transition index (analytical, π=1.15) technically qualifies him for Builder (BIR, moderate power 0.6≤π≤1.0 — Dov sits just outside this at 1.15, which is the tell). This is deliberate: Dov is *not quite* a Builder. He has the Builder's diagnostic clarity (sees how "Resonant Integrity" is scored, sees the ladder's construction) but lacks the Builder's leverage — his χ remains 0.74, still Snare, still trapped regardless of what he now understands. Per Stage 1's error manifestation E2, his correct behavior is to recognize the extraction more clearly, *not* to escape it or act on it successfully. Any scene where Dov's analytical clarity translates into effective intervention on Ines's behalf would misrepresent his structural position — a Builder-shaped ending grafted onto a Snare-shaped reality. Dov must function as a **failed/partial Builder**, a voice that thinks it has purchase and doesn't, collapsing back toward Condemned when tested.
- This produces a **Misrecognition Arc** for Dov specifically: he believes he has become someone who can act on what he sees; the constraint topology shows he is still Snared. The gap between his self-description and his structural position is itself load-bearing for the invariant (his understanding does not put "a single rung back under the girl he left standing on the floor" — Stage 2's own inhabitation sentence for him).
- Mireille and Sannat are **not** POV. Mireille exists structurally to confirm indexical invariance (Chorus function, background); giving her interiority would dilute rather than sharpen the Ines/Dov asymmetry. Sannat (Tangled Rope, χ=0.64) is reserved per Ω_P05 as **externally observed only** — his Zealot-adjacent conviction ("pulling children up out of the water") is far more devastating seen from outside than narrated from within, and a Sannat POV risks the Zealot archetype's requirement (theater_ratio>0.4, high purity) diluting the two-character architecture the invariant needs. Held at Witness-distance through Ines's eyes only.

**Master Diagnostic check:**
- Does the voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot? **Ines:** yes — reader can perceive C3's constructedness (Stage 1 Boltzmann FAIL, χ varies non-factorizably with P) that Ines, as Condemned, experiences as natural law (Error E4 territory — she does not attempt organize_collective because it doesn't occur to her that C3 is a built thing, not weather). **Dov:** yes — reader sees that his analytical gain doesn't convert to leverage; he may believe otherwise for a scene or two before the story corrects him.
- Blurb test: Ines's single most important understanding — "the Rank is a wall that eats children and calls it mercy" — her Condemned voice cannot say this aloud as thesis; it can only be *enacted* (the wall metaphor stays in Stage 2's inhabitation sentence, never in her dialogue). Dov's understanding — "I can see how this is built" — he *can* say this aloud, which is precisely the trap Stage 4 must dramatize as insufficient, not triumphant.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**Selected: ☒ Multi-POV Parallel, restricted to two consciousnesses (Ines / Dov), deployed as Counterpoint + Misrecognition Arc combined.**

Justification: A strict single-POV Gradual Realization would strand Dov's structural correction (E2) offscreen, losing the story's clearest demonstration that analytical distance ≠ escape. Late Revelation would undercut the invariant, which must be *continuously* present (the Shelf-Bond/Second Tongue is not a twist to withhold — it is the substrate the reader should feel accumulating from the first page). Analytical Observer is foreclosed — no character in this cast is licensed to be explicitly aware of indexical variance as such (that would require Witness or Guide, both rejected above).

Counterpoint specifically: Ines and Dov observe the *same* physical marker (the posting board, the cold stair, the dormitory bell) from opposite ends of a power gradient that has *just* enough differential (π=1.5 vs π=1.15) to produce productive friction without either becoming unsympathetic. This is not a Double-Blind (neither is structurally blind to suffering — both know the Rank hurts) — it is a **Misrecognition Counterpoint**: Dov believes his new vantage is power; Ines's chapters demonstrate, without ever saying so, that it isn't.

## 4. Editorial Decisions

```
LENGTH: Short (3,500–4,500 words) — long enough to carry two POVs and the
  quarterly Rank cycle across at least one full turn, short enough that the
  Second Tongue stays a felt accumulation rather than an over-explained motif.

POV: Third Limited, alternating strictly by section — Ines / Dov / Ines,
  three movements. (Not first person: first person risks the Condemned voice
  becoming retrospectively reflective, which she structurally cannot be; not
  omniscient: omniscient would collapse the Counterpoint's productive gap.)

TENSE: Past. (Present tense would import a false immediacy/agency the
  Condemned voice doesn't possess; past tense lets the prose sit inside
  events already underway, which is how Ines experiences the Rank — as
  ongoing weather, not unfolding drama.)

CHARACTER COUNT: 4 — Ines (POV), Dov (POV), Mireille (Chorus/confirmation,
  no interiority), Sannat (externally observed only, no interiority).

NAMING: Cultural/liturgical-index names stripped of lineage marker
  (Ines, Dov, Mireille, Sannat) per Stage 2 Naming layer — naming-as-erasure,
  never explained on the page, never remarked upon by any character.

LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:
  Two verbal registers deployed per Stage 2: Cantor-speech (formal, appears
  only in postings, examination language, the one prayer-before-meals beat)
  and Shelf-speech (clipped, elliptical, what Ines and Dov actually think
  and speak in). The switch occurs at institutional thresholds — any scene
  set at the posting board, in the examination corridor, or involving
  Sannat's official function shifts the surrounding prose's diction toward
  Cantor-speech cadence (longer clauses, Latinate word choice) even in close
  third, without any character narrating the shift. Ines and Dov's internal
  free-indirect discourse never uses Cantor-speech vocabulary — this
  vocabulary gap IS the constraint boundary rendered linguistically.
  The Second Tongue (touch/breath substrate) receives NO orthography at any
  point — never rendered as "three taps meant," always rendered as sensation
  and its consequence only.

EMOTIONAL CORE: Not "will Ines be saved" (she structurally cannot be, and
  the story must not gesture at rescue) but "does the thing between Ines
  and Dov survive Dov's elevation out of daily contact with her" — the
  Shelf-Bond's persistence or erosion under distance is the actual stake,
  because it is the only stake the story's own invariant contract permits
  to be real.

ENDING STRATEGY: ☒ Seeded Possibility close — Rank cycle completes (Ines
  is re-tested, board is posted, nothing about C3 changes structurally),
  while the Second Tongue registers one final, unmeasured act between Ines
  and Dov that the proctor present does not and cannot record. No character
  achieves insight into the system's shape sufficient to name it. The
  invariant is confirmed by its final act being *illegible on the page to
  any instrument the story has established* — reader sees it; Sannat,
  watching from three feet away, sees nothing worth writing down.
```

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The posting board and the stair.**

Two recurring physical objects carry the Counterpoint:

- **The board** (dormitory corridor, chalked Rank postings) — read by Ines with her body (queue position, how close she stands to the drafty end of the corridor, whether her hand shakes reaching to trace her own number) never with a recited figure. Read by Dov from the scriptorium side — he has *seen* the numbers being entered, knows the mechanism, and this seeing changes nothing about what the board does to Ines when she reads it.
- **The stair** to Dov's copy-room — cold, unclimbed by anyone else, a physical index of the distance now built into the Shelf-Bond. Ines's chapters register the stair from below (a door she doesn't have permission to knock on); Dov's chapters register it from above (a door he now realizes he could open, and the story must show him discover that opening it does not undo what the climb cost).

Per the inhabitation-spec anti-pattern rule: neither marker is ever expressed as a number that changes over time. The board is chalk, cold, a queue, a held breath. The stair is stone, temperature, the sound of a door.
