REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The conversion sequence in Section II is visceral and precise—Mina's hands learning tablet-first rhythms, her attention fragmenting before she consciously decides to measure, the physical sensation of keeping becoming accounting. Ahmad's unseeing reads as labor, not blindness: the reader feels him actively pushing down the question about Ibu Salmah's fish, repeating the system's logic until discomfort fades. The substrate (direct reciprocal care) remains ontologically present throughout—fish moving between hands, grief witnessed, bodies remembering who kept them—even as it becomes structurally irrelevant.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The village meeting in Section III stays too comfortably within tutorial boundaries. The conversation circles practical concerns (fees, access, necessity) without anyone naming what Pak Rashid sees clearly: that the choice isn't between efficiency and inefficiency but between two incompatible forms of value. The silence is structural, but it reads as characters not quite reaching the conflict the story has already shown them living inside.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story contains no recoverable "true value" of care that better accounting could capture. The ledger records transactions with perfect accuracy (fish sold, labor logged, scores calculated) and still destroys *timbangan* because measurement itself converts keeping into debt. The substrate—unaccountable reciprocal care held in bodies and memory—remains constitutionally illegible to any instrument. Pak Rashid's final scene with Ibu Salmah demonstrates this: the fish she gives him is ontologically real, but the moment it enters the ledger it becomes a different thing entirely.

BREAK:
The story violates the expectation that objective record-keeping produces justice. From the text: Mina's score rises, her access improves, the system "works" for her—and simultaneously, her capacity for unmediated care erodes. The ledger is perfectly accurate (no measurement errors, no corrupt administrators) and fundamentally destructive (converts keeping into accounting, makes the unmeasured irrelevant). This meets the contract's target_prior: "A system that is technically accurate and internally consistent can still be fundamentally destructive to human value." The violation is demonstrated through Mina's transformation and Ahmad's effortful unseeing, not argued.

READINESS:
The prose is alive and the invariant holds. The village meeting could reach harder into the conflict it circles, but the weakness is containable—the surrounding material (conversion sequence, Ahmad's labor of not-seeing, final substrate demonstration) carries the break clearly. One more pass could sharpen Section III, but the story is close to ready.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
