# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor:** Seeded Possibility

**Verification Against Constraint Logic:**

✓ **Compatible with BIR model:** System achieves satisficing equilibrium (Tangled Rope stable state for participants) rather than running to completion. Ahmad operates ledger for profit, not perfect optimization. Expansion is gradual (local → regional), not immediate maximization.

✓ **Compatible with constraint profile:**
- C₁ (Mountain) foundational: value incommensurability unresolved, will resurface
- C₂ (Tangled/Snare/Rope): ideology provides coordination while justifying extraction
- C₃ (Snare/Tangled/Rope): ledger achieves stable extraction equilibrium across indices

✓ **Surface tragedy evident:**
- C₃ expands (TR₄: local → regional scope)
- G₁ atomizes (E₂: collective action blocked by False Mountain error)
- X₂ converts (TR₂: Mina powerless → moderate, Snare → Tangled Rope)
- X₁ trapped (TR₃: Pak Rashid sees mechanism, cannot escape)

✓ **Underground transformation markers:**
- C₁ (Mountain) ensures *timbangan*/*hutang* incompatibility never resolved
- Ibu Salmah survives as living memory of *timbangan* (Ω_P05 resolution)
- Pak Rashid's analytical recognition persists even if action blocked
- The substrate (unwriteable care) remains ontologically real even as practice erodes

**ATTRACTOR CONFIRMED:** Seeded Possibility is structurally sound for this constraint topology.

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

### Primary Voice: **The Witness** (Pak Rashid POV)

**Justification:**
- Analytical index permits observation of constraint dynamics without direct stake in ledger success/failure
- Can see multiple indices simultaneously (observes Mina's transformation, Ahmad's extraction, village atomization)
- Medical role provides diegetic justification for documentary stance (diagnostician, observer of symptoms)
- Voice is clinical, ethnographic — reports what happens without judgment or intervention
- **Tone:** Neutral, distanced (appropriate for tragedy observed but not prevented)

**Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility
- Witness can observe surface tragedy (expansion, atomization) while noting underground persistence (*timbangan* as memory, unresolved incommensurability)
- Documentary voice allows final-scene juxtaposition: Ahmad's expansion notification vs. Pak Rashid's recognition of closure

**Rationality model compatibility:** ✓ BIR
- Witness observes satisficing behavior (Ahmad's gradual expansion, Mina's strategic scoring) without requiring perfect information

**POV requirement:** Third limited or omniscient
- **Selected:** Third limited, rotating between Pak Rashid (primary Witness), Mina (Condemned → Builder), Ahmad (Beneficiary)
- Pak Rashid chapters carry Witness voice; other POVs provide indexical contrast

### Secondary Voice: **The Condemned** (Mina POV, Act 1)

**Justification:**
- Inside constraint logic (C₃ Snare), cannot see systemic shape
- Acts rationally given limited information (tries to score better, not challenge system)
- Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable (ideology C₂ normalizes ledger)
- Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
- **Tone:** Somber, trapped

**Transition:** Condemned → Builder (Act 2)
- As Mina's index shifts (powerless → moderate), voice shifts from experiencing constraint to testing for leverage
- Builder voice: tactical, observational, adaptive (learns to log favors, game the score)
- This transition IS the tragedy: the Condemned who escapes becomes the system's advocate

### Tertiary Voice: **The Beneficiary** (Ahmad POV)

**Justification:**
- Inside constraint logic, benefits from it, genuinely cannot see extraction
- From institutional index, C₃ reads as Rope (coordination without extraction)
- Voice is confident, unselfconscious — the reader enjoys this perspective initially
- Does not signal awareness of privilege; from this position there is nothing to see
- **Tone:** Warm, assured (produces dark comedy through structural blindness)

**Critical requirement (from archetype spec):**
> The model must *like* this character. The voice must be genuinely appealing — charming, reasonable, kind even. The reader must want to be inside this perspective.

Ahmad is not corrupt. He does not falsify scores. He believes genuinely in fairness. He helps people access credit. He is patient with elders who don't understand the system. He loves his wife. He is proud of his work. **The discomfort must come from the reader's own recognition, not from the text pointing at it.**

**Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility
- Beneficiary voice allows surface tragedy to feel like progress from one index (Ahmad sees expansion as success)
- Reader sees underground cost (Pak Rashid's chapters show what Ahmad cannot)

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected:** Multi-POV Parallel

**Implementation:**
- Rotate between three POVs: Pak Rashid (Witness), Mina (Condemned → Builder), Ahmad (Beneficiary)
- Reader sees indexical variance BEFORE characters do
- Same events (score changes, Cooperative meetings, village decisions) experienced differently from each position
- No character explicitly aware of indexical variance; reader performs the synthesis

**Revelation sequence:**

**Act 1 (Months 0-3):** Establish indices
- Mina chapter: experiences C₃ as Snare (trapped, score 340, cannot access credit)
- Ahmad chapter: experiences C₃ as Rope (coordination, fairness, helping people)
- Pak Rashid chapter: observes both, sees the divergence, cannot intervene

**Act 2 (Months 4-18):** Index transition
- Mina chapters: learns to score, voice shifts from Condemned to Builder
- Ahmad chapters: expansion planning, conviction that system works (look at Mina's rising score!)
- Pak Rashid chapters: watches conversion happen, recognizes E₁ error (Mina misclassifying Tangled as Rope)

**Act 3 (Months 19-24):** Attractor approach
- Mina chapter: score 680, identity-locked, advocates for system expansion
- Ahmad chapter: receives regional expansion approval, experiences as validation
- Pak Rashid chapter: receives same notification, experiences as closure of last exit

**No explicit revelation scene.** The reader sees the full picture by Act 2. Characters never do.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Novelette (12,000-15,000 words)
- Justification: Three POVs across two years requires space for index transitions to feel earned. Mina's transformation (Condemned → Builder → advocate) cannot be rushed. Flash or short story would compress to allegory.

**POV:** Third Limited, rotating
- Three POV characters: Pak Rashid (primary, ~50% of scenes), Mina (~30%), Ahmad (~20%)
- Each POV chapter clearly marked (character name as section header)
- No omniscient narrator; all information filtered through character indices

**TENSE:** Past tense
- Justification: Seeded Possibility attractor benefits from retrospective framing (surface tragedy complete, underground seeds planted). Past tense allows Pak Rashid's Witness voice to carry documentary weight.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 6 named characters
1. **Pak Rashid** (X₁, analytical, Witness)
2. **Mina** (X₂, powerless → moderate, Condemned → Builder)
3. **Ahmad** (X₃, institutional, Beneficiary)
4. **Ibu Salmah** (elder, embodies *timbangan*, survives as marginalized memory)
5. **Mina's daughter** (7 years old, learns ledger as normal, future generation)
6. **Ahmad's wife** (institutional household, private scenes show Ahmad's genuine belief)

Additional unnamed: processor boat crews, traveling clinic staff, other villagers (collective G₁)

**NAMING:** Cultural (Malay naming conventions per Stage 2)
- Respectful titles: Pak, Ibu, Encik, Cik
- Given names: Rashid, Salmah, Mina, Ahmad
- Mina's daughter called "Adik" (little sister/younger one) by village, "Aisyah" by Mina (given name)
- Ahmad's wife: "Zainab"

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**
- **Code-switching occurs:** (i) at POV boundaries (Mina's internal voice more Malay-inflected than Ahmad's), (ii) in dialogue (Malay for intimacy/family, English for Cooperative interactions), (iii) at moments of emotional intensity (Malay breaking through formal register)
- **What the switch signals:** (i) who you are speaking to (kin vs. institution), (ii) what you are speaking about (keeping vs. counting), (iii) compliance vs. selfhood
- **Grammatical substrate:** Reduplication, topic-comment fronting, Malay aspect markers carried into English (per Stage 2 spec)

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (emerges from constraint dynamics, not imposed)
- Grief at watching care become debt
- Betrayal when the Condemned becomes the system's advocate
- Helplessness of seeing clearly and being unable to act
- The specific loneliness of being right too early

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Attractor reached (Seeded Possibility)
- **Final scene:** Ahmad receives regional expansion notification (surface tragedy: system spreads). Pak Rashid receives same notification (underground recognition: last exit closed, but *timbangan* persists as memory in Ibu Salmah, as unresolved incommensurability in C₁).
- **No resolution, no hope-signal, no seed made explicit.** The seed is structural (C₁ Mountain, substrate persistence), not narrative (character action or revelation).

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

### Pak Rashid (Witness, analytical, constrained exit)

**Phenomenological register:** Diagnostic attention
- Notices: patterns in who gets sick (those with low scores delay treatment), wear on people's faces (exhaustion from scoring anxiety), changes in how people talk about each other (from names to numbers)
- Body-read: the weight of medical supplies in his hands (heavier when he knows he cannot get more), the rhythm of his own breathing when he watches someone convert *timbangan* to *hutang* (shallow, held)
- Mode of attention: clinical observation without intervention — sees symptoms, cannot treat the disease

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ Not a metric. Pak Rashid does not count how many people have converted or track score averages. He notices *who* has changed and *how* they speak differently.

### Mina (Condemned → Builder, powerless → moderate, trapped → identity-locked)

**Phenomenological register (Act 1, Condemned):** Survival vigilance
- Notices: the texture of her net (fraying, needs replacement she cannot afford), the angle of the sun (tells her when processor boats arrive), the weight of her daughter's body (growing, needs more food)
- Body-read: hands cramping from net-mending, salt-sting in cuts that won't heal, the hollow in her stomach that is not hunger but fear
- Mode of attention: immediate, tactical — no future tense, only next meal, next repair, next day

**Phenomenological register (Act 2-3, Builder → identity-locked):** Strategic monitoring
- Notices: which favors will log well, which people have high scores (worth cultivating relationships with), when Ahmad reviews scores (timing matters)
- Body-read: the relief of having slack (can rest without panic), the tension of maintaining score (cannot afford to drop), the pride of being called a "good contributor" (warmth in chest, straightened spine)
- Mode of attention: future-oriented, calculating — plans weeks ahead, optimizes for score

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ Not a metric. Mina does not narrate her score as a number changing over time. She experiences the *consequences* of score changes (credit approved, standing elevated) through sensation and social interaction.

### Ahmad (Beneficiary, institutional, arbitrage exit)

**Phenomenological register:** Procedural confidence
- Notices: the elegance of the database interface (clean, logical), the gratitude in people's voices when he approves credit, the growth in registered users (system is working)
- Body-read: the comfort of his climate-controlled office (cool, dry, clean), the satisfaction of solving problems (dopamine hit when he adjudicates a dispute fairly), the pride of his wife's respect (she believes in his work)
- Mode of attention: systemic, optimistic — sees patterns of improvement, believes in progress

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ Not a metric. Ahmad does not narrate verification fee percentages or score distributions. He experiences the system's success through *people thanking him* and *his own material comfort*.

### Substrate (Ibu Salmah, embodiment of *timbangan*)

**Phenomenological register:** Memorial constancy
- Notices: hands that kept her (remembers their shape, their steadiness), the absence of hands that used to keep but now count, the way young people look past her when she speaks
- Body-read: the ache in her knees (seventy years of kneeling to mend nets, to plant, to mourn), the taste of salt (ocean, tears, the brine of memory), the weight of being the last one who remembers
- Mode of attention: retrospective, elegiac — sees the present through the lens of what was lost

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ Not a metric. Ibu Salmah does not count how many people still practice *timbangan*. She *feels* the absence, the silence where reciprocity used to hum.

## 6. NUMERIC REGISTER (GAUGE-INDEXED)

### Diegetic Quantities

**Quantities that exist as OBJECTS in this world:**
1. **Ledger scores (0-1000):** The central instrument of C₃. Exists as certified number in database, visible on tablets/phones to registered users.
2. **Verification fee (8%):** Institutional rate, posted publicly, deducted automatically from certified transactions.
3. **Fish weights (kg):** Certified by Cooperative for processor boat trades. Exists as reading on calibrated scale.
4. **Credit limits (regional currency):** Determined by score, issued by Cooperative. Exists as approved amount in credit agreement.

**Default for all other quantities:** None. No ambient tallies, no countdowns, no emotional beats denominated in numbers.

### Positional Access

**Pak Rashid (analytical, constrained):**
- Can read: his own ledger score (if registered, which he is not), fish weights (when trading), credit limits (when accessing medical supplies on Cooperative credit)
- Cannot read: other people's scores (not an administrator), verification fee calculations (sees only final deduction), database internals

**Mina (powerless → moderate):**
- Can read: her own ledger score (checks obsessively in Act 2), fish weights (when selling catch), her credit limit (when approved)
- Cannot read: other people's scores (unless they tell her), fee calculations, database internals

**Ahmad (institutional, arbitrage):**
- Can read: all ledger scores (administrator access), all transaction records, fee totals, database analytics, credit limits for all users
- Full positional access to all diegetic quantities

### Narration Rule

**Narration is never denominated in the system's numbers.** A number appears only when a character with access ACTS ON IT in-scene:

**Permitted:**
- Mina checks her score on her tablet: "340. Still 340."
- Ahmad reads a score aloud during adjudication: "Your current score is 680, which qualifies you for the bulk-purchase cooperative."
- Pak Rashid sees the fish weight on the scale: "Forty-two kilograms. The processor boat's scale always reads low."

**Forbidden:**
- "Her score had dropped fifteen points since last week." (ambient tally)
- "He needed 200 more credits to afford the repair." (emotional beat in system's numbers)
- "The village's average score was 520." (narrator-performed calculation)

**Test for every number in draft:** WHO read it, on WHAT instrument, doing WHAT with it?

## 7. ACT STRUCTURE

### Act 1: Constraint Activation (Months 0-3, ~4000 words)

**Mina chapter (Condemned voice, C₃ as Snare):**
- Opens with Mina checking her score: 340. Needs credit for new net (current one fraying, will fail soon). Credit denied (score too low).
- Attempts to trade fish without certification: processor boat refuses (Cooperative certification required).
- Registers with ledger (no choice), begins experiencing extraction: 8% fee on every transaction, *timbangan* (care given to Pak Rashid's daughter, net-mending for Ibu Salmah) invisible to score.
- **Physical marker:** The fraying net. Mina runs her fingers over the weak points, counts (in her body, not in numbers) how many more days it will hold.

**Ahmad chapter (Beneficiary voice, C₃ as Rope):**
- Ahmad reviews scores, approves Mina's registration. Experiences this as helping: "She can access credit now. The system works."
- Scene with wife Zainab: discusses expansion plans, genuinely believes ledger is fair and beneficial. Zainab agrees (institutional household, shared worldview).
- **Physical marker:** The climate-controlled office. Cool, dry air. The comfort of being inside while others work in the heat.

**Pak Rashid chapter (Witness voice, observing C₂ and C₃):**
- Treats a patient who delayed care due to low score (infection advanced, harder to treat). Observes the cost of the system.
- Conversation with Ibu Salmah: she explains *timbangan*, why it cannot be written down. Pak Rashid understands intellectually but is constrained (needs Cooperative medical supplies).
- **Physical marker:** The weight of antibiotics in his hand. Knows he cannot synthesize these, knows they come from the system he cannot escape.

### Act 2: Constraint Coupling (Months 4-18, ~6000 words)

**Mina chapters (Condemned → Builder transition):**
- Learns to log favors: when she helps Pak Rashid, she records it. When Ibu Salmah gives her fish, she records it. Score begins to rise.
- Voice shifts: from immediate survival vigilance to strategic monitoring. Begins planning weeks ahead, optimizing for score.
- **Key scene (month 12):** Mina's score crosses 500. Credit approved for new net. She experiences relief, pride, validation. Does not recognize this as E₁ error (Tangled-as-Rope misclassification).
- **Physical marker:** The new net. Smooth, strong, bought on credit. She runs her hands over it and feels the difference. This is what the score bought.

**Ahmad chapters (Beneficiary voice, C₃ expansion planning):**
- Receives approval from Cooperative headquarters to expand ledger to regional scope (five additional villages).
- Experiences this as validation: "The system works. Look at Mina's score — she is thriving now."
- **Key scene:** Ahmad visits Teluk Mawar, sees Mina with new net, congratulates her on "good contribution." Genuinely warm, genuinely believes he helped her.

**Pak Rashid chapters (Witness voice, observing E₁ and E₂ errors):**
- Watches Mina convert *timbangan* to *hutang*. Recognizes the transformation: she is no longer Condemned, but she is not free — she is identity-locked.
- Observes village atomization (E₂: False Mountain error). People stop attempting collective alternatives because ledger appears inevitable.
- **Key scene (month 18):** Ibu Salmah asks Pak Rashid, "Why do they not see?" He cannot answer. He sees, but seeing does not give him power to act.
- **Physical marker:** The traveling clinic boat arrives. Pak Rashid receives supplies, knows he is complicit, cannot refuse them.

### Act 3: Attractor Approach (Months 19-24, ~4000 words)

**Mina chapter (Builder voice, identity-locked, advocates for expansion):**
- Score 680. Moderate power position. Access to bulk-purchase cooperative, credit for boat repair, social standing.
- **Key scene:** Village meeting about Cooperative expansion. Mina speaks in favor: "The ledger helped me. It can help others." Does not see that her success is index-dependent (moderate position), not evidence of system fairness.
- **Physical marker:** Mina's daughter Aisyah learning arithmetic from traveling school-boat. Mina feels pride: "She will do better than I did. She will understand the numbers."

**Ahmad chapter (Beneficiary voice, expansion as validation):**
- Receives formal notification: ledger expanding to five villages, regional scope achieved.
- Scene with Zainab: celebration, pride, conviction that he is doing good work.
- **Physical marker:** The notification on his screen. Clean, official, proof that the system works.

**Pak Rashid chapter (Witness voice, closure of last exit):**
- Receives same expansion notification. Experiences it as the end: system now too large to resist, conversion permanent.
- Final scene with Ibu Salmah: she is still there, still remembers *timbangan*, but no one listens. She is not dead, but she is irrelevant.
- **Physical marker:** Ibu Salmah's hands. Seventy years of keeping, now empty. She offers Pak Rashid fish (unrecorded, *timbangan*), and he takes it, and they both know this is the last language they share.
- **Closing image:** Pak Rashid watches the sun set over the stilt-houses. The ledger is spreading. The keeping is dying. He sees both, and he can do nothing.

**No resolution. No hope-signal. The seed (C₁ Mountain, substrate persistence) is structural, not narrative.**

## 8. CHARACTER ARCS

### Pak Rashid (Witness, analytical, constrained)
**Arc:** Recognition without power → Helpless clarity
- **Month 0:** Sees the ledger's extraction mechanism, believes he can maintain distance (unregistered, skeptical).
- **Month 12:** Recognizes he is structurally complicit (needs Cooperative medical supplies, cannot exit).
- **Month 24:** Accepts that seeing clearly does not grant power to act. The system expands; he remains trapped by role obligation.
- **Transformation:** From analytical observer to trapped witness. The arc is the closing of exits, not the gaining of knowledge.

### Mina (Condemned → Builder, powerless → moderate, trapped → identity-locked)
**Arc:** Victim → Participant → Advocate
- **Month 0:** Experiences ledger as Snare (score 340, trapped, cannot access credit).
- **Month 12:** Learns to score, voice shifts from survival to strategy. Begins experiencing coordination value (credit, standing).
- **Month 24:** Identity-locked. Success within system becomes cognitive anchor. Advocates for expansion, does not see that her success is index-dependent.
- **Transformation:** From Condemned to Builder to system advocate. The tragedy is that escape means conversion.

### Ahmad (Beneficiary, institutional, arbitrage)
**Arc:** Conviction → Validation
- **Month 0:** Believes ledger is fair, helpful, objectively good.
- **Month 12:** Sees Mina's rising score as proof system works.
- **Month 24:** Regional expansion approval experienced as validation. Conviction deepens.
- **Transformation:** No transformation. Ahmad begins and ends with structural blindness. The arc is the *reader's* recognition of what Ahmad cannot see.

### Ibu Salmah (substrate embodiment, elder)
**Arc:** Authority → Irrelevance
- **Month 0:** Respected elder, embodies *timbangan*, teaches younger generation.
- **Month 12:** Marginalized. Young people (including Mina) stop listening. "The old way does not work anymore."
- **Month 24:** Survives but irrelevant. Still practices *timbangan*, but only with Pak Rashid and a few others. The substrate persists, but the practice erodes.
- **Transformation:** From living authority to living memory. The seed of future rupture.

## 9. ERROR MANIFESTATIONS (CONCRETE SCENES)

### E₁: Type III — Snare-as-Rope (Mina, month 12)

**Scene:** Mina receives credit approval for new net (score crossed 500 threshold).

**Observable behavior:**
- Mina experiences relief, pride, validation: "The system works. I just needed to score better."
- Does not recognize that coordination value (credit access) comes at high extractive cost (8% fee, conversion of *timbangan* to *hutang*, identity-lock).
- Treats personal success as evidence of system fairness, not evidence of power position shift (powerless → moderate).

**Physical consequence:**
- Mina begins logging favors obsessively. When Pak Rashid's daughter is sick, Mina helps — but she records it. The care is real, but it is no longer *timbangan*. It is a transaction awaiting score adjustment.

**Correction trigger (not taken):**
- Pak Rashid could explain: "Your score rose because you converted keeping into counting. The system did not become fair; you became complicit."
- But Pak Rashid is constrained (cannot alienate Mina, needs village cohesion for medical work). He watches, does not intervene.

### E₂: Type I — False Mountain (Village collective, month 18)

**Scene:** Village meeting about Cooperative expansion. Discussion of whether to resist or accept.

**Observable behavior:**
- Collective does not attempt alternative coordination mechanisms (mutual aid network, collective bargaining with processor boats, regional alliance with other villages).
- Treats ledger as inevitable: "The processor boats require certification. We have no choice."
- No resistance despite high extraction (8% fee, *timbangan* erasure, atomization).

**Physical consequence:**
- Meeting ends with consensus to accept expansion. Mina speaks in favor (E₁ error). Ibu Salmah speaks against, but her voice is not weighted. Pak Rashid abstains (constrained, cannot lead resistance).

**Correction trigger (not taken):**
- Boltzmann test would reveal C₃ is constructed (classification varies by power position), not natural law.
- But no one in the village has the analytical tools to perform this test. Pak Rashid sees it, but seeing does not give him power to convince others.

### E₃: Type V.a — Tangled-as-Rope (Pak Rashid, month 24)

**Scene:** Pak Rashid receives Cooperative medical supplies on credit (score-based approval).

**Observable behavior:**
- Continues professional role despite recognizing extraction mechanism.
- Justifies participation through coordination value: "I need these antibiotics. People will die without them."
- Minimizes extraction component: "The fee is high, but the alternative is no medicine at all."

**Physical consequence:**
- Pak Rashid treats patient with Cooperative antibiotics. Patient survives. Pak Rashid experiences relief (coordination value real), guilt (complicity real), helplessness (cannot exit).

**Correction trigger (not taken):**
- Explicit χ decomposition: C₃ from analytical index is Snare (χ=0.736), not Rope. Extraction dominates coordination.
- But Pak Rashid already knows this. The error is not cognitive (he sees the mechanism). The error is structural (he cannot act on what he sees).
