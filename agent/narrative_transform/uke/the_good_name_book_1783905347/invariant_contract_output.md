## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The invariant (commitment + falsifier):**
A form of keeping exists only in its direct performance between people who know each other's names. The moment it is written into a number, assigned a value to be compared or traded, it stops being keeping and becomes a different thing entirely — a claim, a debt, an account. The substrate that holds this real is **the practice of direct reciprocal care** — meals shared, tools lent, labor given when someone's roof needs mending, children minded, grief witnessed. This practice cannot be tallied without destroying what makes it work: the fact that no one is counting.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a recoverable "true value" of care that the ledger merely measured incorrectly — if better accounting could capture what was lost — the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**The missing floor (second invariant):**
Beneath the visible system is a founding choice with no neutral ground under it: the decision to assign each person a single number representing their worth to others, and to treat that number as a fact rather than a verdict. The system presents this quantification as neutral record-keeping, but it is a constitutive act — it creates the reality it claims only to measure.

**Falsifier:** If the story frames the injustice as a local error a better authority could correct (a fairer algorithm, less biased administrators), the floor invariant was lost.

**The substrate that holds the invariant:**
The old practice of mutual aid — what people in the stilt-houses call *timbangan*, "the keeping-between-us." It operates through memory, witnessed acts, and reciprocal obligation that no one writes down. The substrate is **unwriteable care**: the fact that you fed someone's children when the nets came up empty, that someone else repaired your boat without asking payment, that grief and celebration are attended because presence matters, not because attendance is logged.

**Substrate inhabitation sentence:**
You know who kept you alive through the dry season not because anyone recorded it, but because you remember their hands and their constancy, and the moment someone tries to write that knowing into a number to be traded or compared, it stops being the thing that kept you.

**The affordance test (applied):**
In this world, could a better instrument measure *timbangan*'s "true value" correctly? **No.** The substrate — direct reciprocal care performed between people who know each other — is structurally unreadable to any measuring apparatus. A ledger can record transactions (fish given, labor performed), but it cannot record *keeping* because keeping is constituted by its unaccountability. The act of measuring converts care into debt, which is a different ontological category. The world's physics has a home for the invariant: a practice that holds real value while remaining constitutionally illegible to any instrument.

**Scored-Snare gate (applied):**
The central Snare (C₃: ledger) is inherently instrument-mediated per Stage 0's flag (`inherent_instrument: yes`). The extraction runs through certified numerical scores. Therefore the instrument may exist diegetically, BUT:
- (i) Narration rule: numbers appear only when a character acts on them in-scene (reads a score aloud, disputes it, pays against it). Narration is never denominated in the ledger's numbers.
- (ii) Extraction is felt and enforced, never tallied, in every POV passage. The score is the mechanism of the taking; it is not the texture of the telling.
- (iii) The orchestrator's deterministic numeric meter will govern every number that reaches the page.

**Break affordance (applied):**
The Break Contract targets the prior: "A system that is technically accurate and internally consistent can still be fundamentally destructive to human value." The naturalization must not foreclose this violation. Test: In this world, can a ledger be perfectly accurate in its recording and still destroy what it measures? **Yes.** The ledger can record every transaction with zero error and still annihilate *timbangan* by converting unaccountable keeping into accountable debt. The substrate affords the break.
