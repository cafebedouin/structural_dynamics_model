# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION
## Editorial Decisions for "The Old Man and the Sea" Translation

---

## I. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

### Selected: Seeded Possibility ✓

**Compatibility Check:**

```
Rationality Model: BIR (Bounded Institutional Rationality)
  ✓ Allows negotiation (boy brings food covertly)
  ✓ Imperfect enforcement (satisficing creates slack)
  ✓ Information asymmetry (parents don't see skill, only results)
  ✓ Uncertainty (cannot predict fish behavior)
  → Seeded Possibility compatible (transformation possible but not guaranteed)

Dominant Constraint Types:
  C1: Mountain (physical contest) → Unchangeable backdrop
  C2: Snare (Santiago) / Rope (Parents) → Indexical variance
  C3: Tangled Rope → Snare (degradation arc)
  → Surface tragedy (Snare + Mountain) + Underground possibility (Tangled recognition)
  ✓ Compatible

Indexical Variance: High
  Santiago: Experiences pure tragedy (immediate, trapped)
  Boy: Sees tragedy + possibility (biographical, constrained)
  Father: Sees confirmation of rational decision (powerful, mobile)
  → Different characters see different endings
  ✓ Compatible with Seeded Possibility structure
```

**Verification:** PASS

**Justification:**
- BIR prevents deterministic tragedy (negotiation possible, enforcement imperfect)
- But structural position (Santiago: powerless, immediate, trapped) makes success highly improbable
- Underground transformation seeded through boy's witness (learns alternative value system)
- Surface: Santiago broken, stigma persists (tragedy)
- Underground: Boy transformed, will teach others (possibility)
- Indexical split allows both to be simultaneously true

## II. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

### Primary Voice: **The Condemned**

**Structural Justification:**

```
Character: Santiago
Index: I_Santiago = (powerless, immediate, trapped, local)
  π = 1.5 (no power to contest judgment)
  T = immediate (survival timeframe)
  E = trapped (cannot leave fishing community)
  S = local (σ = 0.8, face-to-face enforcement)

Constraint Experience:
  C1 (Physical Contest): Mountain → accepts as natural
  C2 (Unluck Stigma): Snare (χ = 0.84) → experiences as inevitable
  C3 (Boy's Duty): Snare (pure loss) → cannot contest

Voice Properties:
  ✓ Inside constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
  ✓ Acts rationally given limited information and position
  ✓ Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable, not constructed
  ✓ Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
  ✓ Makes Type I error (treats C2 as Mountain) — tragic but rational from index
```

**Attractor Compatibility:**
- ✓ Seeded Possibility: Condemned experiences surface tragedy
- ✓ Voice allows reader to see what character cannot (stigma is constructed, not natural)
- ✓ Gap between character's experience and reader's knowledge generates pathos

**Tone:** Tragic, somber, dignified

**POV:** Close third person (we inhabit Santiago's index but maintain slight distance for reader awareness)

**Why Not Other Archetypes:**

```
❌ Builder: Santiago doesn't test for leverage — he accepts the system
❌ Witness: Would require analytical distance Santiago doesn't have
❌ Beneficiary: Santiago is extraction target, not beneficiary
❌ Prophet: Santiago doesn't see future trajectory clearly
❌ Survivor: Wrong temporal position (story is present-tense struggle)
```

---

### Secondary Voice: **The Witness** (Boy's perspective, limited use)

**Structural Justification:**

```
Character: Manolin
Index: I_Manolin = (powerless, biographical, constrained, local)
  π = 1.5 (child under parental authority)
  T = biographical (lifetime relationship)
  E = constrained (can resist at high cost)
  S = local (σ = 0.8)

Constraint Experience:
  C3 (Filial Duty): Tangled Rope (χ = 0.72) → sees BOTH coordination AND extraction

Voice Properties:
  ✓ Observes constraint dynamics with partial stake
  ✓ Can see multiple indices (father's logic, Santiago's suffering)
  ✓ Reports what happens with emotional investment but growing awareness
  ✓ Voice is observational, learning, transitioning
```

**Usage:** Brief interludes, not sustained POV. Boy's perspective provides:
- Indexical contrast (sees what Santiago cannot)
- Underground transformation visibility (seeds being planted)
- Emotional counterpoint (love vs. duty)

**Tone:** Tense, conflicted, gradually awakening

**POV:** Close third person (matches Santiago's register but shows different awareness)

---

### Voice Combination Strategy: **Nested Voice**

**Structure:**
- Primary: Santiago (Condemned) — 85% of narrative
- Secondary: Boy (Witness) — 15% of narrative, strategic placement

**Nesting Pattern:**
```
Act 1: Establish Santiago's voice, brief boy interlude showing his conflict
Act 2: Santiago at sea (pure Condemned, no boy perspective)
Act 3: Return — boy's witness perspective reveals what Santiago cannot see
```

**Function:**
- Santiago's voice: Reader experiences constraint as inevitable (Type I error)
- Boy's voice: Reader sees constraint is constructed (indexical variance)
- Gap between voices: Generates dramatic irony and underground possibility

**Critical Constraint:**
- Boy's voice NEVER explains the framework
- Boy's awareness is emotional/intuitive, not analytical
- No meta-commentary, no explicit recognition of "stigma as social construction"
- Shows through action: brings food despite prohibition, stays despite father's disapproval

## III. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

### Selected: **Gradual Realization (Asymmetric)**

**Structure:**

```
Phase 1: Naturalization (Act 1)
  - Santiago treats stigma as natural law (Type I error)
  - Reader sees enforcement mechanisms (cooperative, parents' decision)
  - Boy experiences duty as normal (Type V.a error — Tangled-as-Rope)
  - Father's logic presented as reasonable (from his index, it IS)

Phase 2: Evidence Accumulation (Act 2)
  - Santiago's skill visible in marlin catch
  - Physical contest (C1, Mountain) distinguished from social judgment (C2, Snare)
  - Reader begins seeing: one is natural law, one is constructed
  - Santiago still cannot see distinction

Phase 3: Partial Reveal (Act 3)
  - Skeleton proves scale of achievement (evidence)
  - Community maintains stigma despite evidence (enforcement visible)
  - Boy sees: stigma ≠ natural law (underground transformation)
  - Santiago dies believing it was inevitable (surface tragedy)
  - Reader sees: both are true from their respective indices

Phase 4: Terminal Indexical Split
  - Santiago: Experienced pure tragedy (immediate, trapped index)
  - Boy: Witnessed tragedy + possibility (biographical, constrained index)
  - Father/Community: Sees confirmation of rational decision (powerful, mobile index)
  - Reader: Sees all three simultaneously, understands structural collision
```

**Revelation Mechanics:**

**NOT through:**
- Explicit statements ("I realize now the stigma was constructed")
- Framework terminology
- Characters explaining to each other
- Authorial voice breaking in

**THROUGH:**
- Physical markers (see §IV)
- Action consequences that confirm different interpretations
- Dialogue showing incompatible worldviews
- Boy's choices revealing his transformation
- Community's response to skeleton (maintains stigma despite proof)

**Key Asymmetry:**
- Santiago never realizes (dies in Type I error — tragic but rational from his index)
- Boy realizes (underground transformation — sees extraction, will resist in future)
- Reader realizes before boy (dramatic irony throughout)

## IV. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

### Length
**Selected:** □ Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)

**Justification:**
- Three-act structure with sea voyage requires space
- Multiple constraint interactions need development
- Indexical variance requires showing different perspectives
- Purity drift arc (C3: Tangled → Snare) needs time to register
- Target: ~12,000 words

### POV
**Selected:** □ Third Limited (Close)

**Justification:**
- Inhabit Santiago's index (Condemned voice) while maintaining reader awareness
- Allows brief shifts to boy's perspective without full POV change
- Creates gap between character experience and reader knowledge
- Maintains intimacy while enabling dramatic irony

**Technical Specification:**
- Primary: Santiago's sensory experience, immediate thoughts, no meta-awareness
- Secondary: Boy's observations, limited interiority, emotional rather than analytical
- No omniscient intrusions
- No framework terminology in any perspective

### Tense
**Selected:** □ Past

**Justification:**
- Allows retrospective weight (this has already happened)
- Compatible with Seeded Possibility (surface tragedy complete, underground seeds planted)
- Natural for close third limited
- Enables subtle foreshadowing without breaking voice

### Character Count
**Selected:** 3 primary, 2-3 secondary

**Primary:**
- Santiago (protagonist, Condemned voice)
- Manolin (boy, Witness voice, underground transformation)
- Father (antagonist without malice, embodies powerful index)

**Secondary:**
- Mother (brief, enforces C3 through social pressure)
- Cooperative representative (brief, embodies C2 enforcement)
- Optional: Another fisherman (contrast to Santiago, shows stigma in action)

**Justification:**
- Minimum for constraint interactions (C2 requires community, C3 requires family)
- Avoids overcrowding (sea voyage is solo, needs space)
- Each character represents distinct index

### Naming
**Selected:** □ Cultural (Spanish names, period-appropriate)

**Character Names:**
- Santiago (the old fisherman) — traditional, dignified, carries weight
- Manolin (the boy) — diminutive, affectionate, youth
- Rafael (the father) — strong, authoritative, respected
- Cooperative members: Don Esteban, Don Miguel (formal address, status markers)

**Justification:**
- Naturalizes 1952 Mexican coastal setting
- Names carry social information (formal address = power differential)
- Avoids abstraction (not "the old man," "the boy" — too Hemingway)
- Cultural specificity grounds constraint logic in material reality

### Linguistic Implementation

**Code-Switching Pattern:**

```
Santiago's Register:
  - Formal Spanish constructions (subjunctive, formal address)
  - Archaic phrases with grandmother's language
  - Treats sea as animate, reciprocal
  - Example: "El mar tiene memoria" (the sea has memory)

Boy's Register:
  - Contemporary coastal Spanish
  - English loanwords from tourism ("tackle," "charter")
  - Formal with father, warm with Santiago
  - Code-switches based on social context

Father's Register:
  - Modern business Spanish
  - Cooperative terminology ("cuota," "asignación")
  - Formal, efficient, practical
  - No archaic constructions

Community Register:
  - Mixed: traditional phrases + modern efficiency language
  - Gossip register (informal, evaluative)
  - Stigma language: "mala suerte" (bad luck), "salao" (cursed)
```

**Code-Switching Signals:**

1. **Power Differential:**
   - Formal address (usted) vs. informal (tú)
   - Santiago uses formal with cooperative members (powerless)
   - Father uses informal with Santiago (powerful)

2. **Temporal Displacement:**
   - Santiago's archaic phrases mark him as obsolete
   - Boy understands but doesn't speak old register
   - Gap = generational constraint transition

3. **Indexical Position:**
   - Cooperative language = institutional power
   - Santiago's sea language = alternative value system
   - Boy navigates between both

**When Code-Switching Occurs:**
- Santiago with boy: Slips into grandmother's language (intimacy, teaching)
- Boy with father: Formal, careful (duty, obedience)
- Santiago with cooperative: Formal, deferential (powerless position)
- Community gossip: Stigma language becomes naturalized

**What Switch Signals:**
- Not just "who's talking" but "what power relation is active"
- Language = structural position made audible
- Reader hears indexical variance through register shifts

## V. PRIMARY PHYSICAL MARKER

### Selected: **The Hands**

**Inhabitation Sentence Connection:**
> "You are a man who knows his skill is real, watching the village decide it isn't, unable to prove yourself without catching a fish, unable to catch a fish because the village has already decided you can't."

**Physical Manifestation:**

Santiago's hands are:
- Scarred coral from forty years of line
- Still strong, still capable
- Visible proof of skill to anyone who looks
- But the cooperative doesn't look at hands — they look at catch records

**Indexical Variance:**

```
Santiago's Experience:
  - Feels hands on line, knows they still work
  - Hands = proof of skill (to himself)
  - But proof doesn't matter if no one sees it

Boy's Experience:
  - Watches Santiago's hands work the line
  - Sees skill the cooperative doesn't
  - Hands = evidence of what's being wasted

Father's Experience:
  - Doesn't look at Santiago's hands
  - Looks at cooperative chalkboard (numbers)
  - Hands = irrelevant data

Reader's Experience:
  - Sees hands throughout (description, action)
  - Understands: skill is real, stigma is constructed
  - Gap between hands (capability) and numbers (reputation) = the tragedy
```

**Narrative Function:**

**Act 1:**
- Establish hands as skilled (mending nets, tying knots)
- Boy watches hands, learns from them
- Father never looks at hands, only at catch records

**Act 2:**
- Hands on the line fighting marlin (skill visible)
- Hands bleeding, cramping (Mountain takes toll)
- Hands prove Santiago's capability to reader
- But no one from village is there to see

**Act 3:**
- Hands empty when he returns (skeleton, no proof)
- Boy sees hands, understands what they accomplished
- Community sees empty hands, stigma confirmed
- Hands = the gap between reality and reputation

**Physical Marker Beats:**

1. **Opening:** Santiago mending nets, hands moving with practiced precision. Boy watching. Father walking past without looking.

2. **Preparation:** Hands coiling line, baiting hooks. Each movement economical, expert. The cooperative representative glances at the chalkboard, not the hands.

3. **Contest:** Hands gripping line, blood mixing with salt water. Skill keeping him alive. No witnesses.

4. **Return:** Hands gripping oars, empty boat behind him. Boy sees the rope burns, understands the scale. Father sees empty hands, decision confirmed.

5. **Final Image:** Boy's hands on Santiago's hands. Inheritance. The skill will continue even as the man ends.

**Why This Marker:**

- Emerges naturally from inhabitation sentence (skill vs. reputation)
- Visible to reader throughout (concrete, sensory)
- Indexically variant (different characters see different meanings)
- Physical consequence of constraints (C1 damages hands, C2 ignores them)
- Carries underground transformation (boy inherits the hands' knowledge)
- Never explained — shown through action and consequence

**Banned Descriptions:**
- "His hands told the story of forty years" (too meta)
- "Hands that the village refused to see" (too explicit)
- "Skilled hands wasted by an unjust system" (framework leak)

**Allowed Descriptions:**
- "The line cut into the scar tissue where older lines had cut before"
- "The boy watched the knot form, trying to memorize the motion"
- "Don Esteban's eyes stayed on the chalkboard. Zero. Zero. Zero."
