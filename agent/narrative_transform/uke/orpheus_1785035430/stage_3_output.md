## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Cal)**
**Secondary: The Beneficiary, restrained toward Negotiator-adjacent good faith (Ossory)**
**Tertiary: A variant Witness-Zealot hybrid, resolved toward Witness-with-stake (Ness)** — see note below.

### Justification

**Cal — The Condemned.** χ(C3) = 0.96 at his index, all three Snare floors cleared. He cannot see the constructed nature of the Backward Glance (E4: Mountain Denial persists until the terminal window closes — no cross-index check is ever evidenced). His voice must carry the walk as *experiential*, not analytical: he is not testing leverage, he is enduring a condition he believes is a fair test of love rather than an engineered trap. Present-tense immediacy and trapped/local index (σ=0.8, T=immediate) both argue for close, unreflective interiority. First person or tight third — the Master Diagnostic applies cleanly: the reader must see what Cal cannot (that the condition was set without margin, that Ossory paid nothing for its severity) while Cal experiences only the doubt whispering *check, check*.

Blurb test: the single most important thing Cal understands about his situation is "I must not look back." He can say this aloud — it is the plot, not the thesis. What he *cannot* say aloud, because the Condemned archetype forbids the analytical register required to say it, is "this rule was designed with no margin because it costs the Warden nothing to make it absolute." That sentence belongs to the reader. Structural fit confirmed.

**Ossory — The Beneficiary**, restrained. χ ≤ 0 at both C1 and C3 for his index — textbook false-Rope signature, constraint reads as coordination (routine casework) from his chair. The Beneficiary directive applies precisely: Ossory must be *likeable*, must genuinely believe he runs the fairest system his family's office has run (per Stage 2 character notes), and must never signal awareness of what the condition costs Cal's index. Ω_P06's recommendation (one scene of Ossory moved by the case, reviewing rolls alone at night) is accepted **with a guardrail**: that scene must show warmth and precedent-consciousness, not doubt about the condition's severity. If Ossory privately worries the Backward Glance is too harsh, the false CI_rope signature dissolves and he becomes a Negotiator — which breaks the certified PIR/Beneficiary pairing. He may be *moved*; he may not be *uncertain about the fairness of his own instrument*.

**Ness — Witness-with-stake, not pure Witness.** She does not fit The Condemned (she is not trapped by C3), nor The Builder (she has no strategic design on the Warden), nor pure Witness (she has direct emotional stake and cannot be neutral/documentary — Stage 2 explicitly notes she is unsure whether to feel proud or afraid). She is closest to a Tangled Rope-native voice: she experiences the Salt-Song as both genuine coordination-comfort and repeated extraction (E3 territory — the correction against Tangled-as-Snare misclassification requires exactly her mixed engagement-plus-distress register). I am authoring her as **a restrained Witness who is also a rope-bearer of the practice itself** — she observes Cal's use of the Keening from inside the tradition, not outside it. She gets short, framing-adjacent passages, not a full parallel arc. This is a deliberate minor deviation from the twelve pure archetypes, justified by C2's own dual-component structure (Coord=true AND Asym=true) — her voice needs to hold both without resolving into either the Beneficiary's blindness or the Condemned's tragedy.

### Attractor Compatibility Check (Positive pairing, per reference)
This maps directly onto the listed positive example: *Multiple archetypes (split POV) + attractor where the constraint has indexical variance* — different blindnesses on the same constraint (C1, C3) produce collision without anyone being a villain. Cal's Condemned blindness and Ossory's Beneficiary blindness never need to meet head-on in dialogue; the reader performs the gap.

**Rejected pairings, for the record:**
- Builder voice for Cal — rejected. He is not testing leverage against C3; he has one shot, no iteration, no learning-from-failure loop available (χ=0.96, no coordination component to work with once the condition is set). Builder would imply agency the constraint doesn't support.
- Negotiator voice for Ossory — rejected. He is not mediating between indices; he genuinely doesn't perceive Cal's index as needing mediation. Negotiator would collapse the Beneficiary's necessary blindness.
- Zealot voice for Ossory — considered and rejected. The Reed-Law isn't sacralized by Ossory as moral imperative; it's administered as ordinary casework (Stage 2: "a filing system with an unusually solemn caseload"). Zealot requires fervor and theater; Ossory's register is bureaucratic calm, which is Beneficiary, not Zealot.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**☑ Multi-POV Parallel**, with a **restrained Late Revelation** flavor at the very end.

The reader alternates between Cal's walk (present-tense, close) and short Ossory interstitials (past-tense, retrospective-administrative) *before* the walk's outcome is known. This lets the reader hold χ=0.96 and χ=-0.13 simultaneously — the core indexical variance of C3 — well before the climax, which is required for the tragedy to land as tragedy rather than twist. Ness's fragments are woven at the seams (before the petition, once mid-walk in memory, once after) rather than given a fully separate arc — she is chorus, not counterpoint-lead.

The single Late-Revelation beat: Ossory's night-scene (Ω_P06) is placed *after* Cal's walk has begun, not before — so the reader learns simultaneously that Ossory was moved by the case AND that being moved changed nothing about the condition's severity. This timing turns potential sentimentality into cost: warmth without margin.

## 5. Inhabitation Spec

**Cal (The Condemned):** Hypervigilance turned inward and backward — not scanning a room for exits (he has none) but scanning his own body for the specific proprioceptive signal of *another person's footsteps behind him*, a sense he cannot verify without the one act that destroys it. His attention should fixate on sound-texture (reed-hiss, mud-suck, the particular give of wet ground under two sets of feet vs. one) rather than sight, since sight is the forbidden sense. The body has learned, across six years of marriage and three years of grief, to distinguish his wife's footfall from any other — this is stated once, early, as ordinary domestic fact, and becomes unbearable exactly because it's reliable enough to torment him without ever being confirmable.

**Ossory (The Beneficiary):** Administrative comfort registered as *physical ease* — a warm room, dry paper, the specific unhurried motion of a hand that has never had to move fast. His body notices weather, ink, the sound of his own seal striking wax — textures of a life where nothing has ever required urgency. When he is "moved" by Cal's case, the marker is that his hand pauses over the rolls slightly longer than usual — not doubt, just a beat of attention. The absence of urgency in his body *is* the tell of his structural position; Stage 4 should never let him feel rushed, cold, or physically at risk, even in the night scene.

**Ness (Witness-with-stake):** The body of someone who has sung the same words so many times the mouth knows them before the mind engages — muscle-memory grief. Her hands at the salt-pan are the register: grain-texture, the damp give of salt underfoot, the specific ache in wrists that has nothing to do with emotion and everything to do with decades of repeated labor that happens to accompany decades of repeated mourning. She should notice Cal's hands (steady/unsteady) rather than his face — she reads him through what his hands do at the pan and the gate, not through direct emotional description.

**Anti-pattern check:** None of the above reduces to a number-over-time. Cal's footstep-discrimination is not a countdown; Ossory's unhurried hand is not a metric of care; Ness's wrist-ache is not a grief-scale.

## 6. Primary Physical Marker

**The keeping-salt barrel and the weighmaster's scale**, per Stage 2's substrate inhabitation sentence — but narrowed for Stage 4 into one recurring, concrete object: **a fistful of keeping-salt Cal carries in his coat pocket during the walk**, unused, not rubbed into any garment. It is there because Ness pressed it on him before he left ("in case," she says, not finishing the sentence). Its narrative function is to be **present and useless** — Cal's hand closes on it once, mid-walk, in the exact moment the urge to turn is strongest, and it does nothing, because it was never built to hold what he's carrying. This is the invariant made tactile without being explained.

## Validation Checklist Results

**Blueprint consistency:** Voice archetypes confirmed compatible with attractor and PIR model. All three constraints have clear activation moments (C1/C2 in Act 1, C3 in Act 2). Character arcs map directly to the three agent indices — no drift.

**Numeric register:** Diegetic quantities limited to salt-weight and imprecise generational count; positional access authored per POV (Ossory owns instruments, Cal and Ness do not); narration rule explicitly bars mile-countdown during the walk — the single highest-risk spot for numeric violation in this story, now guarded.

**Inhabitation spec:** All three specs pass the anti-pattern test — none reduces to a changing number. Cal's is sound-based hypervigilance, Ossory's is ease-as-tell, Ness's is muscle-memory ache.

**Structural prevention — blurb test:** Cal can say "I must not look back" aloud; he cannot say "this rule costs Ossory nothing," and the Condemned archetype structurally forbids that sentence. Confirmed no theme-naming risk.

**Irresolvable tension test:** Identified and marked — Ossory's granting of the petition is *genuinely* generous by his own lights and *genuinely* costless to soften-in-severity, and both of these are true simultaneously with no available reframe that resolves the discomfort. Stage 4 must write toward this, not around it — specifically, must resist any impulse to give Ossory a redemptive doubt-scene that would soften the tension into villainy-avoided-by-remorse.

**Framework terminology:** Confirmed absent from all natural-language blueprint sections; confined to XML tags and this document's own analytical scaffolding.
