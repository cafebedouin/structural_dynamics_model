DISCOVERY REPORT

EMERGENT ELEMENTS:

The three constraint reports converge on a single structural shape wearing three faces: a trial whose one rule (don't verify) makes verification impossible in principle, a "fairness" that means only *consistency of terms* and never proportionality of cost, and a piece of grief-technology (the Keening) that was taught as comfort and works, unmodifiably, as a key. All three reports independently produce the same fingerprint: institutional sees rope, everyone else sees snare. That gap is the story's real subject, and the prose enacts it with unusual precision.

*Institutional seat — Ossory (Section II, VI, X).* His sections are built entirely around procedural self-satisfaction: "the particular warmth of a well-made decision, the kind that asks nothing further of the man who made it." His fairness-logic is explicit and airtight from inside: *had every petitioner in living memory been offered the same unadorned terms? They had.* Symmetry as proof of justice. The Prolog report's "false_ci_rope" / "severity laundering" language names exactly this: the office's definition of fairness structurally excludes proportionality, and Ossory never notices the exclusion because his instruments (the ledger, the rota, "terms standard") aren't built to register severity at all — only consistency. His closing move, filing the void "as ordinary outcome... proof the terms were sound," is the clearest textual instance of the institutional seat's rope-reading: a rule so easily kept would hardly be worth calling a rule.

*Powerless/moderate seat — Cal (I, IV, V, VIII, IX).* Cal's sections carry the snare-reading entirely through the body: the mud's "sucking give," the counting of a decade's worth of gait-knowledge collapsing into one moment of undecidable doubt. The story never lets Cal articulate the systemic critique — he has no vocabulary for jurisdiction, only weather-talk ("Nobody in this parish says wall. Nobody says law"). This absence of vocabulary IS the extraction-blindness the reports flag: the victim seat experiences the structure as fate/nature, not as an authored rule with an author.

*Ness (III, VII, coda).* Ness is the story's most interesting seat because she carries both the transmission (rope: she teaches comfort) and structural complicity (the mechanism becomes a key). Her line — "I don't have room to be delicate about which" — is Cal's version of this same doubled awareness, inherited from her. The ritual_transmission report's institutional beneficiary tag, "grieving_child_recipient," is almost cruelly literal: Ness taught a nine-year-old the exact words that would, decades later, be the precise instrument of his testing and undoing.

The perspectival gap is most alive exactly where Ossory's chapters sit beside Cal's without touching. They never share a scene. The story's structure — alternating close third (Ossory) with first person (Cal, Ness) — enacts the "no global section" / sheaf-disagreement finding directly: there is no vantage from which both readings could be reconciled, because the text refuses to let the two seats occupy the same paragraph.

LATENT POTENTIAL:

- The Ness/Ossory relationship is entirely absent — they never appear in the same frame, yet both are "transmitters" of the mechanism (she teaches the words, he sets the terms). The story gestures at a common cause (both inherit their role from prior generations, both trust inherited form over present cost) without ever letting the reader see that parallel structurally, side by side. A scene, or even a single sentence, juxtaposing "as his father had, and his father's father" (Ossory) against "the way you learn the Keening... before you learn to swim" (Ness) would make the corpus-flagged shared-victim edge between the two constraints visible in the prose itself, not just inferable.

- Alder Voss, the Watchman whose mother pans salt near Ness, is a live wire never used. He is structurally the moderate seat — close enough to power to enforce it, close enough to the parish to feel shame doing so ("looks at me the way a neighbor looks at a neighbor doing something a little shameful in public"). His report to Ness in the coda is secondhand and brief. He is the character best positioned to see *both* sides of the gap and the story doesn't let him say anything about what he's seen.

- The Frankl question: what meaning is available in a constraint that cannot, by its own design, ever be verified? The story gets close — Cal's realization that the salt "cures the manageable" and nothing else, that some griefs are simply too large for any instrument the parish possesses — but stops short of naming what the *un-verifiability itself* might mean, as opposed to merely costing him. Right now the doubt reads purely as a trap (structurally correct, per the engine's "self-defeating trial" framing) but there's unexploited room for Cal to arrive at something the rule didn't intend: e.g., that the one clean instant of seeing her face again was worth more regardless of outcome — the story states this ("it is worth more than the six years I have left to grieve it") but almost in passing, at the moment of loss rather than as an arrived-at understanding earned across the piece.

- Ossory's "beat of attention... not doubt" is the single richest latent seam in the whole piece. Twice the narration insists his hand stills "a moment longer than strictly necessary" and twice insists this isn't doubt. That repeated disavowal is doing enormous structural work — it's the institutional seat almost touching the perspectival gap and flinching from it. The story could let that beat cost him something, or at minimum let the reader see him choose not to look at it, rather than simply asserting the choice was never made.

STRONGEST WRITING:

- The opening epigraph-fragment: *"I taught him the words before he had the grief to put in them... I remember thinking: good, let it stay empty as long as it can. / It didn't stay empty."* Establishes Ness's voice, the story's central irony, and the double-edged-inheritance structure in four sentences.

- "You'd think, to hear us, that Mara had walked into a season rather than a jurisdiction — that she'd been taken up by something as blameless as rain." This is the thesis sentence of the entire piece, and it's doing exactly what the constraint reports describe structurally: naming the mechanism by which extraction gets recoded as weather.

- Section V, the tread-recognition passage: "the particular unhurried set-down of her heel, never quite matching another woman's rhythm, a walk that favored the left slightly from an old fall on the eel-dock steps when she was a girl." This is where the marriage is most alive — intimate, specific, entirely un-sentimental — and it's what makes the doubt in section VIII devastating rather than merely mechanical.

- Ossory's "the particular warmth of a well-made decision, the kind that asks nothing further of the man who made it" — the best single line for the institutional seat; it does more than any exposition to characterize bureaucratic self-forgiveness.

- The coda's closing movement: "and it still steadies me exactly as much as it always has, and it still opens the same old seam doing it, and both those things are true in the same breath." This is Ness's — and the story's — most mature statement, refusing to resolve the comfort/weapon doubleness into either term.

ANCHOR CHECK:
No numeric-escalation, countdown, or tally passages found. Ossory's "nine petitions... refused," "granted one, seventeen years back... his father... two... grandfather... one in fifty" is a *rhythm-of-scarcity* claim rather than a descending-score countdown — it's doing characterization (his trust in inherited cadence) rather than manufacturing arithmetic inevitability, and is not counting toward a climax. No passage in the story uses numbers to simulate precision or dramatize an approaching threshold. None found.

INVARIANT PROBE:
There is a genuine unrecoverable-in-principle real here, and it's the story's best structural feature: whether the footsteps behind Cal ever actually faltered. This is not merely hidden information that a better instrument (a camera, a witness, a more attentive Cal) could recover — the text is explicit that "there is no way inside the rule Ossory gave me to know." The rule's entire design forecloses verification as a category, not merely as a practical difficulty. No Watchman, no Warden, no retrospective inquiry could ever establish what happened on the causeway between Mara's presence and Cal's turn, because the only witness position (Cal's own hearing, mid-doubt, under a rule that prohibits the confirming glance) is structurally incapable of producing evidence rather than merely lacking access to it. This is the invariant the Prolog engine's "self-defeating trial" framing is pointing at from outside, and the prose earns it independently: the doubt is not a fact obscured by darkness, it's a fact whose conditions of existence prevent it from ever becoming legible to any position, including the one having it. Everything else in the story — Ossory's ledger, the office's fairness, Ness's teaching — is in principle recoverable by better instruments or fairer authority (a truthful comparative history could establish whether revision was ever proposed; the shared-victim edges could in principle be traced by a sufficiently attentive genealogist of the office). This one thing cannot be.

SCOPE ASSESSMENT:
3,501 words — this is a short story, not a novella, and it is closer to complete than overlong; if anything it is compressed near its ideal length rather than needing trimming. The triptych structure (Cal/Ness/Ossory) is efficient and none of the sections feel padded. Expansion would most plausibly require either (a) a scene bringing Ossory and Ness/Cal into shared narrative space, which would cost the clean formal separation that currently enacts the sheaf-disagreement, or (b) more of Alder Voss, which would dilute the tight three-voice structure but could open the moderate seat currently underused. Compression below this length would likely sacrifice the Ossory sections' interiority — cutting II/VI/X would leave the institutional rope-reading only inferred rather than dramatized, collapsing the very perspectival gap that is the story's structural achievement. Current length suits a short story; no strong case for novella-length expansion given how self-sufficient the existing gap-structure already is.
