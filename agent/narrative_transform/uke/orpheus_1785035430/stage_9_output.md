REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The braided structure — Ness's fragmentary retrospective frame against Cal's present-tense walk and Ossory's bureaucratic close-third — does real work, letting the reader hold three angles of the same event without anyone narrating the theme aloud. The salt motif (shawl unsalted in the chest, the fistful gone inert in the mud) is the story's best piece of physical thinking: it never explains itself and pays off exactly once, hard, at the end. Ossory's self-soothing ("a beat of attention," the glove/coal image) is genuinely unsettling because his complacency is rendered as procedural comfort rather than villainy.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The Orpheus mechanism (don't look back, footstep ambiguity, the turn) is well-executed but familiar enough that the story's real argument — the invariant about unmeasurable devotion — gets carried almost entirely by exposition in Ossory's sections and the contract-adjacent salt paragraphs, rather than dramatized through Cal's experience of the walk itself. Section VIII does the ambiguity-of-the-footstep work well, but the *reason* the trial was unwinnable (that no rule could have been built to hold what he was carrying) is stated more than felt in the climax itself — it's asserted in III and VII by Ness, not discovered by Cal in the moment he turns.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story never gives up a recoverable true value — Cal's uncertainty about the footstep (real catch, marsh-trick, or his own listening) is never resolved for the reader, and Ossory's rule is shown as structurally incapable of measuring the thing rather than merely miscalibrated. No passage suggests a fairer Warden or sharper instrument would have let her through.

BREAK:
The expectation violated is the fairy-tale promise embedded in "sing it whole and you'll be heard" — the story sets up Cal's flawless Keening as if perfect performance of grief should be sufficient, then lets an unanswerable ambiguity (not a rule-flaw, not villainy) undo it anyway. This matches the target_prior — perfect devotion demonstrated, human fallibility (his own listening, his own doubt) still costing him the outcome — reasonably well. It meets the contract, though the "fallibility" is thin: it's ambiguity/uncertainty rather than a clear human error, which softens the snatched-defeat quality somewhat.

READINESS:
Close — the prose is controlled and the invariant is intact, but the climax leans on inherited myth-mechanics more than it dramatizes its own stated invariant, which is a legitimate structural gap rather than a polish issue.
