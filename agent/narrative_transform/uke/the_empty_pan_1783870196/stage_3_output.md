# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## I. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor:** Seeded Possibility

**Compatibility Check:**

✓ **Constraint Profile:** Mountain-dominated (C₁ true Mountain + C₂ False Mountain) + high-extraction Snare (C₃) → **COMPATIBLE**
  - Mountain-dominated systems support Seeded Possibility when underground transformation space exists
  - C₂ False Mountain creates naturalization that blocks overt reform
  - C₃ indexical variance (Snare/Tangled Rope/Rope) creates differential awareness

✓ **Rationality Model:** BIR (Bounded Institutional Rationality) → **COMPATIBLE**
  - Satisficing behavior (ε = 0.8, not maximal) creates monitoring gaps
  - Principal-agent problems enable covert action within compliance
  - Risk aversion preserves coordination value, preventing pure extraction maximization
  - Imperfect monitoring allows margin-writing to persist undetected

✓ **Indexical Variance:** Present and structural → **COMPATIBLE**
  - X₂ (Tangled Rope): normalized insider, functional within system
  - X₃ (Snare): analytical resistance, maintains critical classification
  - Divergence enables underground documentation while surface appears stable

✓ **Mechanism Viability:**
  - Surface trajectory: X₁→X₂ normalization observable, system appears stable
  - Underground trajectory: X₃ margin-writing covert, analytical index maintained
  - Activation conditions beyond narrative scope (generational time horizon shift)
  - No forced awakening, no implausible rupture, no neat resolution

**VERIFIED:** Seeded Possibility is structurally sound for this constraint network.

## II. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected Archetype:** **The Condemned** (primary) with **Nested Voice** structure (Witness framing)

**Justification:**

1. **Attractor Compatibility:**
   - Condemned + Seeded Possibility: ✓ COMPATIBLE
   - Voice inside constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
   - Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable (matches False Mountain perception)
   - Seeded Possibility works because the *reader* sees what the Condemned cannot
   - Tragic surface (normalization) with underground seeds (margin notes) both visible to reader, not narrator

2. **Index Alignment:**
   - Primary voice: Túpac (X₂, post-normalization)
   - Position: Moderate power (π = 1.0), biographical time, identity-locked, local scope
   - Experiences C₃ as Tangled Rope: "corrupt but functional"
   - Cannot see C₂ as constructed (False Mountain naturalization holds)
   - Cannot see C₃ extraction magnitude (normalization complete)
   - **Perfect Condemned profile:** acts rationally given limited information, experiences constraint as inevitable

3. **Nested Witness Frame:**
   - Opening/closing frame: Martín Quispe (X₃, foreman) as Witness
   - Analytical index (π = 1.15), can see C₂ as constructed, C₃ as Snare
   - Frame provides documentary context: "This is what I recorded in the margins"
   - Does NOT explain or interpret — presents Túpac's account as evidence
   - Witness voice is clinical, archival, not pedagogical
   - Creates gap: reader sees through Witness's analytical lens what Túpac cannot see through Condemned lens

4. **Why Not Other Archetypes:**
   - ✗ Builder: Would require Túpac to test for leverage, but he's normalized (accepts system)
   - ✗ Beneficiary: Túpac experiences extraction, not benefit (χ = 0.64, still positive)
   - ✗ System: Would require impersonal voice, but we need embodied experience
   - ✗ Guide: Would explain too much, collapse the gap between voice and reader
   - ✗ Scavenger: Túpac has normalized, not gaming gaps (that was X₁ trajectory)

5. **Tone Consequence:**
   - Condemned + Seeded Possibility → **Tragic, somber** (surface)
   - Witness frame → **Clinical, documentary** (frame)
   - Gap between Túpac's acceptance and reader's recognition of extraction → **Elegiac**
   - No comedy, no irony — the normalization is complete and real

**SELECTED:** Condemned (Túpac, X₂) nested in Witness (Martín, X₃)

## III. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected Strategy:** **Nested Voice with Analytical Observer**

**Structure:**

1. **Frame (Witness - Martín):**
   - Opens: "I wrote this in the margins of the official log, in Quechua, where the overseers could not read it."
   - Establishes documentary context: margin notes as evidence
   - Closes: Returns to present moment, margin-writing continues
   - Does NOT interpret Túpac's experience — presents it as recorded testimony

2. **Core (Condemned - Túpac):**
   - First-person account of second *mita* year
   - Experiences C₃ as Tangled Rope: system is corrupt but navigable
   - Experiences C₂ as Mountain: vara is simply how things are measured
   - No realization, no awakening — normalization is complete
   - Voice is immediate, material, focused on survival tactics

3. **Revelation Mechanism:**
   - Reader sees through Martín's analytical index (frame) what Túpac cannot see (core)
   - Martín's margin notes appear as fragments between Túpac's sections
   - Notes are in Quechua (or Quechua-inflected Spanish), untranslated
   - Context reveals content: "Mendoza, honest scale, high fee. Do not go."
   - Reader gradually recognizes: Martín sees the Snare, Túpac sees the Tangled Rope
   - No character explicitly states the variance — it emerges from juxtaposition

4. **Indexical Variance Made Visible:**
   - Same events (assay transaction, fee payment) narrated by Túpac
   - Same events annotated by Martín in margins
   - Túpac: "The fee is high, but Mendoza is honest. I will return to him."
   - Martín (margin): "Mendoza, 8% fee. Túpac does not see it anymore."
   - Reader performs the extraction math; neither character does

**Why This Strategy:**
- Preserves Condemned voice integrity (Túpac never becomes analytical)
- Provides analytical index without pedagogical explanation (Martín documents, doesn't teach)
- Makes indexical variance structural (two voices, two classifications, same constraint)
- Supports Seeded Possibility: surface (Túpac's normalization) + underground (Martín's documentation) both present

## IV. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** □ Flash □ Short ☑ Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)
- **Rationale:** Needs space for normalization to feel earned, margin notes to accumulate, nested structure to breathe. Short story too compressed; novelette allows biographical scope without requiring novel-length development.

**POV:** ☑ First Person (Túpac core) + ☑ First Person (Martín frame)
- **Rationale:** Condemned voice requires first person for immediacy. Witness frame in first person maintains documentary feel ("I recorded this") without omniscient distance.

**TENSE:** ☑ Past (Túpac core) + ☑ Present (Martín frame)
- **Rationale:** Túpac narrates completed *mita* year (biographical past). Martín writes in present moment ("I am writing this now"). Tense shift marks frame/core boundary.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 primary (Túpac, Martín, Mendoza the assayer) + collective background (other *mitayos*, overseers)
- **Rationale:** Minimum for constraint interactions. Túpac (Condemned), Martín (Witness), Mendoza (institutional position, experienced as Rope from his index). Other *mitayos* present as collective to show Túpac is not unique.

**NAMING:**
- ☑ Cultural (Quechua/Spanish historical names)
- **Rationale:** Setting is specific (Potosí 1683). Abstract names would break historical naturalization. Use period-appropriate names:
  - Túpac (Quechua, common *mitayo* name)
  - Martín Quispe (mestizo foreman: Spanish first name + Quechua surname)
  - Don Francisco de Ulloa (Spanish assayer: formal title + Castilian surname)

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

*Code-switching occurs:*
1. **Quechua → Spanish:** When Túpac addresses assayer, overseer, or market
   - Signals: performing compliance, entering institutional space
   - Example: Túpac speaks Quechua to fellow *mitayos*, switches to Spanish for "Señor ensayador, traigo cien marcos"

2. **Spanish → Quechua:** When Martín writes margin notes
   - Signals: covert documentation, resistance through opacity
   - Notes remain untranslated; context reveals meaning through Martín's actions

3. **Historical Spanish register:** All Spanish dialogue uses 1680s forms
   - Vosotros forms, future subjunctive, archaic vocabulary (*ensayador*, *marco*, *ley*)
   - Formal address: *Vuestra Merced* for officials, *Señor* for social superiors

*What the switch signals:*
- Quechua = intimacy, solidarity, truth outside the system
- Spanish = institutional performance, market transaction, imperial compliance
- The gap between languages is the gap between what can be said and what can be recorded

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (from constraint dynamics, not imposed)
- Exhaustion (biographical time under high extraction)
- Resignation (normalization complete, resistance abandoned)
- Quiet persistence (Martín's margin-writing as refusal to forget)
- Elegiac tone (reader mourns what Túpac can no longer see)

**ENDING STRATEGY:** ☑ Attractor reached (Seeded Possibility)
- Surface: Túpac completes second *mita*, returns to *ayllu*, will come back for third (normalization stable)
- Underground: Martín continues writing in margins, documentation accumulates
- Final image: Martín's hand writing in Quechua, official log open beside him, system continuing
- No resolution, no rupture, no awakening — tragedy with seeds

## V. INHABITATION SPEC

**Túpac (X₂, Condemned voice):**

*Phenomenological register:*
- **Mode of attention:** Material, tactical, immediate
- **What the body knows:** Which assayers weigh honestly by the steadiness of their hands on the scale. How long you can work a shaft before the air goes thin. The rhythm of the *mita* year — three months until your lungs stop burning, six months until you stop counting days, nine months until you forget what the *ayllu* smells like.
- **Physical markers of constraint:**
  - The weight of the ore sack (100 marcos) against your back — you know it by pressure, not number
  - The assayer's scale: you watch the needle settle, but you cannot read the increments
  - The paper (certification): lighter than the ore, but it is what moves through the market
  - Your hands: cracked, bleeding at the knuckles, the mountain's dust ground into the creases
- **What the constraint feels like:** The fee is not a percentage — it is the gap between what you carried and what you can buy. You brought enough ore to feed your family for two weeks; you leave with enough coin for ten days. The missing four days live in the assayer's pocket, but you do not think of it as theft because this is simply how the paper costs.

*Anti-pattern check:*
- ✗ "The fee was 8% of certified value" — this is a metric, not an experience
- ✓ "I carried a week's breaking on my back. The assayer burned a piece, wrote a number, took his portion. I walked away lighter in silver than I arrived in stone."

**Martín Quispe (X₃, Witness frame):**

*Phenomenological register:*
- **Mode of attention:** Documentary, analytical, bifurcated (sees both what is recorded and what is not)
- **What the body knows:** The weight of the pen in the margin versus the weight of the pen in the official log — same hand, different truth. The sound of Quechua in the shafts (intimacy) versus Spanish in the plaza (performance). Which *mitayos* will survive by the way they move — the ones who have learned to conserve breath, to test the rock before they strike.
- **Physical markers of constraint:**
  - The margin: narrow, unruled, space that does not officially exist
  - The ink: same ink for official log and margin notes, but the margin is where the truth goes
  - The official log: ruled lines, formal Spanish, what the overseers read
  - His position: standing between the *mitayos* (speaks their language) and the overseers (reports to them) — the body itself is the gap
- **What the constraint feels like:** You write in two languages in the same book, and the language the system cannot read is the one that matters, and you do this knowing that if the overseers learn Quechua, the margins will no longer be yours, so you write quickly, in the hour before dawn, when the overseers are still asleep and the *mitayos* are already in the shafts.

*Anti-pattern check:*
- ✗ "I documented the extraction rate at 0.8 epsilon" — this is framework terminology
- ✓ "I wrote in the margin: Mendoza, honest scale, high fee. Túpac does not see it anymore. I will write this until someone reads it, or until I die, whichever comes first."

## VI. NUMERIC REGISTER (GAUGE-INDEXED)

**Diegetic quantities:**
1. **The marco** (unit of weight for silver ore)
   - Exists as OBJECT: physical weights on the assayer's scale
   - Certified by the *vara* standard (C₂)
   - Appears in: assayer's certification papers, market transactions

2. **The real** (unit of currency)
   - Exists as OBJECT: silver coins, wage payments
   - Derived from marcos via certified ore value
   - Appears in: wage negotiations, market prices

3. **The ley** (percentage purity of ore)
   - Exists as OBJECT: written on certification papers
   - Produced by assayer's test (burning sample)
   - Appears in: assayer's shop, market transactions

**Positional access:**

*Túpac (X₂, Condemned):*
- **Can read:** Reales (counts coins), marcos (sees the weights on the scale, but cannot read fine increments)
- **Cannot read:** Ley percentage (illiterate in Spanish numbers), vara measurements (never sees the official rod)
- **Experiences quantities as:** Weight (ore sack on back), coin (in hand), paper (certification, but cannot read the number written on it)

*Martín (X₃, Witness):*
- **Can read:** All quantities (literate in Spanish, keeps official logs)
- **Records in margins:** Quantities as evidence (fee percentages, survival rates, shaft depths)
- **Experiences quantities as:** Data points for documentation, not personal burden

*Mendoza (I₁, assayer):*
- **Can read:** All quantities (owns the instruments)
- **Produces:** Ley certifications (writes the number after testing)
- **Experiences quantities as:** Professional measurements, compensation calculations

**Narration rule:**

Numbers appear ONLY when a character with access ACTS ON THEM in-scene:
- ✓ Túpac watches Mendoza place weights on the scale, sees the needle settle
- ✓ Mendoza writes "82% ley" on the certification paper
- ✓ Martín records in the margin: "Mendoza, 8% fee"
- ✗ "The extraction rate was 0.8" (no character thinks in epsilon)
- ✗ "Túpac had worked for 73 days" (Túpac does not count days as numbers; he counts them as exhaustion)

**Scored-Snare exception (applies to C₃):**

The assay fee is denominated in marcos (inherent to the instrument), but Túpac's narration does NOT tally:
- ✗ "The fee was 6.8 marcos on 82 marcos of certified value"
- ✓ "I brought a week's breaking. The assayer took his portion. I left with ten days of coin instead of fourteen."

The reader can calculate the extraction if they want; Túpac experiences it as *gap* (what I carried vs. what I kept), not as *rate*.

## IX. ERROR MANIFESTATIONS

**E₁: Type I — False Mountain (C₂ at Túpac's index)**

*Scene:*
Túpac measures a shaft depth using a vara rod borrowed from the overseer. He marks the depth on the wall: "Fifteen varas to the vein." He does not question the vara's length, does not know it was chosen by Spanish decree, does not imagine measuring differently. The vara is simply how depth is measured — as natural as counting.

*Observable:*
- No questioning of the vara
- Uses system's units without hesitation
- Treats deviation from standard as objective fact (ore is "82% ley" — not "82% relative to the chosen standard")

*Martín's margin note (untranslated Quechua):*
"The vara is not ours. Túpac does not know this. The Spanish brought the rod from Seville. Before that, we measured differently. He has forgotten."

*Reader recognition:*
Martín sees C₂ as Rope (constructed, changeable). Túpac sees C₂ as Mountain (natural, unchangeable). Same constraint, different classifications. Neither character explains this to the reader — the gap is the story.

---

**E₃: Type V.a — Tangled-as-Rope (C₃ at Túpac's index)**

*Scene:*
A first-year *mitayo* complains to Túpac: "The assayer took half my silver. This is theft."

Túpac responds: "Mendoza is honest. His scale does not lie. The fee is high, yes, but the system works. You will learn. Work faster, bring more ore, the fee matters less."

The first-year *mitayo* stares at him, says nothing, walks away.

*Observable:*
- Túpac acknowledges extraction ("the fee is high")
- Túpac emphasizes coordination ("the system works," "Mendoza is honest")
- Túpac's advice: optimize within constraints, do not question constraints
- Partial error: correctly identifies hybrid nature (Tangled Rope), but misweights components (over-emphasizes coordination, under-emphasizes extraction)

*Martín's margin note:*
"Túpac told the first-year to work faster. He does not see that working faster only increases the assayer's take. The fee is a percentage. More ore = more fee. He has forgotten the math."

*Reader recognition:*
Túpac experiences χ = 0.64 (Tangled Rope). The first-year *mitayo* experiences χ = 0.96 (Snare). Martín sees χ = 0.736 (Snare, analytical). Same constraint, three classifications. Túpac's advice is rational from his index (moderate power, insider knowledge) but harmful from the first-year's index (powerless, no knowledge). The system extracts more from the first-year than from Túpac, but Túpac cannot see this because normalization has collapsed his perception.

---

**E₄: Indexical Variance Blindness (Cross-Agent)**

*Scene:*
Túpac and Martín walk together from the mine to the plaza. Túpac says (in Quechua): "Mendoza is a good assayer. The system is corrupt, but he is honest within it. If all assayers were like him, the *mita* would be bearable."

Martín says nothing for a moment, then: "Mendoza charges 8% of certified value. The same as the others."

Túpac: "Yes, but his scale is true. He does not skim."

Martín: "The fee itself is the skim."

Túpac: "The fee is the fee. It pays for the testing, the certification. Without it, we could not sell."

Martín stops walking, looks at Túpac, says: "You have forgotten what you knew in your first year."

Túpac: "I have learned what works."

They walk in silence. Martín writes in the margin that night: "Túpac defends Mendoza now. He cannot see that Mendoza's honesty does not reduce the extraction. The scale is true, but the fee is the trap. He has normalized."

*Observable:*
- Túpac: C₃ is Tangled Rope ("corrupt but functional")
- Martín: C₃ is Snare ("the fee itself is the skim")
- Both are describing the SAME constraint from different indices
- Neither character says "we are experiencing different constraint types" — they talk past each other
- Debate appears as value disagreement (Túpac: pragmatic, Martín: critical), but is actually indexical variance

*Reader recognition:*
The reader sees what neither character can articulate: Túpac's normalization (π shift from 1.5 to 1.0) has changed his classification of C₃ from Snare to Tangled Rope. Martín's analytical position (π = 1.15) maintains Snare classification. Both are objectively correct from their structural positions. The tragedy is not that one is wrong — it is that they cannot occupy each other's indices, and so they cannot coordinate resistance.
