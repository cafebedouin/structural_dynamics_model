REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The failed letter scene (L172-212) now enacts the gap it describes — Túpac tries system language, reaches for old words he cannot transcribe, tries Spanish (wrong register), tries Quechua again (still certification-shaped). The charcoal breaks. He writes smaller. Each attempt fails differently, making the reader feel the impossibility before the blank page arrives. The pacing shift from summary to enacted struggle works. The margins reveal scene (L244-318) maintains structural distance: Martín shows Túpac the record of his own transformation, Túpac reads it, and returns anyway. The final margin note ("No porque cambie algo. Porque la alternativa es el silencio") lands as archival necessity rather than resistance claim — Martín writes knowing the margins failed for Túpac, making the writing's function honest about its own limits.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The plaza dialogue scene (L214-243) still carries explanatory weight that undercuts the margins' structural work. Martín's line "You have learned to survive" reads as setup for the margin note's payoff, but the dialogue itself ("You knew something then that you do not know now") pre-interprets what the margins reveal scene will show. The scene would be stronger ending at the nod in the plaza, letting the margins reveal scene do all the work of showing Túpac what he's forgotten.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story maintains that Mendoza's scale is accurate (the *ley* percentages are true measurements) while the certification system itself is the extraction mechanism. There is no recoverable "fair fee" or "correct *tasa*" that a better authority could set — the fee structure is extraction-on-extraction by design (fee taken as percentage of already-reduced certified value). Túpac's transformation is not from "wrong about Mendoza" to "right about Mendoza" but from seeing the system as theft to seeing it as the price of doing business. The real (ore's state before certification) remains unreadable to the system; certification creates tradeable paper while the stone's actual composition becomes irrelevant to commerce.

READINESS:
The story is structurally sound and the invariant holds. The plaza dialogue weakness is minor — the scene functions, it just does slightly more interpretive work than necessary. The prose is alive in the enacted struggle scenes and honest about archival limits in the final margin note.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
