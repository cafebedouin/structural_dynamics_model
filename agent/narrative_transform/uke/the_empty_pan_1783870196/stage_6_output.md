# STRATEGY BRIEF

**IMPLIED AUTHOR:**
A documentarian of structural transformation. This author notices: the gap between honest instruments and extractive systems, the language people acquire as they normalize, the moment recognition arrives without producing change, the archival impulse that records cost rather than output. Skips: villains, redemption, escape, the possibility that better measurement could restore justice. The prose lives in plain register — no lyricism, no interiority that the character himself couldn't articulate — because the horror is in what becomes ordinary, not in what remains shocking.

**AUDIENCE:**
Readers familiar with how systems teach their own justification. Assumes: comfort with structural readings over character psychology, tolerance for transformation-without-resolution, interest in the gap between what can be measured and what can be said. Does not assume: knowledge of colonial *mita* specifics (the story provides context), Spanish/Quechua fluency (translation is structural, not ornamental), or need for Túpac to "wake up" at the end.

**SCOPE:**
Short story. Target range: 3,400–3,800 words. Current length (3,626 words) sits in the target zone. The material has found its natural compression — the two-*mita* structure, the Sami death as hinge, the margins reveal as ending. No expansion needed; tightening pass to remove the one over-explained dialogue and sharpen the failed letter scene.

**CREATIVE DEEPENING:**
The failed letter after Sami's death (currently L172-196) is where the story's substrate becomes visible: Túpac confronts the gap between certification language (which he has learned to use) and loss (which that language cannot hold). The scene currently states this gap; it should *enact* it. Show Túpac trying different formulations — attempting to write what happened in the system's terms, recognizing the mismatch, reaching for the old words he spoke on the mountain but cannot transcribe. The blank page with only Sami's name should arrive after visible struggle, not summary. This is the story's pressure point: the moment the character sees what the margins will later name.

The margins themselves deepen if they're not just counter-archive but *failed counter-archive* — Martín writes in Quechua "so we do not forget," but Túpac reads his own transformation and still returns to Mendoza. The margins document; they do not prevent. That gap (between recording and changing) should be present in Martín's final entries: he writes knowing it may not matter, writes anyway because the alternative is letting the ruled lines be the only record.

**FLINCH POINTS:**

1. **Plaza dialogue (L207-235)**: Currently doing: having Martín explain the structural reading ("The fee itself is the corruption," "You have forgotten what you are surviving"). What honesty requires: Let the impasse stand without interpretation. End the scene at "I have learned what works" — the gap between Martín's knowledge and Túpac's is already visible. The margin note does the naming; the dialogue should only show the distance.

2. **Túpac's return decision (L286-295)**: Currently doing: treating the return as inevitable, systemic, almost dignified ("This was what I had learned"). What honesty requires: The return is a defeat, not a rhythm. Túpac has learned to survive by forgetting what survival costs. The passage should not soften this into "the system continues" — it should let the reader feel the weight of Túpac choosing to go back, knowing (because he read the margins) what will happen to his knowing.

**PATTERN CHECK:**
Concern. The ending currently resolves into archival hope: "I will keep writing," the margins as resistance, documentation as meaningful even if it doesn't change the system. This is the pipeline's default — quiet rebellion, organizational dignity, the comfort that someone is recording. But the story's own logic undercuts this: Túpac read the margins and still defends Mendoza. The margins *failed* for him. Martín's final notes should carry that failure, not transcend it. He writes because the alternative is worse, not because writing is resistance. The distinction matters.
