# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

**Selected: Seeded Possibility** ✓

Verification against Stage 1 requirements:
- BIR model confirmed (bounded satisficing under uncertainty, not utility-maximization) — compatible with Seeded Possibility per matrix.
- Tangled Rope dominant (C3, χ=0.90 for Mereth) with Piton-adjacent deferred constraints (C6, C7) — compatible.
- Break-contract structure (external ambiguity persisting / internal transformation completing) matches Seeded Possibility's signature exactly: the tribunal-and-Charter layer does not resolve cleanly (missing floor invariant guarantees this), while something underground — a recognition, a debt finally spoken in the right register — completes or seeds itself without needing Guild ratification.
- Ruled out: Negotiated Equilibrium (would require the external layer to reach stable settlement — blocked by missing_floor invariant); Deterministic Tragedy (would require no seed to survive, but the invariant contract requires the grain-recognition to persist as something the system cannot destroy); Revolutionary Rupture (no BIR-compatible mechanism for total system overturn exists in this network — TR3's blocking omega and TR1's precondition chain both specify partial, negotiated, incomplete shifts, not rupture).

**Confirmed.**

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Mereth) — Split POV / Chorus variant with The Scavenger (Ossin)**

**Justification for Mereth as Condemned:**
- Mereth's index (moderate power under C2, powerless under C3 — π=1.5) and PIR-adjacent *self-model* (she experiences her own lie as fused identity, not strategic position — "you can no longer feel your own pulse quicken") matches the Condemned's core requirement: acts rationally within her structural position, cannot see the system's shape as constructed, experiences the constraint as load rather than architecture.
- Attractor compatibility: Condemned ✓ Seeded Possibility — confirmed compatible per matrix.
- Critically, Mereth is a **Misrecognition Arc** case: she believes herself a Builder (she thinks she is managing the lie strategically, season to season, tribunal to tribunal) but she is structurally a Condemned (the lie has fused into her identity via C1's identity-locked exit; she cannot actually see that stepping down or recanting is legally available, only cognitively unthinkable). This gap — what she believes about herself vs. what the constraint topology shows — is the story's engine on her side.

**Secondary: Ossin as Scavenger, not Builder.**
- Ossin's position (π=1.5 also, powerless/outlawed, but with organized/constrained exit rather than trapped) reads correctly as Scavenger: he has no interest in reforming the Guild, no interest in understanding *why* the tribunal excludes diver-testimony — he has simply mapped where the Charter's boundary-buoys are least enforced and dives there. His voice must stay material, transactional, uninterested in structural analysis. He must never philosophize about the Guild's illegitimacy — he must simply act on the gaps in it.
- This is a deliberate deviation from a more obvious Builder casting. Builder would require Ossin to be testing for leverage to change the *outcome* of his own exile. He isn't. He wants to dive, get paid, and trust his grain. The Scavenger's wry, practical register also supplies tonal counterweight to Mereth's tragic register — Double register, not Double-Blind, since neither voice is comic-blind the way a Beneficiary or Fool would be; both see clearly within their own lane, just different lanes.

**Tertiary, non-POV but present in blueprint: Torvel as a Zealot approaching Condemned.**
- Torvel is not given POV chapters (see editorial decisions below) but his interiority must be legible through dialogue and behavior as Zealot-register: Guild-tongue as identity, not performance. He is included as a structural pressure point, not a third narrating voice — a third POV would dilute the Chorus into a triptych and risk the "every character explains their function" failure mode.

**Voice combination strategy: Chorus (2-voice) with Counterpoint on the physical marker.**
Mereth and Ossin alternate. Both are inside constraint logic (Condemned/Scavenger, not Witness), so neither sees the system's shape whole — but they see *different* slices of it, and their slices collide in the shared stairwell-debt history without either becoming an authorial mouthpiece.

**Master diagnostic check:**
- Does this voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot? Mereth cannot see that C1's exit-lock is cognitive, not structural (Error E3 — False Mountain) — the reader, watching her treat "stepping down" as unthinkable while nothing prevents it, sees the constructedness she can't. Ossin cannot see (or care) that his outlaw status is itself downstream of Mereth's unspoken debt to him — he reads his exile as simply the Guild's stupidity, not as a structure someone is actively maintaining on his behalf. Both blindnesses are load-bearing and distinct.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**Selected: Multi-POV Parallel** with elements of **Late Revelation** on the Torvel axis.

Mereth and Ossin's chapters alternate, and the reader sees the variance in how each experiences "the debt under the water" (Mereth: noose, tightening; Ossin: irrelevant, or at most a mild historical fact) well before either character says so explicitly to the other. Torvel's leverage-without-cost position (χ=−0.12, net beneficiary) is withheld from full disclosure until late — the reader suspects it from his Guild-tongue rigidity but doesn't see the mechanism confirmed until the tribunal scene, which functions as the Late Revelation beat layered onto the ongoing Parallel structure.

This satisfies the **Double register / Counterpoint** requirement (Section on voice combinations) without collapsing into Double-Blind, since neither Mereth nor Ossin is comedy-blind — the counterpoint here is tragic-vs-pragmatic, not tragic-vs-oblivious.

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The palm against the hull** (from the substrate inhabitation sentence) is the master marker, but it needs a second, complementary marker for the surface world to counterpoint it, per the Counterpoint voice strategy.

**Primary marker (underwater/grain register): the held-breath count that is never spoken as a number.**
Divers know their own breath-margin by feel — a pressure behind the sternum, a taste of copper at the edges of vision — never by a gauge reading recited in the text. This is the inhabitation register for both Mereth and Ossin: hypervigilant, body-first attention to margin and current, never rendered as ambient countdown.

**Secondary marker (surface/tribunal register): the sound of the gavel and the dryness of the paneled room.**
Torvel's world is measured in things that click, stamp, and echo — dry sounds — as counterpoint to the marsh-cant world of held breath and current-read. When Mereth must translate a dive into testimony, the prose should register the *wrongness* of trying to fit a held-breath fact into a dry-room instrument, without stating the wrongness analytically.

**Counterpoint deployment:** In at least one scene, Mereth stands in the tribunal room (dry, gaveled, numbered) narrating a dive from months prior while her body — in the scene's present moment, not the recollected past — performs an unconscious breath-holding gesture the reader can see and the tribunal cannot read.

### Validation Notes

- **Blurb test:** Mereth's Condemned-via-Misrecognition voice cannot say "I have mistaken my identity-fusion with Wardenship for a strategic choice" — the archetype's ceiling forbids this level of self-narration; the understanding is distributed instead across the scene where her body rejects the information she's just been told (E3), which the reader can name and she cannot. Ossin's Scavenger voice cannot say "the Guild's exclusion of diver testimony is what enables men like Torvel" — he has no analytical register; that understanding lives in the reader's assembly of E4 against his practical experience. No single character holds the thesis. **Pass.**

- **Irresolvable tension:** Torvel's costless legitimacy (χ=−0.12) is *sympathetic* — he was a drowning child once, pulled out by an outlaw, and has built an entire gleaming, genuinely competent judicial career partly in gratitude-shaped denial. The reader cannot comfortably want him punished without also wanting to punish a boy for having been rescued. This is the marked irresolvable tension Stage 4 must write toward, not around.
