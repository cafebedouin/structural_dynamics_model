  Political:

  Temporal:

    - 3-5 year transition period for existing pipelines

  Person-hours: ~200,000 (rulemaking, industry compliance, replication infrastructure)

  Opportunity cost: Delays beneficial innovations; alternative is better pre-deployment testing protocols

  Optimistic: 3 years (crisis → rapid rulemaking → implementation)
  Realistic: 7-10 years (normal regulatory process, industry negotiation)
  Catastrophe-contingent: 18 months IF major deployment failure (AI harm, medical device disaster, financial algorithm crash)

     - Cannot bypass (need their authority)
