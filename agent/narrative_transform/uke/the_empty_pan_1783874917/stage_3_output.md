# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor:** Seeded Possibility

**Verification Against Constraint Logic:**

✓ **Mountain Foundation (C₁):** The invariant (shoujue collapsing under observation) is unchangeable. Any resolution must work within this constraint. The underground network doesn't solve the measurement impossibility — it routes around it by abandoning certification as the measure of mastery.

✓ **BIR Institutional Model:** Supervisor Liu and the Directorate satisfice rather than maximize. They tolerate underground practice as long as:
- Surface compliance is maintained (certified artisans still exist)
- Palace procurement continues (the official system functions)
- The underground doesn't openly challenge the system's legitimacy

✓ **High-Variance Snare (C₃):** The certification examination is a Snare for X₁ (χ=1.08), Rope for X₃ (χ=-0.144). This creates the structural conditions for underground/overground split: powerless artisans experience trap, institutional agents experience coordination. The underground emerges in the gap between these two realities.

✓ **Transformation Rule Accessibility:** TR₂ (analytical detachment) is blocked for Chen Shu (she can't exit to pure analytical position — she needs to eat). TR₁ (collective organization) is partially accessible — not formal guild organization (C₃ suppression=0.9 prevents that), but informal networks that share knowledge and clients outside the certification system.

**Attractor Mechanics Confirmed:**
- Surface: Official system persists, Supervisor Liu's coordination mechanism remains stable
- Underground: Uncertified artisans develop reputation-based trade networks, share knowledge of shoujue in unobserved practice
- Terminal State: Chen Shu fails the examination but survives through underground network; Master Feng retires knowing his knowledge will persist outside the official system; Supervisor Liu never knows the underground exists

**ATTRACTOR VERIFIED: Seeded Possibility is structurally compatible.**

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Primary Voice: The Condemned**
**Secondary Voice: The Witness (brief interludes)**

**Justification:**

**Why Condemned for X₁ (Chen Shu):**
- Inside the constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
- Experiences certification as natural/inevitable ("this is how mastery is measured")
- Acts rationally given limited information (practices for examination, tries to perform shoujue on command)
- Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
- **Critical match:** The Condemned's structural blindness preserves the invariant. Chen Shu cannot articulate "the examination measures performance, not the unobserved state" because from her position that analysis is not available. She experiences the examination as a test she is failing, not as a system that cannot measure what she knows.

**Why Witness for X₂ (Master Feng):**
- Observes constraint dynamics without direct stake (he's already certified, retiring soon)
- Can see multiple indices simultaneously (remembers his own examination, watches Chen Shu's struggle, understands Supervisor Liu's position)
- Reports what happens without judgment or intervention (he loans her the fee, teaches her the vocabulary, but knows it probably won't be enough)
- Voice is documentary, measured, elegiac

**Attractor Compatibility Check:**
- ✓ Condemned + Seeded Possibility: The Condemned cannot see the underground network forming, but can stumble into it. The seed is visible to the reader (through Witness interludes showing other uncertified artisans surviving) but not stated by the Condemned.
- ✓ Witness + Seeded Possibility: Master Feng can see the possibility without naming it. His final interlude can show him recognizing that Chen Shu will survive differently, without explaining the mechanism.

**Voice Combination Strategy: Nested Voice**
- Primary: Condemned (Chen Shu's immediate experience)
- Framing: Witness (Master Feng's interludes provide analytical distance)
- The Witness frames but does not explain. He observes Chen Shu's hands becoming wooden under examination pressure. He notices other uncertified dyers trading on reputation. He does not connect these observations into a thesis about underground networks — he simply reports what he sees.

**POV Implementation:**
- Chen Shu: First person, present tense (immediacy, no analytical distance)
- Master Feng: Third person limited, past tense (observational distance, retrospective clarity)

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: Gradual Realization (with structural limits)**

**Mechanism:**
Chen Shu slowly discovers that other uncertified artisans experience the examination differently:
- Some have failed multiple times but continue working (she thought failure meant exit)
- Some trade on reputation rather than certification (she thought certification was mandatory for all work)
- Some have clients who prefer their work precisely because it's not standardized to the guanchi baseline (she thought the baseline was universal quality)

**Structural Limits (Condemned Voice Constraint):**
Chen Shu never articulates "the system is extractive" or "the examination is inaccurate." She experiences these discoveries as:
- "Old Dyer Wu failed four times but still has clients — how?"
- "This merchant prefers my red to the certified dyers' red — why?"
- "Master Feng says the examination measures performance, not mastery — what does that mean?"

The reader performs the indexical analysis. Chen Shu experiences the variance without theorizing it.

**Witness Interludes (Master Feng):**
Provide the analytical register Chen Shu cannot access:
- Interlude 1 (early): Master Feng remembers his own examination, recognizes the demonstration format makes hands stupid
- Interlude 2 (mid): Master Feng notices the underground network forming (uncertified artisans sharing clients, trading on reputation)
- Interlude 3 (late): Master Feng watches Chen Shu's examination, knows she will fail, knows she will survive anyway

The Witness does not explain the system to the reader. He observes the indexical variance (Chen Shu's experience vs. his own vs. Supervisor Liu's) without synthesizing it into theory.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Short story (4,000-5,000 words)
- Rationale: The invariant (shoujue collapsing under observation) is intimate and immediate. Needs space for the examination scene to breathe, but not novelette length. The constraint network is tight (3 constraints, 1 primary transformation rule) — doesn't require extended development.

**POV:** 
- Primary: First person (Chen Shu)
- Secondary: Third person limited (Master Feng interludes)

**TENSE:**
- Primary: Present tense (Chen Shu) — immediacy, no retrospective distance
- Secondary: Past tense (Master Feng) — observational distance, elegiac tone

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 primary (Chen Shu, Master Feng, Supervisor Liu), 2-3 secondary (other uncertified artisans in underground network)

**NAMING:** Cultural (Chinese names with pinyin romanization)
- Chen Shu (陳淑) — primary POV
- Master Feng Qiao (馮喬) — secondary POV, always called "Master Feng" by Chen Shu
- Supervisor Liu Han (劉瀚) — appears only through others' eyes, always called "Supervisor Liu" or "the inspector"

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**
Code-switching occurs in three contexts:

1. **Workshop (Vernacular):**
   - Chen Shu speaking to Master Feng: particles (*ne*, *ba*), intimate register
   - Master Feng speaking to Chen Shu: particles, teaching register
   - Render as: "The root is ready, ne?" / "Let it steep longer, ba"

2. **Examination (Attempted Classical):**
   - Chen Shu speaking to Supervisor Liu: formal register, grammatical awkwardness (she's translating from Vernacular to Classical in her head)
   - Supervisor Liu speaking to Chen Shu: formal register, compressed syntax
   - Render as: Topic-comment structure, honorifics (*daren*, "this humble one"), occasional Vernacular slippage when Chen Shu loses the formal register under pressure

3. **Internal Monologue (Vernacular):**
   - Chen Shu's thoughts: Vernacular grammar, particles, immediate sensory language
   - Render as: "My hands are wood already. The examiner has not even spoken, and my hands are wood."

**Code-switch signals:**
- Vernacular → Classical: Chen Shu's anxiety spikes, her internal monologue becomes fragmented
- Classical → Vernacular: Chen Shu loses control of the formal register, slips into workshop language (this is when she knows she's failing)

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** 
Grief for the interval. The thing her hands knew in the dark, alone, that dies under observation. Not anger at the system (she doesn't have the analytical position to be angry at the system). Not hope for reform (she doesn't imagine the system could be different). Just grief for the loss of the state where her hands were certain.

**ENDING STRATEGY:** 
Attractor reached (Seeded Possibility):
- Chen Shu fails the examination (the invariant is preserved — shoujue cannot be certified)
- Master Feng's final interlude shows him noticing she has found work with Old Dyer Wu's network (the underground is seeded)
- Supervisor Liu never appears in the ending (the official system persists, unaware of the underground)
- Final image: Chen Shu's hands in the pre-dawn workshop, alone, the interval returning now that no one is watching

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

**Primary Physical Marker: The hands' certainty/uncertainty**

**Phenomenological Register (Chen Shu):**

**Mode of attention:** Somatic hypervigilance. Chen Shu tracks her hands' state constantly:
- Morning: Do her hands know the interval today, or are they uncertain?
- Workshop: Can she feel the moment the madder root is ready, or does she have to guess?
- Examination: Are her hands wood, or can she make them move?

**What the body has learned that the mind hasn't named:**
- The interval arrives in the body before conscious thought. Her hands know the root is ready a half-breath before she could articulate why.
- Under observation, the body's knowing stops arriving. Her hands wait for conscious instruction, which is always too late.
- The examination format (four hours, three officials watching) makes her body stupid. Not because she's nervous (though she is), but because the state her hands need — unobserved, unhurried, alone — is structurally absent.

**What the character notices about the physical world:**
- The weight of the fabric in her hands (heavy when wet, light when the dye has set)
- The smell of the mordant (sharp when fresh, flat when exhausted)
- The color of the dye bath (deep red when the root is giving, brownish when it's done)
- The texture of her own hands (stained, cracked, certain in the dark, wooden in the light)

**Specific physical details Stage 4 should discover:**
- The moment her hands become wood (when? what triggers it? is it the examiner's voice, or the lamplight, or the awareness of being watched?)
- The moment the interval returns (after the examination, in the pre-dawn workshop, when she's alone again — what does it feel like when certainty comes back?)
- The physical difference between performing the demonstration (hands moving through memorized steps) and inhabiting shoujue (hands moving before thought)

**ANTI-PATTERN CHECK:**
- ✗ "Her skill level decreased from 8/10 to 5/10 under pressure" — this is a metric
- ✓ "Her hands, which knew the interval in the dark, became wood under the lamplight" — this is an experience
