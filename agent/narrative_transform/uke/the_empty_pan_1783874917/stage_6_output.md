**CREATIVE DEEPENING:**
The discovery report identifies the examination's double bind as latent potential: Chen Shu must perform competence under observation, but observation destroys the substrate being measured. The story currently states this clearly but could *embody* it more fully through structural rhythm. The examination scenes should feel different in prose texture from the workshop scenes — not just described as different, but enacted through sentence rhythm, sensory detail, temporal compression. When Chen Shu's hands "become wood," the prose itself should become more effortful, more thought-mediated, less fluid.

The Prolog extraction masking reveals the story is structurally about **the interval** (the breath-to-action gap where body-knowledge lives) versus **the prescribed period** (bureaucratic time that makes practice illegal). These two temporalities never reconcile. The craft passes should ensure every major scene operates in one temporality or the other, making the collision visceral rather than conceptual.

**FLINCH POINTS:**

1. **Feng's forgiveness of the debt (L~420)**: The draft resolves this through quiet generosity — Feng forgives the debt, Chen Shu accepts, both know she cannot repay. What honesty requires: the weight of unpayable debt doesn't disappear when forgiven. It transforms into obligation, into the knowledge that her continued practice costs him money he will never recover. The scene should let this sit without dignifying it into meaning.

2. **Wu's survival in the margins (L~580-620)**: The draft presents Wu's illegal practice as sustainable — he's been doing it for years, pays fines when he can, negotiates when he can't. What honesty requires: the story's own economic facts contradict this. Wu's wife left, he still owes the moneylender, his workshop is a storage closet. The draft should acknowledge that "surviving" in these conditions is a slow collapse, not a stable alternative.

3. **The ending's continuation (L~780-end)**: Chen Shu sits by the vat, hands steady, interval present, work continuing. The draft ends on persistence without acknowledging the math: debt growing faster than she can pay, inspector coming monthly, tools subject to confiscation. What honesty requires: not a darker ending, but one that holds the contradiction — the interval is real AND the conditions are unsustainable. Both true simultaneously.

**PATTERN CHECK:**
CONCERN. The story defaults to quiet resistance (Chen Shu working illegally), systemic framing (the examination measures the wrong thing), and composure under duress (hands steady, work continuing). These emerge organically from the material and are appropriate to the setting. However, the flinch points suggest the draft is softening the economic consequences to preserve the dignity of persistence. The pressure test: Does the story pay the full cost of Chen Shu's choice to continue working, or does it convert that cost into meaning? Currently it leans toward the latter. The craft passes should let the cost remain cost.
