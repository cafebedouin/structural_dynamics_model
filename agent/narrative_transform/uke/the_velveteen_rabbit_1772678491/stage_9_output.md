# REVIEW ASSESSMENT

## STRENGTHS:

The story's architecture of refusal is its greatest achievement. It refuses to explain the impossible recovery, refuses to make Patterson a simple villain, refuses to give the workers' testimony structural efficacy—and these refusals create a devastating portrait of how institutional violence operates through people doing their jobs correctly. The multi-perspectival structure (Morrison's medical documentation, Patterson's ledger, Ah-Yong's phenomenological experience) creates a prismatic view where each perspective is accurate yet inadequate, showing how complicity functions through fragmented knowledge. The breath-counting motif works as both medical symptom and existential measure, grounding abstract violence in bodily experience without becoming metaphorical.

## BIGGEST WEAKNESS:

Part VI loses dramatic tension in its final third. After Ah-Yong's departure (the story's emotional climax), Morrison's closing meditation extends for 400 words without new dramatic information. The repetition of "I have documented" becomes rhythmic rather than revelatory. The final image of Ah-Yong "walking toward something I could not see" is powerful, but the subsequent paragraphs dilute rather than deepen it. The story has already shown Morrison's inadequacy; his explicit acknowledgment of it ("I do not have the language for this") names what the structure has already demonstrated.

## READINESS:

The story is nearly ready for publication. The core architecture is sound, the refusals are powerful, and the prose is alive. One focused pass on Part VI's closing to trust the departure scene as the emotional terminus would complete the work.

## ROUTE: STRATEGY

The weakness is specific and addressable: Part VI's final 400 words extend past the story's natural emotional conclusion. A strategy brief targeting this section's compression (trusting Ah-Yong's departure as terminus, removing Morrison's explicit acknowledgment of inadequacy that the structure already shows) would bring the story to publication readiness. The cost of one more editorial pass is justified by the precision of the remaining issue.
