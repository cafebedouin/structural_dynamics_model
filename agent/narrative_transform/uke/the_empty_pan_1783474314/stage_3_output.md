## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: Gradual Realization (with structural limits)**

**Implementation:**

Liang Mei begins knowing the scores are wrong (local knowledge) but not seeing the full constraint network (systemic blindness). Over the course of the narrative:

1. **Act 1:** Experiences C₂ as trap, knows it's systematic, doesn't see why it persists
2. **Act 2:** Encounters Chen Wei's complicity (C₃), begins to see administrative layer
3. **Act 3:** Glimpses Director Huang's position (through overheard conversation, leaked memo, or Chen Wei's frustrated explanation), recognizes beneficiary blindness

**Critical constraint:** Liang Mei never achieves full systemic vision. She sees *more* by the end, but not *everything*. The Condemned voice cannot become the Witness. Her realization is:

- "They don't fix it because fixing it would disrupt *their* system"

Not:

- "The constraint topology shows χ=-0.144 for institutional agents, creating beneficiary blindness through false CI_rope signature"

The reader sees the full structure. Liang Mei sees enough to recognize the trap is maintained, not enough to map the entire network.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Short story (4,000-5,000 words)

**Justification:** Three-act structure with two POVs, single constraint network, needs space for daily texture but not novelette scope. 4-5k allows:
- Act 1: Establish Liang Mei's daily experience (1,200 words)
- Act 2: Introduce Chen Wei's complicity, show collision (1,500 words)
- Act 3: Glimpse Director Huang's position, seed revelation (1,300-1,800 words)

**POV:** First person (Liang Mei primary) + Third person limited (Director Huang secondary)

**Justification:**
- First person for Condemned voice: immediacy, experiential, trapped inside the constraint
- Third limited for Beneficiary: maintains her structural blindness while allowing reader to see it
- POV shift itself signals indexical variance — same world, different visibility

**TENSE:** Present (Liang Mei) + Present (Director Huang)

**Justification:**
- Present tense for both = no retrospective distance, no future escape
- Liang Mei in present: trapped in ongoing extraction, no narrative escape hatch
- Director Huang in present: her comfort is current, not historical

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 (Liang Mei primary, Chen Wei secondary, Director Huang tertiary)

**Justification:**
- Liang Mei: Condemned, carries reader through daily extraction
- Chen Wei: Tangled Rope, shows complicity burden (brief appearance, Act 2)
- Director Huang: Beneficiary, shows structural blindness (brief appearance, Act 3)
- Minimal cast focuses attention on constraint dynamics rather than character relationships

**NAMING:** Cultural (Chinese names with Pinyin romanization)

**Justification:**
- Setting demands cultural authenticity
- Names carry generational/class information (see Stage 2 naming traditions)
- Liang Mei, Chen Wei, Director Huang — each name signals position

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

Code-switching occurs at moments of institutional performance vs. private recognition:

1. **Liang Mei switches to English when:**
   - Interfacing with Assessment Network terminal
   - Speaking to Chen Wei in official capacity
   - Submitting compliance documentation

2. **Liang Mei uses Mandarin when:**
   - Speaking to family
   - Private conversations with other supervisors
   - Internal monologue about the trap

3. **What the switch signals:**
   - English = performing institutional compliance, public voice
   - Mandarin = private knowledge, authentic self
   - The gap between languages is the gap between what she must say and what she knows

4. **Stage 4 directive:**
   - Use italics for Mandarin phrases with English translation in context
   - Do not translate common terms (Assessment Network, Platform 47)
   - Code-switch at moments of maximum tension between compliance and recognition

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** Exhaustion

Not rage (too active), not despair (too passive). Exhaustion — the daily grind of maintaining a system that extracts from you, the weight of knowing it's wrong and submitting the scores anyway, the tiredness that comes from carrying a burden you cannot put down.

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Seeded Possibility

Surface: Liang Mei submits the scores. The system persists. Complicity continues.

Underground: She teaches her daughter to read the algorithm's bias. The recognition passes to the next generation. The seed is planted in what she says at night, in Mandarin, when the terminal is off.

## 6. ACT STRUCTURE

### Act 1: Constraint Activation (1,200 words)

**Liang Mei's daily experience:**

Opens with Liang Mei at the Assessment Network terminal, inputting harvest data. Present tense, first person. The terminal hums. She knows the score will be wrong before it appears.

**Physical beats:**
- Tank inspection (visual assessment, hands in water, temperature check)
- Sample collection (protocol, muscle memory)
- Terminal interface (English language, loading screen, score appears)
- The number: 73. She knows it should be 82.
- Code-switch: She mutters in Mandarin, *fēnshù xiànjǐng* (score trap)

**Establish C₁ (Mountain):**
- Brief internal recognition: measurement always has ±3 point error
- This is not that. This is systematic.
- She accepts the ±3 (natural law). She rages at the -9 (constructed bias).

**Establish C₂ (Snare):**
- Cannot escape without leaving aquaculture
- Identity-locked: "I am a harvest supervisor" = "I submit scores"
- Economic trap: income 23% below Platform 52
- Her daughter wants to study engineering (can't afford tutoring)

**End Act 1:**
- Liang Mei submits the score
- Goes home to cramped unit, thin walls, daughter's homework
- The exhaustion is physical: jaw ache, shoulder tension, the weight of the terminal's hum still in her ears

### Act 2: Constraint Coupling (1,500 words)

**Chen Wei appears:**

Liang Mei is called to Chen Wei's office (still first person, her POV). He wants to discuss "optimization strategies" (English phrase). She knows what this means: work within the biased system, don't complain.

**Physical beats:**
- Chen Wei's office: better lighting, quieter, climate-controlled
- He speaks Mandarin to signal intimacy: "I understand your frustration, but..."
- She responds in English: "The protocol is clear." (Performing compliance)
- He switches back to English: "Satisfactory compliance is the goal."

**Establish C₃ (Tangled Rope):**
- Chen Wei is not cruel. He is tired.
- He knows the scores are wrong. He defends them anyway.
- Brief glimpse of his complicity burden: he looks away when she asks directly, "Why don't they fix it?"
- His answer (Mandarin, attempting intimacy): "The system works well enough."

**Collision:**
- Liang Mei sees that Chen Wei knows
- She sees that he defends it anyway
- She sees that this costs him something (he looks tired, older than 41)
- But she also sees that he will not help her

**End Act 2:**
- Liang Mei returns to harvest floor
- Submits more scores
- The exhaustion deepens: now she knows the administrators know
- The trap has a second layer: not just the algorithm, but the people who maintain it

### Act 3: Attractor Approach (1,300-1,800 words)

**Director Huang appears (POV shift to third person limited):**

Brief section (300-400 words) from Director Huang's perspective. Present tense, third limited. She is reviewing quarterly reports in her Tianjin office.

**Physical beats:**
- The reports show "satisfactory compliance" across all platforms
- Platform 47's efficiency metrics are strong (high harvest volume, low contamination)
- The bias is noted in a footnote: "Minor calibration variance, within acceptable parameters"
- She approves the report. The system works.

**Establish E₁ (Type III error — Snare-as-Rope):**
- Director Huang genuinely sees only coordination value
- The extraction is invisible from seventeen floors up
- She is not lying. She is structurally blind.
- Her comfort is real. The numbers are clean.

**Return to Liang Mei (first person):**

Liang Mei overhears Chen Wei on a call with Director Huang (or reads a leaked memo, or Chen Wei explains in frustration). She glimpses the beneficiary position:

- "Director Huang says the system is functioning optimally"
- "She doesn't understand why Platform 47 keeps complaining"
- "The transition cost would be too high"

**Realization (bounded):**
- Liang Mei sees: they don't fix it because fixing it would disrupt *their* system
- She sees: Director Huang is not evil, just blind
- She sees: the trap is maintained by people who don't experience it as a trap
- She does NOT see: the full constraint topology, the χ values, the indexical variance formula

**Surface tragedy:**
- Liang Mei submits more scores
- The system persists
- She is exhausted, but she continues

**Underground seed:**
- Final scene: Liang Mei at home, daughter doing homework
- Daughter asks (Mandarin): "Why is your work so hard?"
- Liang Mei explains (Mandarin): "The algorithm has a bias. It underscores our platform."
- Daughter: "Can they fix it?"
- Liang Mei: "They can. They don't."
- Daughter: "Why?"
- Liang Mei: "Because from where they stand, it works."

**The seed:**
- Daughter writes this down in her notebook
- Not for school. For herself.
- The recognition passes to the next generation
- The alternative logic persists in hidden form

**End Act 3:**
- Liang Mei returns to the terminal the next day
- The hum continues
- She submits the scores
- The exhaustion is permanent
- But her daughter knows
