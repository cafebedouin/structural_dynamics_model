## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)
- Justification: Negotiated Equilibrium requires time to show bargaining process. Multiple POVs need development. Drift acceleration needs narrative space to feel gradual.

**POV:** Third Limited (Dr. Okonkwo's perspective)
- Justification: Witness archetype works in third limited. Maintains analytical distance while allowing access to her internal process. Can report others' speech/behavior without omniscience.

**TENSE:** Past
- Justification: Research retrospective. Dr. Okonkwo is documenting what happened during her six-month stay. Past tense naturalizes analytical distance.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 4 primary (Maya, Rafael, Amihan, Dr. Okonkwo)
- Justification: Minimum for indexical variance (3 embedded agents at different power positions + 1 analytical observer). More would dilute focus.

**NAMING:** Cultural (Filipino names for crew, Nigerian-British name for researcher)
- Justification: Orbital setting is multicultural. Names signal cultural identity without exoticizing. Code-switching already marks linguistic identity.

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**
- **Code-switching signals:**
  - Filipino (italicized) = intimacy, private speech, off-record
  - Technical Tagalog (standard text) = operational, work language
  - Institutional English (standard text) = formal, hierarchical, on-record
- **Switching occurs:**
  - When authority enters (Filipino → English)
  - When dropping professional mask (English → Filipino)
  - When shifting from work to emotion (Technical Tagalog → Filipino)
- **Dr. Okonkwo's linguistic position:**
  - Speaks Institutional English natively
  - Learning Technical Tagalog (functional but outsider accent)
  - Studying Bahay-Tagalog through recordings (understands but doesn't speak fluently)
  - Her linguistic limitations are part of her analytical distance

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** Exhaustion of constant translation
- Not imposed — emerges from constraint dynamics
- Maya's mental fatigue choosing which language to use
- Rafael's fear of mistranslation consequences
- Amihan's shame at sounding incompetent
- Dr. Okonkwo's guilt at studying others' labor without sharing the cost

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Equilibrium established (but fragile)
- Crew begins implementing collective semantic stabilization protocol
- Dr. Okonkwo's documentation becomes shared reference system
- Drift slows but doesn't stop (maintenance required)
- System persists with negotiated coordination level
- No resolution, just "good enough" stability

**Network Contamination:**
- **Healthy system:** New crew arrives with stable Bahay-Tagalog, learns Technical Tagalog systematically
- **Neighbor pressure:** Existing crew's rapid Technical Tagalog innovation creates learning pressure
- **Internalization:** New crew members start inventing terms before mastering existing ones (drift accelerates)

## 7. ACT STRUCTURE

### Act 1: Observation (Weeks 1-4, ~3,000 words)
**Constraint Activation:**
- Dr. Okonkwo arrives, begins recording Technical Tagalog
- Observes Maya inventing *bituin na patay* (dead star) for catastrophically failed quantum node
- Rafael translates it as "node malfunction" in incident report
- Earth-side management misunderstands severity, delays replacement parts
- Dr. Okonkwo recognizes extraction differential: Maya's precise term → Rafael's adequate translation → Earth-side's inadequate response

**Character Experiences:**
- Maya: Frustration at mistranslation consequences (array offline longer than necessary)
- Rafael: Exhaustion from translation labor (spent 20 minutes finding "right" Institutional English term)
- Amihan: Confusion (didn't understand *bituin na patay*, asked Maya for translation afterward)
- Dr. Okonkwo: Fascination (documents term, maps semantic drift pattern)

### Act 2: Collision (Weeks 5-12, ~6,000 words)
**Constraint Coupling:**
- Dr. Okonkwo maps drift acceleration: Technical Tagalog innovations increasing 40% per rotation cycle
- Observes three more mistranslation incidents (increasing severity)
- Interviews crew members about language experience:
  - Maya: "*Pagod na ako sa pagsasalin*" (I'm exhausted from translating)
  - Rafael: "I'm losing words. Technical Tagalog terms I knew six months ago, I can't remember the English anymore."
  - Amihan: "*Hindi ako sigurado kung tama ang ginagamit kong salita*" (I'm not sure if I'm using the right words)
- Critical incident: Emergency malfunction, supervisor doesn't recognize new Technical Tagalog term, array offline six minutes
- Earth-side files incident report, threatens contract penalties

**Collision from Different Indices:**
- Maya experiences drift as extraction (constant translation labor, mistranslation consequences)
- Rafael experiences drift as coordination failure (bridge is collapsing, can't maintain it alone)
- Amihan experiences drift as exclusion (can't keep up with term innovations)
- Dr. Okonkwo experiences drift as research data (fascinating pattern, publishable findings)

### Act 3: Negotiation (Weeks 13-16, ~4,000 words)
**Attractor Approach:**
- Dr. Okonkwo presents findings at crew meeting: drift is accelerating, current trajectory leads to communication breakdown
- Shows comparison with previous rotation cycle: they had informal glossary, drift was slower
- Proposes collective semantic stabilization protocol: shared Technical Tagalog reference document, updated monthly
- Crew negotiates implementation:
  - Maya: Wants comprehensive glossary (reduce translation labor)
  - Rafael: Wants Earth-side management buy-in (legitimize Technical Tagalog in reports)
  - Amihan: Wants learning support (can't keep up with innovations)
  - Dr. Okonkwo: Offers to maintain documentation (part of research)

**Resolution:**
- Crew agrees to pilot program: Dr. Okonkwo documents Technical Tagalog terms, crew reviews monthly, Rafael negotiates with Earth-side for recognition
- First glossary published (50 terms, with Institutional English translations)
- Drift slows (measured by mistranslation incident rate)
- System stabilizes at negotiated coordination level: enough innovation for operational needs, enough stability for communication
- Dr. Okonkwo's research becomes infrastructure (documentation enables TR₁)

**Equilibrium Characteristics:**
- Not perfect stability (drift continues at slower rate)
- Requires ongoing maintenance (monthly glossary updates)
- Fragile (depends on collective participation)
- "Good enough" (communication functional, extraction reduced but not eliminated)
