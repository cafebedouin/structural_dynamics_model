STAGE 0 OUTPUT: CONSTRAINT EXTRACTION

**C[1]: The Judgment of Onlookers**
*   **Description:** A social obligation to conform one's actions to the unsolicited, contradictory advice of passersby to avoid shame and ridicule.

*   **ε (estimated):** 0.80
    *   **Evidence:** The constraint extracts the Miller's original plan, his agency, his dignity ("vexed and ashamed"), and ultimately his physical property (the Ass). It provides no coordinating value in return, only contradictory demands. The final outcome is a total loss.

*   **Supp (estimated):** 0.40
    *   **Evidence:** Enforcement is purely social and psychological, not physical. It is applied through exclamations ("Did you ever see such a pair of fools?"), direct advice ("Make him walk, young lazybones!"), shaming ("What a selfish old man!"), and public ridicule ("the people ran out in crowds to laugh at it, and chaffed the Father and Son unmercifully"). The Miller's compliance demonstrates its effectiveness.

*   **Coord:** false
    *   **Why:** The advice given by each group of onlookers is mutually exclusive and contradictory. Following one group's advice guarantees violating the perceived norms of another. It actively prevents the Miller from achieving his goal.

*   **Asymmetric:** true
    *   **Why:** The onlookers bear zero cost for giving advice; they are not invested in the outcome. The Miller and his Son bear the entire cost of implementation, confusion, and ultimate failure.

**Per character/index:**

*   **The Miller**
    *   **I = (powerless, immediate, trapped, local)**
        *   P: `powerless` (π=1.5) — He consistently cedes his agency to the judgment of strangers, acting as if he has no power to resist their opinions.
        *   T: `immediate` — His planning horizon extends only to the next group of people he meets. He does not maintain a consistent, long-term strategy.
        *   E: `trapped` — He behaves as if he cannot exit the social obligation to please others, even when it becomes absurd.
        *   S: `local` (σ=0.8) — All interactions are face-to-face on the road.
    *   **χ = ε × π(P) × σ(S) = 0.80 × 1.5 × 0.8 = 0.96**
    *   **Type = Snare (⊠)**
        *   **Experience:** The Miller experiences the need for social approval as an inescapable trap that extracts his autonomy and property, leading directly to his ruin. His attempts to comply only tighten the trap.
        *   **Error Type:** Type I (False Mountain). He treats the fleeting, contradictory opinions of strangers as an unchangeable force of nature that he must adapt to, rather than a changeable construct he could ignore.

*   **The Son**
    *   **I = (powerless, immediate, trapped, local)**
        *   P: `powerless` (π=1.5) — He has no voice and is subject to his father's decisions, which are in turn subject to the onlookers. He is at the bottom of the power hierarchy.
        *   T: `immediate` — His experience is dictated moment-to-moment by his father.
        *   E: `trapped` — As a child accompanying his father, he has no option to exit.
        *   S: `local` (σ=0.8) — Same as the Miller.
    *   **χ = ε × π(P) × σ(S) = 0.80 × 1.5 × 0.8 = 0.96**
    *   **Type = Snare (⊠)**
        *   **Experience:** He is a passive victim of the same trap ensnaring his father, forced to participate in the escalating absurdity without agency.

*   **The Onlookers (Composite Index)**
    *   **I = (institutional, immediate, arbitrage, local)**
        *   P: `institutional` (π=-0.2) — In the moment of judgment, they act as the rule-making institution ("society"), defining correct behavior and extracting compliance from the Miller. They are the net beneficiaries of the interaction (satisfaction of reinforcing their norms).
        *   T: `immediate` — They offer their opinion and immediately move on, with no investment in the long-term consequences.
        *   E: `arbitrage` — They can choose to offer their opinion or remain silent at zero cost.
        *   S: `local` (σ=0.8) — The judgment is delivered in a face-to-face context.
    *   **χ = ε × π(P) × σ(S) = 0.80 × -0.2 × 0.8 = -0.128**
    *   **Type = Rope (⊞)**
        *   **Experience:** From their perspective, offering unsolicited advice is a zero-cost, beneficial act of social coordination. It reinforces perceived community standards and costs them nothing, appearing as a functional (if trivial) social mechanism.

*   **Indexical Variance:**
    *   The analysis reveals a severe indexical split, which is the central engine of the fable. The same constraint, "The Judgment of Onlookers," is experienced as a destructive, high-cost **Snare (⊠)** by the Miller and Son, but as a beneficial, zero-cost **Rope (⊞)** by the Onlookers who impose it. The Miller's tragedy is his failure to recognize this asymmetry.

*   **UCZ Candidates:**
    *   The constraint's source is decentralized and transient. It is not a single, persistent entity but a "ghost" constraint assembled from a series of uncoordinated, fleeting speech acts by disconnected individuals. This ephemeral nature makes it difficult to identify and resist, yet its aggregate effect is devastating.
