# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

**Selected: ☒ Seeded Possibility**

Verification against Stage 1:
- Constraint profile: Mountain (C1) + Tangled Rope × 2 (C2, C3) — matches Mountain-dominated/Tangled-Ropes-dominant compatibility row
- Rationality model: PIR (no suppression term, no negotiable principal — Reckoners are procedure-bound, not bargaining agents)
- PIR × Mountain-dominated → {Tragedy, Seeded Possibility} both reachable per matrix
- Stage 1's explicit selection reasoning holds: surface tragedy (Asym=true, individuating information lost at C3 convergence) + underlying transformation (C1's Mountain status *enables* the C2→C3 fusion to be a genuine epistemic event, not merely an extraction event) = the dual-layer signature Seeded Possibility requires

**Confirmed compatible.** This is not Deterministic Tragedy because the constraint topology contains a real, if narrow, achievement (the tally's protective/legibility function is not fake — Amrasut's sincerity is structurally licensed by C2's genuine Coord=true). It is not Negotiated Equilibrium because there is no principal to negotiate with — Supp=0.0 across all three constraints forecloses bargaining as a resolution mechanism. It is not Revolutionary Rupture because C1's Mountain status is Boltzmann-certified invariant; nothing in the specification licenses the pan itself, or the *fact* of tallying, being overthrown. What can shift is what Kesar *does* with the gap between tally and texture — a seed, not a rupture.

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned** (Kesar Devsut)

Justification via Master Diagnostic:
- *Does this voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot?* Yes. Kesar experiences the Accounting as her own dread/relief oscillation (per Stage 2 Step 5) without the analytical vocabulary to name why both feelings are simultaneously true. The reader, given the constraint math, can see that C2/C3 are genuinely Tangled (real coordination value *and* real extraction) — Kesar can only feel this as an unresolvable mood she has stopped questioning.
- Attractor compatibility: ✓ Seeded Possibility (Condemned is explicitly compatible)
- Rationality model: PIR-compatible by design (Condemned requires no negotiating counterpart, matches Supp=0.0)
- Power position: π=1.0 (moderate) — spec calls for "powerless/moderate (π ≥ 1.0)" — Kesar sits exactly at the boundary, which is correct: she has *just enough* standing to notice the machinery without having the analytical distance of a Builder or Witness

**Rejected alternatives:**
- *Builder* — requires testing for leverage points and learning from strategic failure. Kesar's identity-locked exit and the Mountain-certified C1 substrate foreclose this; Stage 2's Ω_P02 explicitly recommends her one prior workaround (marriage-linked adjacency) stay backstory-only, which forbids an active Builder arc in the present timeline.
- *Zealot* — Amrasut is the correct index for this (see below), not Kesar. Giving Kesar Zealot voice would collapse the story's central tension (she is supposed to be caught between registers, not resolved into one).
- *Witness* — would flatten the dread/relief oscillation into reportage, exactly the failure mode the Master Diagnostic warns against for Deterministic-Tragedy-adjacent material.

**Secondary (limited, 2 scenes max per Ω_P01 recommendation): The Zealot** (Amrasut the Elder)

Justification:
- Stage 2 designs Amrasut's sincerity explicitly: "sees the Ledger-Stones as a Rope... with total sincerity." This is textbook Zealot, not Beneficiary — Amrasut does not merely benefit from the tally, he has made the tally's necessity his identity and enforces it as moral good ("unrecorded trade is chaos and violence").
- Requires PIR (✓), high purity/theater component — the Accounting is literally a ritual with theater_ratio implications (fired clay, public reading, annual ceremony)
- Attractor: ✓ Tragedy, ✓ Equilibrium — and here functions as *counterweight* texture within a Seeded Possibility frame, not as a competing terminus
- Stage 4 directive applies in full: Amrasut must never waver. His certainty is the fixed point against which Kesar's private fracture becomes visible.

**Bhairon Ashborne: structural pressure only, not POV** (resolving Ω_P01)

Decision: Bhairon appears as a felt structural presence in Kesar's scenes (the Ashborne quarters downwind, the closing-of-tallies work, deference-at-distance per Step 4) but does **not** carry his own POV section. Rationale: Stage 2's own recommendation is followed — the diachronic variance (Kesar's own before/after the Accounting) carries the indexical divergence more intimately than a second full thread would, and a Condemned-plus-Zealot structure already gives the reader the two ends of the false-Rope perception (genuine sincerity vs. private dread) without needing a third full register to make the Snare-counterfactual visible. Bhairon's powerless-index reading of C2/C3 (where χ→0.9, Snare) is dramatized through **one scene**, tightly bounded, from Kesar's external observation — she watches him close a tally and cannot ask him what it costs, because the deference custom (Step 4: "Ashborne speak last, or are addressed through a Reckoner intermediary") structurally forbids the conversation that would explain it. This *is* the point: the story enacts the indexical variance by making it unspeakable across caste lines, rather than narrating it from inside.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**Selected: ☒ Gradual Realization** (primary) **with a single Counterpoint insert** (Amrasut scene)

Justification: Kesar's single POV, moving from unexamined dread/relief toward a partial, unresolved recognition that the tally's comfort and its cost are the same fact, is the core engine. The Amrasut scene is not a POV shift into a second consciousness in the revelation sense — it is deployed as a **Counterpoint marker**: the reader watches Kesar watch Amrasut's total sincerity and feels the gap between his conviction and what Stage 1's counterfactual math shows (his powerful-index reading of the same constraint resolves toward Naturalized/Rope, while hers sits mid-Tangled and Bhairon's sits Snare-adjacent). The variance is shown, not explained, and never through omniscient cross-cutting.

Rejected: Multi-POV Parallel (would require the omniscient or alternating architecture Stage 2 explicitly cautions against by recommending confinement to 2-3 scenes); Late Revelation (the story is not built around a withheld twist — the tension is present-tense and ongoing, not a final-act recontextualization); Analytical Observer (no character in this specification has the vocabulary or standing to be explicitly aware of indexical variance as such — this would violate the invisibility spec).

## 4. Editorial Decisions

```
LENGTH: Short (4000-5000 words) — sufficient room for one full Accounting
  cycle (kacha entry → firing into Stone) plus the Amrasut and Bhairon
  scenes without diluting Kesar's interiority into essay-pacing.

POV: First person (Kesar). 
  Justification: Condemned voice is strongest in first person per Stage 3
  archetype notes. First person also enforces the numeric register
  discipline naturally — Kesar cannot narrate in the Reckoners' figures
  because she has no instrument-access to them except at the moment of
  the Accounting itself, which becomes a scene rather than ambient texture.

TENSE: Past tense, close and immediate (not distanced retrospective).
  This is not a Survivor voice looking back from after rupture — there
  is no rupture. Past tense here functions as "the past that is still
  arriving," not "the past resolved into meaning."

CHARACTER COUNT: 3 — Kesar (full POV), Amrasut (one bounded scene),
  Bhairon (one bounded scene, externally observed). This is the minimum
  required to stage the indexical variance (moderate/powerful/powerless
  counterfactuals from Stage 1) as felt collision rather than stated
  contrast.

NAMING: Cultural, per Stage 2 Step 3/Step 6 exactly as designed —
  given names (Kesar, Bhairon, Zeenat-adjacent minor figures) +
  ledger-surnames (-sut patronymics: Devsut, Amrasut). No deviation;
  Stage 2's naming design already encodes the floor invariant
  ("your name is administrative before it is personal") and must be
  preserved intact.

LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION (from Stage 2):
  Code-switching occurs at the register boundary between kacha/pakka —
  characters switch into the loanword precisely at the moment something
  crosses from provisional to permanent (a debt, a promise, an entry).
  The switch signals the exact instant informality becomes irrevocable.
  "Vansh" is used only when caste is invoked as *record-origin* (never
  as "blood" or "kind") — this preserves the missing-floor invariant
  linguistically. "The Varsat was long" (never "wrong") appears once,
  from an elder or Amrasut, as the community's only available frame
  for the founding sort.

EMOTIONAL CORE: The specific, nameless discomfort of being simultaneously
  protected and diminished by the same annual act — arising directly
  from C2/C3's Coord=true ∧ Asym=true structure, not imposed as theme.

ENDING STRATEGY: ☒ Seed planted, system persists, agent registers the
  seed without acting on it fully. Kesar reaches the Accounting's close
  (C3 fires), the tally is entered, the Compact continues exactly as
  before — but Kesar does one small, private, unrecorded thing (an act
  that stays kacha by choice, never reaching the Stone) that the story
  ends on. This is the Seeded Possibility signature: nothing structural
  changes, but a texture the tally cannot hold has been, once,
  deliberately protected from conversion.
```

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The salt-crust on the hands after a season at the channel-gates**, read against **the smoothness of the Reckoners' hands** (Amrasut's specifically) and **the ash-grey permanent stain on Bhairon's**, which does not wash out and is the only mark of caste that is not administrative but literally bodily — the one piece of "record" that exists outside the Stone entirely, on skin, and cannot be reassigned, renewed, or fired into anything. This marker is chosen because:
- It is sensation, not metric (passes the anti-pattern check — cannot be expressed as a number)
- It varies legibly across the three characters without narration having to explain the variance
- It gives Stage 4 a recurring image that tracks Kesar's arc: she notices her own hands differently before and after the Accounting (Act 1 vs Act 3), without the text naming what changed
