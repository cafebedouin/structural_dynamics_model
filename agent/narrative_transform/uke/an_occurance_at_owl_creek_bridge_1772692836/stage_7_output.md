# STRATEGY BRIEF

## CORE DIAGNOSIS

**IMPLIED AUTHOR:** A structural anatomist with a documentarian's eye and a moralist's restraint. This author notices: the sensory texture of class hierarchy (smell, temperature, floor solidity), the physical manifestation of constraint (vibration, shaking hands, weight in chest), the bureaucratic language that masks violence, the gap between what characters believe they're doing and what the system is doing through them. This author cares about: how extraction mechanisms operate, how complicity is manufactured, how dignity survives or doesn't. This author skips: villains, redemption arcs that don't cost anything, explanations that let readers off the hook, endings that resolve what shouldn't resolve.

**AUDIENCE:** Readers who recognize that "I chose this" and "I had no choice" can both be true. Readers who have felt the weight of family obligation, who understand debt as relationship rather than just transaction, who know what it means to be complicit in your own exploitation. Readers who can tolerate structural horror—not the horror of monsters but of mechanisms. Readers who don't need hope to find meaning. Educated but not academic; politically conscious but not ideological; willing to sit with contradiction.

**SCOPE RECOMMENDATION:** The current 8,500-word draft functions as a complete short story but operates at maximum compression. The constraint systems identified—debt leverage, informant recruitment, retroactive criminalization—are present but rushed. The story needs approximately 12,000-15,000 words to:
- Give the seduction genuine time to work
- Show one of the casualties (Carlos, Jin, or Amara) before Miguel gives the names
- Expand the processing sequence from gesture to embodied experience
- Add a brief coda showing what Miguel becomes

This is still short story territory, not novella. The material is focused enough for the form.

## STRUCTURAL PRIORITIES

### 1. MAKE THE SEDUCTION GENUINE LONGER
**Location:** Coffee scene with Reyes (currently ~600 words)
**Problem:** The trap is telegraphed. The reader sees it coming before Miguel does, creating dramatic irony rather than shared experience.
**Fix:** Expand to ~1,200 words. Show Reyes's history in the outer ring through specific sensory memory—not told, but revealed through how she moves in the space, what she notices, what she doesn't say. Let her care be ambiguous longer. The reader should feel the warmth of the coffee, the relief of the solid floor, the possibility that maybe this is what it seems. The betrayal should land on us too.

### 2. GIVE ONE CASUALTY A FACE
**Location:** Before "I give her the names" (currently compressed to single paragraph)
**Problem:** Carlos, Jin, and Amara exist only as names. The distributed victims remain abstract.
**Fix:** Add ~800 words showing Miguel's last conversation with one of them (recommend Carlos—Filipino, same cultural context of *utang na loob*). Show Carlos talking about his family, his plan for after, his trust in Miguel. Make it specific: his daughter's name, the business he'll start, the exact number of months he has left. Then show Miguel giving the name. Make us watch him do it.

### 3. SHOW THE PROCESSING
**Location:** Final scene (currently ends with Miguel waiting)
**Problem:** The story gestures at horror without embodying it. This is a structural flinch.
**Fix:** Add ~1,500 words. Show what happens in that room. Not torture—re-education. Show the mechanism: recorded conversations played back, contracts analyzed, Miguel's own words used to demonstrate his guilt. Show the psychological method: not breaking him but showing him how he was always already guilty, how the system gave him exactly what he asked for, how his choices were real and also inevitable. Show him signing the revised contract that makes him complicit in his own processing. This is the extraction masking made visible: he chose this, and he had no choice.

### 4. ADD POST-PROCESSING CODA
**Location:** After processing sequence
**Problem:** The story shows the trap but not how it reproduces through its victims.
**Fix:** Add ~600 words. Show Miguel six months later, back on Earth, visiting the house with blue tile. Rosa won't look at him. His brother is gone—dropped out, working to pay the debt. His mother is sick again—no medicine. The house is half-finished, abandoned. Show Miguel receiving a message from TPMC: new contract offer, return to Mars, supervisor position, signing bonus that would cover the family debt. Show him looking at the contract. Show him understanding exactly what it is. Show him signing it anyway. End there. No explanation. The choice is the ending.

## CRAFT MANDATES

**VOICE CONSISTENCY:** Miguel's voice must modulate with constraint pressure but maintain Filipino English throughout. No drift into generic American narrator voice. The code-switching with Reyes is structural—when she speaks Filipino, she's performing intimacy. When Miguel responds in English, he's maintaining distance. Track this carefully.

**ENDING BUDGET:** The post-processing coda must be ≤600 words total. No explanation, no interiority about "what it all means." Just: the house, the debt, the contract, the signature. The choice without commentary.

**SENSORY HIERARCHY:** Class difference is embodied through: smell, temperature, floor solidity, air quality, sound (breathing through walls vs. silence). Every scene transition between rings must register these differences. They're not decoration—they're the constraint system made physical.

**RUPTURE HONESTY:** The processing sequence cannot offer Miguel insight, growth, or understanding as compensation for what's done to him. He can see clearly what's happening and still sign the contract. Clarity is not freedom. This is the Frankl question: meaning is available even when escape isn't, but meaning doesn't resolve the constraint.

## WHAT NOT TO CHANGE

**The structure is sound.** The three-act movement (arrival/seduction/processing) maps correctly to the constraint topology. Don't add subplots or secondary characters beyond the expansions specified above.

**The inhabitation is strong.** Miguel's voice, the sensory texture of the rings, the physical manifestation of constraint in his body—these work. Expand them but don't revise them.

**The ending strategy is correct.** Tragedy attractor, properly executed. The coda doesn't soften this—it shows how the tragedy reproduces. Miguel becomes another Reyes. The cycle continues. This is honest to the material.

## OMEGA LOG

**Ω_E1:** Processing sequence specification required. Recommendation: bureaucratic re-education model. Show Miguel signing documents that retroactively criminalize his actions, show him acknowledging his guilt, show the psychological mechanism that makes this seem reasonable. The horror is in the procedure, not the violence.

**Ω_C1:** Reyes's interiority is conceptually resolved: she is former victim who became perpetrator through the same mechanism Miguel is experiencing. Show this through sense memory in coffee scene—she remembers the outer ring but doesn't articulate what she remembers. The reader should infer her history from what she notices and what she doesn't say.

**Ω_C2:** Post-processing coda is necessary to show reproductive logic. Without it, the story is about one man's tragedy. With it, the story is about how the system perpetuates itself. This is not hopeless—this is structural. Hope and hopelessness are both beside the point. The question is: what choice is available when you see clearly?

**Ω_P1:** The Frankl question is present but needs embodiment in the processing sequence. Miguel can see exactly what's being done to him and still sign because the alternative is his family's destruction. This is not false consciousness—this is constraint. The meaning available is in seeing clearly and choosing anyway, whatever that choice is. The story doesn't need to tell us if this is dignified or defeated. It just needs to show the choice.
