## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected Archetype:** The Condemned

**Justification:**

1. **Structural compatibility:**
   - Condemned archetype requires: PIR model OR Mountain-dominated, powerless/moderate position (π ≥ 1.0)
   - Lian's index: π = 1.5 (powerless), E = trapped, χ = 1.08 (Snare)
   - ✓ PASS - Powerless position with trapped exit meets requirements

2. **Attractor compatibility:**
   - Condemned + Negotiated Equilibrium: ⚠ EDGE CASE but viable
   - Risk: Condemned typically pairs with Tragedy or Seeded Possibility
   - Mitigation: Equilibrium reached through Lian's continued entrapment, not through her agency or death
   - The equilibrium is *about* her remaining condemned, not her escaping or transforming

3. **Voice capabilities:**
   - Cannot see systemic shape (Lian experiences debt as personal failure, not extraction design)
   - Acts rationally given limited information (signs extensions because alternatives appear worse)
   - Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable (the contract "breathes" - organic metaphor, not mechanical)
   - Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective

4. **Tone alignment:**
   - Tragic, somber tone appropriate for Negotiated Equilibrium where equilibrium = continued suffering
   - Reader sees extraction logic that Lian cannot see
   - Gap between Lian's rationality and reader's structural awareness generates tragedy

**Alternative considered and rejected:**

- **The Witness:** Would require analytical distance Lian doesn't have. Her powerless position prevents systemic observation.
- **The Builder:** Would require testing for leverage points. Lian's trapped exit option prevents strategic action.
- **The Survivor:** Would require temporal distance from rupture. No rupture occurs in Negotiated Equilibrium.

**Voice commitment:** First-person Condemned (Lian)

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected Strategy:** Gradual Realization (Modified)

**Structure:**

Lian's single POV throughout, but revelation occurs through *physical markers* rather than cognitive epiphany. She never "realizes" the system is extractive - she experiences its effects on her body and environment with increasing intensity.

**Revelation sequence:**

1. **Act 1:** Lian experiences debt growth as personal failure
   - Counts her debt daily
   - Blames herself for equipment malfunctions
   - Believes quota is achievable if she works harder

2. **Act 2:** Lian observes indexical variance without naming it
   - Sees Jin's viewport, her own bunk pod
   - Hears Rafael's inspection speech about "fair risk-sharing"
   - Notices Cristina's code-switching when trading with pilot
   - Does not synthesize these observations into structural analysis

3. **Act 3:** Lian's body registers what her mind cannot name
   - Breathing rate increases (primary physical marker)
   - Stops calculating debt payoff timeline
   - Signs extension without reading it
   - Physical surrender, not cognitive recognition

**Critical constraint:** Lian never uses framework terminology. No "extraction," "power asymmetry," "structural position." She uses naturalized language: *a pressão* (the pressure), *o contrato que respira* (the breathing contract), *a superfície* (the surface - both literal and metaphorical).

**Indexical variance visibility:**

Reader sees variance through juxtaposition:
- Rafael's inspection speech (C₁ as elegant mechanism) vs. Lian's debt calculation (C₁ as trap)
- Jin's compliance report (objective metrics) vs. Lian's equipment failures (impossible conditions)
- Mateo's code enforcement (necessary coordination) vs. Dao's exile (loyalty extraction)

Lian sees these events but doesn't connect them. Reader performs the synthesis.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Short story (4,000-5,000 words)
- Rationale: Sufficient space for three-act structure and physical marker development. Novelette length would require subplot complexity that risks diluting constraint focus.

**POV:** First person (Lian)
- Rationale: Condemned archetype strongest in first person. Close third would create analytical distance incompatible with Lian's powerless position.

**TENSE:** Present tense
- Rationale: Immediacy appropriate for Condemned voice. Past tense would imply survival and retrospective distance Lian doesn't have.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 5 primary, 2 secondary
- Primary: Lian (X₁), Rafael (X₅), Jin (X₇), Cristina (X₆), Mateo (X₆)
- Secondary: Dao (exiled Salvager), Kao (mentioned, already dead)
- Rationale: Minimum necessary to show indexical variance across all constraint types

**NAMING:** Cultural (Portuguese/Mandarin/English mix)
- Lian (Mandarin) - Miner from Mekong Delta
- Rafael (Portuguese) - Brazilian Shareholder
- Jin (Mandarin) - Second-generation Engineer
- Cristina (Portuguese) - Salvager, former Engineer
- Mateo (Spanish/Portuguese) - Salvager leader
- Dao (Vietnamese/Mandarin) - Exiled Salvager
- Kao (Thai/Mandarin) - Dead Salvager
- Rationale: Names signal cultural backgrounds without exposition. Mix reflects Consortium's multinational composition.

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

Code-switching occurs at power boundaries:

1. **Lian → other Miners:** Portuguese (intimacy, resistance)
   - *"A pressão está pior hoje"* (The pressure is worse today)
   - Signals: We share this experience, we can speak freely

2. **Lian → Jin:** Mandarin (hierarchy, compliance)
   - *"是的，工程师"* (Yes, Engineer)
   - Signals: I acknowledge your authority, I am subordinate

3. **Lian → contract documents:** English (institutional power)
   - Reads but barely comprehends
   - Signals: This language owns me, I cannot fully access it

4. **Jin → Lian:** Mandarin (technical authority)
   - *"检查设备日志"* (Check equipment logs)
   - Signals: This is operational command, not negotiation

5. **Rafael → Jin:** English (corporate hierarchy)
   - "Compliance metrics look solid"
   - Signals: We operate in the same institutional frame

6. **Cristina → submersible pilot:** English (performed Surface Trust)
   - "I'm sourcing components for a private contract"
   - Signals: I am your peer, trust me without verification

**Switch timing:**
- Switches occur mid-scene when power relationship shifts
- No translation provided - reader infers from context
- Italics for non-English dialogue, no quotation marks (signals internal experience)

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (Emerges from constraint dynamics)

Not imposed - discovered through inhabitation sentence:
*You are 4,200 meters underwater in a habitat owned by the company you owe money to, operating equipment you rent from them, eating food you buy from them, and the contract that promised you freedom compounds your debt faster than you can earn.*

Core emotion: **Suffocation**
- Physical (pressure, recycled air, confined space)
- Economic (debt compounds, no exit)
- Temporal (future closes, only present remains)

**ENDING STRATEGY:** System persists, agent removed (from hope, not from life)

Lian signs another contract extension. Debt reduced slightly but not eliminated. She has stopped calculating payoff timeline. Her breathing rate has stabilized at the higher rate - her body has adapted to permanent pressure. The system reproduces itself. Rafael approves next year's quotas. Jin files compliance report. Mateo enforces the code. The stations continue extracting.

Final image: Lian in her bunk pod, fourth tier, listening to the hull creak. She is not dead. She is not free. She is 4,200 meters underwater, and the contract breathes.

## 5. PRIMARY PHYSICAL MARKER

**Selected Marker:** Lian's breathing rate

**Emergence from inhabitation sentence:**
*"You are 4,200 meters underwater..."* - The pressure is both literal (420 atmospheres) and metaphorical (debt, quota, confinement). Breathing is the body's response to pressure. When pressure increases, breathing accelerates. When pressure becomes permanent, breathing stabilizes at the higher rate.

**Not a designed motif** - this is what the body does when trapped at depth with no exit. The reader will understand before Lian does: her breathing rate is not adapting to help her survive, it's adapting to help her endure.

**Tracking through narrative:**

1. **Act 1 (Baseline):** Lian counts breaths during equipment malfunctions
   - *"Twelve breaths while the manipulator reboots. Always twelve."*
   - Breathing as measurement, control, rationality

2. **Act 2 (Acceleration):** Lian notices breathing has changed but attributes it to equipment stress
   - *"Sixteen breaths now. The manipulator is slower, that's all."*
   - Breathing as symptom she misreads

3. **Act 3 (Stabilization):** Lian stops counting
   - *"I don't count anymore. The breaths come as they come."*
   - Breathing as surrender, adaptation to permanent pressure

**Reader recognition point:** When Lian stops counting breaths, reader understands she has stopped calculating escape. The physical marker reveals what the voice cannot state.

**Connection to other indices:**
- Rafael never thinks about breathing (surface air, no pressure)
- Jin breathes normally (upper deck, higher ceilings, less pressure audible)
- Cristina's breathing stabilized years ago (she knows she'll never surface)
- Mateo enforces the code partly because his own breathing rate terrifies him (he needs the collective to survive the pressure)
