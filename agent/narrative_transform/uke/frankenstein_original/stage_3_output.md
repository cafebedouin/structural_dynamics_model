# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected: ☑ Deterministic Tragedy**

**Justification:**

The constraint network exhibits structural inevitability:

- **C₁ (The Pursuit)** blocks **TR₃** (abandonment) via identity_lock
- **C₅ (The Silence)** blocks **TR₂** (revelation) via identity_lock  
- **C₁ + C₅** block **TR₄** (duty fulfillment) via coupled extraction
- **C₃ (The Boundary)** blocks **TR₁** (collective resistance) for Aminah
- **C₄ (The Withdrawal)** prevents coordination needed for any transformation

The rationality model is **PIR** (Perfectly Indexed Rationality) for all agents:
- Rashid acts rationally given his index (moderate power, identity-locked exit, biographical time)
- Aminah acts rationally given her index (powerless, trapped, immediate survival)
- Karim acts rationally given his index (organized power, mobile exit, generational view)
- Hassan acts rationally given his index (organized family position, sees system working)

No agent has access to information that would enable different choices. Each decision is locally optimal. The tragedy emerges from **rational action within incompatible indices**, not from error or irrationality.

**Attractor compatibility check:**
- ✓ PIR model supports Deterministic Tragedy (no learning possible that changes structure)
- ✓ Constraint coupling (C₁ + C₅ → Snare) creates recursive trap
- ✓ Multiple constraints block all transformation rules
- ✓ Indexical variance (C₂: Tangled Rope for Rashid, Snare for Aminah) prevents mutual recognition
- ✓ No deus ex machina available (historical setting, documented social structures)

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected: ☑ The Condemned (Rashid primary) + ☑ The Witness (Aminah secondary)**

**Justification:**

**Rashid as Condemned:**
- Inside C₁ (The Pursuit) and C₅ (The Silence), cannot see systemic shape
- Acts rationally: pursuing breakthrough is legitimate scientific ambition from his index
- Experiences constraints as natural/inevitable: "This is what research requires"
- Voice is immediate, experiential: laboratory details, physical exhaustion, somatic guilt
- **Tone:** Tragic, somber
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Condemned + Deterministic Tragedy (classic pairing)
- **Rationality model:** ✓ PIR (acts rationally given limited information and structural position)
- **Power/exit:** ✓ Moderate power (π = 1.0), identity-locked exit (cannot abandon without ego death)
- **POV:** First person or close third — **selecting close third** to allow physical observation without internal explanation

**Why not other archetypes for Rashid:**
- ❌ Builder: Would require seeing leverage points (he sees only technical problems)
- ❌ Beneficiary: He experiences extraction, not comfort (declining health, guilt, isolation)
- ❌ Zealot: He doubts constantly (guilt over C₂, fear over C₅) — not fervent certainty
- ❌ System: Too impersonal — need embodied experience of constraint

**Aminah as Witness:**
- Observes constraint dynamics (C₂, C₃, C₄) without power to intervene
- Can see multiple indices: father's avoidance, Hassan's blindness, her own exclusion
- Reports what happens without judgment: "Like that only" (macam itu saja leh)
- Voice is documentary, clinical, but **not** analytical — she sees, doesn't theorize
- **Tone:** Neutral surface, tragic depth (numbness with flashes of clarity)
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Witness + Deterministic Tragedy (observes inevitability)
- **Analytical capacity:** ✓ Can see indexical variance (father three floors above, Hassan's family planning that excludes her)
- **POV:** Close third — **matches Rashid's POV for structural parallel**

**Why Witness rather than Condemned for Aminah:**
- She **does** see the structure: "The proper order is everywhere and nowhere"
- Her inhabitation sentence: "learned to read micro-expressions before you learned to read books"
- She has analytical distance born of exclusion — sees what others naturalize
- But she has no stake in the sense of agency — cannot intervene, only observe
- The Witness archetype captures her **clarity without power**

**Why not other archetypes for Aminah:**
- ❌ Condemned: She sees too much (recognizes C₃ as constructed, sees father's avoidance)
- ❌ Builder: No leverage points available (powerless, trapped)
- ❌ Prophet: No future trajectory to warn about (present is already the catastrophe)

**Voice combination strategy: Counterpoint Voice**

Two voices at opposite ends of power scale observing same physical markers:
- Rashid notices Aminah eating alone → experiences guilt, looks away
- Aminah notices Rashid looking away → counts the seconds, files it with other data
- Same moment, incompatible experiences, reader performs the extraction math

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: ☑ Multi-POV Parallel**

**Structure:**
- Alternate between Rashid and Aminah sections
- Reader sees indexical variance **before** characters recognize it
- No character explicitly aware of indexical variance (maintains PIR model)
- Revelation emerges through **incompatible descriptions of same events**

**Key revelation moments:**

1. **The filing room incident** (C₂ + C₃ intersection)
   - Rashid's section: Sees Aminah in canteen, feels guilt, returns to laboratory
   - Aminah's section: Watches father look away, counts to twelve, returns to filing
   - Reader sees: Rashid experiences guilt (C₂ as Tangled Rope), Aminah experiences abandonment (C₂ as Snare)

2. **Hassan's family dinner** (C₃ from opposite indices)
   - Hassan's section: Discusses daughters' marriage prospects, natural family planning
   - Aminah's section: Positioned at table edge, conversation flows around her
   - Reader sees: Hassan experiences C₃ as Rope (coordination value), Aminah as Snare (total exclusion)

3. **The laboratory silence** (C₅ revelation)
   - Rashid's section: Memory of hydrogen sulfide smell, cannot discuss with anyone
   - Karim's section: Reviews incident report, notes "equipment malfunction," moves to next item
   - Reader sees: Rashid experiences C₅ as Tangled Rope (crushing guilt), Karim as Rope (professional discretion)

**Why this strategy:**
- ✓ Compatible with PIR model (no character gains impossible awareness)
- ✓ Compatible with Condemned + Witness voices (neither explains the gap)
- ✓ Compatible with Deterministic Tragedy (reader sees inevitability before characters)
- ✓ Serves historical realism (1960s Brunei had no vocabulary for "indexical variance")

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** ☑ Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)

**Rationale:** 
- Four characters with distinct indices require development
- Five constraints need naturalization through scene
- Multi-POV parallel structure needs space for incompatible perspectives
- Historical setting requires grounding detail
- Target: ~12,000 words (allows depth without sprawl)

**POV:** ☑ Third Limited (rotating)

**Rationale:**
- Close third for Rashid (Condemned voice needs embodied experience without internal explanation)
- Close third for Aminah (Witness voice needs observational clarity without analytical distance)
- Brief close third for Hassan (shows C₃ as Rope from organized position)
- Brief close third for Karim (shows C₅ as Rope from institutional position)
- **No omniscient narrator** — indexical variance emerges through incompatible perspectives

**TENSE:** ☑ Past

**Rationale:**
- Historical setting (1968-1973) requires past tense for authenticity
- Deterministic Tragedy benefits from retrospective inevitability
- Past tense allows "had learned," "had become" constructions that show constraint sedimentation

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 4 (Rashid, Aminah, Hassan, Karim)

**Rationale:**
- Minimum for constraint interactions (C₁, C₂, C₃, C₄, C₅ all require multiple agents)
- Rashid: Primary POV (Condemned, experiences C₁, C₂, C₄, C₅)
- Aminah: Secondary POV (Witness, experiences C₂, C₃, C₄ from opposite index)
- Hassan: Tertiary POV (shows C₃ as Rope, 1-2 sections)
- Karim: Quaternary POV (shows C₅ as Rope, 1 section)

**NAMING:** ☑ Cultural (Malay naming conventions)

**Rationale:**
- Historical accuracy requires culturally appropriate names
- Malay naming: [given name] bin/binti [father's name]
- Rashid bin Abdullah (Rashid, son of Abdullah)
- Aminah binti Rashid (Aminah, daughter of Rashid)
- Hassan bin Abdullah (Hassan, son of Abdullah — Rashid's brother)
- Karim bin Hassan (Karim, son of Hassan — different family)
- Professional titles: Dr. for PhD holders, no title for technical staff

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

**Code-switching occurs:**
1. **Rashid:** English for technical work → Malay for family/emotion
   - Laboratory: "The catalyst decomposition rate exceeds projections"
   - Home: "Kerja lambat lagi" (work is late again)
   - **Switch signals:** Distance (English) vs. vulnerability (Malay)

2. **Aminah:** English for office performance → Malay for private thought
   - Filing: "Alphabetical by project code, chronological within"
   - Internal: "Susunan yang betul. Like that only." (proper order, that's just how it is)
   - **Switch signals:** Competence performance (English) vs. resignation (Malay)

3. **Hassan:** Malay-dominant with English technical terms
   - Platform: "Kerja kuat hari ini. Pressure gauge reading normal."
   - **Switch signals:** Working-class position (Malay base) + technical skill (English terms)

4. **Karim:** English-dominant (institutional power)
   - Office: "The incident report indicates equipment malfunction"
   - Rare Malay: Only for cultural concepts (adat, rezeki)
   - **Switch signals:** Institutional authority (English maintenance)

**Grammatical features in English:**
- Dropped articles: "Father arranged position" (not "the position")
- Lah/leh particles as "already"/"only": "Can already" (boleh lah)
- Reduplication: "slow-slow" (very slow)

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** Guilt and invisibility (emerges from C₂ + C₃ intersection)

**Rationale:**
- Not imposed — emerges directly from constraint dynamics
- Rashid: Guilt from C₂ (father's debt) + C₅ (laboratory silence)
- Aminah: Invisibility from C₃ (social exclusion) + C₄ (forced isolation)
- Emotional core is **structural consequence**, not thematic overlay

**ENDING STRATEGY:** ☑ Attractor reached (Deterministic Tragedy completes)

**Rationale:**
- Rashid cannot fulfill C₂ (blocked by C₁ + C₅ coupling)
- Aminah cannot escape C₃ (blocked by powerless/trapped index)
- No transformation rules execute (all blocked by constraint network)
- Final scene: Physical manifestation of irreversibility
- **Not:** Explicit statement of tragedy
- **Instead:** Body language, physical space, silence that confirms structure

## 5. PRIMARY PHYSICAL MARKER

**Selected: Rashid's avoidance of eye contact with Aminah**

**Rationale:**

This is not a designed motif — it is **what the body does when guilt (C₂) meets isolation (C₄) meets the person you abandoned**.

**How it manifests:**

1. **Ministry canteen:** Rashid sees Aminah eating alone, looks at his watch, returns to laboratory
2. **Hallway passing:** Rashid hears her footsteps (recognizes the rhythm), turns into stairwell before she rounds corner
3. **Family dinner:** Rashid addresses Zainab, addresses Hassan, does not address Aminah directly
4. **Final scene:** Rashid in laboratory, Aminah in filing room three floors below, both aware of the other's location, neither moves

**Why this marker:**

- Emerges directly from inhabitation sentence: "carries your daughter's isolation in your chest"
- Observable by Aminah (Witness): She counts the seconds, files it with other data
- Invisible to Rashid (Condemned): He experiences it as necessary focus on work
- Reader sees before characters: The avoidance **is** the abandonment
- Physical, not psychological: No internal monologue needed — just body movement
- Escalates: Early sections show brief avoidance, final section shows permanent separation

**Secondary marker (Aminah):** Positioning herself at edges of rooms

- Filing room: Desk by door, can leave quickly
- Canteen: Table by wall, back to room
- Family gatherings: Chair at table end, slightly behind others
- **Why:** Physical manifestation of C₃ (social exclusion) + C₄ (protective withdrawal)
