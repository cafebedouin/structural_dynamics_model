REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The physical vocabulary of the gate — palm on wood, "give" in the current, the sound that stops — is consistent and does real work, culminating honestly in the silence-as-held-breath passage. The Marn Kohl thread is the story's best instinct: a character who explicitly gets nothing from the resolution, delivered without a speech about it, just "his family's name had never been on any gate." The Ila confrontation scene earns its dialogue; her accusation lands as specific grievance rather than thesis statement.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The ending over-narrates its own refusal to resolve. Sethrei explicitly tells us he's "not trusting" the easy peace, catches himself "reaching" for false wisdom, and asks whether the knowledge is "a wound or a tool" — this is the prose performing ambiguity through direct statement rather than letting the unresolved image (Marn's row, Ila's held-back place) carry it unassisted. It announces its own honesty, which undercuts the honesty.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. No character or authority in the text can produce an original allotment that predates the measure. Ren's formula, the Ordinate's grant, even Sethrei's own coalition — all operate inside the measure's terms, never behind them. The spring is folded *into* the record, not revealed as proof of a prior truth the record concealed. The who-had-the-right-first question is never answered, only administratively absorbed.

BREAK:
The text sets up an expectation that the coalition's silence-strike will crack the Ordinate open — "some part of me wanted Ren's evenness to crack, just once" states this expectation almost too plainly — and then the system absorbs the rebellion instead: gate reopened, spring "entered... as an Ordinate grant," Ren's recitation "did not change at all." This matches the target_prior (assimilation, not destruction) closely. It is named in the text itself, almost to the point of over-naming — the story tells us this is what happened ("the record now held the whole shape of what had nearly broken it") rather than trusting the folding-in to demonstrate it alone.

READINESS:
Close — one more pass to let the ending's ambiguity land through image and silence rather than through Sethrei explaining his own refusal to resolve.
