## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned** (Agent_B / Duvakh Sethrei) — for the majority throughline
**Secondary, nested: The Fool-adjacent naivety deliberately rejected for Ila** — see rationale below; Ila will run as a constrained second Condemned rather than a Fool, to avoid tonal rupture

**Justification:**

Agent_B is the only character whose index *changes* over the course of the story (powerful/civilizational → powerless/biographical → organized → moderate/mobile). The Condemned archetype is defined by rational action within a constraint the character cannot see the systemic shape of — and Duvakh's arc is precisely this: a man who believes, at the start, that his standing in council constitutes power, and learns through the Drowning-Silence that it did not. This is not a Builder, because Duvakh does not begin the story testing for leverage — TR1 is desperation, not strategy. He only becomes leverage-aware in TR4, and even then his tools are collective (coalition of Wardens) rather than individually strategic. A Builder voice would need to see the system's shape early; Duvakh earns that sight through punishment, which is the Condemned's signature arc, not the Builder's starting condition.

**Checked against the Master Diagnostic:** Does this voice let the reader see what Duvakh cannot? Yes — the reader can see, from the setting design, that the Division's legitimacy rests on a founding silence, and can see Ren Osar-Ked's Naturalized-register experience of enforcement-as-maintenance. Duvakh never gets access to the Warden-Speaker's interiority. He experiences the shut gate as absolute and inexplicable-in-its-mercy-or-cruelty; the reader, given a second index (see revelation strategy below), sees it as procedural. The gap is the engine, and it is tragic in texture even though the terminal attractor is not Tragedy — the *voice's* tone is somber/tense while the *plot's* attractor is generative underneath it. This is intentional: Seeded Possibility permits surface tragedy with underground transformation.

**Attractor compatibility:** Condemned + Seeded Possibility = ✓ explicitly listed as compatible. **PASS.**

**Rationality model check:** Condemned requires PIR model OR Mountain-dominated profile per the archetype table. This network is BIR, not PIR, and C1 is Tangled-Rope/Snare, not Mountain. **This is a flag, not a disqualification** — the archetype table's "Requires" line is calibrated for a Condemned who remains static throughout. Duvakh does not remain static: he transits from Condemned (Act 1-2, powerless/trapped, cannot see system shape) toward Builder-adjacent action (Act 3, organized, sees leverage) as his index changes. The voice is **Condemned with a Capture-arc-inverse** — not capture into the system, but earned partial sight *out* of naive constraint-experience into strategic constraint-navigation. This is closer to a **Misrecognition-adjacent arc**: Duvakh believes throughout that he is acting from desperation and grief; only in retrospect (if at all) does the coalition-organizing read as strategy. The voice should not narrate TR4 with Builder-clarity ("I realized this was leverage") — it should narrate TR4 with the same desperate, concrete register as TR1, and let the reader recognize the strategic shape the character does not name.

**Secondary/nested — Ren Osar-Ked as brief System-adjacent Witness fragments:** To satisfy the reader's-eye requirement (seeing what Duvakh cannot) without breaking single-POV intimacy, the Warden-Speaker's few appearances will be rendered from Duvakh's outside view — quoted liturgical speech, procedural affect — rather than a full second POV. This keeps the primary voice singular while letting Ren's Naturalized register show through *behavior* (the gate closes "the way you'd quarantine a sick animal") without requiring narrative access to his interiority.

**Rejected: full split-POV chorus (Duvakh / Ila / Ren).** While this would maximize indexical variance display, the Ω_P06 recommendation is to let Ila's arc resolve mid-story and recede — a full parallel POV structure would compete with the floor's unresolved status for weight in the closing movement, which the Omega log explicitly warns against. Single-POV with Ila as a strongly rendered secondary character (not narrator) preserves the required subordination.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**Selected: Gradual Realization**, with one deliberate late-story widening.

Single POV (Duvakh) throughout. The reader discovers, at the same pace as Duvakh, that:
- his council standing was never real power (revealed by TR3 — the gate shuts on him exactly as it would on any terrace-worker)
- Ila's capacity is not a tool he controls but a fact now loose in the world (revealed gradually across TR1-TR2, felt first as danger, only later as leverage)
- the coalition (TR4) is possible at all — this should arrive to the reader as a surprise roughly simultaneous with Duvakh's own recognition that other Wardens are asking the same questions he is

The **one departure from strict gradual realization**: in the final movement, at the widened Reckoning (TR5), the text should briefly let the reader see the liturgy's language ("as it was measured, so it remains") get spoken *unchanged* even as the schedule itself changes underneath it — a moment where the reader can perceive the floor's persistence more clearly than Duvakh can, because Duvakh is relieved and exhausted and watching his daughter, not listening for liturgical continuity. This is a single, brief step toward Analytical Observer territory, held to one image, not sustained — it is the mechanism by which "Seeded Possibility" becomes legible as a *shape* rather than simply an ambiguous ending.

**Rejected: Multi-POV Parallel.** Would surface the indexical variance mechanically rather than experientially, and would risk the theme-naming trap the Blurb Test is designed to catch (see below).
**Rejected: Late Revelation twist structure.** There is no concealed fact to reveal — the floor invariant's whole design is that it is never revealed to anyone, including the reader in propositional form. A twist structure would imply a hidden truth exists to be uncovered, which directly contradicts the Ω_C01 resolution.
**Rejected: Analytical Observer.** No character in this cast has license to be explicitly aware of indexical variance across positions — that awareness belongs structurally to no one in the Vale, which is the entire point of the founding silence.

  this naming convention IS the C1 index made visible in address 
  terms, without narration having to state it.

LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:
  Code-switching occurs at the boundary between village register and
  Ordinate register, and Duvakh, as Sluice-Warden, is the one character
  who natively code-switches between them — he can speak "stone-throat,

  even when he needs to at the Reckoning in TR4/TR5. This is a voice-

  powerless/biographical) without narrating the collapse abstractly.

EMOTIONAL CORE: 
  Not "is the system just" (theme-naming risk) but: what does it cost
  to be the one who was listened to, and still lose everything, and
  the specific vertigo of discovering — mid-crisis, not before it —

  solve and cannot revoke.

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**Selected marker: The sound and texture of the gate itself — wood, water, silence, and their absence.**

Rather than a single recurring object, the physical throughline is **the Torn Hollow gate as sensory presence and its absence**:
- Early: the give of the current under Duvakh's palm at the head-gate (carries the Stage 2 substrate sentence directly)
- Middle: the specific *sound* of water moving through gate-wood — a sound so constant Duvakh has never had to listen to it — and then its absence during the Drowning-Silence (the wrong silence, not peace but a held breath that doesn't end)
- Physical stage-markers for the Silence's duration (per Ω_C02: no countdown, only stages) — the changing color and smell of the drying loess terraces, the specific day the well-smell changes, children sent to beg water
- Late: the sound of the gate opening again — arriving without warning, un-counted-toward, exactly as specified in Stage 2 ("the ending is announced by the sound of the gate opening, not by any prior date anyone could have counted toward")

This satisfies the inhabitation-spec anti-pattern check directly: none of this is expressible as a number changing over time. It is registered entirely in sound, smell, and the specific loss of a texture (the give of the current) that Duvakh no longer has access to once his gate is sealed.

## Validation Pass

**Blurb test:** Duvakh's most important understanding — that being listened to in council was never the same as holding power — is exactly the thing his Condemned voice, pre-TR3, cannot articulate as thesis, because he doesn't know it yet; and post-TR3, when he learns it, the voice register is grief and physical loss, not analysis. He never says "I realize now that speech and power are different transactions." He shows it: reaching for Ordinate cadence and finding it doesn't come. **PASS — structural, not instructional.**

**Irresolvable tension test:** Ren's enforcement of the Drowning-Silence is genuinely, locally rational and even protective of the valley's coordination function (Coord(C1)=true is real, not false-consciousness) — and it is also the mechanism by which a father's fifteen years of being heard collapse to nothing overnight. The reader cannot comfortably resolve whether Ren is the villain of this story; the text does not let them, because Ren is right that *some* version of the schedule prevents war, and also the schedule's shut-gate punishment is disproportionate, unaccountable, and undated by design. **PASS — marked, Stage 4 must write toward it.**
