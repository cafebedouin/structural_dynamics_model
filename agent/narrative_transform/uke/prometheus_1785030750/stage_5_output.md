EMERGENT ELEMENTS:

The Prolog reports identify three overlapping constraints — `voice_without_leverage`, `disciplined_self_distrust_as_inherited_suppression`, `collective_refusal_as_sole_leverage` — all sharing the signature `constructed_high_extraction` and all showing the same institutional/powerless split (institutional seat reads "rope," every other seat reads "snare"). This is legible in the prose as a single mechanism wearing three faces:

**voice_without_leverage** manifests directly in Sethrei's fifteen years of council standing. He mistakes "being anticipated" for "being powerful" — the story states this explicitly: "I mistook being anticipated for being powerful." Ren's concessions (the half-hour extension, the gate-turn shift) are the "cover story" the theorem set predicts: real enough to be felt as responsiveness by the moderate/powerless seat, structurally costless to the institutional seat, which is why Ren can revoke everything with the same unchanging voice — "As it was measured, so it remains" — used for comfort and closure alike. The institutional seat's d=0.12 (near-zero directionality) versus the powerless seat's d=0.90 is exactly the gap the narration keeps circling without naming numerically: Sethrei has power *language* but no power *leverage*, and the story's real discovery is watching him learn the difference in his own mouth ("my mouth would not shape them into the cadence").

**disciplined_self_distrust_as_inherited_suppression** is the reading-training passed to Ila: "wait for the Throat to say it's so," the discipline of "finding water for your own people first... is a kind of theft even when your own people are dying of thirst." This is suppression internalized as virtue, generationally transmitted, and the story is unusually direct about the mechanism — it names the training as training, three generations deep, and shows the moment it breaks (Ila's hand moving before her voice can produce "the trained refusal").

**collective_refusal_as_sole_leverage** is the only lever that works. The story dramatizes exactly what the Prolog report calls "the coordination function" — Ren "could shut a gate. He could not make six Wardens speak." This is the piece's actual turn, and it correctly locates power not in argument (which never worked) but in synchronized withholding.

The perspectival gap is carried specifically: Sethrei/Ila/Marn Kohl occupy the powerless-and-moderate seats (the ones the engine's MaxEnt classifies as snare, P>0.999); Ren occupies the institutional seat (rope, d=0.12, chi≈-0.03 — literally near-zero at his own vantage). The story never gives Ren interiority beyond the "even face" and "voice that never changes pitch" — which is itself the correct dramatization of T3 (Spectral Dominance): the institutional observer's read doesn't diverge because he's lying, but because extraction is structurally invisible from where he stands. His own report even calls closing Sethrei's gate "maintenance," not cruelty — that's the cover-story mechanism narrated from inside the beneficiary's honest self-perception.

LATENT POTENTIAL:

The Frankl question is close to the surface but not fully claimed. Sethrei's closing meditation ("Neither of us asks who first put a gate here, or by what right...") gestures at meaning-in-constraint but resolves toward acceptance of the measure's *necessity* rather than into what the constraint specifically enabled him to become. The strongest unexploited seam: Sethrei explicitly says he still doesn't want the measure gone — he argues, "to his own surprise," for keeping the flood-month schedule that once protected his own terraces. This self-contradiction (hating the mechanism that also saved his daughter) is the richest material in the piece and is stated rather than dramatized — it appears in a single paragraph and then the story moves on.

The Oracle Gap (T4) the engine flags is under-instantiated as *character*. Ren is confident and wrong in a structural sense but the story never gives him a scene where the cross-position comparison actually happens to him — where he's shown, even briefly, information from Torn Hollow's vantage that his own instruments can't process. He remains an even face throughout. A scene of Ren almost seeing it — and closing the door on the almost — would embody T4 rather than merely permit the reader to infer it.

The corpus-derived edge between `disciplined_self_distrust_as_inherited_suppression` and `voice_without_leverage` (shared beneficiary: central_authority, but "distinct" independence, weak salience 0.30) suggests these are two faces of one fact rather than two facts — the story already senses this (Ila's reading-discipline and Sethrei's council-voice are explicitly the same training in different registers) but doesn't yet let them collide. Ila never confronts her father with the fact that his fifteen years of trained cadence is the adult version of "wait for the Throat to say it's so." That confrontation is implied and withheld.

Marn Kohl — "I never once thought the measure could bend" — is the story's clearest embodiment of a victim position with *no exit options and no arc*. He appears once, says his line, vanishes. The theorem set's "no_exit_for_victims" void is carried entirely by this single unreturned-to character; his flatness is thematically correct (he is not going to bend) but he's under-used as a counter-instance to Sethrei's eventual mobility.

STRONGEST WRITING:

The gate-silence passage is the spine of the piece: "I stood at my own gate an hour after they'd gone and put my palm flat against the wood the way I had done every dawn of my working life... and there was no give. There was nothing under my hand but wood, and under the wood, silence, and I understood in my body before I understood it in my mind that I no longer had a gate. I had a door someone else had shut." This is where constraint becomes flesh — the sentence rhythm itself withholds and then delivers the reversal ("a gate"/"a door someone else had shut") that is the whole story's argument in miniature.

Equally strong: "I mistook being anticipated for being powerful" — the single sentence that does the theoretical work the Prolog report spends paragraphs establishing (institutional seat vs. powerless seat divergence), earned entirely through Sethrei's own retrospective honesty rather than exposition.

The well-smell detail — "a flatness under the mineral taste, and every mother in Torn Hollow knew that smell meant *ration further* without anyone needing to say a number aloud" — is precise sensory writing that does the escalation work *without* counting, which is worth noting given the anchor check below: this is what non-anchored deprivation-signaling looks like, and it's very good.

Ren's characterization through hands rather than face: "his hands did not hesitate, they had not hesitated at the sluice-wheel above my gate any more than they would hesitate closing a gate against a flood... to him this was not cruelty. It was maintenance." This is the strongest single passage for dramatizing the institutional seat's honest blindness (T4/T3) rather than merely asserting it.

ANCHOR CHECK:

No numeric escalation, descending countdown, or explicit tally structure was found. The piece explicitly refuses to count — "I did not count it as the ninth day at the time... I will not pretend I counted" — which is a deliberate anti-anchor move, substituting qualitative deterioration (loess color, well-smell, jar-fullness) for arithmetic. The "six of the eleven Sluice-Wardens" and "two more... then two more" progression toward coalition is a count, but it functions as *coalition mass* (a structural threshold being crossed, dramatized through accumulating named agreement) rather than as inevitability-through-arithmetic — it doesn't escalate toward doom, it escalates toward leverage, and it's reported in prose rhythm, not tabulated. Borderline but not flagged as spine-displacing. Scanned: the well-smell passage, the ninth-day passage, the Warden-count passage, the "one jar empty" line. None read as the countdown-anchor pattern.

INVARIANT PROBE:

This is the report's central finding. Everything of value in this world is, in principle, readable by a better instrument or fairer authority — and the story knows it, states it, and then quietly closes the door on it anyway. The ledger, the oath, the shrine, the Warden's seal: Sethrei names all of them explicitly as places where "no answer" is written about who first claimed the right to the gate — but this is framed as absence-from-record, not as absence-in-principle. The Ordinate's error was recoverable: the spring gets "entered into the measure... as though it had always been accounted for." The system self-corrects, folds the anomaly in, and recites unchanged words over a changed schedule. Ila's *reading itself* — her hand's knowledge of the spring — was always in principle confirmable by "the Throat"; the entire crisis was that she withheld a fact the Ordinate's own machinery could have processed, not that the Ordinate was structurally incapable of processing it. The one candidate for a true invariant — Ila's bodily knowing prior to and independent of institutional confirmation ("something that had nothing to do with any measure the Throat had ever written down") — is asserted in the closing lines but not tested. We never see an instance where her reading is *right* and the Ordinate's confirmation apparatus is shown incapable, even in principle, of ratifying it. The story gestures at an unrecoverable real (the girl's hand knows before any authority can know) but keeps proving, scene after scene, that the authority eventually *does* fold the knowledge in. That is the gap: the piece asserts an invariant it structurally disproves.

SCOPE ASSESSMENT:

3,347 words — this is short-story length, and reads as a compressed novella-in-miniature: three generations, a political mechanism, a coalition-building arc, and a closing meditation are all present but each gets roughly a paragraph's worth of real estate. Expansion would require: (1) at least one full scene with Ren off his even register — even a failure to stay even — to embody T4 rather than assert it; (2) a scene of Ila and Sethrei in direct confrontation over the shared structure of their two disciplines (his council-cadence, her reading-suppression) rather than parallel-but-unmet arcs; (3) more room for Marn Kohl or an equivalent victim-position character whose story does *not* resolve, to earn the "no exit for victims" void the engine flags as still-active in the corpus classification. Compression would sacrifice the coalition-building middle section (six Wardens) — currently the most schematic, least embodied stretch — at the cost of losing the story's only depiction of collective leverage actually working. Current length serves the frame-narration mode (retrospective first person, "I want to be honest about where I stood before") well; it would strain under much more without either cutting the meditative asides or losing the intimacy that makes Sethrei's self-correction credible.
