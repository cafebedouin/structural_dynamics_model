# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor:** ☑ **Seeded Possibility**

**Justification:**

The constraint topology forbids clean resolution:

- **Deterministic Tragedy** is blocked: C₁ (the foam's silence) is a true Mountain (passes Boltzmann), but C₂ (the Queue) is constructed (fails Boltzmann). The story cannot end in pure inevitability because one constraint is natural law and one is designed extraction.

- **Negotiated Equilibrium** is blocked: PIR model means X₄ (Voss) maximizes extraction without bound. No Pareto-improving negotiation exists between institutional position (π = -0.2, net beneficiary) and powerless position (π = 1.5, Snare). The asymmetry is structural, not resolvable through bargaining.

- **Revolutionary Rupture** is blocked: All POV characters are identity-locked or trapped. Ren (Observer) cannot exit except via airlock. Kael (Archivist) cannot leave the Authority without losing entire identity. Sova (Maintenance Tech) cannot organize collective action (TR₁ blocked by high suppression, 0.90). No character has the structural position to rupture the system.

**Seeded Possibility** is the only viable attractor because:

1. **The seed is real:** The Obsolescence Pact (C₃) exists as a functional integrity practice. It does not reform C₂, but it provides a floor to stand on inside the Mechanism. The Pact is not hope — it is clarity about what translation actually is (unknowable fidelity purchased through extraction) and a refusal to participate in the lie.

2. **The seed is marginal:** C₃ adoption does not transform C₂. The Queue persists. The extraction persists. The Pact is a way to live with integrity inside the system, not a way to change it.

3. **The seed is transmissible:** Kael can adopt the Pact. Sova can adopt the Pact. The practice spreads through example, not organization (which would trigger suppression). It is a coordination mechanism (Coord = true) with zero extraction (ε = 0.00) and zero suppression (Supp = 0.00).

4. **The ending is ambiguous about scale:** The story ends with Kael deciding whether to adopt the Pact after discovering the queue criteria shift. The reader does not know if Kael chooses it. The reader does not know if the Pact spreads or dies with its current practitioners. The seed is planted; its germination is unknown.

**Attractor compatibility check:**
- PIR model: ✓ (Seeded Possibility does not require institutional rationality to change; it routes around it)
- Constraint topology: ✓ (C₁ Mountain + C₂ Snare/Tangled Rope + C₃ Rope = seed exists within unchangeable terrain)
- Character indices: ✓ (identity-locked and trapped positions can adopt C₃ without requiring exit)

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**Justification:**
- Three POV characters require space to establish indexical positions
- Constraint topology is complex (C₁ + C₂ + C₃, plus TR₂ firing mid-story)
- Seeded Possibility attractor requires the seed (C₃) to be dramatized, not just mentioned
- 12K-15K allows: 3 chapters per POV (9 chapters) + epilogue = 10 scenes, ~1200-1500 words each

**Justification:**
- Ren (Condemned): First person present locks the reader into immediate sensory experience, no analytical distance
