# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

**Selected: Seeded Possibility** ✓ CONFIRMED

Verification against Stage 1: C2 remains Tangled Rope/Snare across all agents at network closure. TR1→TR2 dependency chain is mechanically available (reacquisition is *possible in principle*) but blocked by C3's suppression at the point of narrative closure. C3 is BIR-satisficing (Agent_D genuinely convinced, not implacable) rather than PIR-implacable — this is precisely what keeps the door mechanically open rather than formally sealed. A pure Tragedy would require C3 to be immutable-by-design; it isn't. A pure Equilibrium would require some agent to complete the TR1→TR2 chain within the telling; none does.

The seed must be *visible in structure* (reader can see the mechanical path: witnessed-anomaly-event → Reading → TR1 → TR2) while *unrealized on the page*. This rules out any ending where the chain completes. It also rules out an ending that states the possibility outright — the seed is planted through juxtaposition of scenes, not through a character articulating "there is a way this could change."

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Ostrun Vey / Agent_A)**
**Secondary, alternating: The Zealot (Notary Ceth Ambrail / Agent_D)**

**Justification for Ostrun as Condemned:**
Ostrun's index (moderate π=1.0, identity-locked exit) is textbook Condemned territory. He is inside C2's logic — the fused cord is not a metaphor to him, it is the shape of his marriage and his grief simultaneously — and he experiences the block (C3 failing to seat his claim) as an immovable fact of the world rather than a suppression mechanism with a specific, nameable cause. His error manifestation (E2: False Mountain) is the engine of his voice: he does not attempt reacquisition strategies because identity_locked *reads* to him as ontological, not administrative. Attractor compatibility: Condemned ✓ Seeded Possibility per the matrix. PIR/Mountain-adjacent experience despite BIR substrate — this gap (character perceives Mountain, structure is Tangled Rope) is exactly what the voice must inhabit without narrating.

**Justification for Ceth as counterpoint, not co-lead:**
Ceth is the Zealot, not the System — critical distinction. Ceth is a *person* who has made the Reading's necessity their identity, not an inanimate voice of procedure. Ceth's χ=−0.108 Rope-classification is felt as righteousness, and the Omega Log's own recommendation (Ω_P05: remain wholly convinced, permit at most one small crack) matches the Zealot's structural requirement precisely: certainty is structural, not psychological, and the one crack must not become doubt-as-arc. High theater_ratio is present in the Reading's ceremonial form (the hearing, the categories, the seating-ritual) — Zealot requires exactly this. Attractor compatibility: Zealot ✓ Seeded Possibility (marked ⚠ in the reference, meaning it requires care) — the care taken here is that Ceth's chapter never resolves into either vindication or collapse; the crack stays a crack.

**Rejected: Nine-and-Nothing as POV.**
She is the structurally purest embodiment of the extraction (C3's silence, C2's total absence) but giving her voice risks the Condemned's fatal flaw at maximum amplitude: a child narrating her own unfiling would either overclaim analytical awareness she structurally cannot have (violates PIR-adjacent Mountain-perception) or become unbearable misery-narration with no counterweight. She remains the fixed point the two POVs orbit — seen, never narrating. This preserves the invariant: her value is not recoverable through being *heard*, because being heard is precisely the thing the ledger cannot do for her. Giving her narrative voice inside the story's own form would be a formal contradiction of the floor invariant.

**Rejected: Prel Kastade (Agent_B, the Beneficiary-adjacent powerful/mobile position) as POV.**
Tempting — his blindness (E1, Type III Snare-as-Rope) is a clean Beneficiary profile. But two POVs is already the right complexity for the length target below, and Ostrun's fused-cord trap already carries the "Tangled Rope felt as Mountain" error without needing a second demonstration. Prel appears in-scene, unnarrated, visible only through what Ostrun and Ceth notice about him — his ease is legible from outside, which is truer to the Beneficiary logic than would be his own untroubled first-person account.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**Multi-POV Parallel**, alternating Ostrun/Ceth, with Nine-and-Nothing as the shared fixed object both sections orbit.

The reader sees, before either character does, that Ostrun's block and Ceth's certainty are *the same event viewed from two indices* — Ostrun's Reading-failure and Ceth's routine, correctly-functioning Reading are one hearing. Neither character will ever be shown realizing this. The variance is entirely reader-side. This directly serves the irresolvable tension (see blueprint below) and prevents the theme-naming risk inherent in either voice's ceiling.

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The seal-cord itself** — braided, dyed, knotted, worn at the throat. This is the marker the invariant requires: not a disguise over selfhood but the only ground selfhood stands on. Structural uses across the two POVs:

- **Ostrun** feels his cord physically — its weight, its double-knot (marking the fusion), the place at the back of the neck where his and his wife's original cords were spliced by the Table's braiders twenty-two years ago and never re-separated. He touches the splice-knot the way a tongue returns to a missing tooth.
- **Ceth** sees cords constantly and professionally — reads tier, reads legitimacy, reads whether a petitioner's cord matches their claim — and does not feel their own cord at all. Zealot-appropriate: the marker that governs everyone else has become invisible to the person who administers it, precisely because they have never had reason to touch it and doubt it.
- **Nine-and-Nothing** wears no cord. Her throat is bare. This absence must be staged as a *specific, noticed physical fact* by other characters (Ostrun notices it once, viscerally; Prel Kastade does not notice it at all, which is how his blindness gets staged without narration) rather than described analytically.

### Structural Prevention Checks

**Blurb test:** Ostrun's single most important understanding — "the thing that fused me to her is the same thing that could theoretically be unfused, if the room that decides such things could be made to see it" — is *structurally unavailable* to the Condemned voice as stated. He can feel the wrongness of the fusion; he cannot conceptualize its reversibility as a procedural fact rather than a metaphysical one. This is enforced by voice archetype, not by instruction. Ceth's core understanding — "the filter must exist or the Table drowns" — she *can* say aloud, and does, in-scene, to a petitioner; this is permitted because it is her sincere belief, not an authorial thesis smuggled through her mouth, and the story does not endorse or refute it.

**Irresolvable tension:** Ceth is correct. An unfiltered Table probably would drown in unverifiable grief-claims. Nine-and-Nothing is also correctly, unbearably unfiled through no fault assignable to any single villain in the story. Both are true simultaneously and the story does not adjudicate between them. This is the tension Stage 4 must write toward, not resolve.
