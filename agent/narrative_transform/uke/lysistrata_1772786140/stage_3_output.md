## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Primary Voice:** The Builder (Kira)  
**Secondary Voice:** The Condemned (Dmitri)  
**Tertiary Voice:** The Witness (Amira)

### Justification

**Kira as Builder:**
- ✓ Sees partial system shape (recognizes C₂ asymmetry after organizing)
- ✓ Tests for leverage points (TR₁: collective organization, TR₂: Compact deployment)
- ✓ Acts strategically within constraints, learns from failures
- ✓ BIR model compatible (organized collective action)
- ✓ Moderate power (π = 0.4 after organization, was 1.5 before)
- ✓ Tangled Rope dominant (C₂ transforms from Snare to Rope via TR₁)
- ✓ Attractor compatible: Equilibrium (primary), Rupture (possible but not reached)

The Builder voice allows Kira to narrate strategic action without explaining the constraint topology. She can describe testing the Compact's cohesion, adjusting enforcement tactics, reading Coordinator responses — all tactical observations that reveal structure without naming it. Her voice is pragmatic, material, focused on what works.

**Dmitri as Condemned:**
- ✓ Inside constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
- ✓ Acts rationally given limited information (tries to wait it out, seeks individual solutions)
- ✓ Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable (the Pulse) and constructed but inescapable (the Compact)
- ✓ Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
- ✓ Attractor compatible: Tragedy (from his position), Seeded Possibility (if he glimpses the negotiation)
- ✓ PIR model OR Mountain-dominated: Partially — C₁ (Mountain) dominates his experience, but C₃ (Snare) is what traps him

The Condemned voice allows Dmitri to experience the Compact as trap without understanding its strategic logic. He feels the Pulse, counts the days, notices the women's cohesion without grasping how it's maintained. His blindness is not stupidity — it's structural position. He cannot see what Kira sees because he is not part of the organizing collective.

**Amira as Witness:**
- ✓ Observes constraint dynamics without direct stake (Compliance Officer role provides diegetic justification)
- ✓ Can see multiple indices simultaneously (crosses hub-drum boundary, sees both perspectives)
- ✓ Reports what happens without judgment or intervention (audit function)
- ✓ Voice is documentary, analytical, clinical
- ✓ Attractor compatible: Equilibrium (observes negotiation), Seeded Possibility (can see transformation potential)

The Witness voice allows structural exposition without theme-naming. Amira can report: "Compact cohesion remains stable at 94% compliance" without explaining why that matters. She can note: "Director Tanaka's request for psychological support services increased 340% this quarter" without interpreting it. The reader performs the analysis; Amira provides the data.

### Voice Combination Strategy: **Nested Counterpoint**

- **Primary narrative (60%):** Kira (Builder, first person, present tense)
- **Secondary narrative (30%):** Dmitri (Condemned, first person, present tense)
- **Tertiary framing (10%):** Amira (Witness, third person limited, past tense — audit reports, compliance logs)

The counterpoint structure allows the reader to see what each character cannot:
- Kira sees the Compact as strategic tool; Dmitri experiences it as trap
- Dmitri feels the Pulse as personal torment; Kira feels it as shared cost
- Amira sees both perspectives but cannot intervene (compliance role constrains her to observation)

The nested structure (Amira's reports frame the other two voices) provides temporal markers and structural context without omniscient narration.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected:** Multi-POV Parallel with Late Analytical Recontextualization

**Mechanism:**
- Kira and Dmitri narrate in parallel (alternating sections)
- Reader sees indexical variance immediately (Compact as Rope vs. Snare)
- Neither character explicitly recognizes the other's experience
- Amira's audit reports (sparse, clinical) provide third-index perspective
- Final act: Amira's report to Tanaka makes indexical variance explicit without theme-naming ("Compliance data indicates divergent constraint experiences across crew populations")

**Revelation beats:**
1. **Act 1:** Establish both perspectives separately (Kira: organizing, Dmitri: trapped)
2. **Act 2:** Parallel experiences of same events (shift change, crew meeting, Pulse intensity)
3. **Act 3:** Amira's audit report juxtaposes data from both indices, forcing Tanaka to recognize asymmetry
4. **Resolution:** Negotiation occurs offstage; Amira reports outcome; Kira and Dmitri experience consequences from their respective positions

The strategy avoids characters explaining indexical variance to each other (prevents theme-naming dialogue) while ensuring the reader sees it clearly.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Novelette (12,000-15,000 words)  
*Justification:* Three POVs, three-year timeline compression, negotiation arc requires space. Too short and the Compact's duration feels rushed; too long and the constraint topology becomes repetitive.

**POV:**  
- Kira: First person, present tense
- Dmitri: First person, present tense  
- Amira: Third person limited, past tense (audit reports)

*Justification:* First person for Builder and Condemned creates immediacy and limits perspective naturally. Third person for Witness maintains documentary distance. Tense shift (present for lived experience, past for institutional record) marks the boundary between subjective and analytical registers.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 4 primary (Kira, Dmitri, Amira, Tanaka), 6-8 secondary (other Compact members, other trapped men, Coordinator staff)

*Justification:* Minimum viable cast for constraint interactions. Kira needs Compact collective visible (at least 3-4 other women named). Dmitri needs other trapped men visible (at least 2-3 named). Tanaka needs institutional context (1-2 staff). More than 12 total characters risks diffusion.

**NAMING:** Cultural (realistic given setting)  
*Justification:* Ganymede Station is multi-generational, multi-ethnic. Names reflect Earth origins: Kira Okonkwo (Yoruba), Dmitri Volkov (Russian), Amira Osei (Akan), Yuki Tanaka (Japanese). Naming strategy naturalizes the setting's history without exposition.

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

*Code-switching rules:*
- **Drum Cant:** Appears in Kira and Dmitri's internal monologue and Extractor-to-Extractor dialogue. Clipped syntax, technical slang, metaphors from rotation/gravity. Example: "Forty-seven hands on the rope, nobody lets go."
- **Consortium Standard:** Appears in all Coordinator speech, all Extractor-to-Coordinator interaction, all official contexts. Formal, precise, institutional. Example: "Operational compliance remains within acceptable parameters."
- **Code-switch moments:** Mark power transitions. When Kira addresses Amira, she switches to Standard mid-sentence if the interaction becomes official. When Dmitri logs a request, his internal Cant voice stops and Standard takes over.

*Frequency:* Cant appears in ~40% of Kira's narration, ~50% of Dmitri's (he has less reason to perform Standard). Standard appears in 100% of Amira's reports, 100% of Tanaka's speech. Code-switches occur 8-12 times across the narrative, always at institutional boundaries.

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** Exhaustion  
*Not imposed — derived from constraint dynamics:* The Compact has held for three years. Kira is tired of enforcing it. Dmitri is tired of enduring it. Amira is tired of documenting it. Tanaka is tired of managing it. The negotiation happens not because anyone has an epiphany, but because everyone is exhausted and satisficing is rational.

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Equilibrium established, system persists

*Mechanism:* The negotiation produces modification of the reproduction prohibition (Extractors can apply for conception approval, subject to radiation exposure review). The Compact dissolves. Kira experiences this as partial victory — leverage worked, terms shifted, but the Separation remains. Dmitri experiences this as relief — the trap opened, but the Pulse remains and the new system still extracts (approval process, review boards, Coordinator discretion). Amira reports the outcome clinically. Tanaka believes the disruption is resolved. The reader sees that the power differential persists, the architecture persists, the extraction persists — just under modified terms.

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

### Kira (Builder)

**Phenomenological register:** Hypervigilance + Material Accounting

Kira's body has learned to track: rotation schedules (when the next shift change opens the transit tubes), crew positions (who is where, who might defect), physical exhaustion markers (how many hours since last sleep, how much longer she can enforce). She notices exits even when she's not planning to use them. She counts things: days since the Compact formed (1,095), women still holding (47), men who've tried to negotiate individually (23), requests denied (23).

The Pulse for Kira is not romantic longing — it's a specific physical sensation she has learned to recognize and manage. Tightness in the chest during ovulation. Intrusive thoughts about touch during high-stress shifts. The sensation is predictable, cyclical, and she tracks it the way she tracks equipment maintenance schedules. The difference between her and Dmitri is that she has forty-six other people tracking it with her, so the sensation is data, not torment.

The 1.2g gravity is constant work. Standing is effort. Walking is effort. Enforcement is effort. She notices when her legs ache (always), when her back aches (after long shifts), when exhaustion makes her hands shake (she hides this). The high-g environment means her body is always under load, always compensating, always tired.

**Anti-pattern check:** None of this is a single number changing over time. The Pulse is not "intensity level 7." The exhaustion is not "fatigue score 8/10." These are modes of attention — what Kira notices, how she notices it, what her body has learned to do.

### Dmitri (Condemned)

**Phenomenological register:** Numbness + Displacement

Dmitri has stopped noticing things that used to hurt. The first year of the Compact, he felt rage. The second year, he felt despair. The third year, he feels mostly nothing. He is surprised when other men react intensely to situations he finds normal. Someone breaks down during a shift meeting; Dmitri doesn't understand why now, why this moment, when every moment has been like this for three years.

The Pulse for Dmitri is not a sensation he tracks — it's a sensation that ambushes him. He'll be processing ice, focused on flow rates and temperature gradients, and suddenly his hands are shaking and he can't remember why he's standing here. The Pulse doesn't arrive on a schedule. It arrives when his guard is down, and it takes him apart.

He feels the Compact in the wrong places. His hands ache when it's his chest that's compressed. He tastes metal when it's dignity that's corroded. The displacement is not metaphor — it's how his nervous system has learned to distribute a pain it cannot process directly. The 1.2g gravity used to feel normal; now it feels like the station is pressing down on him specifically.

**Anti-pattern check:** The numbness is not "emotional flatness score 6." The displacement is not "pain location error." These are phenomenological modes — how Dmitri's attention has deformed under sustained extraction.

### Amira (Witness)

**Phenomenological register:** Boundary Crossing + Comparative Noticing

Amira's body registers the transition between hub and drums as physical shift. The 0.3g hub feels like floating; the 1.2g drums feel like weight returning. She notices this every time she crosses, and she notices that Coordinators who never cross don't notice it at all. She has learned to read crew exhaustion by watching how people stand — Extractors in the drums stand differently than Coordinators in the hub, and the difference is measurable (posture, gait, how long they can stand still before shifting weight).

She does not feel the Pulse the way Extractors do (she can get conception approval if she wants it; she doesn't want it). She does not feel the Compact the way men do (it does not target her). Her phenomenological register is comparative: she notices what others don't notice, she sees patterns across populations, she registers differences that individuals cannot see because they only inhabit one position.

The audit reports are not emotional distance — they are a specific kind of attention. She notices: compliance rates, defection attempts, request patterns, psychological support utilization. She records these as data because that is her job, but she also knows what the data means. The gap between what she records and what she knows is the source of her exhaustion.

**Anti-pattern check:** The boundary crossing is not "gravity differential measurement." The comparative noticing is not "statistical analysis." These are modes of attention — what Amira sees because she moves between positions that others cannot cross.

## 6. ACT STRUCTURE

### Act 1: Constraint Activation (3,000 words)

**Kira (Builder):** Present day, Year 3 of the Compact. Shift change in Drum Section 7. Kira enforces compliance — a woman (Lena) is wavering, considering breaking the Compact for a specific man (not Dmitri). Kira does not threaten violence; she reminds Lena what social exile means. Lena holds. Kira feels the cost of enforcement (exhaustion, the Pulse hitting her too, the knowledge that she's asking Lena to endure what she herself endures). She counts: 1,095 days, 47 women, zero defections. The Compact is a rope she climbs with her crew.

**Dmitri (Condemned):** Same shift change, Drum Section 5. Dmitri processes ice, tries to focus on work, fails. The Pulse ambushes him mid-shift. He cannot satisfy it without violating the Consortium contract or the Compact terms. He cannot leave. He cannot wait it out. He has tried individual negotiation with Compact members (rejected). He has tried requesting Coordinator intervention (denied). He is trapped. He counts: 1,095 days, zero exits, six years remaining on his contract.

**Amira (Witness):** Compliance audit report, dated three weeks prior. Amira documents: Compact cohesion stable at 94% (three women have left Ganymede via contract completion, no defections among remaining members). Psychological support requests from male Extractors increased 340% over baseline. Director Tanaka has requested options for "resolving the operational disruption." Amira's report is clinical, but she knows what the numbers mean.

**Constraint physics beat:** False Mountain reveal for C₁. Dmitri believes the Pulse is something he can overcome through willpower or distraction (Type II error: Mountain Denial). Kira knows the Pulse is unchangeable terrain — the Compact leverages it, doesn't fight it. The reader sees Dmitri trying to eliminate the drive; the reader sees Kira strategizing around it.

### Act 2: Constraint Coupling (6,000 words)

**Kira (Builder):** Compact enforcement meeting. Forty-seven women in a cargo bay, off-shift, discussing whether to maintain or dissolve. Some argue for dissolution (three years is enough, the point is made). Kira argues for holding (dissolution without concessions means the Separation remains unchanged, the leverage is lost). The vote: hold. Kira sees the cost in every face — exhaustion, the Pulse, isolation. She also sees the coordination: forty-seven people acting as one, the Separation's architecture turned into organizational infrastructure. The Compact is working because it's a rope, not a chain.
