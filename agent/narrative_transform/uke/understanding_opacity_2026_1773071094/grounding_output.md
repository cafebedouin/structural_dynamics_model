`✗` **Contrary position on the substitution hypothesis:**

Evidence supporting this contrary view:
- Accurate prediction requires some model of internal states (not just behavioral patterns)
- Long-term partners often report *both* increased prediction accuracy *and* deeper appreciation of their partner's complexity
- The "surprise" phenomenon in long relationships may indicate understanding's growth, not its absence

The document acknowledges this as "the weakest link" but doesn't fully address why substitution is more likely than complementarity. The Gaiman quote ("people are far more complicated than they appear") supports complexity but doesn't prove substitution over complementarity.

`⚖️` **[MCI:0.58→Check] Medium confidence intervention:**

The conformity extraction claim rests on distinguishing "coordination" from "extraction." The document provides three markers:
1. Non-itemized concessions
2. Distributed enforcement
3. Psychologically costly exit

However, these markers could also describe legitimate collective decision-making where:
- Concessions are implicit because they're minor and consensual
- Enforcement is distributed because norms are genuinely shared

**Alternative interpretation:** What appears as "extraction" may be the normal friction cost of coordination in groups with genuine shared values. The document needs stronger criteria for distinguishing extractive from functional conformity.

`E` **Sharpening the voluntary presence constraint:**

The claim "genuine voluntary presence requires maintained structural capacity for withdrawal" needs precision:

**What counts as "maintained capacity"?**
- Zero exit cost? (Impossible in any meaningful relationship)
- Proportional exit cost? (Cost relative to relationship duration/depth)
- Reversible exit cost? (Can be recovered after leaving)
- Catastrophic threshold? (Cost that doesn't destroy other life domains)

The document uses "prohibitively costly" and "catastrophic cost" but doesn't operationalize these thresholds. Without specification, the claim becomes unfalsifiable—any relationship with friction could be labeled "captive."
