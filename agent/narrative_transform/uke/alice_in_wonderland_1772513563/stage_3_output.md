# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## I. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor:** ☑ **Deterministic Tragedy**

**Justification:**

The constraint topology demands this attractor. Here's why:

1. **Rationality Model Constraint (BIR):** The specification explicitly uses Bounded Institutional Rationality. Thane satisfices rather than maximizes. The Council maintains order through selective enforcement, not perfect control. This creates a stable equilibrium where:
   - Thane genuinely believes he's optimizing within constraints
   - Kael rationally adapts to unchangeable terrain
   - The system persists because no actor has both knowledge and power to change it

2. **Transformation Rule Blockage Pattern:** Every transformation rule is structurally blocked:
   - TR₁ (Collective Organization): Blocked by C₂ itself — formalizing the Contract makes it visible to C₃
   - TR₂ (Exit Attempt): Blocked by C₁ — no verified outside exists
   - TR₄ (Time Horizon Extension): Changes knowledge but not power position

3. **Indexical Variance Lock:** The three-constraint system creates a perfect trap:
   - C₁ (Breath Ceiling) appears as Mountain from (immediate, trapped) index
   - C₂ (Spiral Contract) functions as Rope for Thane, Tangled Rope for Kael
   - C₃ (The Hammer) is Snare for subjects, Rope for enforcer
   
   This variance is irreducible. Thane cannot see what Kael sees. Kael cannot access what Thane has. The revelation (TR₄) breaks the False Mountain but doesn't break the trap.

4. **Why Not Other Attractors:**
   - **Negotiated Equilibrium:** Requires both parties to see the extraction. Thane's naturalization flag (Ω_P03) prevents this — he genuinely believes the system is necessary.
   - **Revolutionary Rupture:** Requires either power shift or collective action. TR₁ shows collective action is blocked by C₂'s structure. No power shift mechanism exists.
   - **Seeded Possibility:** Requires a viable transformation path. All transformation rules are blocked. The "seed" (Vex's revelation) changes understanding but not material conditions.

5. **The Tragic Structure:** The revelation (TR₄) is the tragedy's fulcrum. Kael learns the Breath Ceiling is constructed, not natural. This knowledge:
   - Destroys his adaptation strategy (can't unsee the injustice)
   - Doesn't provide a solution (still powerless, still trapped)
   - Creates moral burden (knowing means complicity if he stays silent)
   - Triggers C₃ if he acts (the Hammer falls on truth-tellers)

**Attractor Compatibility Check:**
- ✓ BIR model supports stable unjust equilibrium
- ✓ All transformation rules blocked by constraint structure
- ✓ Revelation changes knowledge, not power
- ✓ No deus ex machina required — the tragedy is structural
- ✓ Characters act rationally from their indices throughout

**Conclusion:** This is a deterministic tragedy because the system's structure makes the outcome inevitable. The revelation doesn't save Kael — it just shows him why he can't be saved.

## II. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Primary Voice:** ☑ **The Condemned**

**Secondary Voice (Counterpoint):** ☑ **The Beneficiary**

**Justification:**

### Primary: The Condemned (Kael)

**Why this archetype:**

1. **Index Alignment:** Kael occupies (powerless, immediate, trapped, local) — the exact index that produces χ = 1.08 for C₃ (Snare). He is structurally condemned.

2. **Blindness Pattern:** From his index, C₁ (Breath Ceiling) reads as Mountain — unchangeable environmental fact. He experiences constraint as natural/inevitable, not constructed. This is the Condemned's defining characteristic.

3. **Rationality Within Limits:** Kael acts rationally given his information:
   - Submits maintenance requests (rational response to broken systems)
   - Participates in Spiral Contract (rational survival strategy)
   - Avoids organizing (rational fear of the Hammer)
   
   His actions are competent. His position is impossible.

4. **Voice Characteristics:**
   - Immediate, experiential (not reflective)
   - Cannot see systemic shape (experiences effects, not causes)
   - Speaks in Spiral Common (concrete, material, relationship-focused)
   - No analytical register (doesn't theorize about the system)

5. **Attractor Compatibility:** Condemned + Deterministic Tragedy is the classic pairing. The voice experiences the constraint as inevitable while the reader sees its construction. The gap between what Kael knows and what we know *is* the tragedy.

**Stage 4 Directive:** Kael never explains the system. He reports what happens to his body, his daughter, his neighbors. "The air gets thin around day twenty" — not "the Council reduces our allocation mid-month." The reader performs the translation. Kael just breathes.

### Secondary: The Beneficiary (Vex)

**Why this archetype:**

1. **Index Alignment:** Vex occupies (analytical, biographical, arbitrage, local) with π = 0.6 (powerful). For C₂ (Spiral Contract), her χ = 0.24 → Rope. She experiences coordination without extraction.

2. **Structural Blindness:** Vex genuinely cannot see the extraction burden on lower levels. From her position:
   - The Breath Ceiling looks like environmental management
   - The Spiral Contract looks like efficient self-organization
   - The Hammer looks like necessary discipline
   
   This isn't willful ignorance — it's indexical truth. The constraint reads as Rope from where she stands.

3. **Likability Requirement:** Vex must be genuinely appealing:
   - Intellectually curious (values archival accuracy)
   - Professionally competent (maintains critical documentation)
   - Personally kind (helps colleagues, respects the Contract from a distance)
   
   The reader should *want* to be inside her perspective. The discomfort comes from recognizing what she can't see, not from the text judging her.

4. **Voice Characteristics:**
   - Speaks Technical Standard fluently (her native register)
   - Analytical but not systemic (sees patterns, not power)
   - Comfortable, assured (the world works from her position)
   - No signals of privilege awareness (there's nothing to signal)

5. **Counterpoint Function:** Vex's sections show the same physical markers Kael experiences, but from a position where they mean something different:
   - Kael: "The air gets thin around day twenty."
   - Vex: "I noticed the lower-level staff seemed tired this week."
   
   Same reality, opposite experiences. The reader sees both. Neither narrator does.

**Stage 4 Directive:** Vex never becomes self-aware. No moment where she "realizes her privilege." No dramatic irony heavy enough to feel like judgment. If she shares the engineering reports with Kael (TR₄), it's not because she's had an awakening — it's because she values archival accuracy, or because Kael asked a technical question she could answer, or because she genuinely didn't realize the implications. The Beneficiary stays innocent. The reader does the math.

### Voice Combination Strategy: **Counterpoint Voice**

**Structure:** Alternating POV chapters. Kael (Condemned) and Vex (Beneficiary) observe the same events from opposite ends of the power scale.

**Physical Marker Contrast:**
- **Kael counts breaths.** When the allocation runs low, he notices his daughter's breathing rate, the way conversations become effortful, the headaches that start mid-month.
- **Vex notices temperature.** Her climate-controlled archive stays comfortable. When she visits lower levels, she finds the air "stuffy" but attributes it to density, not deprivation.

**The Gap:** The reader sees that Kael is counting breaths because the air is running out. Vex notices temperature because her air never runs out. Neither narrator can see what the other experiences. The friction is the story.

**Revelation Strategy (TR₄):** Vex discovers the engineering reports during routine archival work. She shares them with Kael because he asked a technical question about atmospheric capacity. She thinks she's answering a colleague's curiosity. Kael realizes he's holding proof that the Breath Ceiling is constructed.

The tragedy: Vex's action is structurally innocent (she's just doing her job), but it destroys Kael's adaptation strategy (he can't unsee the injustice). Neither character is wrong. The system is wrong. And the system persists.

## III. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected Strategy:** ☑ **Multi-POV Parallel** (with late-stage convergence)

**Implementation:**

### Phase 1: Parallel Blindness (Acts 1-2)

**Structure:** Alternating chapters, Kael and Vex POV.

**Kael's Arc:**
- Experiences C₁ (Breath Ceiling) as Mountain — environmental fact
- Participates in C₂ (Spiral Contract) as survival strategy
- Fears C₃ (The Hammer) as unpredictable threat
- Voice: Immediate, material, experiential
- Physical marker: Counting breaths, measuring air quality by body sensation

**Vex's Arc:**
- Experiences C₁ as managed environmental system
- Observes C₂ from outside (respects it, doesn't need it)
- Doesn't perceive C₃ (enforcement is invisible from her position)
- Voice: Analytical, comfortable, professionally focused
- Physical marker: Temperature, comfort, spatial ease

**Reader Position:** Sees both indices simultaneously. Recognizes:
- The Breath Ceiling isn't environmental (Vex's air never fluctuates)
- The Spiral Contract is asymmetric (Vex benefits without contributing)
- The Hammer is selective (Kael fears it, Vex doesn't know it exists)

**Critical Rule:** Neither narrator signals awareness of the other's experience. Kael doesn't think "the upper levels have it better" — he thinks "this is how air works." Vex doesn't think "I'm privileged" — she thinks "the system functions well." The reader performs the extraction math. The narrators don't.

### Phase 2: Revelation Asymmetry (Act 3)

**Trigger:** Vex discovers the engineering reports. Shares them with Kael (motivated by intellectual honesty, not moral awakening).

**Kael's Response:**
- C₁ reclassifies: Mountain → Snare (constructed trap, not natural law)
- Knowledge changes, power doesn't
- Must choose: stay silent (complicity) or speak (trigger C₃)
- Voice shifts: gains analytical register (can now see system shape) but loses experiential innocence (can't unsee the injustice)

**Vex's Response:**
- No reclassification (her experience of C₂ as Rope remains true from her index)
- Intellectually curious about the historical discrepancy
- Doesn't connect the reports to current extraction (the gap is too abstract)
- Voice unchanged: still comfortable, still analytical, still structurally blind

**Reader Position:** Sees the tragedy forming. Kael now knows what we know, but he's still trapped. Vex still doesn't know, and her innocence is structural (not moral failure). The system persists because knowledge and power are separated.

### Phase 3: Deterministic Collapse (Resolution)

**Kael's Choice:** [Left open for Stage 4, but constrained by attractor]

Options that preserve Deterministic Tragedy:
1. **Silent Complicity:** Kael stays silent. The knowledge destroys him internally, but he protects his daughter by not triggering C₃. The system persists. He becomes what he feared — someone who knows and does nothing.

2. **Doomed Resistance:** Kael shares the reports with Ren & Davi. They attempt TR₁ (collective organization). C₃ triggers (the Hammer falls). Allocation cuts, maintenance denials, possible exile to the lowest levels. The resistance is crushed. The system persists.

3. **Exit into Void:** Kael attempts TR₂ (exit). Discovers the surface sensors are broken — no one knows if outside is habitable. Must choose between certain extraction (stay) and uncertain death (leave). Either choice is a loss. The system persists.

**Vex's Position:** Unchanged. She filed the reports correctly, answered a colleague's question, maintained archival integrity. From her index, she did everything right. The tragedy is that "doing everything right" from a Beneficiary position means enabling extraction without seeing it.

**Final Image:** Kael counting his daughter's breaths. Vex adjusting the archive temperature. Same silo, same air, opposite experiences. The reader sees both. Neither narrator does. The system persists.

## IV. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

### Length
**Selected:** ☑ **Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)**

**Justification:** 
- Three-constraint system requires space to establish each constraint's texture
- Dual POV needs sufficient chapters to establish both voices before convergence
- Revelation (TR₄) needs setup (Vex's archival work, Kael's growing suspicion)
- Deterministic Tragedy needs time to feel inevitable, not rushed
- Target: ~12,000 words (allows 6 chapters × 2,000 words, alternating POV)

### POV
**Selected:** ☑ **Third Limited** (alternating between Kael and Vex)

**Justification:**
- Condemned archetype works in first or close third; third limited chosen for:
  - Easier POV alternation (clearer chapter breaks)
  - Allows physical description of breathing, body language (harder in first person)
  - Maintains intimacy (close third, not distant omniscient)
- Beneficiary archetype requires third limited to sustain structural blindness:
  - First person would force Vex to articulate her comfort (makes privilege visible)
  - Third limited lets us see her comfort without her naming it
  - "Vex adjusted the temperature" vs. "I adjusted the temperature" — the former is observation, the latter is confession

### Tense
**Selected:** ☑ **Past**

**Justification:**
- Deterministic Tragedy benefits from retrospective framing (the outcome is already determined)
- Past tense allows narrative distance that reinforces inevitability
- Present tense would create false urgency (implies outcomes are still open)
- Condemned voice in past tense: "The air had been thin for three years now" — the duration itself is the constraint

### Character Count
**Selected:** **5 primary, 3 secondary**

**Primary (POV or major presence):**
1. Kael (Condemned, Level 42)
2. Vex (Beneficiary, Level 19)
3. Senna (Level 38, identity-locked, Rope experience of C₂)
4. Thane (Level 3, institutional, enforcer)
5. Kael's daughter (physical marker, stakes embodiment)

**Secondary (structural function):**
1. Ren & Davi (Level 44, attempted organizers, trigger TR₁)
2. Council member (besides Thane, shows institutional consensus)

**Justification:**
- Minimum for constraint interactions: Need representatives at multiple indices
- Kael + Vex = core indexical contrast
- Senna = shows middle position (neither fully condemned nor beneficiary)
- Thane = shows enforcer index (negative χ, experiences C₃ as Rope)
- Daughter = physical stakes (not just abstract extraction)
- Ren & Davi = trigger transformation attempt (show blockage pattern)
