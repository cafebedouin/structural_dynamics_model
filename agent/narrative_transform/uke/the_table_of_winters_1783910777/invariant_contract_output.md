## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The untranslatable real (Detector A — low-ε Mountain recovered):**
A weaver's hands know the tension of silk threads in ways no loom specification can capture. Decades of work develop a tactile vocabulary — the give of warp under humidity, the whisper before a thread breaks, the rhythm that prevents repetitive injury. This knowledge keeps production flowing and bodies intact, but it dies the instant anyone tries to write it down, measure it, or teach it through manual. The Textile Standards Bureau's certification exams test only what can be enumerated: thread count, defect rate, output speed. The real skill — the thing that separates a surviving weaver from a broken one — registers as zero on every instrument the Bureau recognizes.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a recoverable "true skill metric" that the certification system merely measured incorrectly, the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**The missing floor (Detector B — from Stage 0 contract):**
The Textile Standards Bureau does not certify weavers because weaving requires certification. It certifies weavers because the Imperial procurement system refuses to purchase cloth from uncertified workshops. The refusal creates the necessity. There is no neutral ground beneath this: before the Bureau existed, cloth circulated through kinship networks and guild vouching. The current system is not a correction of chaos — it is the replacement of one coordination mechanism with another, chosen by those who profit from the replacement.

**Falsifier:** If the story frames the certification requirement as a necessary correction of a pre-existing market failure, the floor invariant was lost.

**The substrate that holds the invariant:**
The body itself — muscle memory, callus patterns, the specific deformation of finger joints from decades of shuttle work, the lung capacity developed to blow dust from finished cloth. The substrate is biological and sensory. It cannot be photographed for a manual, cannot be transferred except through years of apprenticeship, and becomes illegible the moment it is reduced to a Bureau checklist.

**Substrate inhabitation sentence:**
You are a master weaver whose body has become the instrument, and the only language that speaks this mastery is the one the certification examiners cannot read.
