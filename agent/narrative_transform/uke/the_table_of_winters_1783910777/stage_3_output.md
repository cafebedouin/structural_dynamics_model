# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Stage 1 Selection:** Seeded Possibility

**Verification against constraint topology:**

✓ **BIR compatibility:** The Bureau operates under bounded rationality — Wang Daoming genuinely believes the system works, optimizes for measurable efficiency, but faces principal-agent separation (Ministry goals ≠ weaver welfare) and information asymmetry (cannot measure embodied knowledge). This creates space for institutional uncertainty that PIR would eliminate.

✓ **Constraint profile match:** Snare-dominated (C₁, C₂ both Snares for X₁), with Tangled Rope present (C₃), no Mountains. Analytical agent (X₂) exists with recognition capacity.

✓ **Transformation blockage:** C₁ (certification requirement) blocks TR₁ (collective organization — atomization via individual certification) and TR₂ (alternative system — procurement system refuses non-Bureau cloth). Surface tragedy persists.

✓ **Underground capacity:** X₂'s analytical position (π=1.15) provides recognition that statistical accuracy ≠ neutrality, that Bureau standards measure wrong thing. This recognition is transmissible (Qiu Yue teaching Mei Xiang in "stolen hours") even if not actualized in current generation.

✓ **Generational omega:** Stage 1 specifies: "whether the next generation's awareness of the system's blindness constitutes a potential for rupture or is merely the final stage of acceptance before assimilation." This IS Seeded Possibility structure — transformation potential exists, outcome indeterminate.

**ATTRACTOR CONFIRMED:** Seeded Possibility
- Surface: Deterministic Tragedy (Mei Xiang's body breaks on Bureau timeline)
- Underground: Analytical recognition (Qiu Yue's knowledge transmitted, not actualized)
- Omega: Unresolved generational question (does awareness enable rupture or complete capture?)

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected:** The Condemned (primary) + The Witness (framing device)

**Justification:**

**Primary voice — The Condemned (Mei Xiang, X₁):**
- ✓ Inside constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
- ✓ Acts rationally given limited information and structural position (passes exam, meets quota, sends wages home)
- ✓ Experiences constraint as natural/inevitable, not constructed (Bureau system is "how things work now")
- ✓ Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
- ✓ Attractor compatible: Seeded Possibility ✓ (Condemned can carry surface tragedy while underground transformation happens around them)
- ✓ Requires: PIR model OR Mountain-dominated, powerless/moderate (π ≥ 1.0) — SATISFIED (π = 1.5, powerless position)
- ✓ POV: First person or close third — **SELECTING close third** (creates intimacy while allowing physical observation Mei Xiang herself wouldn't articulate)

**Why this voice works:**
Mei Xiang cannot see that the certification system is constructed (she experiences it as "the way to get work"), cannot see the indexical variance (doesn't know Wang Daoming experiences C₁ as revenue stream), cannot see that her aunt's "old ways" are structurally superior (only that they don't pass inspection). Her blindness is not stupidity — it's structural position. She acts rationally: pass exam, meet quota, keep job, send money home. The tragedy is that rational action within the constraint accelerates bodily breakdown.

**Framing voice — The Witness (archival/retrospective):**
- ✓ Observes constraint dynamics without direct stake
- ✓ Reports what happens without judgment or intervention
- ✓ Can see multiple indices simultaneously (Mei Xiang's experience + Qiu Yue's knowledge + Wang Daoming's metrics)
- ✓ Voice is documentary, clinical
- ✓ Attractor compatible: Seeded Possibility ✓ (Witness can frame the seed without resolving it)
- ✓ POV: Third omniscient, retrospective framing

**Why framing is necessary:**
Pure Condemned voice cannot carry Seeded Possibility — Mei Xiang cannot narrate the underground transformation because she doesn't see it. The Witness frame provides:
1. Temporal distance (story is being reconstructed, not lived)
2. Access to Qiu Yue's analytical position without breaking Mei Xiang's blindness
3. Ability to show indexical variance (Mei Xiang's pain + Wang Daoming's metrics) without either character explaining it
4. Space for the generational omega (frame can note what happened to next generation without resolving it)

**Voice combination strategy:** Nested Voice
- **Outer frame:** Witness (archival reconstruction, 1850s, thirty years after events)
- **Inner narrative:** Condemned close-third (Mei Xiang's immediate experience, 1821-1823)
- **Intercalated sections:** Witness observing Qiu Yue (analytical position) and Wang Daoming (institutional position)

**Tone:** Tragic, somber (from Condemned's experience) + clinical (from Witness's distance)

**Master diagnostic:**
> Does this voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot?

YES. Mei Xiang cannot see:
- That the certification system is constructed (she experiences it as inevitable)
- That her aunt's techniques would preserve her body (she only knows they fail inspection)
- That Wang Daoming's metrics measure the wrong thing (she only knows she must meet them)
- That her rational choices are accelerating her breakdown (she only knows her shoulder hurts)

The reader sees all of this through the gap between Mei Xiang's immediate experience and the Witness's retrospective observation.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected:** Multi-POV Parallel (via Witness frame)

**Structure:**
- **Primary thread:** Mei Xiang (Condemned, close-third) — immediate experience, cannot see system
- **Analytical thread:** Qiu Yue (observed by Witness) — sees system's blindness, cannot act
- **Institutional thread:** Wang Daoming (observed by Witness) — sees metrics, cannot see bodies

**Revelation mechanism:**
Reader sees indexical variance through parallel observation, NOT through character realization. Mei Xiang never understands that Wang Daoming experiences C₁ as coordination tool. Wang Daoming never sees that his efficiency metrics measure bodily destruction. Qiu Yue sees both but cannot transmit the recognition in language the Bureau accepts.

**Key revelation moments:**
1. **C₁ variance:** Mei Xiang borrows exam fee (extraction) // Wang Daoming reviews revenue reports (coordination)
2. **C₂ variance:** Mei Xiang's shoulder pain at quota pace (extraction) // Wang Daoming's completion-time tables (efficiency)
3. **C₃ variance:** Mei Xiang re-weaves "defective" bolt (extraction) // Wang Daoming's defect-rate trends (quality control)

**No character ever explains the variance.** The Witness shows it. The reader performs the extraction math.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)
- Justification: Seeded Possibility requires time to establish surface tragedy AND underground transformation. Flash/short cannot carry generational omega. Novelette allows three-act structure with intercalated analytical/institutional threads.

**POV:** Third Limited (Mei Xiang primary) + Third Omniscient (Witness frame)
- Primary: Close third on Mei Xiang (Condemned voice)
- Frame: Omniscient Witness (archival reconstruction)
- Intercalated: Third limited on Qiu Yue and Wang Daoming (observed by Witness, not narrated by them)

**TENSE:** Past (both frame and primary narrative)
- Frame: "The records from the Jiaqing period show..."
- Primary: "Mei Xiang's hands moved across the warp threads..."

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 (minimum for constraint interactions)
- Mei Xiang (X₁) — primary POV, Condemned
- Qiu Yue (X₂) — analytical position, observed
- Wang Daoming (X₃) — institutional position, observed

**NAMING:** Cultural (Chinese names maintained, no translation)
- 梅香 (Mei Xiang) — "Plum Fragrance"
- 秋月 (Qiu Yue) — "Autumn Moon"
- 王道明 (Wang Daoming) — "Wang, Illuminating the Way"

Names carry cultural weight but are not explained in text. Reader encounters them as names, not symbols.

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

Code-switching occurs in three contexts:

1. **Bureau interactions:** Mandarin administrative register
   - Signals: Official documents, exam instructions, inspection reports
   - Effect: Creates distance, formality, compliance performance

2. **Workshop floor:** Shu dialect informal register
   - Signals: Practical instruction, complaint, embodied knowledge transmission
   - Effect: Creates intimacy, materiality, resistance to Bureau categories

3. **Teaching moments:** Code-switch mid-sentence (Shu → Mandarin → Shu)
   - Signals: Qiu Yue teaching Mei Xiang, then Bureau interruption
   - Effect: Shows how Bureau language colonizes embodied knowledge space

**Example structure:**
> Qiu Yue's fingers traced the warp. "这个线性," she said — *this thread's temperament* — "你要感觉它." *You have to feel it.* The foreman's voice cut through: "秋月,你的侄女需要回去工作." *Qiu Yue, your niece needs to return to work.* Mandarin. Official. The lesson ended.

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (from constraint dynamics, not imposed)
- Mei Xiang: Exhaustion, bodily betrayal, dutiful compliance
- Qiu Yue: Grief for knowledge that cannot be spoken in Bureau language
- Wang Daoming: Satisfaction in measurable efficiency, genuine belief in system improvement

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Seeded Possibility (attractor reached)
- Surface: Mei Xiang's shoulder injury worsens, she is moved to auxiliary work (lower pay), system persists
- Underground: Qiu Yue has transmitted recognition to Mei Xiang in stolen teaching moments, but Mei Xiang cannot act on it within Bureau system
- Frame: Witness notes that Mei Xiang's daughter (next generation) became a Bureau examiner, leaving unresolved whether this represents capture or infiltration
- Omega: "The records do not show whether she carried her mother's knowledge into the Ministry, or whether the Ministry's language finally erased what her grandmother's hands had known."

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

**Mei Xiang (X₁ — Condemned, primary POV):**

**Phenomenological register:** Hypervigilance to bodily signals + forced numbness to pain

**Mode of attention:**
- Tracks foreman's position in workshop (knows when he's watching, when he's not)
- Monitors right shoulder sensation (dull ache → sharp pain → numbness)
- Counts bolts completed (internal tally, never spoken aloud)
- Listens to shuttle rhythm (her own + other weavers, knows who's falling behind)
- Notices dust accumulation in throat (swallows, doesn't cough during inspection)

**What the body has learned that the mind hasn't named:**
- Micro-adjustments to thread tension that prevent immediate breakage but accumulate damage
- The specific angle of shoulder rotation that delays pain onset by two hours
- Which humidity levels require which pace reductions (but quota doesn't allow reduction)
- The difference between "tired" and "injured" (has stopped distinguishing)

**Physical world details that reveal position without explaining:**
- Callus patterns on palms (different from Qiu Yue's, who works slower)
- Right shoulder sits slightly lower than left (visible in mirror, not mentioned)
- Breathing shallows during inspection (holds breath when inspector approaches)
- Eats faster than other weavers (less break time = more weaving time)

**ANTI-PATTERN CHECK:**
- ✗ NOT "shoulder pain increases from 3/10 to 7/10" (metric)
- ✓ "The ache that used to fade overnight now greets her at dawn" (experience)
- ✗ NOT "efficiency drops 15% in humid weather" (metric)
- ✓ "Her fingers slip on damp thread; the shuttle stutters" (sensation)

**Qiu Yue (X₂ — Analytical, observed by Witness):**

**Phenomenological register:** Attunement to material variation + grief for untransmissible knowledge

**Mode of attention:**
- Reads thread "temperament" by touch (knows which threads will behave how under tension)
- Adjusts pace to humidity, thread grade, her own fatigue (no fixed rhythm)
- Observes workshop weavers' bodies (sees injury patterns they don't notice yet)
- Listens for the sound that means "slow down" (thread whisper before break)

**What the body has learned:**
- Fifty-one years of weaving, hands still work (this is the proof the Bureau cannot measure)
- Which deviations from standard technique prevent long-term damage
- How to blow dust from cloth without inhaling (lung capacity intact)
- The future of the cloth (knows which "defects" prevent worse problems later)

**Physical world details:**
- Works in variable light (adjusts position as sun moves, no fixed station)
- Keeps multiple thread grades (switches based on feel, not specification)
- Cloth she produces lasts decades (buyers return, this is her certification)

**Wang Daoming (X₃ — Institutional, observed by Witness):**

**Phenomenological register:** Satisfaction in numerical consistency + genuine belief in improvement

**Mode of attention:**
- Reviews defect-rate trends (sees eleven percent improvement, this is success)
- Compares completion times across workshops (identifies efficiency outliers)
- Reads inspection reports (consistent application of standards = quality)
- Never touches a loom (hands know ink, paper, abacus — not thread)

**What the body has learned:**
- How to sit at a desk for eight hours without pain (this is his embodied knowledge)
- Which calligraphy pressure produces clearest reports
- The rhythm of administrative work (seasonal exam cycles, annual reports)

**Physical world details:**
- Ink-stained fingers (his calluses are from brush, not shuttle)
- Official robes (body marked by institutional position, not labor)
- Travels by sedan chair (does not walk workshop floors, observes from office)

## 6. NUMERIC REGISTER (GAUGE-INDEXED)

**Diegetic quantities (exist as objects in this world):**
1. Exam fee (三个月工钱, "three months' wages") — exists on Bureau fee schedule
2. Bolt count (匹, pǐ) — counted by foreman, recorded in workshop ledger
3. Defect count (per bolt) — counted by inspector, recorded in inspection report
4. Completion time (hours per bolt) — measured by Bureau, recorded in complexity tables

**Positional access:**

*Mei Xiang (X₁):*
- CAN read: Her own bolt count (foreman tells her daily total)
- CANNOT read: Workshop ledger, her own defect rate over time, completion time tables, exam pass rates
- Acts on: Bolt count (knows she needs eight per day to maintain income)

*Qiu Yue (X₂):*
- CAN read: Market prices (knows what temples pay vs. what Bureau pays)
- CANNOT read: Bureau complexity tables, defect-rate trends, certification statistics
- Acts on: None (sells outside Bureau system, no access to its instruments)

*Wang Daoming (X₃):*
- CAN read: All Bureau records (defect rates, completion times, exam pass rates, revenue)
- CANNOT read: Weaver's bodily experience (no instrument for this)
- Acts on: Complexity tables (updates based on measured completion times), exam standards (adjusts based on pass-rate trends)

**Narration rule:**

Numbers appear ONLY when a character with access acts on them in-scene:
- ✓ Foreman tells Mei Xiang: "八匹,全部合格" (Eight bolts, all compliant)
- ✓ Wang Daoming reviews report: "Defect rate decreased eleven percent"
- ✓ Mei Xiang borrows exam fee: "三个月的工钱" (three months' wages)

Numbers NEVER appear as:
- ✗ Ambient texture: "She had woven 847 bolts in her two years at the workshop"
- ✗ Countdown: "Only 200 more bolts until she could afford..."
- ✗ Emotional beat: "The pain was now 7/10, up from yesterday's 5/10"
- ✗ Narrator tally: "This was her fourth failed inspection this month"

**Test application:**
For each number in draft, ask:
1. WHO read it? (Mei Xiang / Qiu Yue / Wang Daoming / Foreman / Inspector)
2. On WHAT instrument? (Ledger / Report / Fee schedule / Verbal count)
3. Doing WHAT with it? (Checking quota / Updating table / Borrowing money / Recording defect)

If answer is "narrator providing context," DELETE THE NUMBER.

## 7. STRUCTURAL PHYSICS BEATS

**Act 1: Constraint Activation (Weeks 1-8 of Mei Xiang's employment)**

*False Mountain reveal: C₁ (Certification Requirement)*
- **Setup:** Mei Xiang experiences certification as "how you get work" (naturalized)
- **Evidence:** Qiu Yue mentions she never certified, still weaves, sells to temples
- **Reveal:** Foreman explains procurement system refuses non-Bureau cloth — certification is market partition, not skill verification
- **Mei Xiang's response:** Confusion (why would temples accept uncertified cloth if certification proves skill?), then dismissal (temples are small market, "real work" is Bureau contracts)
- **Reader sees:** C₁ is constructed gate, not natural law. Mei Xiang cannot see this.

*Purity drift: C₂ (Quota System)*
- **Surface stability:** Mei Xiang meets quota, earns steady wage, sends money home
- **Subtle degradation:** Right shoulder ache appears (week 3), worsens (week 5), becomes constant (week 8)
- **Crisis:** Shoulder locks during weaving (week 8), she forces through it, foreman notices she's slowing
- **Mei Xiang's response:** Assumes she needs to "build strength," tries to work faster to compensate
- **Reader sees:** Quota pace is unsustainable. Mei Xiang experiences this as personal weakness, not systemic extraction.

**Act 2: Constraint Coupling (Months 3-12)**

*Network contamination: C₃ (Inspection Protocol)*
- **Healthy system:** Mei Xiang uses technique Qiu Yue taught her (tension adjustment for humid weather)
- **Neighbor pressure:** Inspector marks bolt as non-conforming thread density
- **Internalization:** Mei Xiang re-weaves to Bureau standard, stops using Qiu Yue's technique
- **Consequence:** Next humid-weather bolt passes inspection but puckers when worn (Mei Xiang doesn't see this — bolt is shipped before defect appears)
- **Reader sees:** Inspection protocol colonizes embodied knowledge. Mei Xiang experiences this as "learning to weave correctly."

*Indexical variance collision: C₂ (Quota) across positions*
- **Mei Xiang's experience:** Shoulder pain forces her to slow down, income drops, she skips meals to maintain remittances home
- **Wang Daoming's experience:** Reviews completion-time data, notes Dongcheng Workshop efficiency is "within normal variance," updates complexity tables to reflect measured averages
- **Collision:** Same constraint, opposite experiences. Neither character sees the other's position.
- **Reader sees:** Wang Daoming's "normal variance" is Mei Xiang's bodily breakdown. The metrics are accurate AND harmful.

**Act 3: Attractor Approach (Months 13-24, plus frame epilogue)**

*Surface trajectory: Deterministic Tragedy*
- **Shoulder injury worsens:** Mei Xiang can no longer maintain quota pace
- **Foreman's response:** Moves her to auxiliary work (thread preparation, lower pay)
- **Mei Xiang's response:** Shame (believes she failed), determination to "recover" and return to weaving
- **Reader sees:** The system has broken her body. She experiences this as personal failure.

*Underground transformation: Analytical recognition*
- **Teaching moment:** Qiu Yue shows Mei Xiang the "defective" selvage technique (prevents edge-curling)
- **Mei Xiang's response:** "But the inspector will reject it"
- **Qiu Yue's response:** "The inspector measures the present. I weave for the future."
- **Transmission:** Mei Xiang learns the technique but cannot use it in Bureau work
- **Reader sees:** Recognition transmitted but not actualized. Seed planted.

*Frame epilogue: Generational omega*
- **Witness notes:** Mei Xiang's daughter (born 1825) became Bureau examiner (1847)
- **Unresolved question:** "The records do not show whether she carried her mother's knowledge into the Ministry, or whether the Ministry's language finally erased what her grandmother's hands had known."
- **Reader sees:** Seeded Possibility structure complete. Transformation potential exists. Outcome indeterminate.

## 8. CHARACTER ARCS

**Mei Xiang (X₁):**
- **Start:** Certified weaver, meets quota, believes system is fair (passed exam = proved skill)
- **Middle:** Body breaks under quota pace, uses Qiu Yue's techniques, inspection rejects them, internalizes Bureau standards
- **End:** Moved to auxiliary work, believes she failed, carries Qiu Yue's knowledge but cannot use it
- **Arc type:** Tragedy of rational compliance (every rational choice accelerates breakdown)

**Qiu Yue (X₂):**
- **Start:** Independent master, sees Bureau system's blindness, teaches Mei Xiang in stolen hours
- **Middle:** Watches Mei Xiang internalize Bureau standards, recognizes transmission is failing
- **End:** Continues teaching, knowing the knowledge may die with her generation
- **Arc type:** Cassandra (sees clearly, cannot prevent tragedy, transmits anyway)

**Wang Daoming (X₃):**
- **Start:** Bureau examiner, believes system produces superior cloth at lower cost
- **Middle:** Reviews efficiency data, sees eleven percent defect-rate improvement, updates standards
- **End:** Writes annual report celebrating system success, genuinely satisfied
- **Arc type:** Stable institutional rationality (no arc — this is the point. The system works from his index.)

## 9. ERROR MANIFESTATIONS

**E₁: Type III — Snare-as-Rope (Mei Xiang + C₂)**

*Scene: Month 6, workshop floor*
- **Observable:** Mei Xiang explains quota system to new weaver: "The faster you work, the more you earn. It's fair — everyone has the same tables."
- **Actual type:** Snare (χ=0.96, forced choice between income and bodily integrity)
- **Perceived type:** Rope (fair payment for measurable output)
- **Physical consequence:** New weaver adopts Mei Xiang's pace, develops wrist pain within weeks
- **Reader sees:** Mei Xiang is teaching the new weaver to break her own body, believing she's teaching "how to succeed"

**E₂: Type I — False Mountain (Mei Xiang + C₁)**

*Scene: Month 10, conversation with Qiu Yue*
- **Observable:** Mei Xiang: "But you have to be certified to work. That's just how it is now."
- **Actual type:** Snare (χ=1.08, constructed market partition)
- **Perceived type:** Mountain (unchangeable terrain)
- **Physical consequence:** Mei Xiang does not consider alternative systems, does not question why temples accept uncertified cloth
- **Reader sees:** Certification requirement is policy choice, not natural law. Mei Xiang cannot see this.

**E₃: Type V.a — Tangled-as-Rope (Wang Daoming + C₃)**

*Scene: Month 18, annual report writing*
- **Observable:** Wang Daoming writes: "The inspection protocol has reduced defect rates by eleven percent while maintaining production efficiency. The system successfully balances quality control with operational necessity."
- **Actual type:** Tangled Rope (χ=0.60 for X₁, hybrid coordination-extraction)
- **Perceived type:** Rope (pure coordination mechanism)
- **Physical consequence:** Protocol optimization increases re-weaving burden on weavers (extraction component), but this is invisible in defect-rate metrics
- **Reader sees:** Wang Daoming's metrics are accurate. They measure the wrong thing.

**E₄: Type III — Snare-as-Rope (Qiu Yue + C₂, analytical normalization failure)**

*Scene: Month 14, teaching moment*
- **Observable:** Qiu Yue explains to Mei Xiang: "The complexity tables measure time, not cost. A bolt that takes four hours and breaks your body is not the same as a bolt that takes six hours and leaves you whole."
- **Correction trigger:** Qiu Yue HAS analytical recognition (π=1.15), but cannot transmit it in language Mei Xiang can act on within Bureau system
- **Physical consequence:** Mei Xiang understands intellectually but cannot apply (quota requires four-hour pace)
- **Reader sees:** Recognition transmitted but structurally unactualizable. This is the seed.
