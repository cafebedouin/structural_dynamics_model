REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The story's dramatic center—Mei Ling in her office, holding cloth that demonstrates knowledge her tables cannot speak—earns its weight through the accumulated texture of what that knowledge is. The Shu dialect vocabulary (线性, 布性, 手感) appears in teaching scenes where Qiu Yue searches for Mandarin equivalents and cannot find them, establishing the pattern the ending depends on: not that knowledge is mysterious, but that the dominant language structurally cannot accommodate it. The pacing expansion in Part Three (humidity sequence, Wang Daoming's confusion, Master Feng's alternative epistemology) gives the epistemological tragedy room to unfold as lived experience rather than thesis.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The archival voice in Part Five occasionally tips from describing historical patterns to explaining what those patterns mean. "What it cannot describe, it cannot see" reads as narrator-thesis rather than emergent from the material. The Mei Ling scene itself holds—she inhabits the choice without resolving it—but the surrounding archival commentary risks pre-digesting what the scene already demonstrates.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story maintains that 线性 (thread temperament), 布性 (cloth nature), and 手感 (hand feeling) are not properties the Bureau's tools measure incorrectly—they are relationships that cannot be translated into the Bureau's language at all. Qiu Yue's selvage technique prevents curling over years, but Inspector Huang's glass tool can only measure present-state thread density. The gap is not measurement error but epistemological incommensurability. No passage suggests a better instrument could recover the "true value" of what experienced hands know.

BREAK:
The story violates the expectation that the Bureau's harm stems from corruption or bad data. Wang Daoming's complexity tables are actuarially sound—they do reduce defect rates and increase efficiency. The puckering problem appears despite correct application of standards, and his investigation concludes (reasonably, from his position) that the anomaly is measurement noise. The harm comes from the logical operation of accurate metrics that cannot see bodily cost or time-dependent cloth behavior. This meets the contract's target_prior: the system's perfect operation is the instrument of damage.

READINESS:
The prose is alive and the structure holds. The archival voice occasionally over-explains, but the dramatic center (Mei Ling's scene) and the teaching sequences (Qiu Yue demonstrating untranslatability) earn the story's epistemological stakes through texture rather than assertion. One more pass could tighten the Part Five narrator commentary, but the core is sound.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
