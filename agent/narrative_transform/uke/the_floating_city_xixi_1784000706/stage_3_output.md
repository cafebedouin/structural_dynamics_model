**Selected: ☑ Seeded Possibility**

This is the Seeded Possibility signature by definition: the story ends with the seed planted (ledger exists, Thessaly doesn't know, Sennet is watching), not with the seed germinated or confirmed dead.

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary structural decision: Chorus Voice, three archetypes, fixed assignment per character — not a Shifting Voice.** Each character's blindness is structural to their index, not developmental; shifting would imply a single consciousness moving between states, which none of these three do within the story's span.

### Odrun Vask → **The Condemned**
- χ=0.72→0.84 across C₁→C₃, PIR-adjacent experience despite the system being globally BIR (his *personal* rationality is bounded-to-zero: he has no model of the system as constructed, only as weather)
- Identity-locked exit at C₁, trapped exit at C₃ — textbook Condemned requirement (Mountain-dominated *from his index*, powerless-moderate π)
- Voice: immediate, agricultural, non-reflective. Cannot narrate "the toll is compounding interest on a 200-year-old triage decision." Can only narrate the chalk-mark, the weight of the cart, the arithmetic of what's left after.
- **Justification against Master Diagnostic:** the reader must see what Odrun cannot — that "the ground gives what the ground gives" is a euphemism for a specific, traceable, human-made schedule. The gap is total. Tone: tragic, somber, matches χ=0.84 terminal Snare.

### Marasz Ilendrë → **The Builder** (not Witness)
- χ=0.55–0.83, BIR-consistent, tests leverage (the illegal ledger, the unofficial sums she does for other silt-hands) without possessing the power position to act on what she sees
- **Rejected Witness** despite her documentary/analytical capacity, because Witness requires no direct stake — Marasz has maximal stake (she is a silt-hand under the same toll) and her analytical clarity does not exempt her from χ. Witness-voice would falsely imply safety-through-observation that her index does not grant.
- Voice: tactical, exhausted, precise about numbers she cannot act on. This is the "loneliness of being right" inhabitation sentence made structural.
- **Attractor fit:** Builder is compatible with Seeded Possibility (⚠ in the compatibility table — flagged risk, not blocked) *because* her leverage-testing does not resolve into transformation within the story. She tests, she does not succeed. This is exactly the ⚠ risk the framework predicts and exactly what the Omega log requires (nothing resolves).

### Thessaly Marrow → **The Beneficiary**
- χ=0.29→−0.11, false CI_rope signature exactly as specified (Naturalized masking extraction, then genuine Rope/net-beneficiary at C₃)
- Powerful/institutional index, constraint reads as *coordination* and *fairness* from her position because it structurally is coordination and fairness from her position
- **This is the load-bearing voice choice for the whole blueprint.** Per the Stage 3 directive: Thessaly must be genuinely likeable. She labors at the locks. She believes in the Cut. Her mother's concealment of the ledger is an act of protective love, not conspiracy — which means Thessaly's blindness is not stupidity or malice, it is structurally complete. The reader must want to be inside her calm.
- **Guardrail check — Beneficiary + Seeded Possibility is ⚠, not ✗.** The risk is that the Beneficiary "can't see what needs rupturing," which would normally threaten Rupture-attractors. Since Seeded Possibility does not require rupture — only an unresolved seed — the Beneficiary's blindness is not a structural blocker; it is the mechanism by which the seed stays unresolved. She must NOT discover the ledger by story's end (per Ω_C02, unresolved). Her blindness persisting *is* the correct terminal state for this attractor, not a failure of the voice.

**Voice combination strategy: Chorus, verging on Double-Blind.** Odrun and Thessaly are close to true Double-Blind (both structurally blind in opposite directions — his toward fatalism, hers toward fairness) but Marasz breaks the pure double-blind symmetry by seeing clearly and still being powerless to act, which is what makes this Seeded Possibility rather than pure tragedy-of-two-blindnesses.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**☑ Multi-POV Parallel**, with one structural refinement: **the reader sees variance before any character does, and no character ever sees the full variance.**

- Not Gradual Realization — no single character arrives at systemic awareness (Marasz already has partial awareness from page one; Odrun never arrives; Thessaly must not arrive).
- Not Late Revelation — there is no twist reveal withheld till act three; the ledger's existence is known to the reader early (via Marasz) precisely so that dramatic tension comes from *whether Thessaly finds it*, not from *whether the reader learns the system is constructed*.
- Not Analytical Observer — no character is permitted the master-diagnostic vocabulary. Marasz gets closest but her knowledge is bounded to "the numbers were never the river's doing," not "this is a Tangled Rope with asymmetric cost distribution."

The three POVs will be interleaved across three sections/movements, each anchored to one character's spring — same season, same toll-mark event (Odrun's plot going under), observed from three positions that never fully intersect on-page except in one scene (the confession beat, Ω_P02).

## 4. Editorial Decisions

```
LENGTH: Novelette (7500–17500 words) — three-POV chorus with generational
        stakes requires room; flash/short would force summary rather than
        inhabited scene, collapsing the Beneficiary voice especially,
        which depends on accumulated warmth before the reader's own
        recognition does its work.

POV: Third Limited, rotating fixed-assignment per section
     (never omniscient — omniscient would destroy Thessaly's Beneficiary
     blindness and Marasz's Builder partial-vision simultaneously;
     never first — third limited permits the register-switch device
     [ritual/field speech] to sit in free indirect discourse without
     requiring an "I" to perform the switch)

TENSE: Past — ritual register requires the weight of the already-sung;
       present tense would flatten the "two hundred and eleventh spring"
       framing into immediacy the liturgical material doesn't want

CHARACTER COUNT: 3 primary POV (Odrun, Marasz, Thessaly) + 1 non-POV
                 pivotal presence (Sennet — witnessed, not narrating,
                 per Ω_P01 resolution below)

NAMING: Cultural (per Stage 2 — Vask/Ilendrë for silt-hands,
        root-syllable riverbed vocabulary; Marrow for holding-families,
        founding-act commemorative vocabulary). Load-bearing, unexplained.

LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:
  Register-switch (ritual ↔ field speech) occurs within EACH character's
  own sections at moments of stress or intimacy — not as code-switching
  between separate languages but as a drop from formal/liturgical
  cadence (no contractions, invocational syntax) into plain field speech
  (contractions, direct address, exhaustion). The switch signals:
  proximity to the felt/subordinate grain vs. the performed/public floor.
  Thessaly's ritual register is the LEAST switched of the three —
  her Beneficiary position means she rarely needs the private register,
  which is itself a silent marker of her insulation. Odrun switches most
  under duress. Marasz switches deliberately, as a kind of code — she
  drops into field speech specifically when she is about to say
  something true that ritual language cannot hold.

EMOTIONAL CORE: Not "will the Vask plot be saved" (it will not — this
  must be treated as near-certain by mid-story, per Condemned voice
  constraints) but "what does each of these three people do with what
  they know, or don't know, in the season it becomes irreversible."

ENDING STRATEGY: ☑ Seeded Possibility compound — the Vask plot goes
  under the toll (Odrun's terminal Snare state reached, not averted);
  Thessaly inherits the Council seat and the right to acquire it,
  moves toward exercising that right without yet knowing about the
  ledger (seed planted, ungerminated); Marasz's confession beat lands
  and recedes without resolving into action. Sennet — not a POV,
  but the last image — is shown in a position that makes clear the
  toll will reach her, and only her, whether or not anything above
  has changed. The final beat is NOT "system exposed" or "system
  intact" — it is the cellar door, unopened this spring, still there
  next spring.
```

### Resolution of Ω_P01 (Sennet's POV status)

**Decision: Sennet does NOT carry an independent POV thread — witnessed only, through Odrun's third-limited sections.**

Reasoning: A fourth POV would dilute the chorus past the point of clean triangulation and would require Sennet's register to carry *temporal* variance (child experiencing C₁ as unquestioned fact, evolving toward something else within the story's span) — this is a strong idea but belongs to a longer work. Within a novelette, giving Sennet POV risks either (a) compressing her arc into a rushed pseudo-realization that reads as precocious theme-naming from a child character, which is a Blurb Test failure waiting to happen, or (b) diluting Odrun's Condemned voice by requiring the narration to hold two registers of blindness simultaneously in one section. Instead: Sennet's questions land *inside Odrun's sections*, refracted through his inability to answer them honestly, which preserves the subordinate-grain effect (her groundlessness is felt, not diagnosed) while keeping the chorus at three.

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The chalk toll-mark on the granary door.**

This is not a metric-in-disguise (validated against the anti-pattern check: it cannot be expressed as a number that changes over time and still do its narrative work — the *mark itself*, its chalk-dust, its height on the door relative to the marks of prior years scratched permanently beneath it, is the object; the toll figure it represents is read once, aloud, by the soil-reader, and never re-stated as ambient arithmetic).

- **Odrun's register:** the height of the new mark against the old, permanent scratches below it — a physical, vertical, bodily-scaled comparison (higher than his own chest-height last year; higher than his father's marks, which he remembers reaching only to his waist as a boy). He does not do the math. He does the height.
- **Marasz's register:** she is the only one who has actually converted the marks into a sequence and knows what the sequence proves — but this conversion happens off-page, in the cellar, with the ledger; it never appears as narrated arithmetic in her sections either. What appears in her POV is the *chalk dust on her hands* after she helps re-mark a neighboring door, and the specific, small physical fact that the soil-reader's chalk is imported, costly, rationed — a detail that lets her feel the theater of the marking without narrating a critique of it.
- **Thessaly's register:** she sees the same door, the same mark, at the Council's spring accounting — and experiences it as *legible, orderly, correctly kept.* Her positional access to the underlying figures is total (she can request the ledger-book of tolls from the Council archive; nothing stops her) — but she has never once asked to see the sequence going back two hundred years, because there has never been a reason to doubt today's number.

### Validation Pass

- **Voice/attractor compatibility:** Beneficiary + Seeded Possibility (⚠ flagged, not ✗) — resolved by confirming Thessaly's unresolved blindness *is* the seed, not an obstacle to it. Condemned + Seeded Possibility (✓) confirmed via Odrun's non-arrival at realization. Builder + Seeded Possibility (⚠) resolved via Marasz's leverage-without-lever terminal state.
- **Blurb test:** Odrun cannot say "this system was built by seven households two centuries ago" — his voice ceiling forbids it structurally (identity-locked, no historical register beyond liturgy). Marasz *could* say it but is denied the scene in which to say it fully (confession recedes before completion). Thessaly cannot say it because she does not know it. No character holds the thesis whole.
- **Irresolvable tension:** Thessaly's acquisition of the Vask plot is both procedurally fair (by every rule she has ever been taught) and the mechanism of Odrun's family's total dispossession — the reader cannot resolve this into villainy or innocence. Marked as the load-bearing irresolvable tension; Stage 4 must write toward it directly in the Act 2 accounting scene, not soften it with authorial signaling.
