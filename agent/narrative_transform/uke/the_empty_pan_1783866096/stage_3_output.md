**Selected Attractor:** Seeded Possibility

**Constraint Profile:**
- C₁ (Mountain, certified): Natural law, cannot be changed
- C₂ (False Mountain): Appears unchangeable from biographical horizon, actually constructed
- C₃ (High variance): Snare/Tangled Rope/Rope depending on index

**Rationality Model:** BIR (Bounded Institutional Rationality)

**Compatibility Assessment:**
✓ **COMPATIBLE** — Seeded Possibility is structurally supported:
- Mountain-dominated foundation blocks Revolutionary Rupture
- BIR creates satisficing space (prevents Deterministic Tragedy)

- Analytical recognition (TR₃) plants underground transformation seeds

## VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

### Selected Voice: **The Condemned**

**Justification:**

1. **Structural compatibility with attractor:**
   - Seeded Possibility requires surface tragedy (visible outcome: system persists)
   - The Condemned experiences constraint as natural/inevitable
   - Cannot see systemic shape, acts rationally within limited information
   - Voice is immediate, experiential, not reflective
   - ✓ Compatible with Tragedy component of Seeded Possibility
   - ✓ Compatible with underground transformation (seeds planted outside narrator's awareness)

2. **Constraint topology alignment:**
   - Primary constraint C₃ reads as Snare from powerless position (χ=0.96)
   - PIR-adjacent experience (system feels implacable, though it's actually BIR)
   - Mountain-dominated network (C₁, C₂) reinforces inevitability perception
   - Voice naturally inhabits the trap without analyzing it

3. **Power range requirement:**
   - Requires powerless/moderate position: ✓ (Āzhēn is powerless, π=1.5)
   - Requires π ≥ 1.0: ✓ (π=1.5 well above threshold)

4. **Rationality model fit:**
   - The Condemned acts rationally given limited information: ✓
   - Experiences constraint as natural, not constructed: ✓
   - BIR context allows for rational action within trap: ✓

5. **Tonal alignment:**
   - Tragic, somber tone matches Seeded Possibility's surface outcome
   - Immediate, experiential voice prevents premature revelation of underground seeds
   - Reader sees systemic shape that narrator cannot

**POV Selection:** First person
- Strongest inhabitation of the trap
- Prevents analytical distance
- Reader performs the gap between experience and structure
- Natural for biographical time horizon

**Alternative Considered and Rejected:**
- **The Witness:** Would see too much, collapse dramatic irony
- **The Builder:** Would test for leverage, incompatible with Snare experience
- **The Analytical Observer (Wèi Shūzhēn as narrator):** Would explain the structure, violating invisibility requirement

## INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

### Selected Strategy: **Gradual Realization (Partial)**

**Structure:**
- Single POV (Āzhēn, The Condemned)
- Slow accumulation of evidence that others experience differently
- No full analytical breakthrough (maintains Condemned archetype)
- Reader sees indexical variance before narrator
- Final act: recognition of *difference* without understanding *structure*

**Revelation Sequence:**

**Act 1 (Constraint Activation):**
- Āzhēn experiences C₃ as pure trap (Snare)
- No awareness that others might experience differently
- C₂ (standard) appears as natural fact
- C₁ (*zhēnwèi*) experienced as loss, not as physics

**Act 2 (Evidence Accumulation):**
- Encounter with Lín: he tastes the same tea, finds it genuinely lacking
- Encounter with Morrison: he sees the grades, signs permits, system runs smoothly
- Encounter with Wèi Shūzhēn: she tries to explain, Āzhēn cannot hear it
- Physical evidence: tribute-garden growers are not struggling (different reality)

**Act 3 (Partial Recognition):**
- Āzhēn recognizes: "They taste different tea than we grow"
- Does NOT recognize: the standard is a choice, the system is changeable
- Remains in Snare, but now aware the trap has different depths for different people
- Underground seed planted: the *difference* itself, not the explanation

**What the reader sees that Āzhēn doesn't:**
- C₂ is a founding choice (False Mountain)
- C₃ is indexically variant (Snare/Tangled/Rope)
- Lín is trapped by identity-lock (moderate position)
- Morrison is net beneficiary (institutional position)
- The system is BIR (satisficing), not PIR (implacable)

**What Āzhēn sees:**
- Her tea grades poorly every year
- Lín's tongue is trained to something else
- Morrison doesn't taste tea at all
- The system takes and takes
- Some people don't struggle the way she does

**The gap is the story.**

## EDITORIAL DECISIONS

### Length
**Selected:** Short story (4,000-5,000 words)

**Rationale:**
- Three-act structure with single POV
- Four key scenes (certification hall, encounter with Lín, encounter with Morrison, final recognition)
- Constraint network is dense but contained
- Seeded Possibility requires restraint (no resolution, only preparation)

### POV
**Selected:** First person, present tense

**Rationale:**
- The Condemned voice requires immediacy
- Present tense prevents retrospective analysis
- First person maximizes inhabitation of the trap
- Reader performs the analytical work the narrator cannot

### Character Count
**Selected:** 4 primary characters

**Core:**
1. **Āzhēn (X₁):** Narrator, The Condemned, powerless position
2. **Lín Yùnzhāng (X₃):** Head taster, moderate position, identity-locked
3. **Morrison (X₄):** Trade inspector, institutional position, beneficiary
4. **Wèi Shūzhēn (X₂):** Analytical position, seed-carrier (brief appearance)

**Background:**
- Āzhēn's brother (unnamed, Hakka dialogue partner)
- Tribute-garden growers (collective, visible in contrast)
- Valley growers (collective X₅, background presence)

### Naming Strategy
**Selected:** Cultural (Chinese names with etymological weight)

**Rationale:**
- Setting is 1840s Fujian, cultural specificity is structural
- Names carry power markers (Lín-dàrén vs. Āzhēn)
- Hakka/Mandarin code-switching requires authentic names
- Names are not explained, they are inhabited

**Name Meanings (for writer's reference, not exposition):**
- 阿珍 (Āzhēn): "Precious" (diminutive, familiar, marks her as young/powerless)
- 林云章 (Lín Yùnzhāng): "Cloud Pattern" (scholarly, elevated, marks his position)
- 魏淑珍 (Wèi Shūzhēn): "Virtuous and Precious" (tribute-garden family name)

### Linguistic Implementation

**Code-Switching Triggers:**
1. **Hakka → Mandarin:** When Āzhēn enters institutional space (certification hall)
2. **Mandarin → Hakka:** When Āzhēn returns to private/intimate space (home, fields)
3. **Mandarin → English:** Morrison's scenes (brief, administrative)

**What the Switch Signals:**
- Hakka = intimacy, resistance, what cannot be said in the system's language
- Mandarin = transaction, deference, performance of subordination
- English = distant power, the empire that structures the Port Authority

**Grammatical Features in English Narration:**
- Measure words: "three *lǐ* north," "forty thousand chests"
- Topic-comment: "The tea, it grades poorly"
- Honorifics: "Lín-dàrén" (never just "Lín" in Āzhēn's voice)

### Emotional Core
**Derived from constraint dynamics, not imposed:**

The emotional core is **exhaustion without language for it**. Āzhēn is not angry (anger requires seeing an alternative). She is not despairing (despair requires hope lost). She is *tired* in a way that has no name, because the system has been there her entire conscious life. The trap is the air. The weight is normal.

The emotional shift in Act 3 is not hope — it is **bewilderment**. She sees that others experience differently, and she has no framework to understand why. The bewilderment is the seed.

### Ending Strategy
**Selected:** System persists, agent recognizes difference without understanding structure

**Final Beat:**
Āzhēn stands in the field at dawn, watching the mist come late (as it always does in the valley). She knows the *zhēnwèi* will arrive in thirty seconds, and she knows the Port Authority's taster will arrive in six hours, and she knows her tea will grade C, and she knows this is how it has always been. But now she also knows: Lín-dàrén tastes something else. Morrison signs something else. The tribute gardens grow something else. The system is not one thing. It is many things, depending on where you stand.

She does not know what to do with this knowledge. She pours the water anyway.

**What this achieves:**
- Surface: Tragedy (system persists, Āzhēn remains trapped)
- Underground: Seed (recognition of indexical variance, even without structural understanding)
- Seeded Possibility mechanics: visible stasis + invisible preparation
- The Condemned archetype maintained (no analytical breakthrough, no exit)

## INHABITATION SPEC

### Āzhēn (The Condemned, Powerless Position)

**Phenomenological Register:**

Āzhēn's attention is **material and immediate**. She does not think in abstractions. She thinks in:
- The weight of the tea chest on her back during the twelve-*lǐ* walk to Fuzhou
- The heat of the clay pot in the first thirty seconds after pouring
- The taste of her family's tea (stony, resinous, deep) vs. the taste of nothing (hunger, when the harvest grades poorly)
- The sound of Lín-dàrén's brush on paper (the scratch that writes "C")

**What the body has learned that the mind hasn't named:**
- Her hands know when the *zhēnwèi* is passing (the heat changes, the steam slows)
- Her back knows the difference between a good year and a bad year (the weight of the chest, the number of trips)
- Her throat knows the difference between tribute tea and valley tea (she has never tasted tribute tea, but she knows it by its absence — the thing Lín-dàrén's tongue expects and does not find)

**What she notices about the physical world:**
- The mist in the valley comes later than in the tribute gardens (she can see their slopes from her family's field)
- The certification hall smells like porcelain and ink (not like tea)
- Lín-dàrén's hands are soft (he does not work the fields)
- Morrison's signature is in a different script (the English letters are angular, incomprehensible)

**Mode of attention:**
- **Hypervigilance to small changes:** A slight shift in Lín's expression when he tastes her family's tea. The pause before he writes the grade. The way Morrison's eyes move over the ledger without stopping.
- **Numbness to large patterns:** The system has always been there. She does not notice it the way she does not notice breathing.
- **Displacement:** The constraint is felt in the wrong place. Her hands ache (from carrying the chest), but the ache is not about her hands — it is about the grade, the price, the year ahead. She does not have language for this displacement.

**Anti-pattern check:**
- ❌ "The system extracts 0.8 of our surplus" — this is a metric, not an experience
- ✓ "We sell for one-third what the tribute gardens sell for, and I do not know why" — this is the experience of extraction without the instrument to measure it

## NUMERIC REGISTER (GAUGE-INDEXED)

### Diegetic Quantities

**Quantities that exist as OBJECTS in this world:**
1. **The grade (A, B, C, D):** Exists as a letter written in Lín's ledger, spoken aloud in the certification hall
2. **The price per *dàn*:** Exists as a number negotiated between grower and buyer, denominated in silver taels
3. **The certification fee (2%):** Exists as a deduction, calculated by Morrison, recorded in the Port Authority ledger

**Quantities that do NOT exist as objects:**
- χ (constraint burden) — no character has access to this calculation
- ε (extraction rate) — no character measures this
- π (power scaling) — no character thinks in these terms
- The "true value" of the tea — this is the invariant, it cannot be captured

### Positional Access

**Āzhēn (powerless position):**
- CAN read: The grade (when Lín says it aloud, when it is written in the ledger she is shown)
- CAN read: The price (when the buyer offers it)
- CANNOT read: The certification fee (she does not see Morrison's ledger)
- CANNOT read: The tribute-garden price (she has never been to their market)

**Lín (moderate position):**
- CAN read: All grades (he assigns them)
- CAN read: The reference standard (he tastes it daily)
- CANNOT read: The prices (he does not participate in sales)
- CANNOT read: The Port Authority's revenue (Morrison's ledger)

**Morrison (institutional position):**
- CAN read: All grades, all prices, all fees (he keeps the master ledger)
- CAN read: The total volume (40,000 chests per year)
- CANNOT read: The *zhēnwèi* (he does not taste tea)

### Narration Rule

**Narration is never denominated in the system's numbers.**

A number may appear only when a character with access ACTS ON IT in-scene:
- ✓ Lín writes "C" in the ledger (Āzhēn sees the letter, the action of writing)
- ✓ The buyer offers "eight taels per *dàn*" (Āzhēn hears the number, the negotiation)
- ❌ "The certification fee extracts 2% of the sale price" — no character performs this calculation in-scene
- ❌ "Her family's tea has graded C for six consecutive years" — this is a tally, not an action

**The body-read alternative:**
When the narration needs to convey accumulation, duration, or pattern, it uses **physical markers** instead of counts:
- ✗ "Six years of C grades"
- ✓ "The ledger's spine is cracked where Lín opens it to her family's page"
- ✗ "The price is one-third of tribute-garden tea"
- ✓ "The buyer counts eight taels into her palm; she has seen him count twenty-four for tribute tea"

## ACT STRUCTURE

### Act 1: Constraint Activation (1,200 words)

**Scene 1: The Certification Hall**
- Āzhēn brings her family's tea samples to Fuzhou
- Lín tastes, assigns grade C
- Physical markers: the weight of the chest, the heat of the tea (already fading), the scratch of the brush
- Code-switch: Hakka (walking to Fuzhou with her brother) → Mandarin (speaking to Lín-dàrén)
- Emotional register: exhaustion, not anger
- What the reader sees: C₃ as Snare (χ=0.96), Lín as moderate position
- What Āzhēn sees: the grade, the inevitable outcome, the year ahead

**Scene 2: The Return**
- Āzhēn walks back to the valley, twelve *lǐ*, empty chest on her back
- Passes the tribute gardens (visible from the road, thriving)
- Code-switch: Mandarin (internal monologue, still performing deference) → Hakka (arriving home, speaking to her brother)
- Physical marker: the mist in the valley comes later, denser
- Emotional register: the trap is the air

### Act 2: Collision from Different Indices (1,800 words)

**Scene 3: Encounter with Lín (The Moderate Position)**
- Āzhēn returns to the certification hall (a question about the grade, a formality)
- Sees Lín tasting tribute-garden tea (his morning ritual)
- Observes: he drinks it with pleasure, with recognition
- Dialogue: Lín explains (without condescension) that valley tea "lacks brightness"
- Physical marker: Lín's hands are soft, his tongue is trained to something else
- What the reader sees: Lín is identity-locked (χ=0.64, Tangled Rope), genuinely cannot taste valley tea as good
- What Āzhēn sees: he tastes different tea than we grow

**Scene 4: Encounter with Morrison (The Institutional Position)**
- Āzhēn sees Morrison in the Port Authority office (brief, transactional)
- He is signing permits, moving papers, the system runs smoothly
- He does not taste tea, does not look at the samples
- Physical marker: his signature is angular, incomprehensible, in a different script
- What the reader sees: Morrison is net beneficiary (χ=-0.144, Rope), the system works *for him*
- What Āzhēn sees: he signs something else, he is not part of the tasting

**Scene 5: Encounter with Wèi Shūzhēn (The Analytical Position)**
- Wèi visits the valley (her husband's family lives there)
- She tries to explain: "The standard is based on tribute-garden tea, your tea is different, not worse"
- Āzhēn cannot hear it — it sounds like philosophy, like something that doesn't help
- Physical marker: Wèi has tasted both teas, Āzhēn has only tasted one
- What the reader sees: TR₃ (analytical recognition) attempted, blocked by Āzhēn's position
- What Āzhēn sees: words that do not change the grade

### Act 3: Attractor Approach (1,000 words)

**Scene 6: The Recognition (Partial)**
- Āzhēn stands in the field at dawn, preparing the first infusion
- She knows the *zhēnwèi* will come in thirty seconds
- She knows the Port Authority's taster will arrive in six hours
- She knows her tea will grade C
- **The shift:** She now knows — Lín tastes something else. Morrison signs something else. The tribute gardens grow something else. The system is not one thing.
- She does not know what to do with this knowledge.
- She pours the water anyway.

**Final Image:**
The steam rises from the clay pot. The *zhēnwèi* is here, now, in this moment. Āzhēn presses her palm to the pot and feels the heat. In thirty seconds, it will begin to fade. In six hours, it will be gone. The Port Authority's instruments will measure something else entirely.

She does not have the words for what she has lost. She only knows: they are tasting different tea.

**What this achieves:**
- Surface: System persists, Āzhēn remains trapped (Seeded Possibility, visible outcome)
- Underground: Seed planted (recognition of indexical variance, preparation for future transformation)
- The Condemned archetype maintained (no analytical breakthrough, no escape)
- Reader sees the structure Āzhēn cannot name

## CHARACTER ARCS

### Āzhēn (X₁, The Condemned)
**Arc:** Exhaustion → Exhaustion + Bewilderment

- **Act 1:** Experiences C₃ as inevitable trap, no awareness of alternatives
- **Act 2:** Accumulates evidence that others experience differently (Lín's pleasure, Morrison's distance, Wèi's explanation)
- **Act 3:** Recognizes *difference* without understanding *structure*
- **Change:** Not hope, not despair — bewilderment. The trap is still there, but now she knows it has different depths for different people.
- **Structural position:** Remains powerless (π=1.5), remains trapped (E=trapped), remains in Snare (χ=0.96)
- **Underground seed:** The recognition itself, wordless, carried forward

### Lín Yùnzhāng (X₃, The Moderate Position)
**Arc:** No arc (stable position)

- Lín does not change. He is identity-locked.
- His tongue knows the tribute-garden standard. He tastes valley tea and finds it genuinely lacking.
- He is not corrupt. He is the system's instrument.
- **Function in narrative:** Shows that the system is not "bad people" — it is structural. Lín is kind, competent, and trapped by his own training.

### Morrison (X₄, The Institutional Position)
**Arc:** No arc (stable position)

- Morrison does not change. He is the beneficiary.
- The system works for him. He has no incentive to question it.
- **Function in narrative:** Shows that the system has coordination value (it prevents fraud, it moves tea efficiently) while also extracting asymmetrically.

### Wèi Shūzhēn (X₂, The Analytical Position)
**Arc:** Attempted transmission, blocked

- Wèi understands the structure. She tries to explain it to Āzhēn.
- Āzhēn cannot hear it (the Condemned position blocks analytical reception).
- **Function in narrative:** Shows that analytical recognition alone is not transformation. Understanding without power is a seed, not a solution.

## ERROR MANIFESTATIONS (Concrete Scenes)

### E₁: Type III (Snare-as-Rope) — Lín's Normalization

**Scene:** Lín explains to Āzhēn why valley tea grades poorly

**Observable:**
- Lín describes the grading system as "necessary coordination mechanism"
- He does not acknowledge extraction (from his index, χ=0.64, it reads as hybrid)
- He focuses on coordination value: "Without the standard, buyers in London would not trust the quality"
- He resists the idea that the standard itself is the problem: "All measurement requires a reference"

**Physical consequence:**
- Āzhēn leaves the conversation with no recourse
- The system is defended by someone who is not corrupt, not cruel, just trained to a different standard
- The error perpetuates because the moderate position cannot see the Snare

### E₂: Type I (False Mountain) — The Standard as Natural Law

**Scene:** Wèi tries to explain that the standard is a choice

**Observable:**
- Āzhēn responds: "But there has to be a standard. How else would they measure tea?"
- She treats the tribute-garden reference as inevitable, not constructed
- She cannot imagine an alternative founding choice (C₂ is outside her biographical horizon)

**Physical consequence:**
- The conversation ends in frustration
- Wèi's analytical understanding cannot translate into action
- The False Mountain blocks perception of reform possibility

### E₃: Type III (Snare-as-Rope) — Morrison's Ledger

**Scene:** Morrison reviews the certification records

**Observable:**
- He sees the grades: 60% A (tribute gardens), 30% B (regional), 10% C (valley)
- He sees the system working: 40,000 chests per year, minimal fraud
- He does not see the extraction (from his index, χ=-0.144, he is net beneficiary)
- He defends the system: "It's not perfect, but it's functional"

**Physical consequence:**
- No reform is proposed
- The institutional position benefits from the status quo
- The error is not ignorance — it is indexical blindness

## STRUCTURAL PHYSICS BEATS

### False Mountain Reveal (C₂)

**Setup (Act 1):**
- The standard appears as objective fact
- Āzhēn does not question it (it has existed her entire conscious life)

**Evidence (Act 2):**
- Wèi mentions: "The standard was chosen in 1838, based on tribute-garden samples"
- Āzhēn does not process this as significant (the date is abstract, outside her horizon)

**Reveal (Act 3):**
- Āzhēn realizes: "They chose tribute tea. They could have chosen something else."
- But she does not realize: "They could *still* choose something else."
- The False Mountain is partially exposed (she sees it was a choice) but not fully (she still treats it as unchangeable)

### Purity Drift Arc (C₃, potential TR₅)

**Not executed in this story (too long), but seeded:**
- Lín's palate is aging (he is 52)
- The tribute-garden reference samples are from 1838 (they are degrading)
- The coordination value of the standard is eroding (buyers in London are starting to complain about inconsistency)
- **Future trajectory:** C₃ will drift from Tangled Rope → Snare as coordination value decays
- **Seed in this story:** Lín mentions (in passing) that the reference samples are "not what they used to be"
