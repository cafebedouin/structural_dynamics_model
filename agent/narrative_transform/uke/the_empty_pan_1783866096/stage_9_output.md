REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The temporal impossibility of *zhēnwèi* operates as genuine physics rather than metaphor — the walk takes days while the perfect moment exists for hours, and this gap is materially irreconcilable, not a logistics problem awaiting solution. The language-switching passage demonstrates conceptual extraction through failed communication: the narrator discovers that Mandarin has vocabulary for harvest time, processing time, steeping time, but no phrase for "the brief window when everything is right," and this absence is institutional design, not linguistic poverty. The ending refuses consolation — the narrator's knowledge changes nothing about walk length, chest weight, grade, brother's schooling, or price, and the story stays in that material register rather than flinching into meaning-making or quiet dignity.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The Wèi Shūzhēn scene risks becoming a permission-granting device — she arrives to tell the narrator "your tea is not inferior" and explain that Lín-dàrén's tongue was trained to tribute tea, which edges toward having another character validate what the narrator already knows from embodied practice. The scene does useful work (it's the only moment where someone who has tasted both teas speaks), but her function as external validator weakens the narrator's authority over their own knowledge.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story contains no recoverable "true value" that better instruments could measure correctly. The *zhēnwèi* is not mismeasured by Lín-dàrén's tongue — it is absent from what he tastes, because it cannot survive the walk to Fuzhou. The temporal impossibility is structural: the certification system requires tea to arrive days after harvest, and the perfect moment exists for hours. No fairer assessor, no better scale, no uncorrupted standard could capture what is already gone. The missing-floor invariant also holds: the story does not frame the injustice as correctable by choosing a more accurate standard. The narrator realizes "the standard was chosen" and that any standard, once institutionalized, becomes the measure against which difference reads as deficiency.

READINESS:
The story is structurally sound and the invariants hold, but the Wèi scene introduces a minor weakness that could be tightened. The prose is alive and the ending refuses consolation. One more pass could strengthen the narrator's authority, but the story is close to publishable.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
