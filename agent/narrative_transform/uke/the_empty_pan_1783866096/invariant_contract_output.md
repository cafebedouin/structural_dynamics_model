## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The Invariant (Commitment + Falsifier):**
There exists a state of perfect equilibrium — the moment when a substance achieves its truest expression, before extraction, before claim — that is real but becomes impossible to capture the instant any interested party attempts to measure, possess, or trade it. This is not a measurement problem to be solved with better instruments. It is the physics of the world: the act of claiming destroys the thing claimed.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a recoverable "true value" that the system's instruments merely measured incorrectly — a number that could be gotten right with a better scale, a fairer assessor, an uncorrupted standard — the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**The Substrate That Holds It:**
The *qì* (氣) — the energetic moment of a tea's perfect steeping, the instant when leaf and water achieve their truest marriage. In the world's own terms: *zhēnwèi* (真味), "true taste," the flavor that exists only in the unrepeatable now, before cooling, before a second infusion, before the leaf is spent. It cannot be captured in a sample, reproduced in a blend, or certified by an inspector's tongue. The moment you try to hold it for sale, for record, for proof — it is already gone.

**The Substrate Inhabitation Sentence:**
You are a tea grower in the valley where the mist comes at dawn, and you know the *zhēnwèi* by the way the steam rises from the cup in the first thirty seconds after pouring, but the Port Authority's certified tasters arrive six hours after harvest and call your tea "Grade C, insufficient body" because the thing they are measuring is no longer the thing you grew.

**The Missing-Floor Invariant (Commitment + Falsifier):**
Beneath the Port Authority's grading system lies a founding choice with no neutral ground under it: the selection of a reference standard — which garden's harvest, which master's palate, which moment in history — that was declared the zero-point against which all other teas would be measured. This choice was made by someone, for a purpose, and then hidden behind the language of objectivity.

**Falsifier:** If the story frames the injustice as a local error that a better, fairer authority could correct by choosing a more accurate standard, the floor invariant was lost. The problem is not that they chose the wrong standard. The problem is that *any* standard, once institutionalized, becomes a weapon.
