## 1. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

### Selected: **The Condemned** (Primary) + **The Witness** (Secondary Frame)

**Primary Voice: Mirae (X₄) — The Condemned**

**Justification:**
- **Structural fit:** Mirae is inside the constraint logic (C₂ as Snare, χ=0.96), cannot see systemic shape, experiences constraint as natural/inevitable despite it being constructed
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Tragedy, ✓ Seeded Possibility — The Condemned's blindness to system structure allows surface tragedy while underground transformation happens around her
- **Power range:** Powerless (π=1.5) ✓ — meets requirement for Condemned archetype
- **Rationality model:** BIR permits this voice — Mirae acts rationally given limited information and structural position
- **Tone:** Tragic, somber — appropriate for the normalized extraction setting
- **POV requirement:** First person or close third — SELECTING close third to maintain intimacy while allowing physical description of Mirae's body that she cannot see herself

**Why not other archetypes:**
- Builder: Requires seeing partial system shape and testing for leverage — Mirae's trapped exit and immediate time horizon prevent this
- Beneficiary: Wrong power position — Mirae experiences extraction, not benefit
- Fool: Wrong tone — the setting's mundane wrongness requires tragic register, not comic naivety
- Zealot: Mirae does not internalize the constraint as righteous — she complies but does not believe

**Secondary Voice: Kiran (X₃) — The Witness**

**Justification:**
- **Structural fit:** Analytical position, observes constraint dynamics without direct stake, can see multiple indices simultaneously
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility — The Witness documents what the Condemned cannot see, creating the knowledge artifact
- **Function:** Provides the analytical register that reveals system structure without theme-naming (observations are clinical, not judgmental)
- **POV requirement:** Third limited — Kiran's sections are observational, documentary, focused on data collection

**Voice Combination Strategy:** Nested Voice

Mirae's close-third narrative is primary (70% of text). Kiran's sections (30%) frame and contextualize without explaining. Kiran never interprets Mirae's experience for the reader — only documents the measurement discrepancies. The reader performs the extraction math by seeing both perspectives.

**Master Diagnostic:**
- Does this voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot? **YES** — Mirae cannot see that C₂ is constructed (E₃: False Mountain). Reader sees this through Kiran's data collection and through the Stage 2 context the reader has but Mirae doesn't.
- Does the blindness come from the archetype itself, not from Stage 4 instruction? **YES** — The Condemned archetype structurally cannot see systemic shape. Mirae's immediate time horizon and trapped exit make the Index appear unchangeable. This is index-appropriate perception, not authorial constraint.

## 2. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: Multi-POV Parallel with Late Synthesis**

**Structure:**
- Mirae's sections show lived experience of C₂ as Snare (χ=0.96) without naming it as extraction
- Kiran's sections show measurement discrepancies and cross-jurisdictional comparisons without interpreting their meaning
- Reader sees indexical variance before characters do
- Final act: Kiran completes research but does not publish widely (Seeded Possibility) — knowledge exists but does not propagate to Mirae

**Revelation Timing:**
- Act 1: Establish Mirae's experience as natural/inevitable (E₃ active)
- Act 2: Introduce Kiran's observations, create reader awareness of constructed nature
- Act 3: Kiran documents findings, Mirae remains trapped — surface tragedy, underground seed

**Why this strategy:**
- Serves Seeded Possibility attractor — transformation happens but doesn't reach the Condemned
- Preserves Mirae's voice integrity — she never gains analytical awareness that would break The Condemned archetype
- Allows reader to see extraction without characters theme-naming it

## 3. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** ☑ Novelette (7,500-17,500 words)

*Justification:* Need space for dual POV, three-act structure, and sufficient scenes to establish both Mirae's embodied experience and Kiran's analytical process. Flash/short would compress to the point where indexical variance becomes schematic rather than felt.

**POV:** ☑ Third Limited (alternating between Mirae and Kiran)

*Justification:* Close third for Mirae maintains intimacy with her bodily experience while allowing physical description. Third limited for Kiran maintains documentary distance appropriate to Witness archetype.

**TENSE:** ☑ Present

*Justification:* Present tense for Mirae emphasizes the immediacy of her trapped position (immediate time horizon). Present tense for Kiran emphasizes the ongoing nature of observation. Past tense would create retrospective distance that undermines both archetypes.

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 3 primary (Mirae, Kiran, Davin), 2 secondary (Mirae's mother, Director Sokol)

*Justification:* 
- Mirae (X₄): The Condemned, primary POV
- Kiran (X₃): The Witness, secondary POV
- Davin (X₂): Moderate position, visible in Mirae's sections, experiences E₁ and E₂
- Mirae's mother: Shows generational persistence of system, provides Creole dialogue
- Director Sokol (X₅): Institutional position, appears briefly in Kiran's sections

**NAMING:** ☑ Cultural (Tibetan/Nepali-influenced given names, no surnames in common use)

*Justification:* Reflects Plateau Creole linguistic base. Given names only (Mirae, Kiran, Davin, Sokol) — surnames exist in official records but are not used in daily life, reflecting the small-community setting.

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**

Code-switching occurs:
- **Mirae ↔ Davin (official interaction):** Mirae attempts Institutional Margravian, slips into Creole syntax under stress
- **Mirae ↔ Mother (private):** Full Plateau Creole (rendered as English with evidential markers and verb-final syntax in emotional moments)
- **Kiran (research notes):** Institutional Margravian only (clinical, technical)
- **Davin (internal):** Margravian with occasional Creole vocabulary (he grew up on the plateau but works for the system)

**Signal:** Switch signals performance vs. authenticity. Margravian = performing for the system. Creole = speaking from the body, the home, the real.

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (emergent from constraint dynamics)

The gap between what the body knows and what the system measures. The quiet violence of being told your stability is a score. The exhaustion of compliance that doesn't work. The small, persistent hope that next year will be different, even when you know it won't.

**ENDING STRATEGY:** ☑ System persists, knowledge artifact created, agent remains trapped

*Justification:* Seeded Possibility attractor. Kiran completes research documenting that the Posture Index measures deviation from a biased template, not fall risk. Research is preserved (published in obscure kinesiological journal, archived in university library) but does not reach Mirae or catalyze reform. Mirae fails her third assessment. Story ends with her preparing for Cliffside relocation, her daughter asking why they have to leave. Surface tragedy. Underground seed.

## 4. INHABITATION SPEC

**Mirae (The Condemned):**

**Phenomenological register:** Hypervigilance to her own body's signals, combined with learned distrust of those signals when they conflict with the Reading. Notices micro-adjustments her body makes (weight shift from heel to ball, ankle correction, hip tilt) but has learned to second-guess them. The platform has taught her that her body lies.

**Physical world attention:** 
- Feels the cold of the platform through her bare feet
- Tracks the Officiant's eyes on the screen, not on her
- Notices the weight of her daughter phantom-pressed into her left hip even when her daughter isn't there
- Counts her breaths during the assessment (a calming technique that doesn't calm)
- Feels the bridge sway under her feet when she crosses accompanied, hyper-aware that she is being watched

**What the body knows that the mind hasn't named:**
Her body knows the difference between instability (the lurch of actual falling) and deviation (standing in a way the platform doesn't recognize). She has never fallen on a bridge. She has crossed thousands of times, carrying loads heavier than herself. But she cannot articulate this distinction to the Officiant, because the system does not have language for it.

**Kiran (The Witness):**

**Phenomenological register:** Analytical attention to discrepancies. Notices what doesn't match the official narrative. Observes without judging, documents without interpreting. Pays attention to patterns across populations, not individual stories.

**Physical world attention:**
- Watches how people stand on the platform (notes asymmetries, compensations, the way bodies adjust)
- Records the numbers but also watches faces (the moment the score appears, the micro-expression of relief or resignation)
- Notices the platform's hum, the slight vibration when weight shifts
- Observes the bridges from a distance (who crosses alone, who waits for accompaniment, the rhythm of traffic)

**What the body knows that the mind hasn't named:**
Kiran's own body passed the assessment easily (PI 7.2) despite having structural asymmetries that should, by the system's logic, indicate instability. This discrepancy is the seed of the research question, but Kiran doesn't experience it as personal — it's data, not injustice.

## 5. NUMERIC REGISTER (GAUGE-INDEXED)

**Diegetic quantities:**
- **Posture Index (PI):** Exists as a number on the assessment screen, on the ID chip record, on the turnstile display. Range 0-10, threshold 6.5.
- **Bridge accident statistics:** Exist in Director Sokol's reports and Kiran's research. Pre-Index: ~25 deaths/year. Post-Index: ~10 deaths/year.

**Positional access:**
- **Mirae (X₄):** Can see her own PI when displayed on the screen during assessment. Cannot access historical scores or aggregate data. Cannot read the platform's raw sensor data.
- **Davin (X₂):** Can see individual PIs during assessment. Can access historical scores for residents he assesses. Cannot access the reference template or calibration data.
- **Kiran (X₃):** Can access aggregate PI distributions (with institutional permission). Can access accident statistics. Can access calibration history through archival research. Cannot access individual scores without consent.
- **Director Sokol (X₅):** Can access all data.

**Narration rule:**

Numbers appear ONLY when a character with access acts on them in-scene:
- Davin reads Mirae's score aloud: "Six point one."
- Mirae sees the number on the screen and her stomach drops
- Kiran writes in research notes: "Mean PI for fieldworkers: 6.2. Mean PI for administrative workers: 7.4."

Numbers NEVER appear as:
- Ambient texture ("Her PI had been dropping for three years")
- Countdown ("Two more fails and she'd be relocated")
- Emotional beat ("The 5.9 felt like a verdict")

**Body-reads instead of tallies:**

When Mirae experiences the constraint tightening, it is felt, not counted:
- NOT: "This was her second fail. One more and she'd lose bridge access."
- YES: "The turnstile had started asking for accompaniment six months ago. She didn't know when it would stop asking and just lock."

- NOT: "Her PI had dropped from 6.4 to 6.1 to 5.9 over three years."
- YES: "Each year the Officiant's frown deepened. Each year the exercises didn't work. Each year the bridge felt farther away."

## 6. ACT STRUCTURE

### ACT 1: Constraint Activation (2,500 words)

**Mirae's thread:**
- Opens with Mirae's annual assessment (present tense, close third)
- Score: PI 6.1 (borderline, third consecutive borderline)
- Davin prescribes exercises, Mirae nods, knows they won't work
- Returns home, her mother asks how it went, Mirae switches to Creole: "Same as last year"
- Scene: Mirae doing the wall-standing exercises at dawn, her daughter watching, asking why mama stands against the wall
- Scene: Mirae crossing the bridge to the medical depot, accompanied by her neighbor, feeling the weight of the asking
- Establishes: Mirae experiences C₂ as inevitable (E₃: False Mountain), does the exercises because she has no other option, cannot see the system as constructed

**Kiran's thread:**
- Kiran arrives at the Margraviate for a one-year research fellowship (kinesiological study of high-altitude populations)
- Undergoes onboarding assessment, scores PI 7.2, is surprised (has scoliosis, expected to fail)
- Asks the Officiant (not Davin, a different one) about the reference template, gets vague answer
- Scene: Kiran observing bridge traffic, noting who crosses alone vs. accompanied
- Establishes: Kiran is outside the system, has analytical tools, begins noticing discrepancies

### ACT 2: Constraint Coupling and Collision (5,000 words)

**Mirae's thread:**
- Mirae's daughter needs to start school (across the bridge, requires daily crossing)
- Mirae's mother (PI 5.4, chronic fail) cannot accompany her
- Mirae must ask her neighbor every morning, feels the accumulating debt of asking
- Scene: Mirae at the field, talking with other fieldworkers, learns that most of them have borderline or failing PIs
- One fieldworker says: "It's because we bend wrong. We need to stand straighter." (C₃: justified compliance internalized)
- Mirae tries to explain that her body is stable, but has no language for it, falls silent
- Scene: Mirae's assessment, year 4, score drops to 5.9 (first fail)
- Davin is troubled, prescribes more intensive exercises, Mirae sees his hesitation but he doesn't name it
- Establishes: C₃ enforces compliance through social pressure, Mirae begins to doubt her own body, Davin experiences E₁ (Snare-as-Rope) but cannot articulate it

**Kiran's thread:**
- Kiran begins systematic data collection: observes assessments, records PI distributions by occupation
- Discovers pattern: fieldworkers, haulers, mothers with young children cluster at borderline/failing
- Requests access to calibration history, learns the reference template was built from soldiers 40 years ago
- Scene: Kiran interviews Davin (professional courtesy, researcher to Officiant), asks about the template
- Davin defends the system: "It works. Accidents are down." But also admits: "Some people never improve, no matter how much they try."
- Kiran asks: "What if the template is measuring the wrong thing?" Davin doesn't answer.
- Scene: Kiran accesses accident data, discovers that fall deaths dropped 60% but bridge maintenance was also increased at the same time (Ω_E01 resolution)
- Establishes: Kiran sees the extraction, begins to understand that the Index is not measuring fall risk, but Davin cannot see it (E₁ active)

**Collision:**
- Mirae and Kiran do not meet directly in Act 2, but their threads intersect thematically
- Reader sees Mirae's lived experience of the constraint and Kiran's analytical deconstruction simultaneously
- Gap between what Mirae feels (my body is stable) and what Kiran discovers (the template is biased) becomes visible to reader

### ACT 3: Attractor Approach (5,000 words)

**Mirae's thread:**
- Year 5 assessment: PI 5.8 (second consecutive fail)
- Turnstile now displays "ACCOMPANIED ACCESS ONLY" when Mirae swipes
- Her daughter asks why they can't cross alone anymore
- Mirae tries to explain in Creole: "The platform says mama doesn't stand right." Daughter asks: "But you don't fall." Mirae has no answer.
- Scene: Mirae receives notice from Social Services — one more fail and she will be relocated to Cliffside
- Her mother says (in Creole): "We'll go together. Lung-ma isn't so bad." (Cliffside as exile, but survivable)
- Scene: Mirae does the exercises every morning, her body unchanged, the score predictable
- Year 6 assessment: PI 5.9 (third fail)
- Davin reads the score, says nothing, hands her the relocation notice
- Mirae asks (in Margravian, then slipping into Creole): "Wǒ zhīdào wǒ wěndìng. Nga thampa shes-tsi." (I know I am stable. I-grounding-know-EXPERIENCED.)
- Davin says (in Margravian): "Píngtái bù huì sāhuǎng." (The platform does not lie.)
- Final scene: Mirae packing, her daughter asking why they have to leave, Mirae saying (in Creole): "Because the platform says so."
- Establishes: Surface tragedy — Mirae is relocated, system persists, E₃ never corrected

**Kiran's thread:**
- Kiran completes research: documents that PI correlates with occupation/body type, not fall risk
- Discovers that bodies shaped by asymmetric labor (fieldwork, hauling, childcare) systematically fail, but have no higher accident rates
- Writes paper: "Posture Index as Socioeconomic Sorting Mechanism: A Critique of Reference Template Bias"
- Scene: Kiran presents findings to Director Sokol (brief, clinical)
- Sokol reviews the data, acknowledges the pattern, but defends the system: "Accidents are down. The Index works."
- Kiran: "It works for accident prevention, but it also extracts mobility from people who aren't at risk."
- Sokol: "That's a cost-benefit question. The Margrave is satisfied with the current system."
- Kiran's paper is published in *Journal of High-Altitude Kinesiological Studies* (obscure, low circulation)
- Archived in the university library, accessible to future researchers
- Final scene: Kiran leaves the Margraviate, research complete, system unchanged
- Establishes: Underground seed — knowledge exists, but does not propagate to Mirae or catalyze immediate reform

**Seeded Possibility resolution:**
- Surface: Mirae relocated, system persists, extraction continues
- Underground: Kiran's research documents the bias, preserved for future use
- No revolutionary rupture, no negotiated equilibrium, no deterministic closure
- The seed is planted, but does not sprout within the story's timeframe

## 8. ERROR MANIFESTATIONS (Concrete Scenes)

**E₁ (Type III: Snare-as-Rope) — Davin missing extraction:**

**Scene (Act 2):** Kiran interviews Davin, asks about the reference template. Davin explains that the Index is a coordination mechanism — it prevents accidents, enables safe bridge access for everyone. Kiran asks: "But what about the people who fail year after year? Are they actually at higher risk?" Davin pauses. "Some people have... structural issues. The exercises don't work for everyone." Kiran: "Do those people have higher accident rates?" Davin: "I don't have that data." Kiran: "I do. They don't." Davin doesn't respond. He believes the Index is fair because it's the same platform for everyone. He cannot see that the template itself is biased.

**E₂ (Type V.a: Tangled-as-Rope) — Davin ignoring extraction in C₃:**

**Scene (Act 2):** Davin administers an assessment to a fieldworker who scores 6.0 (borderline). He prescribes exercises, emphasizes the importance of "doing the work." The fieldworker says: "I do the exercises every day. My back won't straighten." Davin says: "You need to be consistent. Calibration takes time." The fieldworker leaves. Davin believes he is helping — the exercises work for some people, so they must work for everyone who tries hard enough. He does not see that the justification narrative (C₃) functions as extraction for people whose bodies cannot match the template.

**E₃ (Type I: False Mountain) — Mirae treating C₂ as unchangeable:**

**Scene (Act 1):** Mirae's mother asks (in Creole): "Why do they measure standing? What does standing have to do with crossing?" Mirae says: "It's how they know if you're stable. If you might fall." Her mother: "You've never fallen." Mirae: "But the platform knows better than we do." Her mother is silent. Mirae believes the Index measures something real and unchangeable about her body. She does not see that the standard was constructed, that it could be different, that it was built from soldiers' postures forty years ago. The system feels like natural law.

## 9. STRUCTURAL PHYSICS BEATS

**False Mountain reveal:**
- **Setup (Act 1):** Mirae experiences the Index as natural law, does exercises because "the platform knows"
- **Evidence (Act 2):** Kiran discovers calibration history, learns template was built from soldiers
- **Reveal (Act 3):** Kiran documents that the Index measures deviation from a biased template, not fall risk — but reveal does not reach Mirae (Seeded Possibility)

**Purity drift:**
- **Surface stability (Act 1):** System appears to work, accidents are down, most residents pass
- **Subtle degradation (Act 2):** Kiran discovers that the template has drifted through recalibrations that only included passing residents — the reference has become more homogeneous over time
- **Crisis (Act 3):** Mirae's third fail, relocation — but crisis is individual, not systemic (system persists)

**Network contamination:**
- **Healthy system (Act 1):** Mirae believes in the exercises, complies with hope
- **Neighbor pressure (Act 2):** Other fieldworkers internalize the justification narrative (C₃), tell Mirae she needs to "stand straighter"
- **Internalization (Act 3):** Mirae begins to doubt her own body, even though she knows she is stable — the system's judgment has contaminated her self-knowledge
