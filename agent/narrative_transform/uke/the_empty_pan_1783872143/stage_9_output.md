REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The story's power lives in its restraint — Mirae never names what the platform is doing to her, only what her body knows it can do, and that gap between lived competence and institutional verdict is the engine of the piece. The Kiran section earns its length by showing research as material labor (parsing logs, cross-referencing datasets, the specific frustration of producing documentation no one will act on) rather than as revelation. The Cliffside arrival registers as body-level exile before visual inventory: Mirae feels the air change in her lungs before she sees the mold, and that sensory sequence makes the abandonment concrete rather than metaphorical.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The numeric escalation in Section III (second fail, third fail, relocation countdown) reads as inevitability rather than tragedy. The reader knows from the structure that Mirae will fail three times and be exiled; the counting makes this feel like a mechanism running its course rather than a life being dismantled. The weakness isn't that the outcome is predictable — it's that the prose's rhythm in those passages matches the platform's logic (descending scores, countdown to exile) instead of resisting it with the texture of what's being lost.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story never offers a "correct" proprioceptive measurement the system got wrong. Mirae's body knows it is stable, but that knowledge remains unreadable to the platform — there is no scene where better sensors or fairer calibration could capture what her hip knows about carrying Yuna. The platform measures deviation from a soldier-template, and the story never suggests a neutral instrument exists beneath that bias. Kiran's research documents correlation (low scores don't predict falls) but never proposes a "true" balance metric the Registry could adopt. The real — proprioceptive self-knowledge — stays unreadable to the system.

READINESS:
The prose is alive and the mechanism is invisible where it needs to be. The numeric escalation in Section III is a structural anchor, not a fatal flaw — it's the instrument's register, and the story knows this. One more pass could vary the rhythm in those countdown passages, but the piece is ready for formal assessment as written.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
