## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**The invariant (commitment + falsifier):**
There exists a state — the body's own knowledge of itself — that cannot be owned, measured, or made legible to any external instrument without ceasing to be what it is. The instant proprioception becomes a reading, it becomes something else: a number that can be disputed, traded, or used against you. **Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a "true" proprioceptive measurement that the system merely got wrong — a correct balance score the authorities could have read accurately — the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**The substrate that holds it:**
Inner ear equilibrium. The body's sense of where it stands in space. You know when you are falling before any instrument registers tilt. This knowledge is pre-linguistic, pre-numeric, owned by no one because it cannot be extracted from the body that holds it. It exists only in the present continuous of sensing.

**The substrate inhabitation sentence:**
You stand on a platform above the sorting gulf, and your body knows — in the meat of your inner ear, in the weight distributed across the soles of your feet — whether you are stable, but the moment the Equilibrium Registry writes that knowledge into a Posture Index, it becomes a score that decides whether you fall or stand, and the score is never what your body knew.

**The missing-floor invariant + falsifier:**
Beneath the Posture Index there is no neutral measurement. The zero-point — "correct standing posture" — was set forty years ago by the Margraviate's first Equilibrium Officiant, who calibrated the sensors to the standing posture of healthy soldiers in the Margrave's own regiment. Bodies that grew up carrying water on their heads, or bent over rice paddies, or holding children on their hips, register as "deviant" not because they are unstable but because they are measured against a standard that was never theirs. **Falsifier:** If the story frames the Posture Index as a neutral instrument that merely needs better calibration, the floor invariant was lost.
