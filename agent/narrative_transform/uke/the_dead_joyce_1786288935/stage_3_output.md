# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

```
☑ SEEDED POSSIBILITY
```

**Verification against Stage 1:** Confirmed. The constraint profile shows Tangled Ropes dominant (C2, C3 from moderate-power indices) with embedded, unresolved Snares (X2 at C3, X3 at C2) and a background Mountain (C1) that irrupts rather than governs. BIR is correctly justified — enforcement is social/normative (Supp maintained through internalized role performance, not algorithmic or physically implacable), and agents operate under genuine information asymmetry (Anton does not know Klára's χ at C3 until disclosure). Pure Negotiated Equilibrium is disqualified because TR1's chi_recalculation explicitly shows χ unchanged for X2 after disclosure — the underlying structural trap does not renegotiate. Pure Deterministic Tragedy is disqualified because BIR is incompatible with pure Tragedy per the Matrix. The unresolved Ω_P05 (TR2/TR3 ambiguity) is the formal seed. **Attractor confirmed compatible with BIR.**

This also resolves the primary voice risk early: any voice archetype with analytical self-narration would collapse Ω_P05 by definitionally answering it. The voice must be capable of *proximity* to realization without confirming it.

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Klára) — Secondary: The Beneficiary (Anton), in Counterpoint**

**Justification:**

Klára is inside C3's Snare, cannot see its systemic shape (she has no vocabulary for "extraction," only for the specific texture of her days), and experiences the marriage's presumption as simply how things are — not because she's been persuaded of it, but because no alternative frame has ever been offered to her. Her interiority is not analytical; it is sensory and disciplined. This is textbook Condemned: PIR-adjacent experience (though the network runs BIR overall) within her own index, powerless (π=1.5), Mountain-dominated in her personal register even though the *network* is Tangled-Rope-dominant — she experiences C3 the way one experiences weather. The kept weight itself demands this voice: Section 0's falsifier explicitly forbids a "correct account" of her grief's value, which rules out any voice with analytical or comparative registers (Builder, Witness, Negotiator all risk assigning a value to what must stay unpriced).

Anton is the Beneficiary. From his index, χ(C3) = −0.15: net beneficiary, and the marriage reads as coordination, even as generosity. He genuinely cannot see the extraction — this is not villainy, it is structural blindness, and Stage 4 must make him likeable, reasonable, even tender in his own register. The voice must never wink. His discomfort at the end (Ω_P05) must remain genuinely ambiguous — is he re-indexing (TR2) or aestheticizing (absorbing her grief into a new performance under C2's logic)? A Beneficiary voice, sustained without authorial signaling, is the only structure that can hold this open. If he were rendered as Builder or Negotiator, he would necessarily become aware of the leverage points in his own privilege, collapsing the ambiguity into premature insight.

**Counterpoint, not Chorus:** the two voices observe the same markers (the latch, the pump-room clock, the Colonnade schedule) from opposite ends of the power scale without ever converging. This is the specified Counterpoint strategy from the reference material — two voices at opposite ends of the power scale observing the same physical marker, the contrast doing the work no dialogue can do.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- *Witness* — would see everything, collapsing Ω_P05 and violating the E4 Omega's formal unresolved status.
- *Negotiator* — Klára has no mediating position; there is nothing to negotiate from inside a Snare with no slack.
- *Builder* — implies testing for leverage; Klára's practice (the kept weight) is definitionally not leverage-seeking. It confers nothing on anyone who sees it. A Builder voice would instrumentalize the invariant against Section 0's own falsifier.
- *Zealot* for Anton — considered, given C2's theater component, but rejected: Zealot requires the constraint to be experienced as *moral imperative*, and Anton's relationship to C3 is closer to unexamined comfort than fervor. Beneficiary is the correct register — warmth and confidence, not doctrine.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

```
☑ Multi-POV Parallel (bounded to two, in Counterpoint)
```

Alternating close-third sections (Klára / Anton) across the six weeks of the Season, without authorial commentary bridging them. The reader accumulates the variance the characters cannot access — Klára never sees Anton's interior confidence in his own generosity; Anton never sees the latch. Ludmila appears only within both POVs' peripheral vision (per Ω_P06's recommendation of at most one oblique, non-comparative scene) — she is never a POV in her own right in this piece, which keeps the character count structurally minimal and prevents C2's Snare-index from diluting focus away from C3's maximal divergence, which is the spine of the story.

Dr. Reiner remains entirely peripheral — a structural presence (the schedule, the waiting-room order) rather than an interior one. No POV.

```

  sections without novelette pacing diluting the counterpoint's precision.

POV: Third Limited, alternating (Klára / Anton), no omniscient bridge
TENSE: Past

  scene); Dr. Reiner as structural background only, never interiorized

NAMING: Cultural — full period names retained (Rittmeister Anton Vesely,
  Klára Vesely, Frau Ludmila Nemcová, Dr. Reiner) per Stage 2's register.
  Titles used in public/social sections per the linguistic strategy;
  first names permitted only in the private-register passages (the latch
  scenes) as the grammatical marker of the shift to plain register.

LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:
  Public sections (both POVs, pump-room, promenade, dinner, calling hours):
  formal address, conditional/subjunctive hedging, third-person even in
  close-third interiority ("the Rittmeister felt," not "Anton felt").
  Czech surfaces only in Ludmila's oblique scene, in a service exchange —
  never translated on the page, never in gentry dialogue.

  Private sections (Klára alone with the latch; Anton alone, rare):
  grammar thins to short declaratives. First names permitted. This
  register-shift is the primary formal signal of the kept weight's
  presence — it must never be announced, only enacted through sentence
  shape.

  The switch signals: public register = performance is live; private
  register = performance has stopped, whether or not anyone would
  believe it has.

EMOTIONAL CORE: Not "grief" as theme — the specific ache of a discipline
  that must remain uncommunicated to remain true, set against a marriage
  built on the presumption that nothing in her should remain uncommunicated.
  The core is the friction between two definitions of intimacy that never
  meet: his (disclosure as love) and hers (privacy as the only remaining
  self).

ENDING STRATEGY: ☑ Seeded — neither "Attractor reached" nor "Error
  recognized too late" in the pure sense. Klára's practice ends (the
  season closes, the discipline cannot outlast disclosure per Ω_C03) —
  this is a genuine loss, played straight, not redeemed. Anton's final
  scene is the Ω_P05 seed: written so his silence is legible as either
  a first crack toward TR2 or a fully successful TR3 absorption. The
  reader is left to adjudicate; the text does not.
```

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The latch.** Cold brass, touched once nightly at a fixed hour. This is the substrate object named in Stage 2 and must not be embellished or expanded into a "device" (no engraving, no discovered letter inside, no third-party ever touching it). Its entire narrative function is to be *touched and not explained*. 

**Counterpoint marker, Anton's side:** the pump-room clock / the published schedule of the Season itself — the instrument by which he reads order, standing, and his own competence as host. Where Klára reads the latch by feel (an hour, a temperature, a weight in the hand), Anton reads the clock by its face (a posted time, a public consequence for lateness). Same six weeks, two irreconcilable instruments. Neither character ever touches the other's marker.
