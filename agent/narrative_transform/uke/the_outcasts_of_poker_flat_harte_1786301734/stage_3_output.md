# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. Terminal Attractor Verification

**☑ Deterministic Tragedy** — confirmed from Stage 1.

Verification against constraint logic: C1 (the Culling) is a Snare at the powerless index (χ=1.20) governed by PIR — no negotiation channel, no Pareto-improving alternative offered, suppression ceiling at 0.9. C2 (the Long Water) is a certified Mountain, providing the inescapable backdrop that makes exit impossible even in principle. C3 is a Tangled Rope degrading toward Snare as Thess's arbitrage (TR1) shrinks the pool. This is the textbook Mountain+Snare/PIR cell — no alternative attractor is listed for this combination in the compatibility matrix. Confirmed.

Critical constraint on execution: the unresolved Ω_P04 (boat's fate never shown) and the floor invariant (no recoverable "real" standard) mean the tragedy cannot resolve into *revealed injustice*. The tragedy is that the mechanism stays opaque, not that it's eventually exposed as unfair by some discoverable rule. This rules out any voice or structure that promises the reader an answer key.

## 2. Voice Archetype Selection

**Primary: The Condemned (Mirelle Cade) — with a Counterpoint pairing against Bellard Voss reading as suppressed Builder-shading-toward-System.**

Rejected alternatives first, since the specification's guardrails are unusually tight here:

- **Witness** is disqualified structurally: the floor invariant requires that *no one*, including the narrating consciousness, ever sees the captain's private ledger. A Witness voice with documentary reach would imply the ledger is reachable by sufficiently careful observation. It isn't. The invariant is only safe inside a voice that is *structurally barred* from the ledger, not one that merely chooses not to look.
- **Builder** was tempting for Cade specifically, given her literal reassignment to the tally room — she gains tactical position and partial system-sight. But Builder requires BIR and a Tangled Rope/Snare (not Mountain-anchored) dominant constraint, and more importantly, Builder voice implies leverage-testing that could imply the floor is crackable. Cade must be permitted to *understand the scale perfectly* while remaining totally unable to touch the ledger. That's Condemned wearing partial Builder clothing — not enough system-sight to earn the archetype outright.
- **Zealot** fits Voss almost too well (PIR, high suppression, theater component in the dawn ritual of reading the manifest-weight aloud) but a full Zealot POV risks becoming the story's thesis-carrier — his certainty would need to be inhabited with total sincerity, and a second full POV this early risks diluting the invariant's single hardest edge, which is best delivered through absence (Voss visible only through Cade's eyes) rather than through his own interiority.

**Selected: The Condemned, single-anchored in Cade, with Voss rendered only through close-third external observation — never his own free indirect discourse.**

Justification via the Master Diagnostic:

> *Does this voice allow the reader to see what the character cannot?*

Yes, precisely: the reader watches Cade read the scale's true number every dawn and watches her *almost* reach the conclusion the reader has already reached — that the number's honesty is irrelevant to her survival — without her voice ever being permitted the analytical remove to state it as thesis. Cade is positioned close enough to the mechanism (tally room, daily contact with le poids-vrai) to feel its texture in exhaustive physical detail, and far enough from the ledger to never resolve the floor question. That gap — maximum proximity to the instrument, zero access to the judgment — is the story's entire engine.

This also passes the blurb test cleanly: the single most important thing Cade understands is "the honest number was never what saved anyone." Condemned voice, PIR-governed Snare, immediate time-index — she has no register in which to say this as thesis. She can only *show* it, in the specific grammar of watching Voss read a number aloud and then watching him look somewhere else entirely before he speaks a name. The archetype's ceiling naturally prevents theme-naming; no external instruction is needed.

**Tonal note:** Condemned + Deterministic Tragedy produces the somber, immediate register the archetype table specifies. But Cade's specific position — closer to the mechanism than X1/X2 usually get, per Stage 2's promotion beat — allows a colder, more procedural texture than pure Condemned-in-the-hold would have. Not hope. A kind of clinical intimacy with the instrument that makes the eventual Culling scene land harder because she has spent the whole story trusting the scale's honesty.

## 3. Indexical Revelation Strategy

**☑ Late Revelation, with a buried Counterpoint thread — not Multi-POV.**

Single POV (Cade) throughout. The reader does not get Voss's, Duvray's, Farro's, or Thess's interiority directly. Instead:

- Duvray's structural equivalence to Cade (identical χ=1.20, the captain's flat non-distinction between them) is delivered entirely through *Cade's observation of Duvray* — shared tasks, overheard talk, the specific horror that she was reassigned and he wasn't, for no visible reason. The reader assembles the indexical variance (Snare/Snare/Rope split across X1/X2/X3) from what Cade notices without her ever framing it analytically.
- Farro's Tangled Rope position (the private count against the padded spare reserve) is glimpsed only as a habit Cade notices — Farro's lips moving over dry stores, a look she gives the ration mark — never explained until, if at all, a single late gesture (Farro saying something *has a name too*) recontextualizes what Cade has been watching all along without narrating it as commentary.
- Thess's arbitrage (TR1) stays almost entirely off-page, per Ω_C02's explicit ruling that discovery is not required. At most, one physical trace (a loosened lock, a level of water lower than the tally says it should be) that Cade notices and cannot pursue — because pursuing it is not her job and asking costs something, exactly as Farro has already modeled.

This is Late Revelation in the classic sense — one sustained POV, and the final act (the doldrum-flat Culling, Duvray named) recontextualizes everything the reader has watched Cade do in the tally room. The reader has been quietly assembling the Snare/Rope split the entire time; Cade never does, and the story does not let her.

## 5. Primary Physical Marker

**The manifest-weight scale (le poids-vrai) itself — specifically, the sound and small mechanical give of the plate settling under load.**

This is the substrate object from Stage 2, and it should function as the story's one recurring, escalating physical motif — not a metric (it doesn't need to report numbers changing over time), but a body-level, sensory anchor: the specific click-and-settle of the plate, the way the needle finds its rest point without argument, the coldness of the brass at dawn versus the sun-warmed brass at midday tally. Cade's hands should know this object better than she knows anything else on the ship by the end.

**Counterpoint marker, secondary:** the meal-sack itself (from *aller au sac*) — plain canvas, always the same size regardless of who it's given to, never adjusted for the person going. The scale weighs true and specific; the sack is always identical. That contrast — an instrument of perfect precision sitting twenty feet from an instrument of total indifference — is the story's central visual argument, made without a single line of exposition.
