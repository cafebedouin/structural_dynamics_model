STRATEGY BRIEF

IMPLIED AUTHOR: A precise, unsentimental observer who trusts physical detail over statement — someone who has learned that the exact word for a thing (twenty-two pounds four ounces; a quarter-turn off true) is the only honesty available when moral language has been made useless by the system it would describe. This author refuses interiority for power (Voss, the captain) and refuses false comfort for the powerless (no rescue, no revelation). Flat affect is a discipline, not a limitation — the prose earns its restraint by paying full attention to texture. The ending should stay open: the scale keeps working, the ship keeps going, nothing resolves into organizational change, because that would betray the thesis that information was never the bottleneck.

AUDIENCE: Readers of literary short fiction attuned to institutional/bureaucratic cruelty rendered obliquely — the readership of writers like Kazuo Ishiguro's quieter work, or "The Lottery" if it trusted its reader to sit with the flat voice rather than needing the shock beat underlined. Comfortable with unresolved moral weight, low exposition, and a narrator who knows more than she can act on.

SCOPE: Current: 2,303 words. Target range: **3,200–4,600 words**, well inside the 11,468-word ceiling. This is not a story that wants to become a novella — its power is in compression and repetition (the dawn ritual recurring with variation). The expansion budget should go toward exactly two things: deepening the predecessor-echo (the narrator inheriting a dead woman's post) and giving the reserve-cask thread either a real consequence or a more deliberate, visible dead-end (the narrator choosing not to pursue it, dramatized rather than merely stated). Do not pad description or add new characters/subplots.

CREATIVE DEEPENING:
The over-poured water skin is currently a single gesture of defiance; discovery correctly identifies it as the seed of a genuine invariant — care that exists precisely because no instrument registers it. The strongest available deepening is making this *recur or nearly recur* elsewhere in the story before the ending, so the final gesture reads as a practice glimpsed once, not a one-off. Second: the predecessor (the mate who "went to the sack," "good with a needle, sound as a bell") should be given one more concrete beat — not resolution, just weight — so the narrator's position at the scale is felt as standing in a dead woman's footprints, rhyming forward into Duvray's boat. Third: the reserve-cask thread needs a decision, not a resolution — either it earns one more scene where the narrator consciously chooses inaction (making the "flinch" visible and costly to her) or it should be trimmed so it doesn't read as an abandoned plot promise. Do not resolve it by exposing Thess or explaining the House's motives — that would convert the story's executive-gap thesis into a mystery-plot, falsifying the discovery report's central finding.

FLINCH POINTS:
1. **The reserve-cask thread's disappearance** ("I noticed both of these things... and did nothing further with either noticing"): the draft states the flinch beautifully but doesn't dramatize its cost to the narrator afterward — it's mentioned once and the story moves on as cleanly as she did. Honesty would require the narrator to *feel* this non-action again later, ideally at a moment when it would have mattered (e.g., during the culling or the provisioning of Duvray's boat) — not to act differently, but to notice the noticing hasn't gone away.
2. **The predecessor's fate** ("She had *sûreté* till the shoal. Then she didn't"): delivered once, flatly, and never returned to, which the draft may intend as restraint but which discovery flags as underinhabited. The narrator occupies that exact seat every morning; honesty would require at least one moment where she registers standing in it — not grief, just recognition — rather than letting the line function as pure atmosphere.
3. **The ending's ambiguity about whether the water-skin act repeats**: currently a single gesture with the narrator explicitly disclaiming certainty about its effect. This is largely earned, but risks reading as a "quiet rebellion consolation prize" if left completely isolated — the pattern check below flags this as the place most likely to be a default rather than a considered choice.

PATTERN CHECK: **Concern, not failure.** The story does not resolve through organizational change — good, that's earned. But it resolves through exactly one quiet, private, deniable act of generosity (extra water, extra fish), which is the pipeline's classic fallback posture. The discovery report itself frames this as thin/implicit rather than load-bearing. The craft passes should resist the temptation to *validate* this gesture by making it clearly meaningful or clearly futile — either resolution would be a flinch in a new direction. The correct move is to let the gesture stay exactly as uncertain as the narrator's own accounting of it, while giving it enough recurrence or antecedent (via the predecessor thread or a second small instance) that it reads as a considered practice under constraint rather than a single warm beat inserted to soften an otherwise bleak ending.
