REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The physical details carry real weight without over-explaining themselves — the thumb-worn hollow in the cup, the lock left deliberately a quarter-turn off true, the water skin filled past the ration mark and sealed in silence. The narrator's refusal to narrate her own feelings (no "I felt guilt," just hands doing things) keeps the prose honest and lets the reader do the work of naming what she won't.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The story states its own thesis almost too cleanly in the penultimate section ("the true-weight has never once decided anything... Somebody else decides, afterward, in a room with no scale in it at all, what that weight is worth keeping") — this is the invariant spoken nearly aloud, and while it doesn't collapse the mystery into a correctable number, it edges close to explaining the metaphor rather than trusting the scene (the hand-on-plate, Voss's face) to carry it alone.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. No leaked rulebook or fairer captain ever surfaces; Voss reads the number and then separately, privately, decides Duvray's fate off a standard the text never discloses. The scale stays innocent and unreadable as an oracle of belonging throughout — even the narrator's closing hand-on-plate moment confirms only that the instrument is honest, not that it (or anything) could correctly answer the belonging question.

BREAK:
Named from the text first: I expected the narrator's small private honors — the overpoured water skin, the extra fish, the lock turned back and released again — to matter, to build toward some act of defiance or rescue that pushes back against the system. Instead the story insists, flatly, that none of it goes anywhere: "the noticing that goes nowhere... It hadn't gone anywhere in the weeks since." Duvray still goes out in the boat. This matches the target_prior (the expectation that personal code is sufficient to overcome an indifferent system) — the story raises that hope in the reader via the narrator's small transgressions, then explicitly denies it efficacy while still validating the code's realness (the grain holds; the floor doesn't move). Break and target_prior meet.

READINESS:
Close — the ending lands, the invariant is intact, and the break is executed with precision; the thesis-adjacent sentence in the "true thing on the ship" passage is the one spot a careful pass should re-examine, but it is not disqualifying.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
