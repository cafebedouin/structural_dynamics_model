## SECTION 0: INVARIANT CONTRACT

**Primary Invariant (Untranslatable Real):**
There exists a coherence in how stone holds memory — a rightness in the grain, a truth in the stratification — that any hand trying to claim it for proof destroys in the claiming. The geologists call it "structural integrity under non-invasive observation," but the miners who work the deep shafts know: the rock tells you what it is only while you're not trying to own what it says.

**Falsifier:** If the finished story contains a recoverable "true reading" of the stone that better instruments could measure correctly, the invariant was lost and the naturalization failed.

**Substrate:** The Pre-Cambrian shield formation beneath the Sudbury Basin — 2.4 billion years of metamorphic record, older than atmospheric oxygen. The stone holds a register of planetary formation that exists independent of anyone reading it. The Basin's institutions measure, certify, and monetize readings of the stone, but the stone's truth is not in the readings. It is in the *being-read-while-unclaimed* — a coherence that collapses the instant extraction begins.

**Substrate Inhabitation Sentence:**
You descend into stone that has been holding the planet's formation for two billion years, and the company's sensors read the formation every shift and never once translate what the rock is saying — only what the rock is worth.

**Missing-Floor Invariant:**
Beneath the Basin's certification system lies a founding partition: someone decided, without neutral ground to stand on, what counts as "ore-grade" versus "waste rock" — which readings matter, which strata have value, which formations get mapped. That baseline was set by the first colonial mining surveys in 1883, optimized for nickel extraction, and every certification since treats it as geological fact rather than interested choice.

**Falsifier:** If the story frames the injustice as a local error a better authority could correct — a miscalibrated standard, a corrupt inspector, a fixable bias — the floor invariant was lost.
