# STAGE 3: OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATION

## 1. TERMINAL ATTRACTOR VERIFICATION

**Selected Attractor: SEEDED POSSIBILITY** ✓

**Verification against constraint topology:**

```
C1 (Fabricator's Discipline): ROPE for both Yao and younger fabricators
  → Stable, self-maintaining
  → No transformation pressure
  → Can be transmitted (TR4 shows abandonment, but also preservation)

C2 (Reputation System): SNARE (Yao) / TANGLED ROPE (Parents)
  → High extraction for powerless
  → Transformation possible (TR1: collective organization, TR2: reputation redemption)
  → Surface persistence likely (system benefits moderate-power actors)

C3 (Filial Duty): TANGLED ROPE (Chen) / ROPE (Yao)
  → Irreducible complexity
  → Surgical reform possible (TR3: filial emancipation)
  → Underground transformation viable (Chen's loyalty seeds future change)
```

**Rationality model compatibility:**
- BIR model confirmed ✓
- Negotiation possible but asymmetric (parents have leverage, Yao doesn't)
- Satisficing behavior present (parents choose "good enough" boat, not optimal)
- Principal-agent problems (parents' goals ≠ Chen's goals)
- Uncertainty (reputation system is noisy, not deterministic)

**Why Seeded Possibility fits:**

1. **Surface tragedy is structurally inevitable:**
   - Yao remains trapped in C2 (E=trapped, π=1.5, no exit)
   - Parents' decision is rational from their index (moderate power, mobile, see coordination)
   - Chen lacks economic independence to resist C3
   - Reputation system persists (benefits too many moderate-power actors)

2. **Underground transformation is structurally viable:**
   - Chen's loyalty demonstrates alternative values (discipline over efficiency)
   - Yao's work quality proves reputation system is noisy (TR2 partial activation)
   - Code transmission occurs (C1 passes to next generation)
   - Chen positioned differently than Yao (has analytical distance, sees system as constructed)

3. **BIR enables underground organizing:**
   - Not deterministic (PIR would make tragedy inevitable)
   - Allows negotiation and satisficing
   - Permits long-term positioning without immediate payoff

**Attractor is VERIFIED** ✓

## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

**Selected: THE CONDEMNED (Primary) with WITNESS (Framing)**

**Structure:**
- **Primary voice:** Yao (The Condemned)
- **Framing device:** Brief third-person omniscient sections showing Chen's perspective (The Witness)
- **Ratio:** 80% Condemned (Yao's immediate experience), 20% Witness (Chen observing, contextualizing)

### Justification for The Condemned (Yao)

**Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Tragedy, ✓ Seeded Possibility
- Yao experiences surface tragedy (trapped in C2, isolated, dying)
- His voice cannot see the underground transformation (Chen carrying forward the discipline)
- The gap between what Yao experiences (failure) and what Chen receives (transmission) IS the seeded possibility

**Power range:** π = 1.5 (powerless), E = trapped ✓
- Yao is inside the constraint logic, cannot see systemic shape
- Experiences reputation system as natural/inevitable, not constructed
- Acts rationally given limited information and structural position

**Rationality model:** PIR-dominated (from Yao's position) ✓
- Yao experiences C2 as implacable (though it's actually BIR)
- His trapped position + immediate time horizon makes system appear deterministic
- This is Type I error (False Mountain) — treating changeable as unchangeable
- Error is RATIONAL from his index (no visible alternatives, no successful resistance examples)

**Voice characteristics:**
- Immediate, experiential, not reflective
- Inside the work (welding, checking tolerances, filing reports)
- Notices physical sensations (cold in Bay 12, flickering lights, his own breathing)
- Code-switches: Cant when alone (intimacy with self), English for reports (performance of compliance)
- Does NOT analyze the system — lives inside it

**Tone:** Tragic, somber, with moments of quiet dignity

**POV:** First person (Yao's sections)

### Justification for The Witness (Chen, framing only)

**Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility
- Chen observes constraint dynamics without (yet) having power to change them
- Can see multiple indices simultaneously (Yao's skill vs. reputation, parents' logic vs. Yao's reality)
- Positions reader to understand what Yao cannot see

**Power range:** π = 1.5 (powerless) but E = analytical (observing) ✓
- Chen is subject to C3 (filial duty) but has analytical distance on C2 (reputation system)
- Sees that Yao's "jinxed" label is social construction, not natural fact
- Cannot act yet (economically dependent) but can witness and remember

**Voice characteristics:**
- Documentary, observational
- Reports what happens without judgment
- Code-switches: Cant with Yao (solidarity), English with parents (hierarchy)
- Notices things Yao doesn't (how other fabricators avoid Bay 12, how parents calculate risk)

**Tone:** Neutral, distanced, but with undercurrent of loyalty

**POV:** Third person limited (Chen's sections)

### Why This Combination Works

**Indexical revelation strategy:**
- Yao's voice shows constraint experience from inside (Snare, trapped, immediate)
- Chen's sections show same constraints from analytical distance (sees construction, sees variance)
- Reader discovers indexical variance through juxtaposition, not explanation
- Neither voice states the framework — both embody their indices through action and perception

**Seeded Possibility mechanics:**
- Yao's voice carries surface tragedy (isolation, failure, death)
- Chen's sections show underground transmission (loyalty, observation, positioning)
- The gap between voices IS the seed — what Yao cannot see, Chen carries forward

**Avoids common errors:**
- Not "Hopeful Condemned" (Yao never gains agency his voice can't support)
- Not "Overexplaining Guide" (Chen observes, doesn't lecture)
- Not "Invisible Witness" (Chen's position is structurally justified — he's the apprentice, the inheritor)

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected: NESTED VOICE (Condemned framed by Witness)**

**Structure:**

```
Opening: Chen (Witness) — establishes setting, introduces Yao
  ↓
Section 1: Yao (Condemned) — work in Bay 12, discipline, isolation
  ↓
Interlude: Chen (Witness) — observes Yao, notices what Yao doesn't see
  ↓
Section 2: Yao (Condemned) — module failure, ratings drop, crisis
  ↓
Interlude: Chen (Witness) — parents' decision, transfer order
  ↓
Section 3: Yao (Condemned) — final work, alone, maintaining discipline
  ↓
Closing: Chen (Witness) — aftermath, what was transmitted
```

**Revelation mechanics:**

1. **Yao's voice reveals constraint experience from inside:**
   - C1 (Discipline) as existential identity, not technique
   - C2 (Reputation) as natural fact, not social construction
   - Physical sensations of isolation (cold, silence, exhaustion)
   - Code-switching shows relationship shifts (Cant alone = self-intimacy, English in reports = performance)

2. **Chen's sections reveal indexical variance:**
   - Same module failure Yao experiences as personal failure, Chen sees as material defect
   - Same reputation system Yao accepts as fate, Chen recognizes as constructed
   - Same discipline Yao performs for identity, Chen receives as transmission
   - Parents' logic (risk management) vs. Yao's reality (skill despite ratings)

3. **Reader performs the synthesis:**
   - Discovers Yao is trapped in Type I error (False Mountain — treating C2 as unchangeable)
   - Understands error is rational from Yao's index (powerless, trapped, immediate horizon)
   - Sees that Chen's analytical distance positions him differently
   - Recognizes surface tragedy (Yao dies isolated) + underground transformation (Chen carries discipline forward)

**What is NOT revealed explicitly:**
- Framework terminology (index, power-scaling, Snare, Tangled Rope)
- Meta-commentary on constraint types
- Authorial judgment on who's "right"
- Explanation of indexical variance

**What IS revealed through action:**
- Yao checks welds by hand (discipline as identity)
- Chen counts Yao's breathing (physical marker of care)
- Parents speak English to Chen (hierarchy enforcement)
- Yao speaks Cant alone (intimacy with self, no audience)
- Module fails inspection (not Yao's fault, but ratings drop anyway)
- Chen files transfer request but returns to Bay 12 (underground resistance)

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

### Length
**Selected: NOVELETTE (7,500-10,000 words)**

**Justification:**
- Needs space for nested voice structure (Yao sections + Chen interludes)
- Constraint topology is complex (three constraints, multiple indices)
- Seeded Possibility requires showing both surface tragedy AND underground transmission
- Too short (flash/short) would collapse to pure tragedy
- Too long (novella) would risk overexplaining

### POV
**Selected: FIRST PERSON (Yao) + THIRD LIMITED (Chen)**

**Justification:**
- First person for Condemned voice (immediacy, interiority, no analytical distance)
- Third limited for Witness voice (observational, can report what Chen sees without accessing what he doesn't yet understand)
- POV shift signals voice shift (reader knows when they're inside Yao vs. observing through Chen)

### Tense
**Selected: PRESENT (Yao) + PAST (Chen framing)**

**Justification:**
- Present tense for Yao = immediacy, trapped in moment, no future horizon
- Past tense for Chen = retrospective, already knows outcome, positioning for future
- Tense shift reinforces temporal difference (Yao's immediate vs. Chen's biographical)

### Character Count
**Selected: 3 PRIMARY (Yao, Chen, Parents) + 1 SECONDARY (Liu)**

**Justification:**
- Minimum for constraint interactions (need Yao/Chen for C1 transmission, Parents for C3 enforcement, system for C2)
- Liu appears briefly (represents "successful" fabricator, contrast to Yao, shows system works for some)
- Tight focus maintains intimacy, prevents sprawl

### Naming Strategy
**Selected: CULTURAL (Chinese names with station nicknames)**

**Justification:**
- Naturalizes setting (Sino-European consortium, Mandarin-derived Cant)
- Yao = 老药 (Lǎo Yào, "Old Yao") — nickname shows age, respect, distance
- Chen = 陈明 (Chén Míng) — formal name, youth, not yet earned nickname
- Liu = 刘师傅 (Liú Shīfu, "Master Liu") — title shows status
- Parents unnamed in Yao's sections (he doesn't know them), named in Chen's sections (陈's parents)

### Linguistic Implementation

**Code-switching signals:**

1. **Yao alone (Cant):**
   - Self-talk, internal monologue
   - Intimacy with self when no audience
   - *"Gongyi bu shi guize. Shi ni de ming."* (Craftsmanship isn't rules. It's your life.)

2. **Yao filing reports (English):**
   - Performance of compliance
   - Institutional language, no intimacy
   - "Module 7-449-B meets structural specifications."

3. **Chen with Yao (Cant):**
   - Solidarity, apprentice-master bond
   - *"Shifu, ni lei ma?"* (Master, are you tired?)

4. **Chen with parents (English):**
   - Hierarchy enforcement
   - Formal, careful, no negotiation
   - "I understand the reasoning."

5. **Parents with each other (Cant):**
   - Intimacy, partnership
   - *"Haizi bu dong."* (The child doesn't understand.)

6. **Parents to Chen about decision (English):**
   - Authority, non-negotiable
   - "You will transfer to Fabricator Liu's apprenticeship."

**Code-switching reveals:**
- Relationship dynamics (who has power, who's intimate)
- Emotional states (Cant = warmth/solidarity, English = distance/control)
- Constraint activation (English marks C3 enforcement)

## 5. PRIMARY PHYSICAL MARKER

**Selected: Yao's hands checking weld seams in the dark**

**Why this marker:**

1. **Emerges from inhabitation sentence:**
   > "You are a craftsman in a system that measures worth by numbers on a screen, and the only language that is yours is the one you speak to the boy you taught, who is being taken from you because the system says you are unlucky."
   
   - The hands = craftsmanship (C1, discipline)
   - In the dark = isolation (C2, reputation system has removed his apprentice, his light)
   - Checking seams = maintaining discipline when no one watches (identity, not performance)

2. **Reveals constraint experience from inside:**
   - Yao's hands are old, scarred, adapted to microgravity
   - He checks welds by touch because Bay 12's lighting flickers
   - The precision is muscle memory, decades of practice
   - No one sees this work — it's for himself, for the discipline

3. **Reader understands before character:**
   - Reader sees: Yao's skill is real, ratings are wrong
   - Yao experiences: I am unlucky, my work fails
   - Gap = indexical variance (Yao trapped in Type I error)

4. **Chen observes and inherits:**
   - Chen watches Yao's hands, learns the touch-checking technique
   - Later, Chen checks his own welds the same way
   - Physical transmission of discipline (underground transformation)

5. **Contrast with Liu's work:**
   - Liu's bay has bright lights, automated testing, computational aids
   - Liu doesn't check by hand — trusts the machines
   - Same work, different experience (power differential materialized)

**Marker progression through story:**

- **Opening (Chen POV):** Chen notices Yao's hands moving in the dim light, precise despite the cold
- **Section 1 (Yao POV):** Yao checks a weld, feels the seam, knows it's good
- **Section 2 (Yao POV):** Module fails inspection (not the weld — different component), but Yao's hands shake as he files the report
- **Section 3 (Yao POV):** Yao works alone, hands steady again, checking seams no one will see
- **Closing (Chen POV):** Chen's hands check a weld in Liu's bright bay, using Yao's technique

**The marker is NOT:**
- A designed motif deployed for aesthetic effect
- A symbol explained by the narrator
- A metaphor stated explicitly

**The marker IS:**
- What the body does when the system measures worth by numbers
- What remains when everything else is taken
- What gets transmitted when language fails
