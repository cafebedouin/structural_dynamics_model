EMERGENT ELEMENTS:

Three axes surface in the engine reports, all converging on the same beneficiary (foma_silovich) and all sharing the "constructed_high_extraction" signature masked as coordination (rope/tangled_rope) when read institutionally, but reading as snare from powerless, moderate, and analytical seats.

**Axis 1: victim_self_attribution_foreclosure.** Marfa's inherited vocabulary — "a fault of her own thinness, not as a message from anywhere outside herself" — forecloses the very thought that would let her report harm. This is alive everywhere in her sections: the shame at the stove, the refusal to write her mother, the threshold-keeping performed for Xenia while shaking under her shawl. The gap: Marfa experiences this as her own inadequacy (powerless seat: snare — she is being extracted from with no exit); the institutional seat (Prov Andreyevich, the *sobor*) reads the identical facts as a well-run household bearing hardship (rope — legitimate coordination). The engine's H^1=3 split is exactly dramatized: three observers (Marfa, Praskovya at her most honest, the analytical reader) see extraction; one structural position (the council, the marriage-contract logic) sees order.

**Axis 2: diagnostic_taxonomy_blind_spot.** Praskovya's forty-one deliveries and fifteen years of pattern-memory contain no drawer for "the pot, not the marsh." Struve's Dorpat training runs every category it has and finds none for "husband feeding wife" as a vector. This is the axis most explicitly staged as an epistemic structure: two trained observers, one folk and one institutional, both hit the identical wall from different angles and both file the case as within normal variance. The blind spot isn't stupidity — it's the completeness of the wrong map.

**Axis 3: virtue_performance_as_exculpation.** Foma's legibility as a good husband — the red wool, the fish, the broth timed for the doctor's visit, taking over the water-carrying — functions simultaneously as (possibly) genuine care and as evidentiary cover. The engine's "victims=distributed" tag on this constraint (versus "concentrated" on the other two) is worth noting: the servant-girl at the other steading, filed under "the same damp," suggests the performance-as-exculpation logic isn't unique to this house but is a community-wide inferential rule, cheap to fake and never discounted for that cheapness.

**Where the axis is most alive:** the doctor Struve's ledger entry — "Attributable to prolonged exposure, damp climate, and constitutional weakness of the patient" — is the single sentence where all three axes lock together: taxonomy blind spot (no category for the vector), virtue performance (his private approval of Foma's broth-timing), and self-attribution foreclosure (the diagnosis assigns fault to the patient's own constitution).

**Where underdeveloped:** the servant-girl subplot (three steadings over) gestures at the "distributed victims" structure but is compressed into two paragraphs and never returns. The engine's cross-constraint report treats this as a live convergence set (three constraints sharing a beneficiary); the prose only substantiates the convergence for Marfa's household. The parallel case is there but not pressed.

LATENT POTENTIAL:

The Frankl question lands hardest on Praskovya, not Marfa. Marfa's meaning-making is foreclosed by design — that's the point, and the story is right not to give her a breakthrough. But Praskovya has the folk-saying ("some fevers come from the pot, not the marsh"), says it aloud twice, to no one, and dismisses it both times as superstition unworthy of a woman who has learned to "tell one fever from another by better means." The story stops just short of asking what it would cost her to say it to Struve, or to Foma's face, or to write it down somewhere. That refusal — not ignorance, but the deliberate silencing of her own correct intuition because it lacks institutional standing — is the richest unexploited vein in the piece. The abductive triggers (maxent_shadow_divergence, classical_oracle_failure) point exactly here: Praskovya is the confident oracle who, examined from her single position, looks thorough and right; only cross-position comparison (her own two visits, a year apart, to two different houses with the same grey nail-beds) reveals what she's refusing to integrate.

The remarriage in the last line ("Foma Silovich remarried within two years... kept, by every account that reached Praskovya's ears in the seasons after, a good house") is the loudest unexploited gesture in the story — it implies recurrence, a second cycle beginning exactly where the first one closed, and the story ends one beat before Praskovya would have to decide whether to notice.

The servant-girl case is the clearest structural echo the story declines to develop into full parallel: same healer, same grey, same dismissal-by-filing, different household. If the piece wanted to argue system rather than individual monster, this is where that argument lives, currently in embryo.

STRONGEST WRITING:

- The opening: "The stove had been lit since matins and still her hands would not warm." Establishes the whole cosmology of the piece — warmth as measurable, provided, documented, and utterly beside the point — in one sentence.

- "There was nothing wrong in her house. There was only her, wrong, in it." This is the foreclosure axis at its most exact and most devastating; it does in one line what the engine spends pages formalizing as "victim_self_attribution_foreclosure."

- The grief-with-no-object passage: "a grief with nothing to be sad *at*... the grief simply sat in her chest with no direction to travel, the way water sits in a low place with no outlet, going stagnant because it cannot find the sea." The water metaphor doing double duty — her illness and her unnamable grief share one image — is the piece's best formal move.

- Praskovya's unattached unease, stated with total precision: "What she could not tell you... was why none of the six fit." This is a healer's competence and its exact limit rendered in a single clause.

- Struve's diagnostic paragraph and ledger entry, quoted above — the clinical voice convicting itself without knowing it.

- "no version of him that existed only for her" — buried mid-paragraph, easy to miss, doing enormous work about the marriage's structural coldness without a single dramatic scene to carry it.

The story contains factual quantities (forty-one children delivered, fifteen years of practice, two versts, three steadings, six women with matching nail-beds) but these function as characterization of Praskovya's accumulated competence, not as a descending/ascending meter driving dramatic tension. Scanned specifically: the "forty-one children... buried perhaps a third" line and the "six women in fifteen years" line — both are texture, not countdown.

INVARIANT PROBE:
This is the central finding. Every instrument in the story — folk medicine, trained institutional medicine, ecclesiastical authority, the community's inferential rule about visible caretaking — converges on the same wrong answer, and the story gives no character, however placed, a vantage from which the true mechanism becomes legible in principle. Praskovya comes closest and is explicitly shown *declining* the correct frame ("some fevers come from the pot, not the marsh") because her own standards of evidence disqualify it. But nothing in the text suggests the truth is permanently unreadable — only that it requires an instrument (chemical toxicology, a category for caregiver-as-vector, a witness willing to compare cases across households) that does not yet exist in this world but is not depicted as impossible in principle. Struve's training could in principle be extended; Praskovya's filing system could in principle grow a new drawer. The engine's own report says as much: it repeatedly frames this as "extraction masked by perspective" and "coordination_washing" — a taxonomy gap, not an ontological one. So the honest answer here is the second branch: **everything of value in this world is, in principle, readable by a better instrument or a fairer authority.** The poison (if it is poison) is chemically detectable by later-arriving forensic science; the exculpatory performance is discoverable by a community willing to compare notes across households; the self-attribution foreclosure could be interrupted by an outside party asking the right blunt question. Nothing in the text gestures at a truth that would remain dark even to a perfected observer. This absence — no unrecoverable remainder — is worth naming plainly rather than dressing up as ambiguity.

SCOPE ASSESSMENT:
Word count: 3,179 (actual, computed). This is a short story, and a well-proportioned one for its scope — it does not read as either overlong or thin at this length, though the multi-viewpoint structure (Marfa / Praskovya / Struve / the settlement's social read) is doing work usually given more room in a novella. Compression sacrifice: the servant-girl parallel and the ecclesiastical-authority context (Prov Andreyevich) are both compressed to single beats; either could be expanded into a full parallel thread. Expansion would require either (a) a second full case (the servant-girl, or the eventual second wife) developed to the same depth as Marfa's, making the systemic-recurrence argument explicit rather than a closing implication, or (b) deepening Praskovya's interiority around her dismissed intuition into a fuller throughline — currently she disappears for stretches while Struve and the social apparatus take over. The piece is functioning at short-story scale; pushing toward novella would mean committing to the recursion (this happens again, elsewhere, to someone else) as the actual subject rather than as a one-line coda.
