REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The three viewpoint sections build a genuinely layered picture of complicity — Ossit's procedural pride, Iss's professional habit of reading documents, Meret's bodily calendar of the ache — and each register stays distinct without announcing itself. The physical detail is doing real work: Sarn's hands stilling and then fumbling the match, the drawer opening and closing in the dark, the yoke-grooves in the shoulder are earned images rather than illustrations bolted onto a theme. Iss's ending lands on a genuine reversal — not repeating the choice but recognizing, mid-repetition, how little resistance her body offers it — which gives the close weight without a summarizing line.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The story tips its hand explicitly at least twice — "she had never once needed her own silence to be blameless" and "it had never needed a mark to be real" — sentences that state the invariant's logic almost verbatim rather than letting the reader arrive at it through the Notch, the plat, and the drawer. These read as the thesis leaking through the gesture-trust the rest of the piece otherwise earns.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. No character or instrument in the story recovers a "true" elevation the Notch got wrong — Trest is meticulous and correct every year, the survey is honest, and Iss's suspicion about the tent-camp origin is explicitly unprovable ("She had no proof this was what happened here... left no motive on record"). The story keeps the origin-choice permanently outside the reach of any better measurement, which is exactly what the contract requires.

BREAK:
The expectation violated is the corrected-injustice arc: a reader watching Iss accumulate the archive evidence, the plat, the pattern of names naturally expects a scene where she says the thing aloud and the valley's frame shifts. Instead she says nothing to Coyt that changes anything structural — she chooses, once, to plant a seed ("Someone had to") rather than deliver proof, and the story closes on her repeating the old silence with Ossit's parallel machinery humming underneath. This matches the contract's target_prior closely: the story never lets anyone imagine a recalibrated system would fix things — it insists the zero-point's origin is the wrong kind of object to be fixed by adjustment. Break named and met.

READINESS:
Close — one more pass to cut the two thesis-adjacent lines (or bury them further into behavior/image) would bring the prose to publishable clarity.
