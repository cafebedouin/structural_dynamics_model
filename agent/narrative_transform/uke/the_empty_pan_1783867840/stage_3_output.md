## 2. VOICE ARCHETYPE SELECTION

### Primary Voice: **The Condemned** (Kaaro)

**Justification:**
- Inside C₃ (certified assay), cannot see systemic shape
- Acts rationally given structural position (drills, extracts, files reports)
- Experiences constraint as inevitable — "the scanner is what pays me"
- Voice is immediate, experiential, body-centered
- **Tone:** Tragic, somber
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility (Condemned's knowledge seeds underground transformation)
- **Requirements met:** PIR model (C₃ from powerless index), Mountain-dominated upstream (C₁, C₂), powerless position (π=1.5)
- **POV:** Close third person (inhabits Kaaro's sensory world without explaining it)

**Why this works:** Kaaro knows the grain exists — his hands read it — but cannot prove it because proof requires the instrument that lies to him. His knowledge is embodied, not analytical. The voice never explains "this is extraction" or "the system is unjust." It reports: *You drill into rock that your hands read as rich, and the scanner certifies it as marginal.* The gap between body-knowledge and certified number is the story's engine.

### Secondary Voice: **The Beneficiary** (Yuki)

**Justification:**
- Inside C₃, benefits from it (stable CSA salary, professional identity), genuinely cannot see the extraction
- From this index C₃ reads as coordination: the scanner enables the market, miners get paid, ore moves
- Voice is warm, competent, unselfconscious — the reader should *like* Yuki
- Does not signal awareness of privilege; there is nothing to signal because from here there is nothing to see
- **Tone:** Assured, procedural (produces dark comedy through structural blindness)
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility (Beneficiary's blindness is part of what the seed must eventually confront)
- **Requirements met:** Moderate power (π=1.0), false CI_rope signature (C₃ reads as Tangled Rope from this index), identity-locked exit
- **POV:** Close third person (must be inside Yuki's comfort to feel the dissonance)

**Why this works:** Yuki runs the scanner, files certifications, trusts the calibration protocol. When miners complain the numbers feel wrong, Yuki rechecks the calibration — which passes, because the calibration is against the reference standard that produced the problem. The voice is not ironic. Yuki genuinely believes the system works. The horror comes from the reader seeing what Yuki cannot: that Yuki's professional competence is the mechanism of Kaaro's extraction.

### Tertiary Voice: **The Witness** (Amara)

**Justification:**
- Observes C₃ dynamics without direct stake in the system
- Can see multiple indices simultaneously (has data from forty asteroid bodies)
- Reports systematic divergence without judgment or intervention
- Voice is analytical, documentary, clinical
- **Tone:** Neutral, distanced
- **Attractor compatibility:** ✓ Seeded Possibility (Witness documents the knowledge that seeds transformation)
- **Requirements met:** Analytical index, multiple character indices to contrast
- **POV:** Third limited (Amara's analytical perspective, not omniscient)

**Why this works:** Amara has seventeen published papers proving the purity index doesn't predict smelting yield. The CSA has never responded. Amara's voice provides the structural analysis the other voices cannot — but crucially, Amara is *outside* the Belt, cannot intervene, can only document. The witness is helpless, which prevents the voice from becoming a Guide or System voice.

### Voice Combination Strategy: **Counterpoint Voice**

Three voices at different power scales observing the same physical markers:
- **Kaaro (Condemned):** Feels the grain in his hands, sees the scanner output a number that contradicts what he knows
- **Yuki (Beneficiary):** Sees the scanner output a number, trusts it, files the certification
- **Amara (Witness):** Sees the statistical divergence between certified index and smelting yield, documents it, cannot intervene

The reader performs the extraction math. No narrator explains it.

## 3. INDEXICAL REVELATION STRATEGY

**Selected Strategy:** Multi-POV Parallel

**Structure:**
- Alternate between Kaaro, Yuki, and Amara across chapters
- Reader sees indexical variance before characters do
- Each voice reveals its structural position through action and perception, not explanation
- Collision points: Same physical events (a disputed assay, a calibration check) experienced from different indices

**Revelation Sequence:**
1. **Chapters 1-3:** Establish each voice's baseline experience of C₃
   - Kaaro: Drill, scan, number contradicts hands
   - Yuki: Run scanner, file certification, trust calibration
   - Amara: Analyze smelting data, document divergence, submit paper
2. **Chapters 4-6:** Collision — a specific disputed seam
   - Kaaro: Seam certified at 38 (marginal), feels like 50+ (commercial)
   - Yuki: Recalibrates scanner, certification stands, files report
   - Amara: Receives smelting data from that seam, confirms Kaaro's intuition, CSA ignores it
3. **Chapters 7-9:** Attractor approach
   - Kaaro: Continues extracting, knowledge accumulates but cannot be acted on
   - Yuki: Promoted to senior operator, never questions the standard
   - Amara: Archives research, seeds it for future institutional shift

**Key Principle:** No character ever gains full system visibility. Kaaro never reads Amara's papers. Yuki never meets Kaaro. Amara never visits the Belt. The reader constructs the full picture from partial perspectives.

## 4. EDITORIAL DECISIONS

**LENGTH:** Novelette (12,000-15,000 words)
- Justification: Three POVs, nine chapters (three per character), sufficient space for indexical variance to emerge without overextending

**POV:** Third person limited, rotating
- Close third for Kaaro and Yuki (inhabit their sensory/emotional worlds)
- Analytical third for Amara (documentary register, not intimate)

**TENSE:** Past tense
- Justification: Allows retrospective framing for Amara's sections (research published, CSA non-response documented)

**CHARACTER COUNT:** 5 primary (Kaaro, Yuki, Amara, Chen, Olafsson)
- Kaaro, Yuki, Amara: POV characters
- Chen: Appears in one chapter (waste-rock dispute, powerless/immediate horizon)
- Olafsson: Appears in one chapter (adjudicating calibration dispute, institutional position)

**NAMING:** Cultural (heritage names preserved)
- Kaaro Okonkwo, Yuki Tanaka, Amara Okafor, Chen Wei, Director Olafsson
- Justification: Code-switching strategy requires cultural specificity; names signal heritage language contexts

**LINGUISTIC IMPLEMENTATION:**
- **Technical English** for all system interactions (assay reports, certifications, CSA communications)
- **Heritage languages** for private/intimate contexts (Igbo for Kaaro, Japanese for Yuki, Mandarin for Chen)
- **Code-switch signals:** Italics for non-English speech, no translation provided (context makes meaning clear)
- **Switching marks power:** Powerless agents switch for emotional release; moderate agents use Technical English even off-shift; institutional agents switch only in private contexts

**EMOTIONAL CORE:** (Emerges from constraint dynamics)
- Kaaro: Grief at being paid in a number that misrepresents his labor
- Yuki: Confidence in professional competence that is structurally blind
- Amara: Exhaustion at documenting truth that institutions ignore

**ENDING STRATEGY:** Seeded Possibility
- Surface: C₃ persists (certified assay continues, Kaaro's next rotation begins, Yuki gets promoted)
- Underground: Amara's research archived, Kaaro's knowledge embodied, waiting for institutional shift that may never come

## 5. INHABITATION SPEC

### Kaaro (Condemned - powerless/trapped)

**Phenomenological register:** Hypervigilance to material resistance
- What the body learned: Rock that crumbles wrong in your hands will underperform in the smelter, regardless of what the scanner says
- Physical marker: The *texture* of ore under the drill bit — gritty vs. smooth, the way it fractures, the sound it makes
- Mode of attention: Kaaro notices what the scanner cannot measure — the *feel* of the rock, the resistance pattern, the way dust settles

**Inhabitation sentence:**
You press your drill to stone, and the bit catches on something the scanner will never read — a grain structure your hands recognize but cannot name, and the certified number will ignore it, and your paycheck will be calculated from the lie.

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ Cannot be expressed as a single number changing over time. This is sensation, not metric.

---

### Yuki (Beneficiary - moderate/identity-locked)

**Phenomenological register:** Procedural confidence
- What the body learned: Trust the instrument, trust the calibration, deviations are operator error
- Physical marker: The *rhythm* of scanner operation — place sample, initiate scan, wait for tone, read output, file certification
- Mode of attention: Yuki notices procedural correctness — whether the protocol was followed, whether the calibration passed, whether the certification is filed on time

**Inhabitation sentence:**
You place the sample in the resonance chamber, you initiate the scan, you hear the confirmation tone, you read the output, you file the certification — the system works because you make it work, and the miners' complaints are noise against the signal of your professional competence.

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ This is procedural attention, not measurement. Yuki does not count; Yuki *operates*.

---

### Amara (Witness - analytical/universal)

**Phenomenological register:** Pattern recognition across datasets
- What the body learned: Systematic divergence appears in aggregate, not in individual cases
- Physical marker: The *gap* between certified purity index and actual smelting yield, visible only in statistical analysis
- Mode of attention: Amara notices what emerges from comparison — the same certified index producing different yields, the pattern that no single assay reveals

**Inhabitation sentence:**
You overlay forty datasets, and the divergence appears — not in any single case, but in the aggregate, a systematic gap between what the scanner certifies and what the smelter yields, and you publish it, and the CSA does not respond, and the market continues to price ore based on the number that lies.

**Anti-pattern check:** ✓ This is pattern recognition, not tallying. Amara sees *structure*, not counts.

## 6. NUMERIC REGISTER

### Diegetic Quantities
**Only where topology demands an instrument:**
- Purity index (0-100 scale, output by resonance scanner)
- Certified tonnage (mass of commercial-grade ore, calculated from purity index)
- Compensation figures (base salary + tonnage bonus, appears on payment statements)

**Not diegetic:**
- Time (no countdowns, no "three months remaining")
- Emotional intensity (no "anger level 7/10")
- Physical exhaustion (no "energy depleted by 40%")

### Positional Access
**Kaaro (powerless):**
- Can read: Purity index (when scanner outputs it), certified tonnage (on payment statement)
- Cannot read: Reference standard values, calibration protocols, CSA dispute records
- **Narration rule:** Numbers appear only when Kaaro ACTS on them — reads the scanner output aloud, checks his payment statement, disputes a certification

**Yuki (moderate):**
- Can read: Purity index (operates the scanner), calibration status (daily check), certification logs
- Cannot read: Reference standard establishment history, CSA policy documents, aggregate smelting data
- **Narration rule:** Numbers appear only when Yuki ACTS on them — runs a scan, files a certification, recalibrates the instrument

**Amara (analytical):**
- Can read: Aggregate smelting data (research access), published purity indices (public record), historical ore prices
- Cannot read: Individual miner compensation, real-time scanner outputs, CSA internal communications
- **Narration rule:** Numbers appear only when Amara ACTS on them — analyzes a dataset, publishes a figure, submits a paper

### The Narration Rule
**Narration is never denominated in the system's numbers.** A number may appear only when a character with access ACTS ON IT in-scene — reads it aloud, disputes it, files it, analyzes it. Never as:
- Ambient texture ("The purity index hovered at 42")
- Countdown ("Three months until rotation end")
- Tally ("He had extracted 47 tons")
- Emotional beat ("Her frustration reached 8/10")

**Exemplar test applied to this blueprint:**
- ✓ No voice note or scene sketch models a narrator performing a tally
- ✓ All planned number appearances are in-scene actions (Kaaro reading scanner output, Yuki filing certification, Amara publishing a figure)

## 9. ERROR MANIFESTATIONS

### Error E₂: Type III — Snare-as-Rope (Yuki)

**Scene (Chapter 5):**
Yuki re-scans seam 447-D after Kaaro's dispute. Scanner outputs 38.6 (within margin of error of original 38.4). Yuki recalibrates scanner against reference standard — calibration passes. Files dispute resolution: "Certification stands. Scanner calibrated per protocol."

**Observable:**
Yuki experiences professional satisfaction. The system worked. The protocol was followed. The miner's complaint was resolved by demonstrating procedural correctness.

**What Yuki cannot see:**
- Kaaro's compensation just dropped by 40% because of a 0.2-point difference
- The reference standard Yuki calibrated against is the arbitrary foundation (C₂) that produces the systematic bias
- From Kaaro's index, this is extraction (χ=0.96, Snare). From Yuki's index, this is coordination (χ=0.64, Tangled Rope).

**Physical consequence:**
Kaaro receives payment statement showing marginal-grade tonnage bonus. Calculates the difference between what he was paid and what he should have been paid if the scanner had read the grain correctly. The number is material: it determines whether his family on Mars can afford medical care this quarter.

**Scene (Chapter 6, brief appearance):**
Olafsson reviews Amara's investigation request. Reads her data showing systematic divergence between certified purity index and smelting yield. Drafts response: "Smelting yield variations reflect processing error, not measurement error. Scanner calibration protocols are sound."

Olafsson genuinely believes this. The reference standard has enabled sixty years of Belt commerce. Ore that cannot be assayed cannot be traded. The purity index is the foundation of the market. Acknowledging the grain would delegitimize the index, which would collapse the market.

**What Olafsson cannot see:**
- The reference standard was established by committee vote in 2121, not scientific discovery
- The calibration protocol perpetuates the founding bias by calibrating against the same arbitrary standard
- From Kaaro's index, the system is extraction (χ=0.96, Snare). From Olafsson's index, the system is pure coordination (χ=-0.144, Rope — net beneficiary).

**Physical consequence:**
Amara's research is dismissed. No investigation occurs. The standard persists. Kaaro continues to be paid in numbers that misrepresent his labor. The institutional position cannot see the extraction because seeing it would end the institution.

## 13. IRRESOLVABLE TENSION TEST

**Identified tension:**
Yuki's professional competence is the mechanism of Kaaro's extraction. Yuki is not malicious. Yuki is good at her job. Yuki follows the protocol. Yuki trusts the calibration. And every certification Yuki files correctly is a number that misrepresents what Kaaro extracted.

**Why irresolvable:**
- Yuki cannot see the extraction without losing her professional identity (identity-locked exit)
- Kaaro cannot prove the extraction without the instrument that produces it (trapped in C₃)
- Amara can document the extraction but cannot intervene (witness position, no institutional authority)
- Olafsson cannot acknowledge the extraction without delegitimizing the standard that grants his authority (institutional position depends on standard being right)

**Reader position:**
The reader sees all four positions. The reader knows Yuki is wrong. The reader knows Kaaro is right. The reader knows Amara has proven it. The reader knows Olafsson will never acknowledge it. There is no comfortable resolution. The system persists not because anyone is evil, but because each position is structurally rational from its own index.

**Stage 4 directive:** Write toward this tension, not around it. The story must end with the reader unable to resolve it. Yuki gets promoted. Kaaro signs the next contract. Amara archives the research. The grain is known, the system persists.
