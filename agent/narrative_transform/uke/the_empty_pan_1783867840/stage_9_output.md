REVIEW ASSESSMENT

STRENGTHS:
The story's central mechanism—measurement destroying what it measures—is rendered through three distinct bodily registers that never converge into shared knowledge: Kaaro's hands reading density the scanner can't see, Yuki's procedural competence protecting her from data that would invalidate it, Amara's archival choice typed and deleted and retyped until the cost becomes visible in the rhythm itself. The pacing shifts earn their weight: the extended silence after "Then certify it wrong" makes the vacuum audible, the failed voice memo attempts land because they're physical acts (recording, cutting, trying again) rather than emotional states, and Amara's type-delete-retype pattern shows institutional capture as a muscular habit, not a philosophical position.

BIGGEST WEAKNESS:
The final section (Kaaro beginning his next rotation) reads as thematic summary rather than scene. "The grain was real. The numbers lied. And tomorrow, he would drill again" uses technical terms from the world, but the rhythm is conclusive—it tells the reader what the story demonstrated rather than letting the demonstration stand. The line would work as an epigraph; as an ending it feels like the story explaining itself.

INVARIANT FALSIFIER:
HOLDS. The story contains no recoverable true value. The grain exists but cannot be measured without destruction; Amara's data proves systematic divergence but cannot specify what any individual seam "should have" certified at; Kaaro's hands know the rock is good but that knowledge has no institutional translation. The reference standard is revealed as arbitrary (set to serve specific technologies and economics) but no alternative standard is proposed—there is no neutral ground under the classification system. The real stays unreadable to the system throughout.

READINESS:
The prose is alive in its pressure points and the structural integrity holds completely. The final section weakens the ending by summarizing rather than landing, but this is a minor flaw in an otherwise rigorous piece. One more pass could address the conclusive rhythm of the final lines without requiring structural changes.

ROUTE: VALIDATION
