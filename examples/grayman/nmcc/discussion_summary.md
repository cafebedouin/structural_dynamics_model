## Situation

Maxwell's equations exhibit exact electric-magnetic duality: exchange E↔B, apply sign convention, equations unchanged. The Standard Model contains one sector built on electric charge — electron, quarks, protons, atoms, chemistry. Magnetic monopoles remain unobserved despite 93 years of search since Dirac's 1931 theoretical foundation.

## Complication

The asymmetry runs deeper than non-observation. Standard monopole theories propose single particle types: Dirac monopoles, GUT monopoles, topological defects. Each treats the magnetic sector as producing one object where the electric sector produces dozens. Abundance calculations predict detectable flux. Experiments find nothing. The community concluded the symmetry is decorative rather than physical — not because the math requires this, but because nobody built a full sector and checked the consequences.

## CPT Constraint

CPT theorem requires particle and antiparticle to share identical mass — no exceptions. Every prior monopole model treats north and south monopoles as CPT partners. This assumption is so deeply embedded that it has never been stated as an assumption: it simply defines how monopole experiments are designed and what mass ranges they target.

But genuine distinct species are not antiparticles. The NMCC framework proposes a magnetic lepton and a magnetic hadron as separate particles from a real second sector. Mass inequality between them is not a free parameter to be tuned — it is a logical requirement of the framework's structure.

Therefore the CPT argument functions as a discriminator against the entire prior experimental program. ## Chirality Resolution

The weak force violates electric-magnetic duality directly. Weak interactions couple only to left-handed fermions. A naive magnetic mirror would require left-handed magnetic fermions, which breaks the exchange symmetry and explains why no one has built the full sector.

But parity-violating sectors restore global parity through mirroring. Left-right symmetric models — Pati-Salam structure — demonstrate the mechanism. The electric sector is left-chiral. The magnetic sector is right-chiral. Global parity invariance holds at GUT level.

Therefore magnetic weak bosons do not couple to electric weak bosons. The sectors remain isolated except through gravity and Higgs-sector interactions. This explains electromagnetic invisibility while maintaining theoretical consistency. The symmetry breaking that made the sector look impossible is actually the mechanism that makes it stable.

## Distinction From Prior Models

Dirac (1931) proposed single monopoles with quantized charge. No sector structure. No compositeness. No mass hierarchy.

Vento et al. (2021) proposed monopolium — monopole-antimonopole bound states that are electromagnetically inert and gravitationally detectable. This is precisely what NMCC is not. Monopolium annihilates. NMCC persists because its two components are distinct species, not antiparticles.

Terning and Verhaaren (2019) built dark monopoles requiring new gauge forces. Dark photons. Hidden sectors. Additional coupling constants. NMCC requires none of this.

Therefore NMCC occupies genuinely unexplored theoretical territory: full sector, lepton-hadron hierarchy, no new forces, cosmological and planetary consequences emergent from existing physics.
